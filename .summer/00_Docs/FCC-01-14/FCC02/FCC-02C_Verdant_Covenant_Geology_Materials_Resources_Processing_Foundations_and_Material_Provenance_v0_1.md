# 1. Purpose

FCC-02C defines the authoritative Verdant Covenant canon for:

- geology and terrain-material foundations;
- principal realm resources;
- ordinary and specialist material families;
- raw-resource provenance;
- renewable, regenerative, finite, and conditional extraction rules;
- material-state architecture;
- processing foundations;
- export and stabilisation requirements;
- resource eligibility for blocks, items, equipment, construction, machines, magic, and realm infrastructure;
- minor-resource reconciliation;
- duplicate-material prevention;
- provenance and validation requirements.

This document converts the older foundation-level Verdant resource lists into a coherent material ecology suitable for the final content catalogue.

It does **not** own:

- exact tree/flora biology or crop rosters - FCC-02D;
- exact creature sources or creature drops - FCC-02E;
- exact settlement industries - FCC-02F;
- final block/item definitions and recipe quantities - FCC-02G;
- detailed corruption transformations - FCC-02I;
- final stable-ID normalisation and art/material inheritance - FCC-02J and FCC-12/FCC-14.

# 2. Canonical Material Philosophy

The Verdant Covenant is not an "ore dimension" and must not be designed as one.

Its material identity is built from a deliberate mixture of:

- living wood;
- ordinary timber;
- biological fibre;
- sap and botanical catalysts;
- pollen/resin composites;
- living or memory-bearing seeds;
- root-geology mineral growth;
- ordinary local stone;
- aquatic minor resources;
- soils, silts, fungi, reeds, and specialist botanical reagents;
- imported metals and technologies where local cultures use them.

The realm has **no required native metal family**. Verdant industry is intentionally differentiated by living materials, biological infrastructure, specialised fibres, amber-like optics, root-grown minerals, and imported or traded metalwork.

The guiding rule is:

> **A material exists because its physical, ecological, magical, cultural, or gameplay role is distinct - not because every biome or realm needs its own version of every material class.**

A named material must therefore have at least one justified provenance, physical identity, processing path, and gameplay role.

# 4. Canonical Material Tiers

Verdant materials are classified by content role rather than arbitrary rarity alone.

## 4.1 Terrain Foundations

Common world-forming materials:

- Verdant Living Soil;
- Rootstone;
- riverbed silt and wetland substrates;
- worldroot/root mass;
- ordinary water through the universal fluid system;
- organic litter, fungal substrate, and related biome-state materials where needed.

## 4.2 Ordinary Botanical Construction Materials

- Greatheart Wood;
- Dawnwood;
- Bloomwood.

These are genuine timber families and may support normal derived construction forms where their physical properties justify them.

## 4.3 Principal Specialist Realm Resources

- Living Heartwood;
- Season Sap;
- Thornsilver Fibre;
- Pollen Amber;
- Memory Seed;
- Bloomstone.

These define the distinctive material progression of the realm.

## 4.4 Minor Realm Resources

Minor resources may support trade, food, alchemy, ritual, ecological crafting, decorative work, specialist machinery, or local progression without becoming major construction families.

Canonical examples include:

- River Pearl;
- Root Amber;
- botanical reagents owned by FCC-02D;
- selected creature products owned by FCC-02E;
- seasonal fruit, nectar, fungi, reeds, herbs, and similar resources where they have real uses.

## 4.5 Crafted / Processed Specialist Products

Examples include:

- Living Heartwood billet and joinery forms;
- Seasonal Resin;
- refined Season Sap vial or reservoir forms;
- Thornsilver Cord;
- Thornsilver Lattice;
- Oath-thread;
- Pollen Amber Lens;

- Memory Seed Capsule;
- Bloomstone Stabiliser Core;
- Verdant Anti-Blight Treatment;
- Covenant Heart portal keystone/core;
- realm-specific diplomatic or oath offerings.

These are **products**, not new raw-material families unless a later reconciliation proves a distinct substance genuinely exists.

# 5. Geology of the Verdant Covenant

## 5.1 Geological Identity

Verdant geology is shaped by long-term interaction between:

- ordinary mineral strata;
- colossal root systems;
- water movement;
- organic deposition;
- seasonal magical pressure;
- living infrastructure;
- local realm law;
- restoration or blight history;
- rare cross-realm or Void disturbance.

The realm must not imply that every stone is alive or magical.

The baseline geological palette contains an ordinary local stone foundation plus specialist root-transformed mineral growths.

## 5.2 Rootstone

**Rootstone** is the canonical ordinary native stone family of the Verdant Covenant.

### Identity

Rootstone is a common geological stone strongly associated with ancient root systems but is not itself a premium magical material.

It may display:

- root staining;
- mineral veining;
- compacted organic inclusions;
- moisture variation;
- pressure lines from root growth;
- minor realm resonance.

These features do not automatically turn it into Bloomstone.

### Role

Rootstone supplies:

- foundations;
- ordinary masonry;
- roads and retaining structures;
- walls;
- drainage works;
- settlement construction;
- non-premium dungeon stonework;
- support for living architecture.

### Derived Eligibility

Valid families include, subject to FCC-02G final catalogue:

- Rootstone block;
- Cut Rootstone;
- Rootstone Brick;
- Rootstone Slab;
- Rootstone Stair;
- Rootstone Wall;
- Rootstone Pillar;
- selected trim or foundation forms.

Rootstone must not be artificially given optical, fibre, catalyst, memory-storage, or high-level stabilisation functions simply because it is realm-native.

## 5.3 Bloomstone Formation Zones

Bloomstone deposits occur where large or ancient living root systems interact with suitable magical geology.

Formation suitability may depend on:

- root age and vitality;
- local mineral chemistry;
- moisture;
- seasonal state;
- long-term magical flux;
- biome family;
- restoration history;
- extraction history.

This creates a geological-biological boundary material rather than a conventional ore vein.

## 5.4 Riverlands Geology

Living Riverlands may contain:

- silt beds;
- Rootstone channels;
- clay-like local sediments where globally supported;
- organic bank layers;
- freshwater gravel;
- living reed mats;
- minor pearl-bearing aquatic habitats.

The universal water/fluid system remains authoritative for water behaviour.

## 5.5 Root Depth Geology

Dreaming Root Depths may expose:

- deep Rootstone;
- compressed root mass;
- fossilised root material;
- Root Amber;
- buried Bloomstone growth;
- ancient trapped sediment;
- rare relic-bearing strata.

Non-standard local orientation affects placement and presentation but does not create an entirely separate geological material taxonomy.

# 6. Verdant Living Soil and Terrain Substrates

**Verdant Living Soil** is the canonical broad native soil family for the realm.

It is not a single identical block everywhere. Biome presentation may modify:

- moisture;
- organic content;
- root density;
- fertility;
- litter cover;
- fungal activity;
- seasonal state;
- blight state;
- corruption state.

These are normally states or profiles of a common substrate family rather than separate canonical materials.

Possible world forms include:

- fertile forest soil;
- meadow loam;
- wet riverbank soil;
- root-packed soil;
- rot-rich humus;
- dormant/frozen seasonal state;
- blighted soil;
- restored soil.

Void-corrupted soil is governed in detail by FCC-02I and may become a distinct material only after irreversible transformation passes the global transformation threshold.

# 7. Greatheart Wood

## 7.1 Source

**Greatheart Wood** is the ordinary timber obtained from the **Greatheart Tree**.

It is distinct from **Living Heartwood**.

A Greatheart Tree may contain:

- bark;
- ordinary Greatheart wood/sapwood;
- structural heartwood;
- under valid conditions, a rarer Living Heartwood core.

## 7.2 Material Role

Greatheart Wood is the Verdant Covenant's principal heavy structural timber.

Expected properties:

- high structural strength;
- good durability;
- suitability for beams and supports;
- good compatibility with living architecture;
- substantial weight relative to Bloomwood;
- less specialised magical interaction than Dawnwood;
- less active living behaviour than preserved Living Heartwood.

## 7.3 Processing State

Ordinary cutting may yield inert or conventionally living timber depending on the harvesting/process method.

Special Verdant harvesting and preservation may retain selected biological responsiveness.

This distinction must be represented through explicit material/process states rather than assuming every Greatheart plank remains magically alive.

## 7.4 Derived-Form Eligibility

Greatheart Wood is eligible for ordinary structural wood families such as:

- logs;
- stripped/processed logs where supported;
- planks;
- beams;
- posts;
- stairs;
- slabs;
- doors;
- trapdoors/shutters where supported;
- panels;
- structural furniture;
- selected bridge and infrastructure components.

The exact catalogue belongs to FCC-02G.

# 8. Living Heartwood

## 8.1 Source Conditions

**Living Heartwood** is the rare specialist inner material associated primarily with mature Greatheart Trees.

Its formation depends on combinations of:

- tree age;
- tree health;
- ecological conditions;
- realm magic;
- long-term growth conditions;
- local stewardship;
- biome suitability.

Not every Greatheart Tree contains the same quantity or quality.

Young, damaged, diseased, heavily exploited, or poorly situated trees may contain little or no true Living Heartwood.

## 8.2 Harvest Philosophy

Living Heartwood must not be designed as generic "chop the rare tree" loot.

High-quality harvesting should favour:

- negotiated or recognised access;
- stewardship;
- managed pruning/extraction;
- naturally shed or exposed material where applicable;
- restoration-linked grants;
- approved cultivation;
- authority/faction outcomes;
- careful specialist tools and processes.

Destructive harvesting may:

- wound the source tree;
- reduce future yield;
- damage local ecology;
- affect reputation or Covenant state;
- produce lower-quality material;
- trigger defensive ecology;
- permanently remove regenerative capacity in severe cases.

Consent-like or recognised-harvest states are explicit gameplay states, not natural-language interpretation.

## 8.3 Physical and Magical Identity

Living Heartwood is a specialist structural biological material capable of retaining controlled living behaviour after harvesting when processed correctly.

Potential capabilities include:

- gradual repair under valid conditions;
- living joinery;
- compatibility with biological conduits;
- growth anchoring;
- integration with living buildings;
- specialised magical or realm-compatible construction;
- use in Covenant portal and high-tier Verdant infrastructure.

These capabilities must consume owning-system interfaces and must not imply unrestricted self-growing structures.

## 8.4 Processing Foundations

Canonical processed families include:

- Living Heartwood Billet;
- Living Heartwood Joinery;
- Living Heartwood Structural Core;
- selected living panels/beams/components;
- Covenant Heart ingredient role where FCC-02G validates the recipe.

## 8.5 Export

Living Heartwood may function outside the Verdant Covenant, but active living properties may require:

- adequate moisture;
- nutrients;
- mana or realm-compatible energy;
- Bloomstone stabilisation;
- Season Sap treatment;
- maintenance;
- compatible climate;
- purpose-built anchors.

Export does not automatically destroy the material; it changes the conditions required for active behaviour.

# 9. Dawnwood

**Dawnwood** is the timber family of the **Dawnwood Tree**, the major sun-bearing upper-canopy tree associated with intense light, fruit, sap, and radiant magical outputs.

- light;

- upper-canopy construction;

## 9.3 Derived-Form Eligibility

Dawnwood may support:

- logs;

# 10. Bloomwood

## 10.1 Source

**Bloomwood** is the timber family of the **Bloomwood Tree**, the major flowering tree associated with pollen, nectar, seasonal colour, flexible timber, and Pollen Amber ecology.

## 10.2 Material Role

Bloomwood is lighter and more flexible than Greatheart Wood.

Its preferred roles include:

- fine construction;
- decorative joinery;
- lightweight furniture;
- panels;
- containers;
- musical/instrument-like craft where appropriate;
- lightweight frames;
- ecological structures;
- support for pollinator infrastructure.

It should not outperform Greatheart in heavy structural load-bearing by default.

## 10.3 Pollen Amber Relationship

Bloomwood ecosystems are a principal source environment for resin/pollen interactions that create Pollen Amber.

Bloomwood itself is not Pollen Amber and does not inherit optical/inscription behaviour merely because they share ecology.

# 11. Season Sap

## 11.1 Canonical Identity

**Season Sap** is one canonical charged botanical fluid/resource family.

It is a realm-wide magical botanical phenomenon expressed through approved vegetation rather than being unique to one tree species.

Different plants and regions may produce Season Sap with different source states without creating separate raw-material families.

## 11.2 Required State Model

Season Sap may carry explicit metadata such as:

- seasonal charge: Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter;
- mixed/convergence charge;
- source species/category;
- source biome;
- purity;
- stability;
- concentration;
- resonance tag;
- contamination/blight state;
- preservation state.

The material remains **Season Sap**.

## 11.3 Historical Name Reconciliation

The following older terms do **not** become independent major raw materials:

- **Moon Sap** -> Moonroot/lunar-resonant Season Sap source/state;
- **Dream Sap** -> dream-resonant Season Sap source/state near Dreaming Root Depths;
- **Seasonal Sap variants** -> state values of Season Sap;
- **Seasonal Resin** -> hardened/processed Season Sap product where the recipe requires it.

## 11.4 Uses

Season Sap may support:

- catalyst recipes;
- biological machine fuel/input;
- living-structure maintenance;
- growth treatment;
- medicine/alchemy;
- seasonal activation;
- ritual processes;
- portal-component crafting;
- stabilisation support;
- environmental restoration.

## 11.5 Collection

Collection should occur through compatible taps, reservoirs, plant nodes, cultivation, or authorised harvest systems.

A source may become:

- active;
- dormant;
- depleted;
- seasonally shifted;
- damaged;
- restored.

Renewal rate depends on the source and ecology rather than infinite per-frame generation.

# 12. Thornsilver Fibre

## 12.1 Source Ecology

**Thornsilver Fibre** is a biologically grown flexible material produced through the symbiotic ecology of:

- **Thornsilver Vine**;
- **Thornsilver Weaver** and related approved ecological interactions.

The fibre should read as biologically produced material with metallic-like characteristics, not mined silver wire.

## 12.2 Material Behaviour

Thornsilver can exist in controlled flexibility states.

Depending on processing, it may remain:

- soft/flexible;
- corded;
- woven;
- tensioned;
- hardened;
- lattice-formed;
- conduit-ready.

It may visually resemble extremely fine silver-like living fibre while retaining an organic provenance.

## 12.3 Uses

Canonical use families include:

- cordage;
- textiles;
- reinforced fabric;
- armour components;
- bridge/tension members;
- bindings;
- living-machine components;
- conduits;
- ritual thread;
- Oath-thread products;
- flexible structural lattices.

## 12.4 Historical Name Reconciliation

**Oath-thread** is a crafted ritual product, not an independent raw-material family.

The default provenance is Thornsilver Fibre plus appropriate ritual or contextual processing.

# 13. Pollen Amber

## 13.1 Formation

**Pollen Amber** forms when compatible Verdant resin and pollen combine and naturally or deliberately harden under suitable conditions.

Bloomwood ecology is a principal formation environment.

It may occur as:

- hardened nodules;
- resin deposits;
- hive/nest-associated accumulations where ecologically justified;
- carefully cultivated deposits.

## 13.2 Visual-State Principle

Pollen Amber retains one canonical material identity while its tint may vary according to:

- plant source;
- season;
- pollen composition;
- clarity;
- ageing;
- contamination;
- treatment.

These are governed variations, not separate material names.

## 13.3 Uses

Pollen Amber supports:

- lenses;
- optical interfaces;
- magical inscription;
- inlays;
- preservation devices;
- map or perception tools;
- decorative high-value craft;
- selected magical-machine components.

It is not a general heavy structural material.

## 13.4 Historical Name Reconciliation

**Glamour Crystal** is retired as a required Verdant major material family.

Where an older design needs a Verdant glamour/optical component, the default solution is:

- Pollen Amber;
- Pollen Amber Lens;
- another already-canonical material if its function fits;
- or a future globally reconciled magical crystal only if FCC-12 proves a genuine gap.

No separate Glamour Crystal is created merely to preserve an old placeholder name.

# 14. Memory Seed

## 14.1 Identity

**Memory Seed** is a magical biological information-and-growth medium.

It can hold structured information such as:

- memories;
- plans;
- experiences;
- ecological information;
- growth patterns;
- authorised living-structure templates;
- selected environmental records.

Under valid conditions it may use stored information to guide growth or reconstruction of authorised living forms or structures.

It is not unlimited digital storage, unrestricted resurrection, or a universal duplication mechanism.

## 14.2 Source

Memory Seeds may form in several approved Verdant plants under special ecological, magical, cultural, or cultivated conditions.

There is no requirement for a separate full timber family solely called a "Memory Tree."

## 14.3 Intentional Encoding

Skilled Verdant cultivation can intentionally create or encode Memory Seeds.

Recording a living being's significant memories, identity-bearing information, or comparable protected content requires:

- consent;
- explicit authorised ritual/state;
- or another deliberately authored lawful exception.

The implementation must not continuously scrape NPC memories or world state into seeds.

## 14.4 Processing and Transport

Canonical processed forms may include:

- Memory Seed Capsule;
- growth anchor;
- archive planter;
- authorised blueprint/growth seed.

The capsule is a container/product, not a new raw material.

## 14.5 Fae Seed Reconciliation

**Fae Seed** is retired as an independent canonical material family.

The term may remain as:

- a generic cultural/category label for magical Verdant seeds;
- flavour text;
- a broad loot/category tag.

Concrete inventory content must use actual seed definitions such as Memory Seed or plant-specific seeds.

# 15. Bloomstone

## 15.1 Identity

**Bloomstone** is a hybrid geological-biological mineral formed where massive living root systems interact with magical geology.

It should visually and materially read as stone/mineral growth that appears to "bloom" around living root structures.

## 15.2 Resource Behaviour

Bloomstone can regenerate very slowly around healthy root systems when:

- extraction is controlled;
- the root network remains healthy;
- the local geology remains viable;
- the area is not overharvested;
- required ecological conditions persist.

Destructive mining may:

- damage the root network;
- permanently reduce yield;
- exhaust the deposit;
- destabilise local infrastructure;
- increase blight risk;
- change settlement/faction state where the deposit is protected.

It is therefore **conditionally regenerative**, not an ordinary infinite renewable node.

## 15.3 Uses

Bloomstone supports:

- stabilisation;
- realm-compatible infrastructure;
- portal support components;
- route anchors;
- wards;
- living machinery;
- imported living-material maintenance;
- selected masonry;
- structural interfaces between living and non-living construction.

## 15.4 Processed Forms

Canonical processed families may include:

- Bloomstone fragment/chunk;
- tuned Bloomstone;
- Bloomstone Stabiliser Core;
- Bloomstone anchor;
- selected cut/masonry forms where physically justified.

## 15.5 Seasonal Crystal Reconciliation

Old **Seasonal Crystal** references are not a new major mineral family by default.

The default FCC interpretation is:

> **season-charged Bloomstone growths carrying explicit seasonal state.**

FCC-12 may later split a truly distinct crystal family only if cross-realm material reconciliation demonstrates distinct chemistry, provenance, art identity, and gameplay need.

# 17. Root Amber

**Root Amber** is retained as a **minor fossilised-root reagent**, not a major material family.

It forms where ancient resins, root secretions, sediments, or related organic deposits become preserved within deep root/geological environments.

Potential roles include:

- alchemy;
- archaeological analysis;
- ritual reagents;
- limited inscription;
- historical/ecological clues;
- specialist device components.

Root Amber must not duplicate Pollen Amber's primary optical role or Bloomstone's stabilisation role.

Where an old "Root Amber" entry is actually resin+pollen amber by provenance, it should be classified as Pollen Amber instead.

# 18. Rotwood, Blighted Wood, and Corrupted Wood

## 18.1 Rotwood

**Rotwood** is normally a condition/state of compatible wood rather than a species or base material family.

A Greatheart, Dawnwood, Bloomwood, or other compatible wood can become rot-affected.

State may influence:

- durability;
- moisture;
- fungal load;
- structural integrity;
- fuel value;
- alchemy uses;
- restoration potential.

## 18.2 Blighted Wood

Ecological or magical blight is a separate condition from natural rot.

Blighted material may have:

- disease load;
- damaged growth behaviour;
- invasive magical effects;
- reduced living-system compatibility.

## 18.3 Void-Corrupted Wood

Void contamination is not synonymous with rot or blight.

Early contamination may exist as a reversible state.

Severe or ancient corruption may permanently transform matter into a genuinely distinct corrupted material definition where FCC-02I proves:

- new stable composition;
- different behaviour;
- different processing;
- different art identity;
- meaningful gameplay use.

**Corrupted Heartwood** is a permitted candidate distinct material after irreversible transformation. It is not automatically generated for every contaminated Living Heartwood node.

# 19. Covenant Heart

The **Covenant Heart** is not a naturally occurring raw material.

It is a crafted/installed high-value functional component and the canonical keystone/core associated with the **Covenant Portal**.

Its exact recipe and first-access acquisition path belong to FCC-02G.

The design intent is that its recipe should use a thematically coherent combination of already-canonical materials rather than inventing a new unexplained substance.

Likely ingredient roles may be fulfilled by materials such as:

- Living Heartwood;
- Bloomstone;
- Season Sap;
- appropriate Overworld-accessible components for first access;
- later upgraded portal components using native Verdant materials.

This list is a dependency direction, not an exact recipe.

The Covenant Heart must never be treated as a mined "Covenant Heart Ore" or naturally dropped generic loot item.

# 20. No Native Verdant Metal Rule

The Verdant Covenant does **not** require a native metal ore or alloy merely to mirror Overworld metallurgy.

### Consequences

- Thornsilver remains a biological fibre, not a metal ore.
- Imported metals may be used by Verdant cultures.
- Cross-realm trade may be strategically important for metalworking.
- Living materials may replace metal in some engineering roles but not every role.
- Verdant structures may visibly combine stone, wood, fibre, amber, living material, and imported metals.
- Future global reconciliation may permit a local metallic trace resource only if a concrete content dependency proves necessary, but no new major Verdant metal family may be introduced casually.

# 21. Resource Provenance Contract

Every harvestable Verdant resource must expose provenance sufficient to answer:

1. **What physical/ecological source produced this material?**
2. **Which biome or world condition permits that source?**
3. **Is the source finite, renewable, regenerative, cultivable, migratory, or event-dependent?**
4. **Does harvesting harm the source?**
5. **Does law, territory, consent, or faction ownership matter?**
6. **What process converts it into the usable material?**
7. **Can the material function outside the realm?**
8. **Which states or qualities materially affect crafting?**
9. **Can it be restored, replenished, or exhausted?**
10. **What source identifier or origin metadata should persist into trade/quest systems where provenance matters?**

A resource must not simply appear in arbitrary loot because a table needs a reward.

---

# 22. Resource Renewal Classes

Verdant resources use the following broad renewal classes.

| Renewal Class | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cultivated Renewable** | Can be deliberately grown/managed on practical gameplay timescales | crops, selected flora, some fibre sources |
| **Biological Renewable** | Returns through ecological reproduction/growth, but may require habitat and time | timber, pollen/resin resources |
| **Conditionally Regenerative** | Can regrow only if the underlying source system remains healthy | Bloomstone, high-value living materials |
| **Seasonally Renewable** | Availability shifts according to seasonal state | Season Sap, fruit, pollen |
| **Ecologically Cyclic** | Depends on migration, decomposition, symbiosis, water state, etc. | River Pearl, fungi, pollinator-linked resources |
| **Finite Geological** | Ordinary extracted material with no practical regeneration | many Rootstone deposits/large excavations |
| **Authored / Outcome-Bound** | Granted by quests, authorities, diplomacy, restoration, or special sites | certain Living Heartwood grants, authority components |

A renewable classification never implies unlimited extraction without consequence.

# 23. Quality and State Architecture

Verdant materials may expose compact state metadata only when it changes gameplay, art, provenance, or processing.

Possible state dimensions include:

- quality;
- health;
- maturity;
- season;
- purity;
- clarity;
- flexibility/hardness;
- moisture;
- living/inert;
- tuned/untuned;
- stable/unstable;
- depleted/renewing;
- blighted;
- contaminated;
- corrupted;
- restored;
- source biome;
- lawful/authorised harvest provenance where gameplay-relevant.

The system must avoid combinatorial item explosion.

Where practical, states remain metadata on one canonical material family rather than separate item definitions such as:

- Spring Season Sap;
- Summer Season Sap;
- Autumn Season Sap;
- Winter Season Sap;

as four independently authored materials.

# 24. Material Processing Foundations

FCC-02C defines processing **families**, not exact recipe quantities.

## 24.1 Wood Processing

Typical chain:

> Tree Source -> Log/Wood -> Plank/Beam/Panel/Joinery -> eligible block or crafted component

Special Living Heartwood preservation may branch from ordinary timber processing.

## 24.2 Season Sap Processing

# 26. Block / Item Single-Definition Contract

FCC-02C inherits the universal Leyforge rule:

> **If something is fundamentally a placeable block and, when broken, is meant to remain/recover as that same block, it has one canonical Block definition and must not also exist as a separately authored duplicate Item definition.**

Inventory/carrying uses the generated/reference-only block-form representation derived from the canonical Block definition.

Distinct Item definitions are created only when the material exists as a genuine non-block object or when processing transforms it into a different object.

Examples:

- Rootstone Brick block -> one canonical Block; inventory uses block-form reference.
- Pollen Amber Lens -> distinct Item if not itself a placeable block.
- Season Sap Vial -> distinct Item/container product.
- Memory Seed -> distinct Item/biological object.
- Bloomstone deposit block -> canonical Block; extracted Bloomstone Chunk may be a distinct Item if the mining design transforms the deposit.

FCC-02G owns the final concrete application of this rule.

# 28. Extraction and Ecological Consequence

Resource extraction can affect world state where the source is meaningfully ecological.

Possible consequences include:

- reduced local regeneration;
- damaged habitat;
- lower future yield;
- altered creature presence;
- settlement reaction;
- Court or territory response;
- blight vulnerability;
- route instability;
- restoration quests;
- local visual state changes.

This system must be bounded.

The game should not simulate every plant root and mineral molecule. Resource nodes use explicit health, depletion, regeneration, ownership, and ecological-pressure states at suitable granularity.

Low-end settings may simplify off-screen ecological accounting while preserving:

- player-caused major depletion;
- important restoration outcomes;
- progression-critical availability;
- visible local consequence near active settlements or player areas.
