# 1. Purpose

FCC-02J is the final **content-canon certification document** for the Verdant Covenant.

It consolidates the authoritative decisions of FCC-02A through FCC-02I into one durable index so that:

- the realm can be reconstructed after conversation compaction or file migration;
- designers can identify exactly what content currently exists;
- implementation work can distinguish locked canon from deferred global bindings;
- later art documentation can consume a stable asset-bearing roster;
- final cross-realm reconciliation can detect collisions and dependencies;
- retired or superseded source terms do not silently re-enter production;
- realm-local completeness can be certified without pretending global reconciliation is already finished.

FCC-02J does not replace the detailed A-I documents.

Instead:

> **FCC-02J is the authoritative index, certification record, dependency register and art-handoff summary for the detailed Verdant Covenant content canon defined by FCC-02A through FCC-02I.**

Where FCC-02J provides a concise registry entry and an earlier FCC-02 document provides detailed behaviour, the detailed owning document remains authoritative for that behaviour.

# 2. Certification Statement

The Verdant Covenant has now been **completely theoretically designed for FCC content purposes**.

This means the realm's asset-bearing and gameplay-bearing content is either:

1. explicitly canonically named;
2. explicitly represented by a bounded family/archetype;
3. explicitly delegated to a universal Leyforge system;
4. explicitly deferred to later global reconciliation because another realm or the Overworld owns the missing final binding.

The realm is **not** considered globally final yet.

It remains pending:

- all other realm FCC content sets;
- FCC-01 Overworld Final Content Canon;
- FCC-12 Universal Materials, Processing & Derived-Form Registry;
- FCC-13 Definitive Blocks, Items & Recipe Canon;
- FCC-14 Content Canon Certification & Art Handoff;
- the later global Material, Colour & Texture Style Governance set;
- realm-specific Verdant art documents FCC-02K/L.

The distinction is:

> **Realm content-complete now; globally reconciled later.**

No missing implementation tuning, balance number, shader value, final texture palette, final Overworld ingredient ID or global enum ID is treated as a hidden Verdant design gap.

# 4. Global Invariants Consumed by Verdant Covenant

The following are not optional local conventions.

They are governing Leyforge/FCC rules consumed by FCC-02.

## 4.1 One Canonical Portal Per Realm

Every major realm/dimension has one specific canonical portal type for normal entry.

For Verdant Covenant that portal is:

# **Covenant Portal**

No Dreamroot path, Seasonal Gate, Fae invitation, ancient gate, Rootway, Court route or narrative crossing is a second ordinary interdimensional access system.

Such concepts may provide:

- discovery;
- knowledge;
- permission;
- keystone ingredients;
- portal clues;
- stabilisation;
- intra-realm travel;
- authored story context.

Normal realm travel remains portal-based.

## 4.2 Universal Death / Respawn

Verdant Covenant does not own player death.

The universal Leyforge death/respawn system remains authoritative.

A player who dies in Verdant Covenant does not enter another realm because of FCC-02.

## 4.3 Universal Naming Convention

Ordinary real-world-equivalent substances use clear ordinary names.

Distinctive fantasy names are reserved for genuinely unique Leyforge substances, organisms, systems or phenomena.

Derived forms remain predictable.

## 4.4 Block / Item Single-Definition Rule

A placeable Block that remains the same object when carried does not receive a duplicate authored Item definition.

Inventory representation references the canonical Block.

Distinct Items exist when the object is genuinely a different material/component/product.

## 4.5 Physical Derived-Form Eligibility

Materials receive only forms that make physical and gameplay sense.

No automatic:

`Every Material x Every Block Shape`

expansion is permitted.

## 4.6 Corruption-as-State Default

Corruption, season, damage, age, wetness, blight and similar conditions remain states on canonical definitions unless irreversible transformation justifies a new definition.

## 4.7 Universal-System Ownership

FCC-02 consumes rather than duplicates universal:

- movement;
- combat;
- inventory;
- crafting runtime;
- economy;
- NPC AI;
- settlement needs;
- events/quests;
- multiplayer;
- save/load;
- Realm Access;
- weather/runtime systems;
- status effects;
- accessibility;
- world-generation runtime.

# 6. Canonical Realm Laws

Verdant Covenant uses explicit bounded state-driven laws.

## 6.1 Identity Law

Names, titles and recognised identities matter only when represented by supported gameplay state.

Examples:

- true name deliberately given;
- Court registration;
- recognised title;
- named living structure;
- identity-bound permission.

Identity Law may affect:

- doors;
- routes;
- wards;
- contracts;
- diplomacy;
- targeting;
- services.

It does not allow arbitrary natural-language interpretation.

## 6.2 Covenant Law

Promises become magical/legal Covenant state only through explicit mutually understood formal interactions.

Casual speech is not automatically binding.

## 6.3 Threshold Law / Hospitality

Host, guest, invitation, place and threshold state can create bounded protections and obligations.

## 6.4 Resonance Law

Strong or sustained represented community/emotional states may affect local ecology or environment.

Individual moment-to-moment NPC mood does not continuously rewrite terrain.

## 6.5 Seasonal Law

Season operates through:

- Court alignment;
- regional season;
- local seasonal modifiers;
- bounded convergence.

## 6.6 Living Territory Law

Certain rivers, ancient organisms, Court territories and living structures may have recognised territorial state.

## 6.7 Authored Fae-Law Exceptions

Rare powerful authored content may impose specific folkloric exceptions.

These are state-driven authored rules, not unbounded procedural law generation.

# 13. Principal Specialist Realm Resource Registry

The six principal Verdant specialist realm resources are:

1. **Living Heartwood**
2. **Season Sap**
3. **Thornsilver Fibre**
4. **Pollen Amber**
5. **Memory Seed**
6. **Bloomstone**

These retain the principal realm-resource role inherited from the Atlas and reconciled by FCC-02C.

## 13.1 Living Heartwood

Rare living inner material of sufficiently mature/healthy Greatheart Trees under suitable ecological/magical conditions.

Not every Greatheart Log is Living Heartwood.

## 13.2 Season Sap

One canonical material with state dimensions such as:

- season;
- source;
- purity;
- stability;
- resonance.

Moon Sap and Dream Sap are source/resonance descriptions of Season Sap, not separate materials.

## 13.3 Thornsilver Fibre

Biologically grown flexible living fibre produced through the Thornsilver Vine / Thornsilver Weaver symbiosis.

May be processed from flexible fibre into hardened or structural forms without becoming a native metal.

## 13.4 Pollen Amber

Resin-pollen composite material formed through compatible botanical/pollinator ecology.

Source and season may alter controlled tint/state while preserving one material identity.

## 13.5 Memory Seed

Biological magical storage/growth medium capable of holding encoded patterns, plans, memories or other approved information.

Consent/provenance requirements apply where living beings or important memories are involved.

## 13.6 Bloomstone

Root-geology hybrid material created where major living root systems interact with Verdant geology.

It can regenerate around healthy root systems under careful extraction and can be permanently exhausted through destructive mining.
