# 2. Civilisation Canon Statement

Ascendant Reach contains permanent societies from the lowest habitable floating plateaus to the highest Divine Concord territories.

Permanent and long-term populations may include:

- realm-native peoples;
- descendants of ancient migrants;
- cross-realm settlers;
- mixed-ancestry cities;
- sky-island farming communities;
- airship and flotilla populations;
- industrial forge settlements;
- storm-research communities;
- underside cultures;
- pilgrims;
- route custodians;
- temple polities;
- divine institutions;
- celestial administrative populations;
- divine-created persons;
- Awakened Constructs;
- exiles;
- dissidents;
- refugees;
- merchants;
- scholars;
- soldiers;
- monastic communities;

The realm is not socially defined by:

- one ancestry;
- one empire;
- one religion;
- one god;
- one divine court;

Ordinary people still farm, build, trade, raise animals, repair homes, argue over taxes, maintain roads and docks, form families, migrate, establish institutions, disagree about religion, oppose rulers and negotiate with neighbours.

The upper realm remains divine without ceasing to be social and political.

# 3. Ancestry, Culture, Faction, Polity, Profession, Institution and Origin Separation

FCC-05F adopts the universal Leyforge separation rule.

- **Ancestry** describes biological, constructed or metaphysical lineage where the global ancestry system recognises one.
- **Culture** describes learned values, customs, architecture, language, food, art, ritual, law and social behaviour.
- **Faction** describes organised allegiance or interest.
- **Polity** describes a governing political community or territorial sovereign.
- **Settlement** describes a populated place or mobile community.
- **Profession / Order** describes vocational, institutional or trained function.
- **Institution** describes a durable civic, religious, legal, economic, military, academic or administrative organisation.
- **Citizenship** describes recognised political membership.
- **Realm origin** describes provenance.
- **Divine affiliation** describes relation to a god/domain/doctrine and does not automatically define ancestry or citizenship.
- **Realm affinity** describes adaptation or magical relationship and does not imply biological ownership of the realm.

Any approved Leyforge ancestry may potentially participate in Ascendant societies where history, environment and progression support it.

The following must remain distinct data concepts:

- ancestry;
- culture;
- faction;
- polity;
- citizenship;
- settlement residence;
- profession;
- institutional office;
- religious affiliation;
- divine patronage;
- sacred-law permission;
- pilgrimage status;
- visitor status;
- Airship League membership;
- Windway access right;
- realm provenance.

A person may simultaneously be, for example, a citizen of a Sky Freehold, a member of an Airship League, trained by a Storm Monastery, employed by an Atmospheric Guild, a devotee of one Divine Domain and legally recognised as a pilgrim in another.

# 4. Legacy Civilisation Reconciliation

Earlier Aetheric Reach material described skyport city-states, sky freeholds, Airship Leagues, nomadic vessel cultures, storm monasteries, gas miners, cloud farmers, aerial beast riders, underside communities, floating settlements, storm collectors, airship ports, current beacons, anchor authorities and wind-right disputes.

Earlier Divine Concords material described Pilgrimage Republics, Temple Polities, Covenant Courts, theological factions, Celestial Bureaucracies, divine servants, heretics, exiles, neutral custodians, pilgrimage routes, shrine networks, celestial courts, threshold cities and contested Concord spaces.

FCC-05F preserves all strong social identities but reconciles the former two-realm assumption.

- Former Aetheric cultures are now lower/middle/high-sky Ascendant cultures and institutions.
- Former Divine Concord societies now inhabit the upper Ascendant Reach where divine overlap becomes dominant.
- “Sky people” and “divine people” do not become mandatory ancestries.
- The existence of real gods and Divine Domains does not require all upper settlements to be theocracies.
- A mandatory realm-wide Ascendant empire is rejected.

# 6. Sky Freeholds

**Sky Freeholds** are independent or semi-independent floating-island communities whose identity is strongly tied to stewardship, habitation or control of one island or island cluster.

Possible governments include elected councils, communal assemblies, hereditary houses, guild councils, military protectorates, cooperative governments and mixed civic institutions.

Freeholds may control island land, local docks, farms, anchor systems, water catchments, nearby short-range routes and local airspace under universal law.

Control of one island does not automatically grant ownership of every Windway passing nearby, every lower/upper air layer, neighbouring islands or portal destinations.

---

# 7. Airship Leagues

**Airship Leagues** are federations, guild networks, trade associations, convoy powers, political coalitions or mobile city cultures organised around aerial transport.

They may operate merchant fleets, passenger fleets, rescue ships, pilgrimage fleets, military escorts, cloud vessels, mobile workshops, mobile settlements, Stormharbours, navigation beacons and route surveys.

An Airship League may be commercial, civic, cooperative, aristocratic, military, religious, diasporic or mixed.

League membership does not automatically grant sovereignty over every route used.

---

# 8. Nomadic Vessel Cultures

**Nomadic Vessel Cultures** live primarily aboard airships, cloud vessels, linked platforms, migrating flotillas, convoy cities or temporary anchorage settlements.

They are broader than formal Airship Leagues.

A flotilla may belong to a League, contract with several Leagues, remain independent, migrate seasonally, follow Cloudwhale routes, follow trade or pilgrimage cycles, or avoid permanent Divine Domain jurisdiction.

Mobile communities still require universal settlement support for Housing, Provisions, Health, Work, Safety, Infrastructure and Morale.

---

# 9. Storm Monasteries

**Storm Monasteries** are disciplined communities or institutions centred on mastery, study or philosophy of storm conditions.

They may specialise in weather observation, storm magic, Lightning Core stewardship, grounding, Stormglass study, aerial combat, rescue, meditation, engineering, current forecasting, ritual and elemental philosophy.

A Storm Monastery need not worship a deity.

Some may develop strong relationships with Divine Domains; others may explicitly reject divine authority.

---

# 10. Skyforge & Atmospheric Guilds

**Skyforge & Atmospheric Guilds** are an umbrella economic/craft foundation covering institutions focused on Ascendant industrial resources and engineering.

Possible specialisations include Cloudstone quarrying, buoyancy-grade classification, Skyiron mining/smithing, Stormglass harvesting, Lightning Core handling, Aether Gas capture, Sunmist collection, airship construction, pressure systems, anchor engineering, atmospheric research and Cloudstone Foundry operation.

No guild automatically owns a resource simply because it specialises in it.

---

# 11. Cloud Farmers and Sky Ranchers

**Cloud Farmers / Sky Ranchers** form the principal lower/middle Ascendant agrarian foundation.

They may cultivate Cloudgrain, Windbean, Skyroot, Cloudfruit, Sunleaf, Kelpheart and compatible imported crops.

They may manage Skyback herds, Cloudfin aquaculture, orchard systems, Aerial Kelp farms, cloudwater catchments, vertical agriculture and fodder systems.

Ascendant civilisation must contain ordinary food production; divine abundance does not eliminate agriculture.

---

# 12. Aerial Beast Rider Traditions

**Aerial Beast Rider** is a cultural/professional tradition, not an ancestry.

Traditions may include Skyback riders, couriers, herders, scouts, rescue riders, ceremonial riders, mounted guards and military traditions.

The principal currently locked work/mount relationship is the **Skyback Grazer**.

Skyback use remains limited traversal and does not replace airship, glider or advanced flight progression.

---

# 13. Underside Communities

**Underside Communities** live permanently or semi-permanently beneath major floating continents.

Habitats may include hanging caves, root networks, underside ruins, inverted settlements, waterfall shafts, mining districts and suspended platforms.

They may develop distinctive route traditions, building orientation, gravity practices, mining economies, salvage customs, surface relations, lighting systems and emergency rescue infrastructure.

They are not automatically poor, criminal, primitive or isolated.

---

# 14. Pilgrimage Republics

**Pilgrimage Republics** are civic societies organised substantially around maintaining, regulating and serving pilgrimage routes.

They may be pluralist, secular administrative states, coalitions of faiths, route republics, sanctuary states or commercial pilgrimage cities.

They may provide traveller housing, maps, interpretation, legal assistance, ritual services, customs, medicine, permits, sanctuary and dispute mediation.

Pilgrimage service does not automatically grant religious authority over travellers.

---

# 15. Temple Polities

**Temple Polities** are settlements or territories where a religious institution also performs substantial civic/government functions.

Possible governments include priestly councils, elected temple assemblies, saint-led institutions, hereditary religious offices, monastic republics and mixed civic-religious government.

The god does not need to personally govern.

A Temple Polity is not automatically authoritarian or benevolent.

---

# 16. Covenant Courts

**Covenant Courts** adjudicate recognised sacred agreements, permissions, vows, legitimacy disputes, ritual disputes, inter-domain law and pilgrimage claims.

The term **Covenant** here refers to divine/legal agreement.

It does not claim ownership of the Verdant Covenant's separate magical promise mechanics.

Court processes must consume explicit game-state evidence and recognised law.

---

# 17. Celestial Bureaucracies

**Celestial Bureaucracies** are durable administrative systems supporting upper-realm institutions and Divine Domains.

They may administer petitions, recognised miracles, sanctuary access, relic custody, domain borders, pilgrimage permits, legal records, court schedules, titles, institutional archives and authorised sacred infrastructure.

They may be competent, compassionate, corrupt, fragmented, outdated, rivalrous, revolutionary, understaffed or excessively procedural.

Divinity does not eliminate bureaucracy.

---

# 18. Divine-Servant Communities

Some persons may originate as beings created, shaped, appointed or historically defined to serve a god, saint, Domain or divine institution.

**Divine servant** is not automatically a species and never permanently determines property status.

Where a being qualifies as a person under universal rules, origin does not make them property and assigned purpose does not eliminate agency.

Recognised persons may resign, change allegiance, rebel, form relationships, hold citizenship, own property, seek protection and challenge creators.

Awakened Edict Sentinels may potentially participate in such communities.

---

# 19. Theological Factions

Ascendant Reach contains many theological factions.

They may disagree over the nature of gods, divine legitimacy, sacred law, saint authority, Law Engine interpretation, worship, non-worship, miracles, corruption, divine intervention, Pilgrim Star meaning, acceptable war, sanctuary and divine succession.

The physical existence of a god or Domain does not prove every theology about it.

---

# 20. Heretical and Reform Communities

**Heretic** is relative to a specific doctrine.

There is no universal Ascendant “Heretic” alignment.

Communities may be labelled heretical because they reinterpret doctrine, reject institutional authority, oppose a god's policy, deny saint legitimacy, reject a Law Engine interpretation, support reform, merge traditions or expose institutional corruption.

Being a heretic is not evidence of Void corruption.

---

# 21. Exile Communities

**Exile Communities** may include people displaced by political expulsion, sacred-law exclusion, failed war, doctrinal conflict, domain collapse, environmental disaster, corruption, route closure or institutional purge.

Exiles may establish refugee settlements, floating camps, new Freeholds, diaspora districts, caravan communities and upper/lower-altitude resettlements.

Exile does not erase citizenship claims, culture or personhood.

---

# 22. Neutral Custodians

**Neutral Custodians** maintain infrastructure whose value depends on trust across competing factions.

They may maintain pilgrimage routes, archives, neutral sanctuaries, Current Beacons, Law Engines, border markers, rescue stations and diplomatic facilities.

Neutrality may be institutional, treaty-based, traditional, conditional or contested.

Neutral Custodians are not universally incorruptible.

---

# 23. Government and Sovereignty

No one government model defines Ascendant Reach.

Possible government forms include republics, councils, monarchies, guild governments, military governments, temple governments, monastic orders, cooperatives, bureaucratic administrations, divine rulership, mobile fleet councils, constitutional systems and mixed systems.

A Divine Domain can exist inside or across a polity, and a polity can contain several Domains.

A god may rule directly, influence government, hold ceremonial status, reject government, be imprisoned, be absent or be politically opposed.

Divine existence does not erase ordinary sovereignty.

---

# 24. Citizenship and Legal Status

Ascendant societies may distinguish citizen, resident, visitor, pilgrim, guest, protected refugee, exile, institutional member, crew member, League member, diplomatic representative, divine office-holder, sacred-law recognised person and temporary route-permit holder.

Sacred-law recognition does not automatically equal citizenship.

Citizenship does not automatically equal religious membership.

Airship crew status does not automatically equal Freehold citizenship.

---

# 25. Wind Rights and Route Sovereignty

**Wind Rights** are a major Ascendant legal/political concept.

They may govern route use, tolls, beacon access, maintenance responsibility, traffic control, convoy protection, emergency closure, rescue obligations, commercial priority and military transit.

Controlling a Windway does **not** automatically grant sovereignty over every island along it, all nearby airspace or every destination it reaches.

Windway rights, island sovereignty, docking rights, portal ownership and Divine Domain authority are distinct claims.

---

# 26. Airspace, Docking and Island Rights

Ascendant law may separately recognise surface land rights, island interior/mining rights, underside rights, docking/berthing rights, local airspace rights, route transit rights, salvage rights, water/cloud collection rights, grazing rights, migration protections and emergency landing rights.

Owning a Stormharbour does not mean owning every vessel that docks there.

Owning an island does not mean owning every Cloudwhale passing over it.

Owning an Ascension Gate does not automatically mean owning its destination.

---

# 27. Divine Domain Civic Boundaries

A Divine Domain is a bounded territory whose local law stack is partly defined by a divine authority, sacred principle, institution or metaphysical condition.

A Domain may overlap a city, garden, mountain, temple, island, island chain, storm, fortress or civic district.

Domain law may influence guest protection, property, ritual access, challenge rules, testimony, sanctuary, weapons, paths and authorised acts.

Domain law may not override universal save integrity, player/NPC identity, multiplayer authority, universal death/respawn or unrelated inventory truth.

Civilisations may negotiate with, resist, exploit, reform or reject Domain institutions.

---

# 28. Universal Settlement Needs

Ascendant settlements consume Leyforge's seven universal settlement needs:

1. **Housing**
2. **Provisions**
3. **Health**
4. **Work**
5. **Safety**
6. **Infrastructure**
7. **Morale**

Realm adaptation changes how a need is satisfied.

No Ascendant settlement receives a parallel settlement system.

Housing may require storm-resistant structure, anchors, stable platforms or sealed High-Aether rooms. Provisions may use the Ascendant crop canon, fisheries, ranching and imports. Health may include altitude/storm treatment. Safety may include rescue, grounding and sacred-law refuge. Infrastructure may include docks, lifts, beacons, condensers, foundries, courts and Law Engines.

# 29. Housing and Altitude Adaptation

## Lower Reach / Aetheric Reach

Housing may use ordinary breathable interiors, storm shutters, anchored foundations, Cloudstone/Skywood construction, open terraces and cargo tie-downs.

## Storm Heights

Housing may require stronger anchoring, lightning protection, enclosed storm refuge, robust roofs, secured storage and protected docking.

## High Aether

Housing may require sealed/protected atmosphere, thermal control, pressure-safe rooms, redundant life support, intense-light protection and rescue infrastructure.

## Divine Concords

Housing may additionally require declared-domain compatibility, recognised sanctuary, permitted construction and sacred-law boundary compliance.

Persons, citizenship, sacred-law recognition and divine servants are not commodities.

Finding a wreck does not automatically grant ownership of everything inside.

# 36. Airship and Vessel Civilisation

Airships and cloud vessels are foundational to Ascendant civilisation.

Settlements may require docks, berths, repair yards, cargo handling, lift/fuel storage, navigation, rescue, customs, quarantine, passenger infrastructure and crew housing.

Mobile vessels may also function as homes, workshops, farms, markets, civic spaces or temporary settlements.

Exact vessel systems remain owned by universal transport/maritime content.

# 37. Windways and Current Beacons

Windways are realm-spanning route/environment infrastructure.

Civilisations may build and maintain Current Beacons, route markers, weather stations, emergency platforms, rescue stations, toll stations and convoy assembly areas.

They do not generate ownership of the entire current.

---

# 38. Pilgrimage Infrastructure

Upper-realm civilisation may require pilgrim housing, food, medical care, route information, interpretation, ritual preparation, sanctuary, customs, legal guidance, access permits and transport.

Pilgrimage remains physical travel through the realm.

Pilgrim Roads and celestial routes do not become alternate interdimensional portals.

High-Aether settlements may require sealed habitats, atmospheric support, thermal protection, redundant power, protected agriculture, Sunmist handling, emergency descent, rescue docks, pressure-safe storage and intense-light protection.

Major aerial-port infrastructure supporting docking, cargo, passengers, repair, customs, weather information, rescue and convoy staging.

Windway navigation and route-safety infrastructure supporting current identification, hazard warning, signalling and coordination.

Signature agricultural infrastructure for Cloudfruit, vertical agriculture, pollinator habitat, cloudwater use and public/community cultivation.

Civic/engineering governance infrastructure coordinating loads, anchor safety, construction approval, route access and emergency response. The name does not require council government in every settlement.

Major storm-control/harvesting megastructure supporting monitoring, storm-energy capture, research, Lightning Core infrastructure and regional protection within bounded limits.

Neutral/broadly accessible traveller infrastructure supporting lodging, food, information, interpretation, ritual preparation, medicine and sanctuary where recognised.

## Covenant Court
Sacred-law/legal infrastructure supporting recognised agreements, testimony, inter-domain disputes, permissions, records and appeals. It does not own Verdant Covenant magic.

## Archive of Oaths
Major civic/institutional archive storing treaties, recognised vows, permissions, titles, precedents, institutional records and relic provenance. It is not the same definition as the Celestial Archive dungeon family.

## Radiant Tribunal
High-level divine civic/judicial infrastructure supporting hearings, domain disputes, major appeals, diplomacy and public records.

## Mercy Sanctuary
Refuge/healing infrastructure supporting medical care, shelter, protected guest status, refugees, Mercy Water and medicinal gardens. The building alone does not grant universal invulnerability.

## Concord Spire
High-altitude diplomatic/assembly infrastructure supporting embassies, councils, inter-domain negotiation, route coordination and major diplomacy. It does not imply one unified divine government.
