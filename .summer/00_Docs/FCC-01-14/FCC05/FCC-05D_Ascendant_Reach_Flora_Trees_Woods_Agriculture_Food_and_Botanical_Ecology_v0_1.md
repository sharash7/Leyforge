# 1. Purpose

FCC-05D defines the authoritative **plant, lichen, agricultural, timber and plant-food ecology** of the Ascendant Reach.

It owns realm-local canon for:

- the exact twelve named gameplay-bearing flora foundations;
- the six canonical cultivated crop foundations;
- the principal native timber organism, **Skywood Tree**;
- biological provenance of **Skywood**;
- the **Cloudfruit Tree -> Cloudfruit** orchard relationship;
- **Aerial Kelp** as a realm-defining vertical plant ecology;
- **Windherb**, **Stormreed**, **Hangroot**, **Starlichen**, **Law Seed**, **Mercy Bloom**, **Shelter Reed**, **Cloudgrass** and **Sunmist Bloom**;
- plant-side provenance of **Windspun Fibre**;
- plant oils/waxes and botanical inputs that may participate in crafted **Covenant Wax**;
- the biological relationship between cloudwater, atmosphere, Sunmist, Aether Gas and plant growth;
- native plant food and settlement-provision foundations;
- cultivation, propagation, harvest, regrowth and ecological consequence;
- altitude-band and biome suitability;
- sacred-law-responsive botanical states;
- export and off-realm cultivation boundaries;
- ecological interfaces consumed by creatures, settlements, structures, recipes, events, corruption and Pocket Realm modules.

FCC-05D does **not** own:

- final post-harvest material definitions, transformed divine materials or metal/stone processing — FCC-05C;
- creature definitions, pollination behaviour, grazing, fisheries, domestication or animal-derived materials — FCC-05E;
- civilisation-specific farming institutions, land law, trade ownership, sacred stewardship or settlement food policy — FCC-05F;

- universal farming, hunger, nutrition, fluid, weather, time, AI or settlement simulation systems.

The governing goal is:

> **Ascendant Reach botany must make a vertical sky civilisation biologically believable without requiring a separate plant registry for every altitude, storm, god or sacred law.**

# 2. Botanical Canon Statement

Ascendant Reach is one connected vertical ecology.

Its plant life becomes increasingly specialised as altitude, weather, atmospheric density, radiant exposure and sacred-law influence change, but the realm does not contain five unrelated botanical worlds stacked on top of one another.

The realm distinguishes between:

1. **canonical gameplay-bearing flora**, which receive persistent named identities and defined ecological/gameplay roles;
2. **canonical cultivated crop families**, which sustain native agriculture and settlement provisioning;
3. **ambient vegetation**, which may provide extensive biodiversity without every grass, moss, flower, fungus or shrub receiving a stable gameplay identity;
4. **runtime botanical states**, which express storm charge, altitude stress, sacred-law alignment, seasonal/event pressure or corruption without multiplying species definitions;
5. **cultivated strains/products**, which may differ economically or nutritionally while remaining members or products of an existing biological family.

The core rule is:

> **Visual biodiversity may be broad; gameplay-bearing species identity remains bounded, mechanically justified and provenance-safe.**

# 4. Naming, Species and Registry Rules

Ascendant flora follows universal Leyforge naming rules:

- one canonical player-facing identity per gameplay-bearing species/family;
- clear ordinary names where ordinary language is sufficient;
- fantasy names only where the biology or material is genuinely unique;
- predictable harvested and processed naming;
- runtime states do not automatically become species;
- cultural aliases do not create duplicate registry identities.

Examples:

- Skywood Tree -> Skywood Log / Skywood -> Skywood Planks / Beam where FCC-05G admits them;
- Cloudfruit Tree -> Cloudfruit;
- Aerial Kelp -> Aerial Kelp Frond / fibre-bearing harvest -> Windspun Fibre processing where appropriate;
- Stormreed -> conductive fibre/stem harvest -> Windspun Fibre or electrical components where recipes justify it;
- Law Seed -> law-aligned growth state, not Truth Seed / Mercy Seed / War Seed species proliferation;
- Mercy Bloom -> Mercy Bloom, not a new species for every healing doctrine;
- Kelpheart -> cultivated edible Aerial Kelp product/strain, not a new wild family.

# 5. Skywood Tree

## 5.1 Canonical Identity

The **Skywood Tree** is the principal specialist timber-bearing tree family of Ascendant Reach.

It is adapted to exposed floating-island environments through:

- strong but lightweight woody structure;
- flexible branch architecture;
- high wind tolerance;
- deep or wide anchoring root systems appropriate to shallow floating-island soils;
- efficient water use under cloud-fed rainfall patterns;
- compatibility with managed orchards, settlements and vertical farms.

Skywood is not intrinsically divine. It is ordinary native sky-realm biology whose material properties make it exceptionally useful to aerial civilisation.

## 5.2 Principal Habitat

Preferred habitat includes:

- **Cloudgrass Plateaus**;
- protected **Aerial Kelp Field** islands/platforms;
- lower/middle **Stormglass Archipelago** shelters where lightning risk is manageable;
- maintained settlement terraces across the Lower Reach and Aetheric Reach;
- selected protected Pilgrim Threshold gardens.

Skywood is uncommon in the High Silence Expanse without habitat engineering.

## 5.3 Material Provenance

Skywood Tree is the biological source of **Skywood**, whose material classification and derived-form eligibility are consumed by FCC-05C/G.

Potential harvests include:

- logs/wood blocks;
- branches;
- bark;
- leaves;
- seeds or planting stock;
- minor oils/resins where later recipes justify them.

The tree does not generate Cloudstone, Sunmist, Aether Gas, Lightning Cores or sacred materials merely because it grows in Ascendant Reach.

## 5.4 Construction Role

Skywood is the realm's principal renewable structural wood for:

- housing frames;
- platforms;
- furniture;
- airship/vessel framing;
- walkways;
- agricultural infrastructure;
- crates and ordinary containers;
- repair work.

Its specialist identity is **strength-to-weight and flexibility**, not unrestricted magical buoyancy.

## 5.5 Divine Treatment Boundary

Skywood may be ritually or law-attuned in compatible upper-realm contexts.

Terms such as sanctuary-treated, oath-marked or mercy-blessed Skywood describe **states/treatments** unless an irreversible material transformation later proves distinct enough to justify a new material identity.

There is no separate universal “Divine Wood” family.

# 7. Aerial Kelp

## 7.1 Canonical Identity

**Aerial Kelp** is one of Ascendant Reach's defining native organisms.

It is a vast buoyant plant family whose long fronds, stems and bladder structures can extend vertically through open sky and dense cloud layers, producing three-dimensional living forests.

It may anchor to:

- floating-island edges;
- suspended mineral masses;
- natural cloudwater structures;
- artificial cultivation frames;
- hanging agricultural platforms.

## 7.2 Ecological Roles

Aerial Kelp provides:

- habitat and nesting cover;
- nursery zones for Cloudfin and other cloud-sea organisms where FCC-05E supports it;
- shelter from strong winds;
- vertical movement corridors;
- organic debris/food-web input;
- fibre-bearing biomass;
- edible young tissue;
- buoyancy-organ harvests;
- oils/spores/reagents where justified;
- cultivated Kelpheart food.

## 7.3 Windspun Fibre Interface

Aerial Kelp is a principal botanical contributor to **Windspun Fibre** provenance.

Windspun Fibre is a material family under FCC-05C; this document establishes that compatible Aerial Kelp tissue can be processed into that fibre lineage.

It is not necessarily the exclusive source. Stormreed and compatible wind-adapted plants may also contribute where FCC-05G validates the processing chain.

## 7.4 Buoyancy Bladders

Aerial Kelp buoyancy bladders are harvested **biological components**, not a separate magical ore/resource family.

They may support:

- emergency descent devices;
- primitive lift assistance;
- vessel components;
- floatation;
- alchemy;
- cultivation systems.

Their lift contribution remains bounded by universal physics/vehicle systems.

## 7.5 Kelpheart

**Kelpheart** is the canonical cultivated edible Aerial Kelp line/product.

It may represent selectively cultivated inner tissue, heart-frond or equivalent edible harvest chosen for:

- predictable food yield;
- texture;
- storage;
- settlement farming;
- culinary use.

It does not require a separate wild species identity.

# 12. Law Seed and Law-Grown Flora

## 12.1 Canonical Identity

**Law Seed** is one specialist botanical/ritual family whose growth state can align with a recognised local sacred principle.

It does **not** become a separate species for every law.

There is no default registry explosion into Truth Seed, Mercy Seed, War Seed, Hospitality Seed, Craft Seed and similar identities.

## 12.2 Growth Contract

When planted in a compatible Law Garden or explicitly prepared sacred site, a Law Seed may grow into one of several **authored law-responsive botanical states**.

A supported state may:

- mark a boundary;
- change posture or flowering when a declared law is violated;
- produce a specific ritual ingredient;
- support local Domain stability;
- act as an environmental indicator of recognised legal/sacred status;
- interact with Law Engine or sanctuary infrastructure through explicit systems.

A Law Seed cannot invent a law or interpret arbitrary freeform morality.

## 12.3 State Authority Boundary

Law-grown flora consumes a **declared Domain law profile** supplied by FCC-05A/B/I.

It does not own:

- universal law;
- player morality;
- save authority;
- identity truth;
- arbitrary thought detection.

# 25. Windspun Fibre Botanical Provenance

FCC-05C locks **Windspun Fibre** as the realm's principal specialist flexible fibre.

FCC-05D locks the plant-side provenance boundary:

- Aerial Kelp is a principal source;
- Stormreed may contribute specialised conductive fibre;
- compatible wind-adapted plants may contribute minor/ordinary inputs where recipes justify them;
- no thirteenth flora species may be invented solely to source Windspun Fibre;
- Grace Thread remains a processed/divinely attuned Windspun Fibre lineage and is not grown directly from a “Grace Plant.”

Exact fibre extraction, retting, spinning and recipe quantities belong to FCC-05G/FCC-13.

**Covenant Wax** is a crafted specialist product under FCC-05C.

The defining feature of Covenant Wax is its **crafted ritual/legal preparation**, not a unique naturally mined or harvested species.

No final exclusive plant source is required at FCC-05D level.

Characterised by:

- Cloudgrass;

- Cloudfruit orchards;
- Cloudgrain agriculture;

- common Windherb;

Characterised by:

- Starlichen;
- Sunmist Bloom;

Characterised by:

- Mercy Bloom;
- Shelter Reed;

# 31. Regional-State Botanical Responses

Plants may react to the eight FCC-05B regional state families through bounded state changes.

## 31.1 Clear Current

Supports stable agriculture, reliable pollination and ordinary route-side cultivation.

## 31.2 Stormfront

May increase Stormreed activity, damage exposed crops, change Aerial Kelp posture and reduce ordinary harvest reliability.

## 31.3 Gravity Drift

May alter plant orientation, hanging-root stress, cultivation supports and Aerial Kelp alignment.

It does not instantly uproot every plant.

## 31.4 Migration Tide

May affect pollination, grazing pressure, seed dispersal, cloud-sea nutrient cycles and harvest timing.

## 31.5 High-Aether Surge

May increase Sunmist Bloom/Starlichen activity and stress lower-altitude crops without protection.

## 31.6 Pilgrimage Convergence

May increase demand for food, shelter plants, ceremonial flora and sanctuary garden maintenance.

## 31.7 Concord Tension

May alter Law-grown flora states where sacred-law conflicts are explicit.

Ordinary plants do not become corrupted simply because politics are tense.

## 31.8 Void-Corrupted State

May contaminate plants under FCC-05I.

Void corruption is not equivalent to storm damage, divine attunement or ordinary plant disease.

# 32. Biome Suitability Matrix

- cloudwater access;
- altitude band;
- atmospheric density;
- wind exposure;
- gravity profile;
- storm exposure;
- Sunmist/Aether Gas profile;
- shelter;
- biome suitability;
- settlement infrastructure;
- Divine Domain compatibility where applicable;

Growth should use:

- deterministic stage timers;
- condition checks;
- saved growth state;
- bounded strain/quality states;

- colony entities for large Aerial Kelp/lichen systems;
- saved regional modifiers;
- event-driven state changes;
- near/far simulation;

# 52. Canon Summary

Ascendant Reach's plant life is a **single vertical ecology** rather than a lower sky biome catalogue plus an unrelated divine garden catalogue.

Its lower world is sustained by **Cloudgrass**, **Skywood**, **Cloudfruit orchards**, **Cloudgrain**, **Windbean**, **Skyroot**, **Windherb** and managed **Aerial Kelp**. Storm regions favour **Stormreed** and storm-adapted kelp. The undersides of floating continents develop **Hangroot** ecologies. The High Silence is colonised by **Starlichen** and **Sunmist Bloom**. At the highest altitudes, **Law Seeds**, **Mercy Bloom** and **Shelter Reed** allow living landscapes to interact with declared sacred conditions without turning every divine principle into a new species.

The realm's agriculture can support ordinary civilisation through six bounded crop foundations, while Sky Orchards, vertical farms, cloudwater management and Aerial Kelp cultivation give it a distinct aerial identity.

Most importantly, the botanical registry remains comprehensible:

> **Skywood is the principal timber; Cloudfruit is orchard food; Aerial Kelp is vertical habitat and fibre/food ecology; divine influence changes compatible plant states without creating a new plant for every law.**
