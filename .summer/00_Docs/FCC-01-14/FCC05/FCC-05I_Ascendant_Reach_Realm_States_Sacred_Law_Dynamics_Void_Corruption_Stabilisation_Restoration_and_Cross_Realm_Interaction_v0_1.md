# Leyforge Final Content Canon Collection

## FCC-05I — Ascendant Reach Realm States, Sacred-Law Dynamics, Void Corruption, Stabilisation, Restoration & Cross-Realm Interaction

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Content Canon — Realm-Local Lock Candidate  
**Realm:** Ascendant Reach  
**Stable Realm ID:** `realm.major.ascendant_reach`  
**Legacy Realm Aliases:** `realm.major.aetheric_reach`; `realm.network.divine_concords`  
**Parent Collection:** Leyforge Final Content Canon (FCC)  
**Depends On:** FCC-05A through FCC-05H  
**Primary Scope:** regional state machinery; bounded sacred-law state; Divine Domain compatibility; law priority and conflict; authority/event aftermath; storm/gravity/high-aether state propagation; portal-state interaction; ordinary damage/failure versus Void corruption; corruption state-first rules; corrupted gods/authorities/structures/creatures; cleansing; stabilisation; restoration; persistent aftermath; cross-realm interfaces with Overworld, Verdant Covenant, Ancestral Veil, Somnolent Expanse, future realms, Pocket Realms and Void Between; ownership boundaries; save/multiplayer determinism; accessibility and art-handoff requirements.  
**Final Global Status:** Pending final cross-realm reconciliation after all realm FCC sets and the Overworld FCC are complete.

---

# 1. Purpose

FCC-05I defines the authoritative **dynamic-state, sacred-law, corruption, restoration and cross-realm interaction canon** for Ascendant Reach.

It exists because Ascendant Reach contains several overlapping systems that must remain distinct but interoperable:

- weather;
- Windways;
- floating-island stability;
- gravity;
- High-Aether exposure;
- Divine Domains;
- sacred-law infrastructure;
- institutional authority;
- realm events;
- world-scale authorities;
- Void corruption;
- portals;
- cross-realm contact.

Without a strict state model, these systems could easily collapse into one generic “magic realm state” or one universal “holy/corrupt” axis.

FCC-05I therefore owns:

- the exact eight regional state families;
- compatible and incompatible state combinations;
- state priority and layering rules;
- Divine Domain state architecture;
- explicit sacred-law declaration and applicability;
- law compatibility/conflict;
- law suspension, revision and invalidation;
- route, settlement and dungeon interaction with domain law;
- authority and event-driven state changes;
- ordinary environmental damage versus systemic failure versus Void corruption;
- state-first corruption;
- corrupted authority/person/creature/structure handling;
- cleansing;
- stabilisation;
- repair;
- restoration;
- persistent aftermath;
- cross-realm interface contracts;
- portal corruption/quarantine;
- multiplayer authority and save persistence;
- low-end simulation rules;
- accessibility-safe communication of state.

FCC-05I does **not** own:

- universal atmosphere, temperature, damage, inventory, identity or death/respawn systems;
- universal law/faction/reputation runtime;
- universal portal architecture;
- universal creature AI;
- universal settlement simulation;
- final recipe quantities;
- final material IDs;
- final portal dependency chain;
- final art palettes;
- the deeper origin/nature/endgame of the Void Between.

Those remain owned by universal systems, FCC-10, FCC-12, FCC-13 and FCC-14.

The governing rule is:

> **Ascendant Reach may layer weather, gravity, sacred law, politics, infrastructure and corruption over the same location, but each layer must remain explicit, bounded, inspectable and reversible only through its owning system.**

---

# 2. State Ownership Principle

Ascendant Reach uses **separate state families** rather than one universal realm-state meter.

A location may simultaneously possess:

- biome identity;
- altitude band;
- gravity profile;
- atmosphere/exposure profile;
- Windway relationship;
- weather state;
- migration state;
- Divine Domain state;
- sacred-law state;
- settlement state;
- dungeon state;
- authority influence;
- corruption state.

These layers may interact.

They must not silently overwrite one another.

Example:

A Law Garden may simultaneously be:

- in High Aether;
- under Pilgrimage Convergence;
- inside a Mercy-aligned Divine Domain;
- operating under an explicit sanctuary law;
- affected by Gravity Drift;
- free of Void corruption.

Nothing about “holy area” is allowed to erase the other states.

---

# 3. Exact Regional State Families

Ascendant Reach has exactly **eight major regional state families**:

1. **Clear Current**
2. **Stormfront**
3. **Gravity Drift**
4. **Migration Tide**
5. **High-Aether Surge**
6. **Pilgrimage Convergence**
7. **Concord Tension**
8. **Void-Corrupted State**

These are regional overlays/state families.

They are not additional biomes.

They do not replace local hazard profiles.

---

# 4. Clear Current

**Clear Current** represents a comparatively stable travel/environment state.

Typical effects may include:

- more predictable Windways;
- safer aerial travel;
- improved visibility;
- increased trade;
- increased ordinary outdoor activity;
- lower emergency demand.

Clear Current does **not** mean:

- no wind;
- no weather;
- no fall risk;
- no predators;
- no sacred law;
- no ordinary local hazard.

It is a relative stability state.

---

# 5. Stormfront

**Stormfront** represents severe or advancing storm conditions.

Possible effects include:

- turbulence;
- lightning;
- static buildup;
- Windway disruption;
- reduced visibility;
- route closures;
- Stormglass formation opportunity;
- Static Serpent activity;
- settlement shelter activation;
- Tempest Crown demand.

Stormfront is not automatically:

- Void corruption;
- Tempest Leviathan presence;
- divine wrath;
- a dungeon event.

Stormfront may be entirely natural.

---

# 6. Gravity Drift

**Gravity Drift** represents bounded changes in local gravity, island support, buoyancy relationship or relative island position.

Possible effects include:

- altered jump/fall behaviour;
- changed island altitude;
- bridge misalignment;
- route change;
- structural stress;
- anchor demand;
- exposed Underside access;
- new navigation opportunities.

Gravity Drift does not automatically mean:

- Gravity Choir Sovereign is hostile;
- the realm is collapsing;
- Void corruption is present.

The cause must be explicit.

---

# 7. Migration Tide

**Migration Tide** represents major ecological movement.

Possible contributors include:

- Cloudwhales;
- Cloudfin;
- Skyback herds;
- Storm Roc movement;
- Windlace Swarms;
- Starveil Rays;
- other aggregate fauna.

Possible effects include:

- changed fisheries;
- route congestion;
- hunting/protection policy;
- festivals;
- predator movement;
- ecological opportunity;
- settlement adaptation.

Migration Tide is ecological state, not a combat alert.

---

# 8. High-Aether Surge

**High-Aether Surge** represents increased exposure to high-altitude radiance, Sunmist, magical radiation, thin atmosphere effects and associated energetic conditions.

Possible effects include:

- greater environmental exposure;
- Sunmist activity;
- altered specialist flora/fauna activity;
- processing opportunity;
- increased habitat stress;
- increased High-Aether settlement demand;
- stronger visibility of some Divine Domain effects.

High-Aether Surge is not automatically:

- divine blessing;
- divine hostility;
- Void corruption.

---

# 9. Pilgrimage Convergence

**Pilgrimage Convergence** represents large-scale movement of pilgrims, vessels, institutions and sacred activity.

Possible effects include:

- increased population density;
- lodging demand;
- trade;
- route congestion;
- ritual activity;
- legal disputes;
- sanctuary demand;
- disease/logistics pressure;
- public events;
- political demonstrations.

Pilgrimage Convergence is a social/route state.

It does not automatically strengthen every god or law.

**Concord Tension** represents increased conflict between:

- Divine Domains;
- sacred laws;
- institutions;
- gods;

- contested boundaries;

Concord Tension is not automatically:

---

# 11. Void-Corrupted State

**Void-Corrupted State** represents genuine Void-provenance corruption affecting a region.

It may alter:

- terrain;
- gravity;
- atmosphere;
- routes;
- structures;
- sacred-law systems;
- creatures;
- divine authorities;
- settlement safety;
- Ascension Gate operation.

Void-Corrupted State requires explicit provenance.

It must never be inferred solely from:

- hostility;
- darkness;
- ruin;
- moral disagreement;
- divine failure;
- abandonment;
- storm damage;
- gravity instability.

# 12. Regional State Compatibility

Regional states may combine when mechanically compatible.

Examples of valid combinations include:

- Clear Current + Migration Tide;
- Stormfront + Migration Tide;
- Gravity Drift + Stormfront;
- Gravity Drift + Concord Tension;
- High-Aether Surge + Pilgrimage Convergence;
- Pilgrimage Convergence + Concord Tension;
- Stormfront + Void-Corrupted State;
- Gravity Drift + Void-Corrupted State;
- Concord Tension + Void-Corrupted State.

Some combinations require interpretation.

Example:

**Clear Current + Stormfront** normally conflict at the same exact route scale, but may coexist at different nested scales:

- regional skies may be Clear Current;
- one local storm cell may be Stormfront.

State systems should therefore support:

- scope;
- priority;
- nesting;
- local override.

---

# 13. State Scope

Every major state should define a scope such as:

- local site;
- settlement;
- route segment;
- island;
- island cluster;
- biome region;
- altitude region;
- Divine Domain;
- realm-scale event corridor.

State scope must be queryable.

A player should not be told “Ascendant Reach is under Stormfront” when only one small island is affected.

# 14. State Cause

Where relevant, state should preserve cause/provenance.

Examples:

- natural storm;
- Tempest Crown action;
- Tempest Leviathan migration;
- Gravity Choir retuning;
- anchor failure;
- authority decision;
- Law Engine failure;
- Domain law revision;
- Pilgrim Star Passage;

Cause matters because it determines:

- available remedies;

- restoration;
- recurrence.

# 15. State Persistence

Significant state changes persist through:

- player travel;
- region unload;
- save/reload;
- portal travel;
- multiplayer reconnect.

Examples:

- a shifted island remains shifted;
- a changed Windway remains changed;
- a revised Domain law remains revised;
- a corrupted Law Engine remains corrupted;
- a restored sanctuary remains restored;
- a quarantined Ascension Gate remains quarantined.

Temporary timed states may expire only when their explicit timer/condition resolves.

# 17. Divine Domain Definition

A **Divine Domain** is a bounded territory inside Ascendant Reach whose local law stack is partly defined by:

- divine authority;
- sacred principle;
- institution;
- saintly authority;
- recognised metaphysical condition;
- compatible Law Engine infrastructure.

A Domain is not automatically:

- a separate realm;
- a Pocket Realm;
- a portal destination class;
- an afterlife;
- a god's private save space.

It remains part of Ascendant Reach.

---

# 18. Domain Boundary

A Domain must have an explicit boundary representation.

Possible boundaries include:

- geographic;
- architectural;
- legal/ritual;
- Law Engine network;
- environmental;
- recognised route markers;
- combination.

Boundary state must be readable through at least one accessible system.

A Domain may not secretly extend everywhere because a god “feels like it.”

---

# 19. Sacred-Law Definition

A **sacred law** is a bounded authored rule that applies within a recognised scope.

It must define:

1. **Law ID / identity**
2. **Display name**
3. **Owning Domain / authority**
4. **Scope**
5. **Trigger condition**
6. **Recognised exceptions**
7. **Warning**
8. **Violation state**
9. **Consequence**
10. **Appeal/recovery pathway where supported**
11. **Suspension state**
12. **Conflict priority/compatibility metadata**

A sacred law is not a freeform natural-language rule parser.

---

# 20. Sacred-Law Examples

Valid authored law families might include:

- sanctuary;
- hospitality;
- declared challenge;
- weapon restriction;
- testimony;
- property/permission;
- pilgrimage observance;
- protected threshold;
- duel protocol;
- relic custody;
- neutral corridor.

These are examples of supported structured rules.

They are not a mandatory final global sacred-law catalogue.

---

# 21. Sacred-Law Consequences

A sacred-law violation may cause explicitly authored consequences such as:

- warning;
- loss of recognised guest status;
- guardian challenge;
- restricted access;
- route closure;
- suspended blessing;
- legal summons;
- trial state;
- temporary property/use restriction within scope;
- local environmental response;
- institutional reputation consequence.

Physical harm may occur where the law explicitly supports it.

Generic instant “holy damage” is not the default.

---

# 22. Sacred-Law Universal Boundaries

Sacred law may not override:

- universal save ownership;
- player identity;
- NPC identity;
- multiplayer authority;
- universal death/respawn;
- unrelated inventory truth;
- arbitrary permanent deletion;
- universal personhood.

A sacred law may restrict use/access within its legitimate scope.

It may not rewrite the core game.

---

# 23. Law Readability

Before meaningful punishment, a law should be discoverable through one or more of:

- signage;
- herald;
- Law Lion warning;
- Edict Sentinel warning;
- interface;
- NPC explanation;
- Codex;
- ritual marker;
- domain boundary display;
- court record.

Some uncertainty is allowed in mystery content.

But catastrophic consequences may not depend on an unknowable hidden rule.

---

# 24. Domain Law Compatibility

Two overlapping laws may be:

- compatible;
- conditionally compatible;
- nested;
- priority-ordered;
- temporarily suspended;
- contradictory.

Compatibility should be explicit data.

Example:

A sanctuary law and a no-weapons law may be compatible.

A mandatory-duel law and a sanctuary-no-violence law may conflict.

The system must not guess.

---

# 25. Law Priority

If two applicable laws conflict, resolution may use:

- explicit precedence;
- geographic scope;
- institutional treaty;
- Tribunal ruling;
- neutral corridor;
- temporary suppression;
- event state.

Priority must be authored and inspectable.

No “the stronger god wins invisibly” rule is universal canon.

---

# 26. Concord Tension and Law Conflict

Concord Tension may arise when:

- Domain boundaries overlap;
- laws conflict;
- institutions disagree;
- recognised authority is disputed;
- Law Engines disagree;
- pilgrimage access changes.

During Concord Tension:

- neutral corridors may activate;
- Tribunal activity may increase;
- guardians may pause or change behaviour;
- settlements may restrict access;
- diplomacy may intensify.

Concord Tension does not automatically invalidate all Domain laws.

---

# 27. Law Suspension

A law may enter suspended state because:

- Law Engine failure;
- authority absence;
- Tribunal order;
- Domain collapse;
- negotiated ceasefire;
- corruption quarantine;
- maintenance;
- event.

Suspension must be visible.

Suspended law does not silently continue punishing the player.

---

# 28. Domain Law Revision

A valid **Domain Law Revision** must occur through an authorised process.

Possible mechanisms include:

- Domain Sovereign decision;
- Concord Judge ruling;
- recognised court process;
- Law Engine restoration;
- constitutional/institutional procedure;
- valid succession;
- authority outcome.

The player cannot rewrite laws simply by editing arbitrary text.

---

# 29. Law Engine Interaction

Law Engines may:

- stabilise law;
- transmit law state;
- enforce bounded permissions;
- maintain borders;
- store recognised precedent;
- support neutral corridors.

Law Engine failure may cause:

- suspension;
- partial enforcement;
- conflict;
- stale precedent;
- boundary instability.

Law Engine failure is not automatically Void corruption.

# 30. Guardians and Sacred Law

Their behaviour should reference:

- applicable law;

May alter inter-domain recognition, treaty, legal precedent and sacred access.

These outputs must be expressed as explicit saved state.

# 35. Ordinary Mechanical / Institutional Failure

Examples include:

- broken Current Beacon;
- depleted Lightning Core;
- miscalibrated Law Engine;
- failed seal;
- ordinary technical data corruption;
- biased court;
- incompetent bureaucracy;
- false record;
- damaged Tempest Crown;

These may be serious.

They are not automatically Void corruption.

---

# 36. Divine Hostility

A god or divine authority may be:

- hostile;
- tyrannical;
- selfish;
- violent;
- doctrinally extreme;
- cruel;
- politically oppressive.

This does not prove Void corruption.

Ascendant Reach must permit morally bad divinity that is still genuinely itself.

---

# 37. Void Corruption Definition

Void corruption requires actual influence from the Void Between or the older source acting through it.

It may manifest as:

- reality rewriting;
- identity distortion;
- impossible state propagation;
- law corruption;
- geometry instability;
- material contamination;
- creature transformation;
- authority corruption;
- portal interference.

Void corruption remains separate from ordinary Ascendant weirdness.

---

# 38. Corruption State-First Rule

Void corruption is applied as a **state/variant first**.

Affected content keeps its underlying identity unless irreversible transformation justifies a new definition.

Examples:

- corrupted Law Lion remains Law Lion unless permanently transformed;
- corrupted Edict Sentinel remains that Sentinel;
- corrupted Cloudstone remains Cloudstone with corruption state unless transformed;
- corrupted Law Engine remains Law Engine;
- corrupted god remains the same god/person.

This preserves history, ownership, personhood, provenance and restoration possibility.

---

# 39. Irreversible Transformation Threshold

A distinct new entity/material/structure definition is justified only when corruption creates a persistent new identity with major changes such as:

- anatomy;
- lifecycle;
- material composition;
- behaviour;
- ecology;
- processing;
- structural rules;
- personhood identity;
- world role.

A simple visual or stat change does not justify a new registry family.

---

# 40. Corruption of Divine Authorities

A corrupted divine authority remains the same person/entity unless irreversible transformation establishes otherwise.

Possible effects include:

- distorted law;
- altered judgement;
- changed territory;
- corrupted blessings;
- hostile guardians;
- contaminated infrastructure;
- new Void hazards;
- portal instability.

Corruption does not automatically erase responsibility, memory or political consequences.

---

# 41. Corruption of Law

Void corruption may affect sacred-law systems.

Possible effects include:

- law scope spreading incorrectly;
- contradictory permissions;
- impossible enforcement;
- false authority recognition;
- corrupted Law Engine state;
- broken exception logic;
- sanctuary becoming predatory.

This must be represented as an explicit corruption state.

It must not become an excuse for arbitrary mechanics.

---

# 42. Corruption of Gravity

Void corruption may distort:

- local gravity;
- island orientation;
- route geometry;
- buoyancy;
- falling behaviour.

These effects must still obey bounded authored states.

Void corruption does not permit unbounded non-Euclidean behaviour in every Ascendant region.

---

# 43. Corruption of Portals

Ascension Gates may be:

- contaminated;
- unstable;
- quarantined;
- unable to bind destination;
- unable to activate safely.

Corruption may justify:

- shutdown;
- repair;
- cleansing;
- route quarantine.

Corruption does not create:

- a Void fast-travel shortcut;
- a second normal Ascendant portal family;
- arbitrary random realm travel.

---

- suspected;
- exposed;
- quarantined;
- partially corrupted;
- evacuation;

- irreversibly transformed where explicitly authored.

Population responses may include:

- panic;
- denial;
- cult formation;
- evacuation;

- institutional breakdown;
- reform.

Creature corruption may alter:

- aggression;
- migration;
- feeding;
- anatomy presentation;
- ability;
- habitat tolerance;
- law responsiveness;
- electrical behaviour.

The base species remains authoritative until irreversible transformation.

Natural predation, disease or territorial aggression remain distinct.

---

# 46. Corruption of Materials

Materials may acquire corruption states.

Examples:

- Cloudstone becomes unstable;
- Stormglass transmits corrupted charge;
- Hymnstone carries distorted resonance;
- Lightning Core behaves unpredictably;
- Judgement Crystal references false/corrupted law state.

A material is not automatically a new `Corrupted X` item/block family.

State/provenance should be used first.

---

# 47. Corruption Detection

Detection may use:

- instruments;
- magic;
- Law Engine diagnostics;
- creature behaviour;
- material analysis;
- visual evidence;
- Codex research;
- institutional investigation.

No one method needs perfect certainty.

Ordinary political accusation cannot set corruption state without system evidence.

---

# 48. Cleansing

**Cleansing** means removing, separating, suppressing or neutralising genuine Void corruption where possible.

Cleansing may require:

- ritual;
- material reagent;
- Law Engine support;
- authority help;
- specialised facility;
- cross-realm resource;
- destruction of corrupted component;
- isolation;
- multiple stages.

Exact recipes remain FCC-13/global reconciliation data.

---

# 49. Cleansing Does Not Rewind History

Cleansing does not automatically restore:

- prior politics;
- prior doctrine;
- prior trust;
- lost population;
- destroyed structure;
- old route;
- old ecology;
- old leadership;
- old memories;
- previous law.

It removes/neutralises corruption.

Restoration is a separate process.

---

# 50. Stabilisation

**Stabilisation** prevents further deterioration and creates a safe condition for later repair/restoration.

Examples:

- quarantine corrupted zone;
- ground unstable storm system;
- anchor drifting island;
- suspend corrupted Law Engine;
- isolate corrupted Ascension Gate;
- contain Lightning Core;
- establish neutral corridor;
- seal contaminated archive.

Stabilisation may be temporary or persistent.

---

# 51. Repair

**Repair** restores physical or technical function.

Examples:

- rebuild Current Beacon;
- replace anchor;
- repair hull;
- restore Law Engine hardware;
- reseal High-Aether habitat;
- reconstruct Ascension Gate frame.

Repair does not automatically:

- cleanse corruption;
- restore legal legitimacy;
- restore sacred-law recognition;
- restore politics.

---

# 52. Restoration

**Restoration** is the broader process of bringing a site/ecology/institution into a stable functional future.

Restoration may involve:

- cleansing;
- repair;
- replanting;
- migration recovery;
- law revision;
- institutional reform;
- resettlement;
- diplomacy;
- new leadership;
- habitat reconstruction;
- route reopening.

Restoration need not mean returning to the exact prior state.

---

# 53. Restoration Outcomes

Valid restoration outcomes include:

- historical restoration;
- adaptive reconstruction;
- repurposing;
- rewilding;
- new settlement;
- new institution;
- new law;
- new authority;
- memorialisation.

The game should allow “better but different” outcomes.

---
