FCC-05C defines the authoritative geology, material and resource canon for the **Ascendant Reach**.

It owns the realm-local definition of:

- the principal geological substrate of the floating realm;
- the canonical native metal lineage;
- storm-formed glass/crystal resources;
- aerial fibre resources;
- condensed storm-energy resources;
- atmospheric gas and luminous condensate resources;
- upper-realm resonant sacred stone;
- the relationship between lower-realm base materials and upper-realm divine transformations;

- material provenance and where resources are generated or harvested;
- which material families may sensibly receive construction, mechanical, textile, optical or ritual forms;

- universal processing reuse and the limited specialist Ascendant processing conditions that are actually distinctive;

# 2. Material Canon Statement

Ascendant Reach uses a **vertical material progression** rather than two disconnected resource lists inherited from its former realm identities.

The realm's lower and middle altitudes provide practical sky-world materials used by ordinary civilisations:

- Cloudstone;
- Skyiron;
- Stormglass;
- Windspun Fibre;
- Lightning Cores;
- Sunmist;
- Aether Gas.

The upper realm introduces one additional truly distinctive geological family:

- Hymnstone.

High-Aether and Divine Concord processing then **transforms, attunes or specialises** compatible base materials rather than introducing a second unrelated periodic table.

The canonical pattern is:

> **Lower sky geology and storm industry -> High-Aether processing -> divine transformation -> specialised sacred products and infrastructure.**

This means:

- Celestial Metal descends from Skyiron;
- Halo Glass descends from Stormglass;
- Judgement Crystal is a law-attuned Stormglass/Halo Glass state;
- Grace Thread descends from Windspun Fibre;
- Storm Metal is storm-attuned Skyiron;
- Concord Metal is a specialised Celestial Metal formulation;
- Saint Steel is a crafted alloy, not a native ore;
- Threshold Stone, Sanctuary Stone and Silent Stone are context/attunement states rather than three unnecessary stone families;
- Sacred Fibre is a cultural/category term rather than a separate base fibre;
- Covenant Wax is crafted rather than mined;
- Blessing Salt remains a specialist mineral/reagent rather than an equal foundational geological family.

The material system must preserve strong provenance. A material should exist because the realm's geology, atmosphere, ecology, storm systems, sacred-law conditions or civilisation processes can actually produce it.

# 3. Canonical Material Hierarchy

## 3.1 Major Native Material and Resource Families

The Ascendant Reach has eight principal realm-local material/resource families.

1. **Cloudstone** — principal floating-realm stone and structural geology.
2. **Skyiron** — principal native metal lineage.
3. **Stormglass** — lightning-formed natural glass/crystal family.
4. **Windspun Fibre** — principal specialist aerial flexible fibre.
5. **Lightning Core** — condensed storm-energy mineral/electromagical resource.
6. **Sunmist** — luminous high-atmosphere magical aerosol/condensate.
7. **Aether Gas** — strategic atmospheric gas family.
8. **Hymnstone** — rare sacred resonant upper-realm stone.

These eight are the realm's primary material foundations for FCC purposes.

## 3.2 Canonical High-Altitude and Divine Transformations

The following are derived from, attuned from, or formulated from the major families:

- **Celestial Metal** <- Skyiron;
- **Storm Metal** <- storm-attuned Skyiron;
- **Concord Metal** <- specialised civic/divine Celestial Metal;
- **Halo Glass** <- divine/high-aether-attuned Stormglass;
- **Judgement Crystal** <- law-attuned Stormglass/Halo Glass;
- **Grace Thread** <- refined/divinely attuned Windspun Fibre.

These remain meaningful named materials/products because they have distinct gameplay properties, processing requirements and cultural uses, but they do not require independent ore/ecology foundations.

## 3.3 Specialist Resources and Crafted Products

Canonical specialist resources/products include:

- Blessing Salt;
- Covenant Wax;
- Lightning Essence;
- Thunder Pearl;
- Saint Steel;
- Threshold Stone state;
- Sanctuary Stone state;
- Silent Stone state;
- Underside Stone facies/state.

Additional minor compounds, mixtures, treated forms or culturally named grades may exist only where a real recipe, system role or provenance justifies them.

# 4. Geological Framework

## 4.1 Floating-Realm Geology

Ascendant Reach geology must support continent-scale floating landmasses without requiring every individual block to simulate unrestricted buoyancy.

The floating-realm geological model may include:

- Cloudstone-dominant continental cores;
- dense or grounded mineral strata;
- buoyant Cloudstone seams;
- high-buoyancy structural masses;
- Skyiron-bearing veins and caldera deposits;
- Stormglass fields created by repeated extreme lightning interaction;
- volcanic and gas-rich Skyforge geology;
- underside metamorphism caused by pressure, gravity profile and weathering;
- High-Aether weathering and radiation exposure;
- Hymnstone growth or transformation in long-lived upper sacred environments;
- artificial anchor, stabilisation and gravity-support structures produced by civilisation.

World generation remains deterministic and save-persistent.

## 4.2 Buoyancy Is a Material Property Interface

Cloudstone can carry a **bounded buoyancy grade/state**.

Canonical grades may include conceptually:

- grounded/low-buoyancy Cloudstone;
- ordinary structural Cloudstone;
- buoyant Cloudstone;
- rare high-buoyancy Cloudstone.

Exact implementation names, numeric coefficients and physics representation are technical concerns rather than FCC content.

Buoyancy grade can influence:

- landmass generation;
- structural engineering;
- vessel or platform design;
- anchor requirements;
- salvage value;
- damage/failure consequences;
- gravity/Choir event aftermath.

A single Cloudstone brick does **not** automatically levitate arbitrary mass. Supported mass and movement remain governed by universal construction, vehicle and physics systems.

## 4.3 Underside Geological Facies

**Underside Stone** is not a separate unrelated stone family.

It is a Cloudstone facies/state associated with the lower surfaces and deep interiors of major floating continents.

It may differ through:

- increased density;
- different mineral inclusions;
- altered gravity response;
- stronger weathering;
- darker or compressed structure;
- different extraction difficulty;
- association with ancient anchor systems and underside ruins.

Only physically sensible derived forms should inherit the Underside state.

## 4.4 Skyforge Calderas

Skyforge Calderas are the principal high-energy geological regions for:

- Skyiron extraction;
- metal-rich gas capture;
- specialised thermal processing;
- storm-metal production;
- Lightning Essence handling;
- volcanic/atmospheric industry.

They do not automatically create a second metal tier merely because their deposits are hotter or more charged.

## 4.5 High-Aether Geology

High-Aether conditions can alter existing materials through:

- extreme cold;
- thin atmosphere;
- magical radiation;
- Sunmist exposure;
- unusual gravity profiles;
- long-duration divine overlap;
- sacred-law resonance.

These conditions are important for Celestial, Halo, Grace and Hymnstone processing but do not imply that all High-Aether terrain is a unique material.

# 5. Cloudstone Family

## 5.1 Identity

**Cloudstone** is the principal native stone family of Ascendant Reach.

It forms the majority of:

- floating continental masses;
- island cliffs;
- ordinary stone terrain;
- civic foundations;
- anchor structures;
- sky-settlement construction;
- selected vessel/platform structural systems.

Cloudstone is fantastical in its buoyancy relationship but should still read and behave as **stone** in ordinary material contexts.

## 5.2 Provenance

Cloudstone may be obtained from:

- natural floating-island terrain;
- surface outcrops;
- quarries;
- underside mines;
- caldera strata;
- salvaged structures;
- ruin reclamation.

Mining it does not automatically produce a separate generic `Cloudstone Item` if the mined object remains the same placeable block. The universal Block/Item single-definition rule applies.

## 5.3 Canonical Derived-Form Eligibility

Cloudstone may sensibly support:

- natural block;
- cut Cloudstone;
- bricks;
- slabs;
- stairs;
- walls;
- pillars;
- selected arches/trim;
- foundation blocks;
- anchor/load-bearing components;
- carved or inscribed civic forms.

It should **not** receive every possible geometry merely for completeness.

## 5.4 Buoyancy States

Buoyancy is represented as grade/state data where practical rather than duplicating every Cloudstone shape into multiple registry families.

For example, a buoyant Cloudstone stair and grounded Cloudstone stair need not become separate authored material families if one state-capable definition can express the difference safely.

## 5.5 Export Behaviour

Cloudstone remains physically stable outside Ascendant Reach.

Its strongest buoyancy behaviour may:

- weaken;
- become inactive;
- require stabilisation;
- depend on imported Ascendant components;
- function only inside compatible engineered systems.

Physical existence degrades **after specialised capability**, not before.

# 6. Skyiron and Metal Lineages

## 6.1 Skyiron

**Skyiron** is the principal native metal of Ascendant Reach.

It is associated particularly with:

- Skyforge Calderas;
- deep floating-continent mineral strata;
- underside mining;
- storm-exposed deposits;
- ancient sky-industry sites.

Ascendant Reach does not require separate native ores for every named metal adjective in legacy material.

## 6.2 Canonical Skyiron Processing Forms

Skyiron can support the standard metal lineage where gameplay requires it:

- ore/raw concentrate;
- ingot;
- plate;
- rod;
- wire;
- fittings;
- fasteners;
- selected gears/mechanical components;
- structural reinforcement;
- tools, weapons and armour components where progression permits.

Exact recipes and tool tiers are deferred to FCC-05G/FCC-13.

## 6.3 Celestial Metal

**Celestial Metal** is a high-altitude/divinely transformed Skyiron lineage.

It is not an unrelated native ore.

Its canonical identity may include:

- reduced effective weight;
- strong magical conduction;
- High-Aether durability;
- compatibility with sacred-law infrastructure;
- specialist enchantment response;
- high-value celestial construction.

The canonical production pattern is:

> **Skyiron + validated High-Aether/divine processing conditions -> Celestial Metal.**

Its precise reagents and recipe quantities are deferred.

## 6.4 Storm Metal

**Storm Metal** is storm-attuned Skyiron or compatible Skyiron-derived product.

It may prioritise:

- charge conduction;
- lightning resistance;
- storm machinery;
- vessel grounding;
- weather instrumentation;
- power infrastructure.

It is not another ore family.

## 6.5 Concord Metal

**Concord Metal** is a specialised civic/divine formulation of Celestial Metal.

It may be used for:

- Tribunal infrastructure;
- sacred civic fittings;
- law-compatible mechanisms;
- diplomatic structures;
- high-grade seals and architectural systems.

Its name reflects formulation and cultural function, not a new geology.

## 6.6 Saint Steel

**Saint Steel** is a crafted martial alloy family.

It can use ordinary universal steel plus appropriate Ascendant/Celestial components where final recipes justify the combination.

It is not mined as Saint Steel Ore.

## 6.7 Charged Metal

“Charged Metal” is a state applied to a compatible material after receiving electrical/storm energy.

A charged state may decay, discharge or be stabilised.

It is not a separate material definition unless irreversible transformation creates genuinely different physical behaviour.

Stormglass may support:

- plate;

- conductive/storm component;
- sensor component;

## 7.3 Halo Glass

**Halo Glass** is divine/high-aether-attuned Stormglass.

It may display:

- improved magical clarity;
- radiance interaction;

- optical or ritual properties;

## 7.4 Judgement Crystal

**Judgement Crystal** is a law-attuned Stormglass/Halo Glass state.

Stormglass remains physically stable outside Ascendant Reach.

Halo Glass and Judgement Crystal retain physical form, but their highest sacred-law sensitivity may weaken or become dormant outside compatible conditions.

## 9.1 Lightning Core

A **Lightning Core** is a rare condensed electomagical nodule formed where repeated extreme lightning interacts with Stormglass, charged mineral structures and compatible high-energy terrain.

It is explicitly **not** a generic creature heart.

Creature storm organs remain creature-derived resources owned by FCC-05E.

## 9.2 Canonical Core States and Forms

Lightning Core may exist as:

- natural condensed core;
- contained core;
- calibrated core/component;
- charged state;
- depleted state;
- damaged/unstable state where supported.

It does not receive arbitrary construction-brick forms.

## 9.3 Uses

Lightning Cores may support:

- storm-energy capture;
- power storage/interface;
- airship systems;
- defence;
- weather machinery;
- advanced automation;
- high-energy magic;
- Ascension infrastructure;
- specialist settlement systems.

Exact machine recipes and energy values are deferred.

## 9.4 Lightning Essence

**Lightning Essence** is a harvested or contained energetic reagent.

It can be used for:

- charging;
- calibration;
- storm alchemy;
- Lightning Core processing;
- temporary electrical effects;
- specialist manufacturing.

It is not a replacement for Lightning Core.

## 9.5 Thunder Pearl

**Thunder Pearl** is a minor biological/mineralised Thunderhead resource where appropriate ecology can produce it.

Because its source may involve living organisms, exact source anatomy and ethical harvesting rules are owned jointly with FCC-05E.

This document recognises the material identity and its possible polished/calibrated component forms.

## 9.6 Export and Containment

Lightning Cores remain physical outside Ascendant Reach but:

- may discharge;
- require containment;
- may lose charge without maintenance;
- can become dangerous when damaged;
- do not gain unrestricted power merely because they are imported elsewhere.

# 15. Processing Foundations

## 15.1 Universal Processing Reuse

Ascendant Reach reuses universal Leyforge processing systems wherever possible.

Compatible existing systems may include:

- crafting;
- stone cutting;
- sawmill/wood processing;
- furnace/smelting;
- forge/anvil;
- loom/textile processing;
- alchemy;
- enchanting;
- rune/ritual systems;
- machine assembly;
- fluid/gas containment;
- automation/logistics.

The realm must not create a new station solely because a material has an Ascendant name.

## 15.2 Distinctive Ascendant Infrastructure

Specialist infrastructure is justified where the process itself is unique.

Canonical specialist infrastructure may include:

- **Cloudstone Foundry** — large-scale Cloudstone/Skyiron structural and industrial processing complex;
- storm-harvesting and Lightning Core infrastructure;
- High-Aether processing environments;
- sacred/ritual processing sites that provide validated divine conditions;
- Law Engine interfaces where a material requires explicit law attunement;
- specialised gas capture/containment systems;
- existing universal advanced forge/furnace and loom systems operating under Ascendant conditions.

A distinctive structure can be culturally important without requiring an entirely new universal crafting mechanic.

## 15.3 Celestial Transformation Condition

Celestial Metal, Halo Glass and Grace Thread require more than ordinary recipe execution.

Their transformation requires:

1. a compatible base material;
2. a valid processing station or process;
3. a validated High-Aether/divine condition;
4. any required reagent/catalyst;
5. sufficient authority/access where the processing site is institutionally controlled.

Possible condition sources include:

- altitude/environment;
- recognised Law Garden;
- authorised sacred workshop;
- compatible Divine Domain;
- Sunmist;
- Hymnstone;
- a ritual or Law Engine interface;
- specialist high-tier machinery.

The exact combination is recipe content deferred to FCC-05G/FCC-13.

## 15.4 Processing Must Not Become Divine Arbitrary Approval

A god does not need to manually bless every ingot.

Where divine recognition or permission matters, it is represented through explicit gameplay states such as:

- site access;
- recognised ritual status;
- domain permission;
- required blessing state;
- legal workshop ownership;
- quest/authority outcome.

The recipe remains deterministic once its stated conditions are satisfied.

# 16. Derived-Form Governance

## 16.1 No Material x Shape Explosion

A material only receives a derived form when:

- the form is physically sensible;
- the form has construction, equipment, machine, ritual or decorative use;
- the form is likely to appear in actual content;
- the form preserves material readability;
- the form does not merely duplicate another state/variant.

## 16.2 Representative Eligibility Matrix

| Family | Construction Shapes | Mechanical Forms | Flexible/Textile Forms | Optical/Ritual Forms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cloudstone | High | Medium | None | Medium |
| Skyiron | Medium | High | None | Medium |
| Celestial Metal | Medium | High | None | High |
| Stormglass | Medium | Medium | None | High |
| Halo Glass | Medium | Low/Medium | None | High |

Create a genuinely separate material only when transformation is irreversible enough to produce distinct physical/gameplay identity.

- Blessing Salt;

**Rare / institutionally constrained**
- high-grade Celestial Metal;
- specialised Halo Glass/Judgement Crystal;

Exact rarity numbers are not locked here.

## 17.2 Resource Regeneration

Resource families do not all regenerate identically.

Examples:

- Cloudstone and Skyiron are geological and generally finite per generated deposit unless a world event explicitly creates/exposes new material;
- Stormglass can form through suitably extreme persistent storm conditions/events;
- Lightning Cores may form/reform only in rare validated high-energy conditions;
- Sunmist and Aether Gas are atmospheric and can be renewable under controlled extraction rules;
- Windspun Fibre depends on living ecology and sustainable harvest;
- Hymnstone may develop extremely slowly under stable sacred conditions rather than respawning as ordinary ore;
- Blessing Salt can be renewable where the relevant water/mineral system continues functioning.

This preserves different economic identities.

## 17.3 Sustainable Harvest

Harvest methods should preserve consequences where appropriate.

Examples:

- over-extracting buoyant Cloudstone can destabilise structures or landmass-support systems if gameplay explicitly exposes that risk;
- destructive Aerial Kelp harvesting can damage ecology and future fibre supply;
- aggressive storm harvesting can affect local energy infrastructure or safety;
- sacred-site extraction can create legal/political consequences without pretending the material itself is morally sentient.

Exact settlement/economy simulation belongs to FCC-05F and global systems.

# 18. Cross-Realm Export and Stabilisation

## 18.1 General Rule

Ascendant materials follow the cross-realm FCC principle:

> **Specialised capability normally degrades before physical existence.**

A material does not disappear merely because it leaves its native realm unless its actual physical state cannot be maintained.

## 18.2 Material-Specific Export Behaviour

### Cloudstone

Remains stone. Strong buoyancy may weaken or require stabilisation/engineering.

### Skyiron

Remains functional metal.

### Celestial Metal

A properly completed transformation remains Celestial Metal. Some domain-specific properties may be weaker without compatible conditions.

### Stormglass

Remains glass/crystal. Stored charge may dissipate.

### Halo Glass / Judgement Crystal

Remain physical glass/crystal. Sacred-law sensitivity may weaken, become dormant or require maintained attunement.

### Windspun Fibre

Remains fibre/textile.

### Grace Thread

Remains textile. Stronger divine effects may require maintenance or compatible conditions.

### Lightning Core

Remains physical but requires containment and may discharge.

### Sunmist

Requires proper containment. Uncontained Sunmist disperses according to universal gas/aerosol rules.

### Aether Gas

Requires pressure/containment appropriate to its state.

### Hymnstone

Remains stone. High-order resonance may depend on suitable ritual/domain conditions.

### Blessing Salt

Remains salt/reagent. Specific blessing effects remain recipe/state dependent.

### Covenant Wax

Remains crafted wax/seal material. Legal or sacred recognition may depend on the institution/domain that recognises the seal.

Material properties do not silently create Divine Domain authority.

- Blessing Salt can carry a ritual effect but does not automatically cleanse every corruption state;
- Celestial Metal can be compatible with sacred infrastructure without being morally “good.”

# 31. Canon Summary

Ascendant Reach's material identity is built around a compact set of strong foundations rather than a collection of unrelated magical substances.

Its ordinary civilisations quarry **Cloudstone**, mine and work **Skyiron**, harvest **Stormglass**, process **Windspun Fibre**, capture **Aether Gas** and **Sunmist**, and exploit rare **Lightning Cores**. At the highest altitudes, long-lived sacred conditions produce **Hymnstone** and allow compatible materials to be transformed into **Celestial Metal**, **Halo Glass** and **Grace Thread**. More specialised names such as **Storm Metal**, **Concord Metal**, **Saint Steel**, **Judgement Crystal**, **Blessing Salt** and **Covenant Wax** retain their identities through clear provenance, formulation or ritual use rather than becoming unnecessary parallel ore families.

The result is a material progression that physically reflects the realm's core fantasy:

> **The higher the player climbs, the more ordinary sky-world industry becomes capable of interacting with the divine—without abandoning understandable geology, resource provenance or predictable material families.**
