# 1. Purpose

FCC-05A defines the authoritative high-level identity, vertical world structure, realm-law families, divine-domain model, progression architecture, portal-access contract, canon boundaries, system ownership, supersession rules and implementation-facing constraints for the **Ascendant Reach**.

The Ascendant Reach is a **complete persistent major vertical realm** formed by the canonical merger of the former **Aetheric Reach** and **Divine Concords** concepts.

It contains, at realm-local canon level:

- five macro-altitude bands;
- twelve major biome families;
- floating continents, cloud seas, Windways, storm belts, underside ecosystems, High-Aether regions and divine territories;
- bounded gravity, atmosphere, exposure, sacred-law and domain-state systems;
- common geology, native metals, storm materials, atmospheric resources and divine transformations;
- native trees, crops, Aerial Kelp, high-altitude flora and sacred-law ecology;
- twelve principal creature families and a complete vertical food web;
- lower-sky civilisations, vessel cultures, guilds, agrarian communities, pilgrimage societies, temple polities, Covenant Courts and celestial bureaucracies;
- twenty-four settlement archetypes and twelve signature structures;
- eight canonical dungeon families, reusable trial/domain frameworks, guardian families, fixed realm authorities and variable authority foundations;
- twelve principal hazard families, eight regional state families and twelve realm-event families;
- storm, aerial, buoyancy, vessel, High-Aether, celestial-material and sacred-law progression;
- one canonical interdimensional portal family: the **Ascension Gate**;
- bounded relationships with other realms, future Pocket Realms and the Void Between.

FCC-05A does **not** hold every individual asset definition. Later FCC-05 documents own the detailed canon for biomes, terrain, materials, flora, crops, creatures, civilisations, settlements, structures, blocks, items, recipes, dungeons, guardians, authorities, events, corruption states, restoration, cross-realm state interaction and final art handoff.

This document establishes the realm-level rules those later documents must obey.

# 2. Canon Statement

The **Ascendant Reach** is a vast persistent vertical realm in which the player physically ascends from habitable floating worlds through storm-dominated sky layers and thin High Aether into territories where **divine law increasingly overlaps ordinary physical reality**.

Its central fantasy is:

> **A world where ascent is simultaneously geographic, environmental, technological, cultural and metaphysical: the player begins among floating lands and sky civilisations, climbs through dangerous weather and altered gravity, and eventually enters divine territories whose declared laws can become part of local reality.**

The Ascendant Reach is one realm, not two dimensions hidden behind one name.

The former **Aetheric Reach** survives as the lower and middle physical-sky identity of the merged realm. The former **Divine Concords** survives as the upper divine territories where sacred law, divine institutions and metaphysical authority become dominant.

The realm may contain:

- broad floating grassland continents;
- storm-shaped crystal archipelagos;
- Thunderhead Seas containing dense cloud-fluid systems and suspended true-water regions;
- enormous Aerial Kelp forests spanning altitude bands;
- volcanic Skyforge Calderas;
- dark inhabited undersides of floating continents;
- near-silent High-Aether expanses;
- pilgrimage thresholds into upper divine civilisation;
- Law Gardens and Mercy-aligned sanctuaries;
- Radiant Tribunal Cities;
- War-Saint Fields;
- the Concordant Firmament;
- ordinary farmers, miners, herders, pilots, traders, builders and families;
- nomadic vessel cultures and Airship Leagues;
- Storm Monasteries and atmospheric guilds;
- pilgrims, priests, heretics, exiles and neutral custodians;
- divine servants and self-aware constructs with normal personhood protections;
- gods, saints, divine authorities, sacred institutions and contested domain rulers;
- corrupted gods where genuine Void provenance exists;
- hostile, broken or tyrannical divinity that is **not** automatically Void-corrupted;
- persistent environmental, political and divine consequences after major events and authority outcomes.

Ascendant Reach is **divine without being a universal heaven**, **vertical without being a simple level ladder**, and **dangerous without reducing every challenge to combat**.

# 3. Explicit Non-Identity: What Ascendant Reach Is Not

The Ascendant Reach is **not**:

- two active realm instances disguised under one display name;
- a separate Aetheric dimension plus a separate Divine dimension;
- a universal heaven or afterlife;
- the automatic destination of good, holy, ascended or dead beings;
- proof that one religion or theology is objectively correct;
- a realm in which all gods are benevolent;
- a realm in which all divine authorities are omnipotent;
- a realm where higher altitude always means every encounter is numerically harder;
- a realm where every piece of Cloudstone floats without limits;
- a realm where falling from an island automatically teleports the player to another dimension;
- a physical route into the Void Between merely because the player climbs high enough or falls low enough;
- a realm where Windways, celestial roads, miracles or pilgrimage routes bypass the canonical portal system;
- a realm where sacred laws can silently overwrite universal save integrity, player identity, inventory authority, multiplayer authority or death/respawn ownership;
- a morality parser that evaluates arbitrary player thoughts;
- a system in which every domain violation produces generic holy damage;
- a system in which hostile, abandoned, tyrannical or doctrinally extreme divinity automatically equals Void corruption;
- a system in which gods or Divine Domains own universal death, souls or resurrection;
- a second Pocket Realm construction system;
- a justification for unrestricted per-block gravity simulation or arbitrary physics rewriting;
- a reason to create one new crafting station for every Ascendant material;
- a reason to create a new ore, glass, fibre or sacred material for every biome adjective or divine law.

These exclusions are canon, not implementation suggestions.

# 5. Realm Fantasy Pillars

## 5.1 Ascent Is the Realm

Ascendant Reach is designed around a meaningful climb.

The first visit does not place the player directly in the highest divine territory. Normal progression begins in a validated lower or middle arrival region and expands upward through increasingly difficult environmental, technical and political conditions.

The player should experience the realm as a changing vertical world rather than a menu of disconnected altitude zones.

## 5.2 Floating Worlds Are Habitable Worlds

Floating islands and continents are not merely combat arenas.

They support:

- farms;
- settlements;
- mines;
- roads and bridges;
- vessel infrastructure;
- livestock;
- trade;
- political borders;
- ordinary family life;
- industrial sites;
- wilderness ecology;
- long-term construction and settlement growth.

The lower and middle Reach must feel like a civilisation-scale world in its own right.

## 5.3 Weather Is Geography

Storms, Windways, Thunderhead Seas and high-altitude currents shape travel, ecology, settlement placement and economy.

Weather can affect:

- vessel routes;
- Cloudwhale migration;
- storm harvesting;
- settlement safety;
- resource access;
- Current Beacon operation;
- travel windows;
- dungeon access;
- event state;
- authority outcomes.

Storm behaviour must be readable enough for meaningful preparation and counterplay.

## 5.4 Gravity Is Regional, Not Arbitrary

Gravity can vary by altitude, landmass, regional state, ancient infrastructure and divine territory.

This variation is represented through **bounded gravity profiles and authored state transitions**, not unrestricted per-voxel physics simulation.

Gravity must be deterministic, save-safe and understandable enough to support traversal, construction and world-state change.

## 5.5 Divinity Is Political and Metaphysical, Not Automatically Moral

The upper realm contains gods, saints, sacred institutions, divine servants, tribunals, theological factions and bounded Divine Domains.

Divine authority may be:

- benevolent;
- protective;
- indifferent;
- authoritarian;
- frightened;
- compromised;
- divided;
- corrupted;
- absent;
- dying;
- reforming;
- revolutionary;
- internally contradictory.

The presence of a god proves that a powerful divine being exists. It does not automatically prove every theological claim made about that being.

## 5.6 Sacred Law Is Declared and Bounded

Divine Domains may enforce selected local laws concerning recognised concepts such as:

- hospitality;
- violence;
- ownership;
- challenge;
- truthfulness;
- access;
- ritual conduct;
- recognised sanctuary;
- declared obligations.

These laws are authored gameplay contracts.

They do not parse arbitrary morality, infer private thoughts or override unrelated universal systems.

## 5.7 Many Cultures Share One Sky

No single Ascendant civilisation represents the whole realm.

The realm deliberately supports overlapping identities including:

- island freeholds;
- trade leagues;
- vessel nomads;
- farming cultures;
- storm orders;
- mining and forge guilds;
- underside peoples;
- pilgrimage republics;
- temple polities;
- Covenant Courts;
- celestial bureaucracies;
- heretics and reformers;
- exiles;
- neutral custodians;
- divine-domain societies.

Cultures may combine several of these at once.

## 5.8 Multiple Outcomes Matter

Major conflicts may support:

- rescue;
- negotiation;
- treaty;
- repair;
- retuning;
- restoration;
- legal challenge;
- political reform;
- cleansing;
- containment;
- redirection;
- alliance;
- recognition;
- deposition;
- combat;
- killing where appropriate.

Fixed realm authorities are not designed solely as mandatory kill-bosses.

# 6. Canonical Macro-Altitude Structure

Ascendant Reach uses five broad macro-altitude bands. These bands are progression and environmental regions, **not five biomes**.

## 6.1 Lower Reach

The most broadly habitable and forgiving altitude band.

Typical characteristics:

- large floating continents and plateaus;
- broadly breathable atmosphere;
- conventional settlement viability;
- common Cloudstone geology;
- Cloudgrass agriculture;
- ordinary sky livestock and trade;
- common first-arrival regions;
- early Windway access.

Lower Reach content may still contain dangerous storms, hostile factions or severe local hazards.

## 6.2 Aetheric Reach

The mature sky-civilisation layer.

Typical characteristics:

- major floating-island civilisations;
- Windways and aerial trade routes;
- Airship Leagues;
- Aerial Kelp systems;
- Stormglass Archipelagos;
- Skyforge industry;
- more variable gravity;
- stronger weather pressure;
- larger aerial predators and vessel infrastructure.

## 6.3 Storm Heights

The major severe-weather altitude band.

Typical characteristics:

- Thunderhead Seas;
- extreme storm systems;
- high lightning frequency;
- storm harvesting;
- Thunderhead Citadels;
- severe turbulence;
- specialised vessels and shelter requirements;
- dangerous large-creature migration.

Storm Heights are generally more demanding but do not make every location uniformly hostile.

## 6.4 High Aether

A thin-atmosphere transition toward divine reality.

Typical characteristics:

- High Silence Expanse regions;
- weak sound propagation;
- severe cold/exposure;
- low-pressure or sealed-habitat requirements;
- unusual gravity;
- Sunmist and rare high-altitude resources;
- Starlichen and High-Aether fauna;
- first strong sustained divine overlap;
- transition toward Pilgrim Thresholds.

High Aether does not physically merge into the Void Between.

## 6.5 Divine Concords

The highest normal major altitude territories, where sacred/divine law becomes a dominant part of local reality.

Typical characteristics:

- Pilgrim Thresholds;
- Law Gardens;
- Radiant Tribunal Cities;
- War-Saint Fields;
- Concordant Firmament;
- Divine Domains;
- sacred-law infrastructure;
- celestial institutions;
- gods, saints and domain sovereigns;
- divine-material transformation conditions;
- inter-domain diplomacy and conflict.

Divine Concords are part of Ascendant Reach, not a separate dimension.

# 7. Canonical Realm Laws

Ascendant realm laws are divided into bounded rule families. They are gameplay contracts, not unrestricted metaphysical simulation.

## 7.1 Vertical Continuity Law

Ascendant Reach is one continuous realm whose altitude bands can be physically traversed through supported local movement and infrastructure.

The player may move between bands using:

- conventional movement where terrain permits;
- gliders and controlled descent systems;
- mounts;
- airships and cloud vessels;
- Windways;
- lifts;
- bridges;
- anchor chains;
- pilgrimage routes;
- specialised High-Aether infrastructure;
- other realm-local traversal systems.

No secondary interdimensional portal is required merely to move between macro-altitude bands.

## 7.2 Gravity Profile Law

Gravity varies through bounded regional profiles.

Possible supported profiles include:

- near-standard island gravity;
- reduced gravity;
- weak drift zones;
- local island-centred gravity;
- storm turbulence effects;
- infrastructure-stabilised gravity;
- Divine Domain gravity overrides.

Gravity changes must use deterministic saved state and clear system ownership.

The realm does not require arbitrary per-block gravity simulation.

## 7.3 Falling and Descent Law

Falling from a floating island is a genuine traversal emergency, not automatically an instant-kill trigger or an interdimensional transition.

A fall may lead through lower altitude regions and can allow recovery through:

- lower islands;
- gliding;
- emergency descent equipment;
- mounts;
- vessels;
- rescue infrastructure;
- updrafts and Windways;
- other explicitly supported systems.

Universal fall damage, health and death rules remain authoritative.

## 7.4 Atmospheric Gradient Law

Atmospheric conditions change by altitude and region.

At broad scale:

- Lower Reach is generally breathable;
- Aetheric Reach is breathable but increasingly exposed to weather;
- Storm Heights introduce severe local atmospheric and pressure hazards;
- High Aether contains thin-air, cold and sealed-habitat challenges;
- Divine Concords may contain locally sustained life conditions according to explicit Domain rules.

Ascendant Reach does not create three new permanent player meters for oxygen, pressure and cold. Regional environment profiles feed the universal survival systems.

## 7.5 Weather Readability Law

Storms and severe current states must be sufficiently telegraphed for meaningful play.

Supported cues may include:

- cloud shape and colour;
- lightning frequency;
- Windlace Swarm behaviour;
- Current Beacon warnings;
- vessel instruments;
- sound;
- NPC forecasts;
- map knowledge;
- event-state information.

The realm may contain surprise weather, but ordinary traversal must not rely on untelegraphed unavoidable gusts that arbitrarily delete player agency.

## 7.6 Divine Overlap Law

Divine influence increases gradually with altitude rather than beginning only at one invisible boundary.

As the player rises, the realm may show increasing:

- celestial phenomena;
- sacred materials;
- divine architecture;
- recognised Domain boundaries;
- miracles;
- sacred-law effects;
- divine institutions;
- direct activity by powerful divine beings.

The **Divine Concords** begin where that overlap becomes a dominant regional condition.

## 7.7 Divine Domain Law

A **Divine Domain** is a bounded territory inside Ascendant Reach whose local rule stack is partially defined by a divine authority, sacred principle, recognised institution or long-established metaphysical condition.

A Domain may occupy:

- a floating continent;
- city;
- garden;
- mountain;
- storm;
- temple territory;
- island chain;
- pilgrimage route;
- other geographically coherent territory.

A Divine Domain is not automatically a Pocket Realm or separate dimension.

## 7.8 Sacred-Law Declaration Law

A sacred law must be explicit enough for systems and players to understand its gameplay meaning.

Valid examples include:

- weapons may not be drawn before formal challenge;
- recognised guests receive sanctuary protection;
- stolen property becomes unusable within a defined Domain;
- deliberate false testimony triggers a declared consequence;
- selected paths require an accepted vow or recognised permission;
- violence changes a supported Domain state.

Sacred laws do not infer arbitrary hidden morality or private player thought.

## 7.9 Sacred-Law Consequence Law

Breaking a sacred law does **not** automatically cause generic holy damage.

Consequences are authored according to the law and may include:

- loss of guest status;
- route closure;
- guardian hostility;
- trial activation;
- suspended blessing;
- property restriction;
- legal penalty;
- political consequence;
- environmental state change;
- physical damage where specifically justified.

Access restrictions may be meaningful, but normal progression must retain a valid return/recovery path and may not silently strand the save.

## 7.10 Domain Conflict Law

Where Divine Domains overlap or their recognised laws conflict, resolution uses explicit priority and compatibility state.

Supported outcomes may include:

- negotiated boundary;
- neutral corridor;
- temporary suppression;
- Tribunal judgement;
- Law Engine arbitration;
- unstable overlap;
- event-driven political conflict.

No hidden universal rule automatically makes one theology supreme.

## 7.11 Divine Power Boundary Law

Gods and divine authorities may be extraordinarily powerful without being omnipotent.

They remain bounded by:

- domain;
- history;
- rivals;
- vulnerabilities;
- law;
- infrastructure;
- relationships;
- corruption state;
- political legitimacy;
- authored capabilities.

Divine entities cannot silently override universal:

- save integrity;
- player identity;
- inventory authority;
- multiplayer authority;
- universal death/respawn;
- unrelated stable IDs or progression.

## 7.12 Religion and Truth Law

The physical existence of divine beings and Divine Domains does not make one religion universally correct.

Cultures may:

- worship;
- reject;
- reinterpret;
- misunderstand;
- politically oppose;
- philosophically analyse;
- coexist with

recognized divine beings.

The game may establish developer/runtime facts about an entity without validating every cultural or theological claim made about it.

## 7.13 Personhood Independence Law

Origin, construction, divine assignment or intended service do not determine personhood.

Self-aware beings such as divine servants, awakened Edict Sentinels or ancient constructs receive normal personhood treatment where durable identity and agency are established.

They may not be treated as property merely because they were created for service.

## 7.14 Corruption Provenance Law

A dangerous, tyrannical, hostile, broken, fallen, abandoned or doctrinally extreme divine being or territory is **not automatically Void-corrupted**.

Void corruption requires explicit Void provenance.

Corruption applies state-first where practical. A separate transformed entity/material definition is justified only when irreversible transformation creates genuinely different anatomy, physical properties, identity or gameplay role.

Cleansing corruption does not automatically restore former doctrine, politics, relationships or physical conditions.

# 8. Internal Travel and Route Infrastructure

## 8.1 Windways

**Windways** are realm-spanning route/environment infrastructure rather than one top-level biome family.

They may:

- connect settlements;
- carry vessels;
- shift between bounded route states;
- support trade;
- create piracy/toll disputes;
- become blocked or redirected;
- respond to storm events and authority outcomes.

Local **Windway Corridor** sub-biomes may exist where the route itself dominates the environment.

## 8.2 Physical Internal Travel

Internal movement may use:

- roads and bridges on large islands;
- lifts and vertical structures;
- gliders;
- flying or controlled-descent mounts;
- airships;
- cloud vessels;
- Windways;
- anchor chains;
- pilgrimage roads;
- celestial bridges;
- domain-approved routes.

These systems move travellers **inside Ascendant Reach**.

They do not replace the Ascension Gate for normal interdimensional entry or exit.

## 8.3 Route Sovereignty

Control of a route does not automatically confer sovereignty over every territory reached by that route.

Separate claims may exist over:

- island territory;
- airspace;
- Windway tolls;
- convoy protection;
- docking rights;
- Ascension Gate infrastructure;
- Divine Domain authority;
- sanctuary access.

This distinction is foundational to Ascendant politics.

# 9. Universal Death and Respawn Ownership Exclusion

Ascendant Reach does not own universal death, afterlife or respawn.

## 9.1 Player Death

Player death remains governed by Leyforge's universal death/failure system.

Depending on universal progression/settings, that system may use:

- bed;
- shrine;
- home;
- village;
- world spawn;
- tombstone/recovery logic;
- other universally defined locations or recovery rules.

Falling from Ascendant Reach does not automatically send the player to a divine afterlife or another realm.

## 9.2 NPC and Creature Death

Ascendant Reach does not automatically absorb or judge the dead from other realms.

A god, saint, Concord Judge or Divine Domain does not become the universal authority over deceased NPCs or creatures.

## 9.3 Resurrection and Divine Intervention

FCC-05 does not own universal resurrection.

Specific divine content may interact with healing, restoration, protection or a later universal resurrection system only through explicit interfaces.

No Ascendant authority may silently override universal death-system ownership.

# 10. Portal Access Contract

All major Leyforge realms use **one canonical normal portal type**.

Ascendant Reach's canonical portal is the:

# **Ascension Gate**

The universal Realm Access system owns:

- portal runtime;
- route binding;
- transition orchestration;
- destination resolution;
- multiplayer authority;
- save/load behaviour;
- safe-return handling;
- portal linking;
- throughput execution;
- failure/recovery handling.

FCC-05 owns the Ascension Gate's:

- realm identity;
- physical construction language;
- realm-specific progression role;
- local discovery context;
- first-arrival rule;
- Ascendant-native upgrade path;
- realm-specific stabilisation requirements;
- high-altitude destination eligibility;
- realm-specific portal hazards and corruption interfaces.

## 10.1 Physical Gate Identity

The Ascension Gate should visually express **grounded support below and increasing vertical lift/celestial alignment above**.

Its realm-local contract includes:

1. structural frame/foundation;
2. conductive/stabilising fittings;
3. crafted **Ascension Keystone**;
4. vertical/aetheric attunement layer;
5. activation interface;
6. validated destination link.

The first-access construction package must not require materials that can only be acquired after successfully entering Ascendant Reach.

## 10.2 One Normal Portal Rule

The following are not alternate normal interdimensional portal types:

- Windways;
- celestial roads;
- pilgrimage routes;
- divine dismissal;
- miracles;
- airships;
- cloud vessels;
- falling out of the realm;
- flying above the Concordant Firmament;
- Storm Temples;
- Law Engines;
- Current Beacons.

Where useful, these may provide:

- clues;
- portal knowledge;
- permissions;
- destination binding knowledge;
- stabilisation;
- internal travel;
- quest progression;
- political access.

They do not bypass the Ascension Gate.

## 10.3 First Arrival

The first successful Ascension Gate entry resolves to a validated safe/recoverable **Lower Reach or lower Aetheric** destination.

It does not place the player directly into the Divine Concords.

The player's first meaningful access to the upper realm should normally require physical ascent, environmental adaptation and route/civilisation progression.

## 10.4 Advanced Destination Binding

Later progression may allow the **same Ascension Gate family** to bind to discovered and authorised Ascendant destinations.

Advanced upper-realm targeting may require:

- route discovery;
- portal upgrades;
- recognised permission;
- High-Aether capability;
- safe destination infrastructure;
- later global portal progression prerequisites.

No separate Divine Portal family is created.

## 10.5 Native Upgrade Path

Once Ascendant materials become available, the Ascension Gate may be upgraded through realm-native materials and technologies such as:

- Cloudstone;
- Skyiron;
- Celestial Metal;
- Stormglass;
- Lightning Core technology;
- Sunmist;
- Hymnstone;
- other validated realm-local components.

Upgrades may improve:

- stability;
- cargo throughput;
- route safety;
- recovery;
- advanced destination binding;
- high-altitude targeting.

## 10.6 Exact Recipe Deferral

The exact final Ascension Gate recipe, quantities, external prerequisite materials, portal-chain position and global progression dependency are intentionally deferred to the **final global portal-progression reconciliation**.

This is not a missing realm-local design decision.

Portal dependencies must be reconciled across all realms together so the full progression graph remains achievable and internally coherent.

# 11. Progression Model

Ascendant Reach progression is primarily **capability- and access-based**, not a single linear level ladder.

## Stage 1 — Foreshadowing and Research

The player learns that Ascendant Reach exists through supported clues such as:

- ancient portal remains;
- sky phenomena;
- religious or historical records;
- weather research;
- vessel cultures;
- divine traditions;
- recovered route knowledge;
- cross-realm discoveries;
- authored quest lines.

## Stage 2 — Ascension Gate Knowledge

The player discovers:

- the Ascension Gate identity;
- its broad physical construction logic;
- Ascension Keystone requirements;
- safe first-destination principles;
- basic return requirements.

Exact final recipe dependencies remain globally deferred.

## Stage 3 — First Lower-Reach Arrival

The player enters through a validated lower destination and gains access to:

- Cloudgrass and lower skyland exploration;
- Sky Freeholds and agrarian communities;
- early Windways;
- lower-sky resource gathering;
- Skyback transport/husbandry;
- basic aerial safety and rescue infrastructure;
- first Ascendant settlements and trade.

## Stage 4 — Aerial Traversal and Settlement Integration

Progression expands through:

- gliding/descent capability;
- improved route knowledge;
- vessel access;
- Windway navigation;
- Skywood/Cloudstone construction;
- Skyiron processing;
- Airship League relationships;
- settlement engineering and anchor safety;
- broader exploration of Aetheric Reach biomes.

## Stage 5 — Storm Mastery

The player gains capability around:

- Stormglass Archipelagos;
- Storm Temples;
- Storm Heights;
- Lightning Cores;
- grounding and electrical protection;
- Thunderhead Seas;
- advanced vessel operation;
- storm harvesting;
- dangerous migration events;
- storm-aligned dungeons and authorities.

## Stage 6 — High-Aether Adaptation

The player develops:

- High-Aether survival equipment;
- sealed or protected habitat capability;
- thin-atmosphere navigation;
- advanced Sunmist/Aether Gas handling;
- High Silence exploration;
- access to Pilgrim Threshold transition regions;
- upper-realm portal/destination knowledge.

## Stage 7 — Divine Concord Access

The player enters sustained divine territories through physical ascent and supported route/permission systems.

Progression expands into:

- Law Gardens;
- pilgrimage networks;
- temple polities;
- Covenant Courts;
- Radiant Tribunal Cities;
- War-Saint Fields;
- sacred-law systems;
- domain-specific permissions;
- celestial political conflicts.

## Stage 8 — Celestial Processing and Sacred Infrastructure

Advanced progression unlocks or teaches:

- Celestial Metal transformation;
- Halo Glass;
- Grace Thread;
- Hymnstone use;
- sacred-law-compatible infrastructure;
- advanced Ascension Gate upgrades;
- high-altitude settlement engineering;
- divine-domain construction and restoration.

## Stage 9 — Realm Authorities and Persistent Outcomes

The player may engage with fixed and variable authorities through supported outcomes involving:

- Tempest Leviathan;
- Gravity Choir Sovereign;
- Law Lion Ascendant;
- Fallen Saint;
- Concord Judge;
- Pilgrim Star;
- Sky-King Automata;
- Storm Sovereigns;
- Law-Bound Heralds;
- Domain Sovereigns;
- political or theological crisis frameworks.

Outcomes may permanently change:

- route safety;
- island position;
- storm behaviour;
- domain law;
- settlement viability;
- recognised permissions;
- celestial institutions;
- resource access;
- dungeon occupation;
- faction relationships.

## Stage 10 — Advanced Realm Integration

Late Ascendant progression may support:

- advanced Ascension Gate destination binding;
- high-capacity aerial construction;
- cross-realm export/stabilisation;
- advanced storm-energy networks;
- multi-domain diplomacy;

- Law Engines;
- Cloudstone systems;
- Windways;
- gravity infrastructure;
- Ascension Gates;

- settlements;

Removing corruption does not automatically:

- make a god benevolent;
- restore an old government;
- reverse political oppression;
- erase historical damage;
- restore a former doctrine;
- repair every structure;
- resolve every faction conflict.

Cleansing, restoration, reform and reconciliation are separate gameplay operations/outcomes.
