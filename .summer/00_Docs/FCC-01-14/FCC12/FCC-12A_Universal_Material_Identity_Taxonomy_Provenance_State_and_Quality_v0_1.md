# 1. Purpose

FCC-12A establishes the universal ontology by which Leyforge identifies and tracks matter across the Overworld and all current realms. It defines the boundary between **material identity**, **material family**, **physical form**, **state/facies**, **provenance**, **quality/grade/purity**, **ownership/location** and **manufactured component identity**.

The purpose is to let Leyforge contain both ordinary physical matter and highly specialised fantasy matter without either flattening everything into generic tiers or multiplying every adjective, shape, realm and condition into a duplicate Block/Item identity.

FCC-12A does **not** define exact recipes, quantities, stable IDs, final registry rows, portal recipes, station throughput or final art values.

---

# 2. Canonical Material Definition

> **A canonical material is a persistent underlying substance or materially distinct physical, magical or biological medium. One material identity may possess many physical forms, states, conditions, qualities, provenances, locations, owners and aliases. Those dimensions do not create new material identities unless composition, persistent structure or intrinsic behaviour changes enough to constitute genuinely different matter.**

This is the primary FCC-12 material-identity rule.

A material therefore does not become a different material merely because it is cut, moved, sold, exported, damaged, charged, polished, named differently by another culture or stored in another container.

---

# 3. Universal Material Axes

Leyforge separates the following axes rather than encoding them into one flat identity:

1. **Exact Material Identity** — what the matter fundamentally is.
2. **Material Superclass / Composition Family** — broad taxonomy such as metal, stone, glass, fibre or salt.
3. **Physical Form** — block, rubble, plank, plate, wire, lens, powder and other shapes/products.
4. **Phase** — solid, liquid, gas or other physically meaningful phase.
5. **Condition / State** — wet, charged, damaged, corrupted, polished and similar modifiers.
6. **Facies / Environmental Conditioning** — pressure, gravity, seasonal, coherence and other environmental expressions.
7. **Quality / Structural Quality** — mechanical integrity or object/material quality.
8. **Grade / Concentration** — richness of ore/feedstock or comparable composition concentration.
9. **Purity** — unwanted inclusions relative to intended material/formulation.
10. **Charge / Energy State** — stored energy independent of material quality.
11. **Affinity / Resonance / Attunement** — compatibility or alignment with magical/systemic contexts.
12. **Contamination / Corruption** — foreign matter or harmful influence.
13. **Provenance** — origin and meaningful history.
14. **Ownership** — current legal/social owner.
15. **Current Location** — current world, realm, region, container or structure.
16. **Processing History** — meaningful prior transformations or treatments.

These dimensions may interact, but none is automatically a substitute for exact material identity.

---

# 4. Material Identity and Physical Form

Material identity answers **what something is made of**. Physical form answers **what shape/product form it currently occupies**.

Examples:

- Granite may be raw rock, rubble, cut masonry, brick, slab or stair.
- Copper may be refined metal, ingot, plate, rod or wire.
- Oak Heartwood may be timber, plank, beam or post.
- Lucid Glass may be raw material, pane, plate, shard or lens.

Changing form does not create a new material unless the process also crosses the transformation threshold defined below.

---

# 5. Material Superclasses

Universal superclasses exist so systems can reason about broad physical and functional categories without merging exact substances.

Principal broad classes include:

- stone / rock;
- soil / earth;
- sediment / aggregate;
- clay / ceramic feedstock;
- ceramic;
- glass / vitreous material;
- crystal / crystalline mineral;
- gemstone;
- metal;
- alloy;
- wood / timber;
- fibre / textile feedstock;
- hide / leather;
- biological hard material;
- resin;
- sap;
- wax;
- oil;
- salt / mineral reagent;
- pearl / biomineral;
- fluid;
- gas / aerosol;
- particulate;
- energetic physical resource;
- manufactured formulation;
- manufactured component.

These classes are hierarchical and composable. One material may simultaneously be metal, conductive, structural, forgeable and magically attunable.

**Superclass membership never implies exact-material equivalence.** Silver and Soul Silver can both be metals while remaining distinct. Thunder Glass and Stormglass can both be vitreous/crystalline materials while remaining distinct.

---

# 6. Composition, Structure and Intrinsic Behaviour

Composition is a major criterion of material identity, but it is not the only one. Persistent microstructure, magical structure, biological organisation or other intrinsic architecture may also justify a distinct material where it materially affects behaviour.

This allows fantasy materials to be modelled without abandoning physical logic.

Examples of deliberately distinct sibling materials include:

- ordinary Silver vs Soul Silver;
- ordinary Iron vs Abyssal Iron;
- ordinary Iron vs Skyiron;
- ordinary Iron vs Ember Iron;
- ordinary Glass vs Memory Glass;
- ordinary Glass vs Stormglass;
- ordinary wood vs Soulwood;
- ordinary wood vs Living Heartwood.

Temporary charge, external influence or reversible attunement does **not** automatically create a new material.

---

# 7. Biological Matter

Biological materials use the same universal ontology as mineral and manufactured matter.

This includes wood, fibre, hide, leather, bone, horn, shell, chitin, sap, resin, wax, oils, pearls and biominerals. Biological-content systems continue to own species, anatomy, ecology and harvesting; FCC-12 owns the material identity once that matter enters material processing and exchange.

---

# 8. Energetic Matter, Fluids and Gases

Energy-related things are not flattened into one generic `energy material` class. Leyforge distinguishes physical energy-bearing material, stored charge, captured phenomenon, environmental field, fuel, magical reagent and manufactured storage/component identity.

Likewise, fluids and gases use the same core material-identity principles as solids. Container, pipeline, tank, ambient cloud or world-fluid representation does not create another material where composition and intrinsic behaviour remain the same.

A container is an object; its contents are material. A Furnace Core is a component; a Furnace Heart is a distinct resource. Raw Mana Crystal is physical material; mana charge is an energy/resource state.

---

# 9. Shared-Matter Doctrine

> **Physically and intrinsically equivalent matter uses one canonical material identity regardless of realm, region, current owner or current location. Provenance and ordinary environmental conditioning remain separate metadata/state. Broad physical terms such as sand, gravel, lava and clay may describe form/class/composition families rather than one monolithic substance. Specialist realm materials remain distinct when their persistent composition, structure or intrinsic behaviour is materially different.**

Realm boundaries do not create material duplicates.

Prohibited duplicate logic includes creating separate `Overworld Basalt`, `Ashen Basalt`, `Verdant Iron` or `Deep Copper` identities where the matter is physically the same. Use provenance instead.

---

# 10. Shared Ordinary Matter

## 10.1 Basalt

Basalt is the clearest already-locked shared example. Overworld and Ashen Basalt use the same canonical material identity where physical identity matches. Source realm, stratum, volcanic field or archaeological history may remain provenance.

## 10.2 Ordinary Water

Ordinary physical Water is one shared universal material identity wherever the source means genuinely ordinary water. Temperature, salinity, contamination, dissolved material, magical charge and provenance are represented separately where appropriate.

**Dreamwater remains distinct.** It is not simply ordinary Water with Somnolent provenance.

## 10.3 Lava / Magma

`Lava` and `magma` describe molten geological matter rather than one realm-specific fantasy substance or one compositionless universal exact material. Exact identity follows composition/material lineage. Basaltic, silica-rich and magically transformed molten matter can therefore differ while using one molten-rock architecture.

Ashen provenance alone does not create `Ashen Lava` as a separate universal material.

## 10.4 Sand

`Sand` is primarily a particle-size/material-form class. Exact material follows composition and intrinsic behaviour. Quartz-rich, volcanic and specialist magical sands do not merge merely because they are granular.

**Dream Sand remains distinct.**

## 10.5 Gravel

`Gravel` is likewise a coarse aggregate/particle-size class. Exact composition may be retained where gameplay needs it. A mixed/common gravel product may exist, but realm provenance alone does not create separate gravel substances.

## 10.6 Clay

`Clay` is a mineral/feedstock superclass rather than one universal exact substance. Composition-bearing clays can share processing capabilities without becoming one material.

Current relationships remain:

- River Clay — clay/mineral source/deposit terminology;
- White Kaolin — specialised kaolin clay/mineral feedstock;
- Brick Loam — clay-rich/alluvial/brickmaking-quality soil profile;
- Clay Soil — one of the eight principal Overworld soil families.

## 10.7 Ordinary Manufactured Glass

Physically equivalent ordinary silica-based manufactured glass is one shared material. Colour, ordinary tempering and finish can be state/formulation/quality where appropriate.

Specialist glasses remain distinct, including Memory Glass, Lucid Glass, Stormglass, Ventglass and Hellglass. Thunder Glass remains distinct from Stormglass.

## 10.8 Common Metals and Alloys

Common metals such as Copper, Tin, Iron, Lead, Nickel, Cobalt, Silver and Gold retain one physical identity across realms. The same materially equivalent alloy formulation also remains one identity across realms.

Distinct specialist metals remain distinct, including Soul Silver, Abyssal Iron, Skyiron and Ember Iron.

## 10.9 Ordinary Salt

Ordinary Salt uses one shared identity where it is materially the same salt. Grave Salt, Blessing Salt, Black Brine Salt and Ember Salt remain distinct specialist salts. `Salt` is a chemical/material superclass, not a merge command.

---

# 11. Specialist Matter Off-Realm

A specialist material does not collapse into an ordinary material when exported.

Examples:

- Soul Silver remains Soul Silver;
- Stormglass remains Stormglass;
- Contract Iron remains Contract Iron;
- Living Heartwood remains Living Heartwood;
- Pressure Pearl remains Pressure Pearl.

Specialist behaviour may remain active, weaken, become dormant or require environmental support. FCC-12C owns the detailed support/stabilisation rules, but identity persists.

---

# 12. Provenance

Provenance is first-class historical metadata describing where matter came from and what meaningful history it carries.

Possible provenance dimensions include:

- origin realm;
- region/biome;
- deposit/site;
- plant or creature source;
- workshop/settlement;
- civilisation/culture;
- archaeological era;
- historical incident;
- contract/sacred source;
- transformation chain;
- custody chain.

Provenance can affect trade, legality, contracts, research, authentication, cultural value and quest logic without changing material identity.

---

# 13. Bounded Provenance and Lots

Leyforge does not require infinite chain-of-custody simulation for every grain of material.

Ordinary bulk matter may retain only material-significant provenance. Important artifacts, regulated materials, sacred/contract goods, archaeological material or disputed goods may retain deeper history.

The rule is:

> **Preserve the provenance required by gameplay truth; aggregate or omit irrelevant detail.**

Material identity alone does not force separate stacks. Lots remain separate only where merging would erase information that must survive, such as contamination, certification, stolen status, contractual provenance or materially significant quality differences.

---

# 14. Claimed and Verified Provenance

The system may distinguish claimed, verified, unknown, forged and disputed provenance. Counterfeiting changes records/claims rather than actual physical history.

Research, law, appraisal and authentication can therefore interact with provenance without changing the underlying material.

---

# 15. Provenance Through Transformation

When an input genuinely transforms into a new material, the output receives the new material identity while preserving relevant source history.

Examples:

- Ember Iron → Contract Iron;
- Skyiron → Celestial Metal;
- Bone Stone → Emberbone;
- Steel + Ascendant components → Saint Steel.

Transformation changes what the material is; it does not erase where it came from.

---

# 16. Universal State Rule

A state is a modifier to existing matter whose underlying material identity remains continuous.

Reversible or routinely removable changes default to state/condition rather than new material identity.

Examples include:

- wet/dry;
- hot/cold;
- charged/depleted;
- damaged/worn;
- polished/rough;
- ordinary weathering;
- pressure-conditioned;
- gravity-conditioned;
- coherence-conditioned;
- seasonal attunement;
- ordinary contamination.

Persistence alone does not create another material.

---

# 17. Facies and Environmental Conditioning

Geological and environmental facies remain expressions of one parent material where continuity is preserved.

Locked examples include:

- Inverted Stone → Pressure Stone facies/state;
- Floating Stone → Pressure Stone facies/state;
- Underside Stone → Cloudstone facies/state;
- Gravity Ore → gravity-conditioned Abyssal Iron deposit/state;
- Orientation/Compressed/Coreward Crystal → Seismic Crystal forms/states;
- Blackwater Pearl → Pressure Pearl provenance/state;
- Seasonal Crystal → season-charged Bloomstone;
- Moon/Dream Sap → Season Sap source/state.

This explicitly prevents adjective-driven material proliferation.

---

# 18. Charge, Attunement and Context

Charge is an energy/state dimension rather than material quality or identity by default.

Attunement similarly remains state where matter stays continuous. Seasonal, ritual, law, dream, pressure, gravity or magical alignment may affect behaviour without creating a new substance.

A persistent deliberately authored transformation can become a new material where the transformation threshold is met, as with Contract Iron or Celestial Metal.

---

# 19. Rot, Blight and Corruption

Rot and natural blight default to state/condition. Rotwood remains wood carrying rot/blight condition unless irreversible transformation creates genuinely distinct matter.

Void corruption is also **state-first**. It must not automatically create generic `Corrupted Stone`, `Corrupted Wood` or `Corrupted Shard` identities.

A new corrupted material is admitted only where transformation is sufficiently persistent/irreversible and materially changes composition, structure or intrinsic behaviour, with explicit transformation provenance.

Nightmare, darkness, rot, undead themes and ordinary blight do not automatically imply Void corruption.

---

# 20. Surface Finish, Damage and Weathering

Polished, roughened, scratched, stained, tarnished, patinated, weathered, cracked, chipped, fatigued and similar conditions remain state/finish unless matter is physically converted into another product/form or the transformation threshold is crossed.

A cut stone becoming rubble may change form while remaining the same underlying stone material.

---

# 21. Transformation Threshold

A new material identity is created only when materially significant change occurs to one or more of:

1. composition;
2. persistent structure;
3. intrinsic physical behaviour;
4. intrinsic magical behaviour;
5. intrinsic biological organisation;
6. stable energetic/material architecture.

Supporting indicators include difficult reversal, substantially changed capability profile, new processing requirements and reuse as a distinct feedstock.

**Provenance alone is never sufficient.**

---

# 22. Permanent Composition and Structural Change

Chemically or compositionally new reusable substances normally become new material identities. Stable structural or microstructural transformation may also justify a new identity when behaviour substantially changes.

This includes appropriate cases of:

- alloying;
- vitrification;
- ceramic transformation;
- persistent magical restructuring;
- contract binding;
- heat-transformed geological material.

Not every heat treatment, polishing operation or environmental exposure reaches this threshold.

---

# 23. State Persistence Through Processing and Export

States survive processing only when physically/magically meaningful. A surface contaminant may be polished away; charge may divide across cut material; refining may remove impurities; destructive processing may kill living behaviour.

States that physically/magically persist may survive realm export. Realm-dependent functions may become dormant or unsupported rather than being erased.

FCC-12B owns detailed transformation/processing inheritance. FCC-12C owns detailed off-realm support/stabilisation.

---

# 24. Quality Is Multidimensional

Leyforge rejects one universal material-quality number as the authoritative model.

Separate dimensions can include:

- ore grade/concentration;
- purity;
- structural quality;
- workmanship quality;
- crystal stability;
- affinity/resonance;
- contamination;
- moisture;
- age/weathering;
- biological maturity;
- alloy composition;
- heat treatment;
- charge;
- provenance.

Different systems may derive a summary score for convenience, but the underlying dimensions remain distinct.

---

# 25. Ore Grade and Purity

Ore grade records the concentration of valuable material within source rock/feedstock. It does not create separate elemental identities such as `Poor Iron` and `Rich Iron`.

Purity records unwanted inclusions relative to intended material/formulation. Purity can affect processing, strength, conductivity, magical behaviour and value without becoming a different material by default.

---

# 26. Structural and Workmanship Quality

Structural quality tracks defects, cracking, fibre integrity, internal flaws and usable mechanical condition of material stock.

Workmanship quality belongs primarily to the crafted object/form. A poorly forged Iron sword is not made of a new material called `Poor Iron`.

---

# 27. Crystal Stability, Affinity and Contamination

Crystal stability is separate from purity. Affinity/resonance is separate from stability, purity and charge. Contamination is separate from all three.

A crystal can therefore be:

- highly pure;
- structurally unstable;
- strongly aligned to one magical use;
- uncharged;
- uncontaminated.

This richer model replaces a generic rarity-tier approach.

---

# 28. Moisture, Age and Biological Maturity

Moisture, weathering, age and biological maturity can significantly influence timber, fibre, leather, soils and other biological/natural materials without creating duplicate base material identities.

They become new matter only if the universal transformation threshold is crossed.

---

# 29. Alloy Composition and Heat Treatment

Alloys carry explicit composition/formulation. Controlled composition ranges may remain one alloy identity with formulation/grade metadata. Materially significant formulations may become named variants or distinct alloys.

Heat treatment usually remains processing history/state where the same alloy identity persists, even though hardness, toughness and microstructure can change.

FCC-12B owns detailed metallurgical transformation architecture.

---

# 30. Charge, Rarity and Quality Are Different

Charge is not quality.

Rarity is not quality.

Realm origin is not quality.

A rare material can be poor quality, and a common material can be exceptionally pure or well processed. No rarity colour, realm label or generic tier is allowed to replace material capabilities.

---

# 31. UI and Information Exposure

Interfaces should expose material dimensions relevant to the task rather than one overloaded universal score.

Examples:

- miners care about grade, hardness and extraction requirements;
- mages care about purity, stability, affinity and charge;
- builders care about strength and structural quality;
- traders care about provenance, legality, certification and market quality;
- researchers may care about origin, composition and transformation history.

The underlying ontology remains richer than any one interface.

---

# 32. No Realm Material Tier Ladder

FCC-12A creates no universal hierarchy such as Overworld Tier → Verdant Tier → Ascendant Tier.

Realm origin does not imply superiority. Materials have individual capability profiles and may be optimal for different purposes.

A compatible material from any realm can satisfy a requirement when its actual capabilities meet that requirement, subject to exact-material exceptions defined later.

---

# 33. No Realm-Prefixed Duplicate Rule

Do not create a new exact material by prefixing an ordinary material with a realm name unless the matter itself is genuinely distinct.

Use provenance for ordinary shared matter.

This rule protects registry coherence, trading, automation, world simulation and cross-realm progression.

---

# 34. No Adjective-Equals-Material Rule

Descriptive terms such as charged, sacred, dream, blackwater, seasonal, corrupted, wet, pressure-conditioned or rot-affected do not automatically become material identities.

The ontology always asks:

> **Did the underlying matter actually become materially different?**

If not, represent the difference as state, facies, provenance, quality, role or alias.

## U01 — Universal Material Ontology
- **Q1 — Canonical material identity:** A canonical material is a persistent underlying substance or materially distinct physical, magical or biological medium whose materially significant composition, persistent structure and intrinsic behaviour remain coherent across forms and ordinary states. A new material identity is justified when the matter itself becomes genuinely different, not merely because its name, shape, location, owner, quality or ordinary condition changes.
- **Q2 — Material identity vs physical form:** Independent axes. Granite remains Granite as rubble, cut block, brick or stair. Copper remains Copper as ingot, plate, rod or wire.
- **Q3 — Material identity vs provenance:** Independent axes. Origin/history may remain mechanically meaningful without creating a duplicate substance.
- **Q4 — Material identity vs location/ownership:** Location or ownership never redefines material identity by itself.
- **Q5 — Superclass vs exact identity:** Separate layers. `metal`, `stone`, `glass`, `wood`, etc. are taxonomic classes; Iron, Soul Silver, Stormglass and Soulwood remain exact identities beneath them.
- **Q6 — Multiple material classes:** Allowed. A material may simultaneously be conductive, forgeable, structural, magical, heat-resistant, optical, biological, brittle, etc.
- **Q7 — Role of composition:** Composition is a major criterion but not the only criterion. Persistent microstructure, intrinsic magical structure and biological/material behaviour may also justify distinct identity.
- **Q8 — Persistent magical structure:** Persistent intrinsic magical structure may justify distinct material identity. Temporary charge/environmental influence/reversible attunement remains state unless the transformation threshold is crossed.
- **Q9 — Biological materials:** Wood, fibre, resin, bone, shell, hide, chitin, pearls and similar matter use the same universal material framework. Species/ecology remains owned by biological-content systems.
- **Q10 — Energetic resources:** Use the broader material/resource framework while distinguishing physical energetic materials, energy charge, fields, captured phenomena and manufactured storage components.
- **Q11 — Fluids and gases:** Use the same core material-identity concept. Phase, containment and world representation are separate from underlying substance identity.
- **Q12 — Components:** Components are designed object/product identities made from materials, not material families merely because they are craft inputs.
- **Q13 — Physical form:** First-class independent axis.
- **Q14 — State/condition:** First-class independent axis. Wet, hot, charged, damaged, corrupted, pressure-conditioned, polished, etc. do not automatically create new materials.
- **Q15 — Provenance:** First-class independent axis. Origin realm, deposit/source, biological source, workshop/process and relevant historical chain may be retained.
- **Q16 — Quality, purity and grade:** Separate from identity and from each other where they mean different things. Do not collapse ore concentration, chemical purity, structural quality, crystal stability, craftsmanship, affinity and contamination into one rarity number.
- **Q17 — Taxonomy/tags:** Hierarchical and composable. Broad material classes coexist with property/capability tags.
- **Q18 — Cultural names:** Cultural terms are aliases/display vocabulary on one canonical identity unless the culture actually creates a transformed material.
- **Q19 — Regional/local names:** Local terminology may display contextually while the underlying canonical material remains one identity.
- **Q20 — Universal material-definition principle

> **A canonical material is a persistent underlying substance or materially distinct physical, magical or biological medium. One material identity may possess many physical forms, states, conditions, qualities, provenances, locations, owners and aliases. Those dimensions do not create new material identities unless composition, persistent structure or intrinsic behaviour changes enough to constitute genuinely different matter.**

**U01 Result:** 20/20 resolved autonomously; no creative escalation required.

## U02 — Shared Matter & Cross-Realm Identity
- **Q21 — Shared-identity standard:** Matter uses one shared canonical identity wherever materially significant composition, persistent structure and intrinsic behaviour are genuinely the same. Realm boundary alone never creates a duplicate.
- **Q22 — Natural compositional tolerance:** Exact atomic sameness is not required. Natural compositional ranges may remain one material where differences do not cause materially significant behaviour. Significant differences become composition profile, grade, variant or distinct material according to the transformation threshold.
- **Q23 — Magical contamination:** Reversible/external magical influence is state or contamination metadata. Persistent intrinsic transformation may create distinct identity.
- **Q24 — Environmental conditioning:** Pressure, gravity, heat exposure, moisture and ordinary weathering remain state/facies while underlying matter remains continuous. Permanent transformation may create a new identity.
- **Q25 — Provenance on shared matter:** Shared material identity and provenance coexist. Example: Basalt with Ashen origin remains Basalt.
- **Q26 — Realm-prefixed duplicates:** Rejected by default for physically identical matter. Do not create separate Overworld Basalt, Ashen Basalt, Verdant Iron or Deep Copper merely because of origin/location.
- **Q27 — Imported materials:** Imported matter retains its canonical identity unless actual transformation occurs.
- **Q28 — Ordinary Water:** Ordinary physical Water is one shared universal material identity wherever the source means genuinely ordinary water. Temperature, salinity, contamination, magical charge and provenance are state/composition/context unless transformation occurs. **Non-merge:** Dreamwater remains distinct.
- **Q29 — Lava / Magma:** Use a universal magma/lava material-family architecture based on molten geological composition. Where molten-rock composition is materially the same, identity can be shared. Basaltic/silica-rich/gas-rich/magically altered compositions remain composition profiles, states or distinct transformed matter as appropriate. `Lava` is primarily a molten-rock form/state/class, not automatically one exact universal substance and not automatically Ashen.
- **Q30 — Sand:** `Sand` is primarily a particle-size/material-form class, not one universal exact substance. Exact identity follows underlying composition where relevant. **Non-merge:** Dream Sand remains distinct.
- **Q31 — Gravel:** Same principle as Sand. `Gravel` is a coarse aggregate/particle-size class. Exact identity follows underlying rock/mineral composition where gameplay requires it. A generic mixed gravel product may exist where useful.
- **Q32 — Clay:** `Clay` is a universal mineral/feedstock class with composition-bearing identities rather than one realm-specific material per world. Physically equivalent clays may share identities. White Kaolin remains a specialist clay/mineral source; River Clay remains a source/deposit relationship.
- **Q33 — Ordinary manufactured glass:** Ordinary silica-based manufactured glass uses a shared universal identity where formulation is materially equivalent. Colourants, formulation, tempering, magical structure and specialist transformations may be state, formulation or distinct material depending on significance. **Distinct specialist glasses remain distinct:** Memory Glass, Lucid Glass, Stormglass, Ventglass, Hellglass and Thunder Glass.
- **Q34 — Common metals:** Common metals use one universal physical identity across realms: Iron, Copper, Tin, Lead, Nickel, Cobalt, Silver, Gold, etc. Distinct realm metals remain distinct: Soul Silver, Skyiron, Abyssal Iron, Ember Iron.
- **Q35 — Common alloys:** The same materially significant alloy formulation remains the same material across realms. Different formulations/heat treatment become grade, state, formulation variant or distinct alloy according to metallurgy rules.
- **Q36 — Ordinary Salt:** Ordinary Salt uses one shared identity where materially the same ordinary salt is meant. Grave Salt, Blessing Salt, Black Brine Salt and Ember Salt remain distinct.
- **Q37 — Imported ordinary timbers:** Imported timber retains exact identity. Oak Heartwood remains Oak Heartwood outside the Overworld.
- **Q38 — Imported ordinary fibres:** Same rule. Sunflax Fibre, Mirehemp Fibre and other physical fibres retain identity after export.
- **Q39 — Imported ordinary biological products:** Leather, bone, shell, resin, oil and similar goods remain the same physical substances unless transformed. Source-species/provenance may remain metadata.
- **Q40 — Shared matter entering specialist realms:** It remains the same material unless transformed. Realm environment may add condition, charge, contamination, attunement, damage, suppression or enhancement without automatic identity change.
- **Q41 — Specialist matter entering ordinary realms:** Specialist identity persists. Soul Silver stays Soul Silver; Stormglass stays Stormglass; Contract Iron stays Contract Iron; Living Heartwood stays Living Heartwood. Later U13 decides whether specialist behaviour weakens, goes dormant or needs support off-realm.
- **Q42 — Display naming with provenance:** Use canonical material name by default. Optional contextual provenance qualifiers may appear when useful, without creating new identities.
- **Q43 — Stacking across provenance:** Material identity should not force separate stacks solely because provenance differs. Provenance-sensitive gameplay may require metadata-aware lot handling where mixing would destroy meaningful information. Exact inventory behaviour remains downstream.
- **Q44 — Shared-matter doctrine

> **Physically and intrinsically equivalent matter uses one canonical material identity regardless of realm, region, current owner or current location. Provenance and ordinary environmental conditioning remain separate metadata/state. Broad physical terms such as sand, gravel, lava and clay may describe form/class/composition families rather than one monolithic substance. Specialist realm materials remain distinct when persistent composition, structure or intrinsic behaviour is materially different.**

## U04 — State, Facies, Condition & Transformation
- **Q61 — What is a material state?:** A state is a persistent or transient modifier to an existing material whose underlying identity remains continuous.
- **Q62 — How are reversible changes treated?:** Reversible or routinely removable changes default to state/condition, not new material identity.
- **Q63 — How are geological facies treated?:** Facies are state/profile expressions of one geological material when composition and intrinsic behaviour remain within the parent material envelope.
- **Q64 — Temperature state:** Hot, cold, molten/frozen where chemically continuous, thermally stressed and similar conditions are state/phase unless permanent transformation occurs.
- **Q65 — Moisture state:** Wet, dry, soaked, waterlogged and ordinary drying states remain condition metadata.
- **Q66 — Damage and wear:** Cracked, chipped, worn, fatigued and damaged conditions remain state unless the object/material physically becomes a different product such as rubble.
- **Q67 — Charge state:** Electrical, mana or other stored charge remains state/energy data unless charge causes permanent structural transformation.
- **Q68 — Attunement state:** Reversible/bounded magical attunement is state. Persistent intrinsic restructuring can cross into transformed material.
- **Q69 — Pressure conditioning:** Pressure-conditioned variants remain state/facies when material continuity is preserved; this explains Pressure Stone facies and related Deep behaviour.
- **Q70 — Gravity/orientation conditioning:** Gravity-conditioned/oriented expressions remain state/form where locked, including Inverted/Floating Pressure Stone and Seismic Crystal orientation forms.
- **Q71 — Coherence conditioning:** Somnolent coherence/lucidity/nightmare conditioning defaults to state unless the source canon identifies a genuinely different material.
- **Q72 — Sacred-law conditioning:** Law/domain exposure is state/context by default. A crafted law-attuned product may be a processed product where its intrinsic behaviour persists.
- **Q73 — Contract binding:** Ordinary contractual status is external state/legal context. Deliberate material binding that permanently creates Contract Iron is a transformed-material lineage.
- **Q74 — Seasonal attunement:** Seasonal charge/attunement remains state unless a separately authored irreversible transformation is proven; Seasonal Crystal therefore remains charged Bloomstone.
- **Q75 — Blight and natural decay:** Blight/rot default to biological/material condition; Rotwood remains state unless irreversible self-propagating transformation creates genuinely different matter.
- **Q76 — Void corruption:** Void corruption is state-first. It never automatically creates generic corrupted material identities.
- **Q77 — When can corruption become new matter?:** Only when transformation is irreversible or materially persistent enough that composition/structure/intrinsic behaviour becomes distinct, with explicit provenance to the transformation.
- **Q78 — Polish, finish and weathering:** Polished, roughened, oxidised surface, patina and ordinary weathering are finish/condition unless chemistry changes enough to affect material identity.
- **Q79 — Permanent microstructure change:** Permanent structural/microstructural change can justify a transformed material when it substantially changes capability/behaviour and is not merely a grade or heat-treatment profile.
- **Q80 — Permanent composition change:** A chemically/compositionally new reusable substance is normally a new material identity.
- **Q81 — Irreversible magical transformation:** A stable magical restructuring that becomes intrinsic and changes behaviour can create a new material identity even if mundane chemistry remains related.
- **Q82 — Do states survive form changes?:** Relevant states carry through processing only when physically meaningful. Cutting a charged crystal may distribute/preserve charge according to the process; sanding away a surface coating may remove that state.
- **Q83 — Do states survive realm export?:** Yes if the physical/magical condition itself persists. Realm-dependent effects may become dormant or require support rather than being erased.
- **Q84 — Universal transformation test:** Create a new material only when materially significant composition, persistent structure or intrinsic behaviour changes enough to make the output a distinct reusable substance. Otherwise use state, facies, quality, provenance or form.

## U05 — Quality, Grade, Purity & Composition
- **Q85 — Should quality dimensions be separate?:** Yes. Grade, purity, structural quality, workmanship, stability, affinity, contamination and condition are independent dimensions where they represent different behaviour.
- **Q86 — Ore grade/concentration:** Ore grade records concentration/richness of valuable material in source rock/feedstock; it is not a new metal.
- **Q87 — Purity:** Purity records unwanted inclusions/contaminants relative to the intended material or formulation.
- **Q88 — Structural quality:** Structural quality records defects, grain/fibre condition, cracking and usable mechanical integrity.
- **Q89 — Workmanship quality:** Workmanship belongs primarily to the crafted object/form, not the base material identity. A poorly forged Iron sword does not create Poor Iron as an element.
- **Q90 — Crystal stability:** Stability is a dedicated property/state dimension for crystals and magical matter where instability changes handling/behaviour.
- **Q91 — Affinity/resonance:** Affinity/resonance is separate from purity and charge; a crystal can be pure yet poorly aligned to a given magical use.
- **Q92 — Contamination:** Contamination records foreign matter, toxins, corruption or other impurities and may require treatment without redefining the base material.
- **Q93 — Moisture:** Moisture content is condition data for wood, soils, fibres and other hygroscopic materials.
- **Q94 — Age/weathering:** Age and weathering are provenance/condition dimensions; they create new identity only if transformation criteria are crossed.
- **Q95 — Biological maturity:** Growth/maturity can affect harvested material quality/properties but does not automatically create separate material families.
- **Q96 — Alloy composition:** Alloy composition/formulation is explicit. Small controlled ranges can remain one alloy with composition/grade metadata; materially different formulations become variants or distinct alloys.
- **Q97 — Heat treatment:** Heat treatment is usually material state/processing history where the substance remains the same alloy, with capability changes recorded separately.
- **Q98 — Charge is not quality:** Charge/energy content is its own dimension and must not be conflated with material quality.
- **Q99 — Rarity is not quality:** Rarity/provenance scarcity never functions as a universal quality/tier ladder.
- **Q100 — Stacking implications:** Stacks may merge only where required metadata can be preserved/aggregated correctly. Exact inventory lot logic remains FCC-13/implementation-owned.
- **Q101 — UI representation:** UI should expose only dimensions meaningful to the current task rather than one overloaded rarity colour or universal star rating.
- **Q102 — Universal quality doctrine:** Material variation is multidimensional: composition/grade, purity, structural quality, condition, charge, affinity/attunement, contamination and provenance remain separate unless a specific system intentionally derives a summary metric.
