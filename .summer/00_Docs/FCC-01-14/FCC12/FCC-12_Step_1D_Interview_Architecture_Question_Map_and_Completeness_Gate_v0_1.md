# 1. Purpose

FCC-12 Steps 1A–1C established the complete current material corpus, authority order, cross-realm collisions, shared/non-shared identities, state/facies relationships, transformed-material lineages, component/material boundaries, migration issues, candidate universal form families and candidate processing families.

Step 1D turns that work into a finite decision interview.

The interview will decide the universal material ontology and hand a reconciled semantic model to FCC-13.

It will not decide exact recipe quantities, stable IDs, station timings, final art values, portal dependency order or runtime schemas.
