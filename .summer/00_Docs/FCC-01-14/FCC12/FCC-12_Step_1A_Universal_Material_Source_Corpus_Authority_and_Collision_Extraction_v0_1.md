# 1. Purpose

FCC-12 is the first **global material reconciliation document** after the current realm FCCs and the Overworld were individually content-locked.

Its job is not to invent another realm resource catalogue.

Its job is to answer questions such as:

- when two realm documents are describing the **same physical material**;
- when similarly named materials are actually **distinct**;
- when a realm name describes **provenance/state/grade** rather than a separate material;
- when a processed form deserves its own definition;
- when multiple realm processing stations can inherit one universal process;
- when a special realm process is genuinely unique;
- how exported materials retain or lose realm-dependent behaviour;
- what material/provider substitutions are legitimate;
- how biological, mineral, glass, metal, fibre, wood, salt, gas, energetic and magical lineages relate;
- how old Atlas/POC registry entries migrate without restoring superseded canon.

Step 1A does **not** make new cross-realm merge/split decisions except where those decisions are already explicitly locked by the realm FCCs or FCC-01.

It extracts the source corpus and identifies the questions FCC-12 must resolve.

# 3. Universal Rules Already Locked Before FCC-12

The following are **inputs**, not open FCC-12 questions.

## 3.1 Single-Definition Rule

A placed Block that remains the same object when broken must not gain a second duplicate Item identity merely for inventory.

Use the canonical Block definition plus inventory projection/stack representation.

Separate Item definitions remain valid where the object is genuinely different, such as:

- ore concentrate;
- ingot;
- plate;
- rod;
- wire;
- fibre;
- thread;
- powder;
- contained fluid/gas;
- machine component;
- crafted consumable.

---

## 3.2 State Before New Material

Prefer state/profile/variant where the underlying matter remains materially continuous.

Examples may include:

- wet/dry;
- hot/cold;
- charged/depleted;
- corrupted/cleansed;
- pressure-conditioned;
- gravity-conditioned;
- season-attuned;
- law-attuned;
- naturalised;
- polished/weathered.

A new material identity is justified only where composition, transformation, provenance or gameplay behaviour becomes genuinely distinct enough.

---

## 3.3 Capability Before Origin

Tools, materials, machines and processes satisfy **capabilities**.

Realm origin alone cannot make a capable provider invalid.

A material or tool can function off-realm if:

- it physically survives;
- it satisfies the required capability;
- any declared environmental/magical/legal context is present.

There is no universal realm-material tier ladder.

---

## 3.4 Physical Matter Generally Persists Across Realms

Exported physical matter generally retains its ordinary physical properties.

Realm-dependent properties may:

- remain active;
- weaken;
- become dormant;
- require stabilisation;
- require compatible mana/law/pressure/coherence/environment.

Leaving a realm does not normally delete the material.

---

## 3.5 Provenance Is Not Identity

Current location and current owner do not erase origin.

A material can remain:

- Verdant-origin;
- Ancestral-origin;
- Somnolent-origin;
- Ascendant-origin;
- Deep-origin;
- Ashen-origin

after being exported, traded, inherited or reused.

Provenance can matter without forcing duplicate material definitions.

---

## 3.6 Processing Must Not Multiply Without Need

Realm documents explicitly prefer reuse of universal:

- cutting;
- grinding;
- milling;
- crushing;
- sawing;
- drying;
- firing;
- smelting;
- casting;
- forging;
- pressing;
- weaving;
- spinning;
- distilling;
- chemical/alchemical treatment;
- assembly

unless the realm process genuinely requires special environmental or magical behaviour.

# 18. Registry / Stable-ID Boundary

Set-25 governance establishes that:

- one stable machine identity must exist independently of display name, file path, runtime state and current owner;
- definitions are separate from runtime instances/states;
- old POC IDs are migration inputs;
- capability queries should be used where a generic functional requirement can be satisfied by equivalent providers;
- display names are not stable IDs;
- aliases/migrations must preserve save compatibility.

FCC-12 therefore should not casually invent permanent implementation IDs.

It should define the **universal material ontology and relationship graph** that FCC-13 can bind to definitive IDs.

# 24. Step 1A Findings

## Finding A — The Realm FCCs Are Much More Reconciled Than the Old Registry

The current FCC material sets have already retired many old “one adjective = one material” assumptions.

Examples:

- Deep Forge Stone retired;
- Infernal Brick folded into Ash Brick;
- Heat Glass reduced to role descriptor;
- Seasonal Crystal folded into Bloomstone state;
- Moon/Dream Sap folded into Season Sap;
- Blackwater Pearl folded into Pressure Pearl provenance/state;
- Orientation/Compressed/Core Crystal folded into Seismic Crystal forms/states.

FCC-12 should preserve this cleanup rather than re-expand it.

---

## Finding B — Shared Superclasses Are Needed Without Forced Merging

Many materials clearly share broad taxonomy:

- stones;
- metals;
- glasses;
- crystals;
- woods;
- fibres;
- salts;
- pearls;
- resins;
- gases;
- energetic resources.

But sharing a superclass does **not** mean sharing one material identity.

This will be one of the central FCC-12 design principles.

---

## Finding C — Realm Processing Is Often a Mode, Not a Separate Industry

Most realm C-documents already expect ordinary processing to remain reusable.

The genuinely distinctive candidates are narrower:

- spirit-active processing;
- coherence/dream stabilisation;
- high-aether/divine attunement;
- extreme-pressure Deep processing;
- infernal contract-binding;
- living Verdant processing.

FCC-12 should test whether these are:

- process modes;
- station capability requirements;
- environmental conditions;
- or genuinely separate processing families.

The POC/old production registry contains:

- duplicate Block/Item identities;
- retired realm placeholders;
- old realm names;
- generic dimensional resources;
- old visual placeholder values;
- legacy stable IDs.

FCC-12 must give FCC-13 an unambiguous ontology and migration disposition for these rows.
