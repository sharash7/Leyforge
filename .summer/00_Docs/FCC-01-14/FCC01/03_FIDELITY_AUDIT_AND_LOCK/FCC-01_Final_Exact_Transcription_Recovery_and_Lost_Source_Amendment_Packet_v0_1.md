# 12. S19 — Six Preserved Names, Reconstructed Roles

These six accepted names are secure; only their exact historical role wording is lost.

## River Shoalfish
**Recommended role:** small schooling freshwater forage fish.  
**Habitats:** rivers, tributaries, floodplain channels, connected lakes.  
**Hooks:** common fishery, prey foundation, seasonal spawning runs, water-quality/barrier/overfishing response.

## Lakeback Grazer
**Recommended role:** large freshwater littoral herbivore / aquatic-plant grazer.  
**Habitats:** lakes, broad slow rivers, vegetated inland shores.  
**Hooks:** controls aquatic vegetation, supports predators, may be managed/protected, overabundance can strip plant beds.

## Tideclam
**Recommended role:** intertidal/sheltered-coast filter-feeding bivalve colony.  
**Habitats:** mudflats, estuaries, sheltered bays, shallow tidal beds.  
**Hooks:** shellfish harvest/aquaculture, water-quality indicator, filtration, depletion/restoration.

## Reef Grazer
**Recommended role:** reef herbivore / algae-control grazer.  
**Habitats:** warm reefs, reef flats, shallow rocky/coral systems.  
**Hooks:** prevents algal overgrowth, supports predators/fisheries, collapse can alter reef state.

## Silver Shoalfish
**Recommended role:** migratory coastal/shelf schooling fish.  
**Habitats:** coasts, shelf waters, bays, seasonal migration corridors.  
**Hooks:** fishery/trade population, seasonal runs, predator aggregation, stock responds to storms/temperature/spawning/harvest.

## Bluewake Grazer
**Recommended role:** open-water plankton grazer / pelagic schooling prey.  
**Habitats:** productive shelf and open-ocean surface waters.  
**Hooks:** follows plankton blooms, creates readable wake/school signs, supports large predators/migratory fauna, links productivity to fisheries.

# 13. S19 — Four Lost Principal Names: Replacement Amendment

## 22. Marshbell Frog
**Role:** wetland amphibian insectivore / seasonal-water indicator.  
**Habitats:** reed marshes, floodplains, fens, temporary pools.  
**Hooks:** insect control, breeding chorus as water/season clue, wetland prey, pollution/drying sensitivity.

## 23. Reedback Newt
**Role:** small freshwater amphibian / invertebrate predator and water-quality indicator.  
**Habitats:** quiet streams, pond margins, reedbeds, cool wetlands.  
**Hooks:** larval/invertebrate control, shallow-water breeding, contamination/restoration response.

## 24. Mudfin Hopper
**Role:** amphibious estuary/mudflat grazer-scavenger.  
**Habitats:** estuaries, brackish creeks, tidal mudflats and mangrove-like coastal wetlands.  
**Hooks:** biofilm/algae grazing, detritus consumption, tide-linked movement, prey for shore birds/crabs/fish.

## 25. Springback Salamander
**Role:** spring/cave-mouth amphibian and groundwater-health indicator.  
**Habitats:** springs, seepfields, karst pools, shaded stream heads, cave mouths.  
**Hooks:** surface-groundwater ecology link, aquifer pollution/drying response, small-invertebrate predation, hidden-water clue.
