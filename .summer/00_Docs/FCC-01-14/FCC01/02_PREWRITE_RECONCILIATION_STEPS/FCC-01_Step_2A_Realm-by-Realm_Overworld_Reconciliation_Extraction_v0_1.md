# 1. Purpose

This document is the hard-reference extraction pass that must exist **before the FCC-01 Overworld questionnaire begins**.

Its job is to collect, realm by realm:

1. explicit Overworld-facing obligations;
2. portal and first-access dependencies;
3. cross-realm interfaces that FCC-01 must be aware of;
4. source-declared global deferrals;
5. collision/watch items that must be carried into Step 2B.

This document **does not resolve collisions**, choose final Overworld materials, assign final global IDs, balance recipes, or redesign any locked realm-local canon.

Where a realm document explicitly assigns a decision to FCC-01, FCC-12, FCC-13, FCC-14, universal systems, or later art governance, that ownership is preserved here.

# 3. Extraction Rules

The following labels are used throughout this register.

- **OVERWORLD BINDING** — the realm documents explicitly leave a concrete Overworld-side choice or implementation point open.
- **OVERWORLD INTERFACE** — the realm has a canonical relationship the Overworld must be capable of representing.
- **PORTAL / PROGRESSION** — first-access, gate construction, destination, prerequisite, or realm-order information.
- **GLOBAL DEFERRAL** — explicitly owned by FCC-12/FCC-13/FCC-14, universal systems, balance/implementation work, or later art governance.
- **STEP-2B WATCH** — a collision, overlap, naming issue, or shared-system dependency that must be compared in Step 2B rather than resolved here.

No new canon is introduced by these labels.
