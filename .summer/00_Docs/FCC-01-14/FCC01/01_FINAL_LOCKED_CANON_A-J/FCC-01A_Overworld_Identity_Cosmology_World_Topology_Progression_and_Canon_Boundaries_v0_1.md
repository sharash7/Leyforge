# 1. Purpose

FCC-01A defines the authoritative high-level identity, cosmological role, world topology, progression philosophy, realm-access boundaries, provenance model, canon ownership rules, deferred-scope boundaries and implementation-facing constraints for the **Overworld**.

The Overworld is the primary civilisation heart and central sandbox world of Leyforge. It is the principal long-term space for survival, exploration, settlement development, agriculture, industry, automation, magic, trade, politics, archaeology, large-scale construction, world events and cross-realm contact.

This document establishes the rules that every later FCC-01 document must obey.

FCC-01A does **not** attempt to contain the complete Overworld asset roster by itself. The remaining FCC-01 documents own the detailed canon for:

- continents, climate, hydrology, oceans, sky, underground and biome families;
- geology, materials, resources and provenance;
- flora, fungi, crops and agriculture;
- creatures, ecology and creature-derived materials;
- peoples, cultures, faiths, governments, factions, settlements and economy;
- structures, routes, infrastructure, wonders and portal archaeology;
- dungeons, ruins, lairs, bosses, titans and major encounters;
- magic ecology, generated history, events, realm interfaces and corruption boundaries;
- final package registry, completeness certification and downstream handoff.

The governing rule is:

> **FCC-01A defines what the Overworld fundamentally is, how it fits into Leyforge, what progression is allowed to mean, and which boundaries later Overworld content must never violate.**

# 2. Canon Statement

The **Overworld** is Leyforge’s primary persistent civilisation sandbox: an enormous, deterministic, seed-generated world capable of supporting survival, exploration, ecology, agriculture, settlement growth, industry, automation, magic, politics, trade, archaeology, dungeons, world-scale threats and cross-realm civilisation over extremely long play periods.

Its central fantasy is:

> **A vast living fantasy world in which the player can begin as one person trying to survive and, without changing games, grow into a builder, explorer, engineer, mage, trader, ruler, restorer, conqueror, scholar, realm traveller or civilisation-shaping force while the world continues to develop around them.**

The Overworld is central to gameplay without being cosmologically supreme.

It is not the metaphysical source of all matter, magic, life, law, gods or realms. The six current non-Overworld realms are independent persistent worlds with their own ecologies, civilisations, resources, hazards, politics, histories and internal identities.

Likewise, the Overworld is not a neutral storage container into which all foreign content is silently reclassified.

A Verdant organism remains Verdant-origin after being naturalised in an Overworld forest. An Ashen material remains Ashen-origin after being forged into an Overworld machine. An Ancestral visitor can become an Overworld citizen without ceasing to have Ancestral provenance. Location, culture, citizenship, ownership and provenance are separate relationships.

The Overworld must therefore be broad enough to host cross-realm civilisation without losing its own identity.

# 4. Explicit Non-Identity: What the Overworld Is Not

The Overworld is **not**:

- the metaphysical centre of creation;
- the source from which every other realm is derived;
- the owner of all universal gameplay systems merely because most play occurs there;
- one fixed handcrafted starter valley;
- one predetermined continent layout;
- a collection of disconnected biome skins;
- an empty default world whose interesting content exists only in other realms;
- the literal upper geological layer of Impossible Deep;
- a lower layer of Ascendant Reach simply because it has a sky;
- physically continuous with Ashen Lower Realms through magma or depth;
- automatically mirrored into the Ancestral Veil;
- physically connected to Somnolent Expanse through ordinary sleep;
- physically connected to Verdant Covenant through roots or Greatroot networks;
- automatically Void-corrupted whenever something strange, dark, unstable or magical occurs;
- a fixed civilisation scenario with one mandatory starter culture, village, kingdom, quest or raid;
- a linear sequence of technology tiers culminating in one correct build;
- a sequence of mandatory realm visits in a fixed order;
- a game that ends when one boss or story arc is completed;
- a world in which every player must become a mage, ruler, industrialist or hero;
- a content dumping ground for retired POC IDs, old realm concepts or unreviewed Atlas placeholders.

These exclusions are canon.

# 5. Overworld Fantasy Pillars

## 5.1 Survival That Can Scale Into Civilisation

The same world must support play ranging from immediate shelter, food and tools through villages, regional logistics, automated industry, magical infrastructure, politics, large cities, megaprojects and realm-spanning civilisation.

Late-game play scales the same fundamental loops upward rather than replacing them with a separate game mode.

## 5.2 A Living World Rather Than a Player Stage

The Overworld contains persistent ecological, economic, political, historical and magical systems that can continue changing without requiring the player to stand nearby.

Settlements may grow, decline, rebuild or disappear. Routes may open or collapse. Populations may migrate. Factions may split or reconcile. Boss territories may move. Ecosystems may recover or degrade. Player actions become part of world history.

The world is not frozen until the player enters a quest trigger.

## 5.3 Geography Has Consequence

Continents, mountain chains, river basins, coastlines, climates, underground provinces, ocean basins, skylands and resource distributions exist as connected world systems.

Geography influences:

- travel;
- settlement placement;
- agriculture;
- trade;
- defence;
- culture;
- ecology;
- mining;
- industry;
- portal discovery;
- political boundaries;
- historical development.

## 5.4 Civilisation Is Plural

No ancestry, culture, faith, government or faction owns the Overworld.

The world can contain:

- mixed settlements;
- mono-ancestry settlements;
- multicultural cities;
- diasporas;
- frontier societies;
- monarchies;
- republics;
- councils;
- guild states;
- clan confederacies;
- religious polities;
- cooperative systems;
- construct-led governments;
- mobile cultures;
- underground societies;
- maritime societies;
- realm-contact communities.

Culture, ancestry, government, religion, faction and citizenship remain independent dimensions of identity.

## 5.5 Magic Is Native, Practical and Geographical

The Overworld possesses genuine native magic.

Mana, leylines, magical ecology, rituals, wards, runes, enchanted infrastructure and magical anomalies do not require foreign-realm leakage to exist.

Foreign realm influence may occur, but provenance must be explicit.

## 5.6 Building and Engineering Matter Physically

Player construction, roads, bridges, mines, canals, warehouses, farms, fortifications, automation, magic infrastructure and settlements are persistent parts of the world.

The game does not treat construction as cosmetic terrain editing detached from civilisation and simulation.

## 5.7 Multiple Valid Player Identities

Leyforge supports many long-term identities without forcing class selection.

Examples include:

- survivor;
- wandering explorer/helper;
- settlement founder;
- farmer;
- builder;
- trader;
- industrialist;
- automation master;
- mage;
- mage-engineer;
- archaeologist/scholar;
- political leader;
- ruler;
- conqueror;
- restorer/world-shaper;
- dimensional explorer;
- chosen or story-significant hero where generated content supports it;
- peaceful civilisation builder.

These are emergent paths, not mutually exclusive permanent classes.

## 5.8 Endgame Is Scale and Consequence

The endgame is not defined by one final boss followed by credits.

Major bosses, Void-related conflicts, realm authorities, faction wars and world-scale crises may provide climaxes, but the world continues afterward.

Endgame progression is expressed through greater capability, influence, infrastructure, knowledge, political reach, magical mastery, automation, restoration, exploration and world-scale consequence.

# 6. World Topology

## 6.1 Canonical Spatial Hierarchy

The Overworld uses the following high-level spatial/content hierarchy:

> **World → Continent / Macro-region → Region → Biome / Environmental Context → Site / Settlement / Local Feature**

This hierarchy is supplemented by domain-specific nested structures where required, including:

- watershed and river-basin structure;
- ocean basin and marine-region structure;
- sky provinces and altitude contexts;
- cave systems and cave provinces;
- route networks;
- settlement districts;
- territories and political claims;
- historical and magical overlays.

The hierarchy is descriptive and content-facing. It does not require every system to use one identical technical data structure.

## 6.2 Minecraft-Scale Default

The primary/default design target is **Minecraft-scale world size and exploration extent** with similarly enormous streamed/procedural generation.

This is a design baseline, not a requirement that every world use one fixed numeric size.

Alternative world-size profiles may exist later, but reduced profiles must preserve the same canonical relationships rather than becoming a different game.

## 6.3 One Continuous Overworld

The ordinary surface, coastlines, oceans, islands, sky volume, underground and finite deep Overworld belong to one persistent world identity.

They are not separate dimensions merely because they use different streaming, biome or simulation profiles.

## 6.4 Surface

The surface supports continental and regional geography, settlements, agriculture, forests, plains, deserts, wetlands, mountains, rivers, lakes, coastlines, infrastructure and the majority of ordinary civilisation.

Detailed landform and biome rules are owned by FCC-01B.

## 6.5 Oceans and Inland Waters

Oceans are full regions with depth, currents, ecology, structures, settlements, routes, hazards and history.

They are not empty map boundaries.

Freshwater and marine content remain part of the same Overworld world topology.

## 6.6 Sky

The Overworld has a real sky volume and may contain high-altitude routes, aerial ecology, floating terrain and rare skyland provinces.

The governing boundary is:

> **The Overworld has a sky. Ascendant Reach is a sky-world.**

Flying upward does not cross the realm boundary.

## 6.7 Underground

The underground is a genuine world layer supporting:

- cave systems;
- cave provinces;
- underground settlements;
- deep trade routes;
- mining;
- subterranean water;
- geothermal zones;
- archaeology;
- artificial strata;
- ecology;
- dungeons;
- megadungeons.

Its canonical hierarchy is:

> **Local Feature → Cave System → Cave Province → Deep Route → Subterranean Region**

## 6.8 Finite Deep Overworld

The Overworld has a finite physical depth.

Its deepest regions may contain extreme geology, subterranean seas, ancient machine strata, seismic anomalies, deep resonance and clues associated with Impossible Deep.

However:

> **Deep Overworld remains Overworld.**

No natural tunnel, mine, cave, shaft, root network or excavated route physically becomes Impossible Deep.

# 7. Seed, Determinism and World Profiles

## 7.1 Deterministic Seed Foundation

Every normal production world is assembled deterministically from:

- world seed;
- authored content families;
- world-profile settings;
- compatibility constraints;
- procedural history;
- progression guarantees;
- persistent runtime deltas.

“Random” means seed-selected and rule-constrained, not arbitrary.

## 7.2 Seed Plus Persistent Delta

The seed establishes initial world truth.

Player actions and simulation create persistent deltas such as:

- mined terrain;
- built structures;
- destroyed structures;
- settlement growth;
- faction change;
- ecological change;
- discovered routes;
- dungeon resolution;
- boss outcomes;
- portal construction;
- historical records;
- event aftermath.

Runtime state is not regenerated from seed in a way that erases those consequences.

## 7.3 No Fixed Production Starter Constellation

Normal production worlds do not depend on the retired POC constellation.

There is no required:

- Forest Hamlet;
- fixed valley;
- fixed starter meadow;
- fixed cave;
- fixed mana pocket;
- fixed goblin camp;
- fixed rune clearing;
- guaranteed raid;
- mandatory named villager;
- guaranteed starter portal arrangement.

Tutorial and regression content may use controlled fixtures outside normal production world canon.

## 7.4 World-Profile Variation

World profiles may vary:

- overall size;
- climate balance;
- danger;
- event frequency;
- settlement density;
- peaceful/harsh-world behaviour;
- consequence severity;
- simulation intensity;
- other globally exposed generation settings.

Variation must not silently invalidate mandatory progression or violate realm/portal contracts.

## 7.5 Content Availability Versus Guaranteed Spawn

A canonical content foundation is not required to appear in every seed unless its owner explicitly requires universal availability.

The generator may satisfy progression through:

- alternate provider families;
- equivalent capabilities;
- alternate sites;
- different cultures;
- different routes;
- substitute materials where globally valid;
- different discovery pathways.

This is fundamental to sandbox variation.

# 8. Provenance and Content-Origin Classification

The Overworld uses explicit provenance/origin categories to prevent foreign content from losing identity or native content from being incorrectly attributed to another realm.

The principal classification families are:

1. **Native**
2. **Universal / Common**
3. **Imported**
4. **Diaspora**
5. **Historical-contact**
6. **Anomaly**
7. **Realm-derived**

These categories can coexist with more specific material, biological, cultural, legal and ownership relationships.

## 8.1 Native

Content whose ordinary origin belongs to the Overworld.

## 8.2 Universal / Common

Content shared across worlds because the physical identity is genuinely the same or governed by a universal system.

Examples may include ordinary water, ordinary common metals, universal movement systems or common physical concepts where later global reconciliation confirms shared identity.

## 8.3 Imported

Matter, organisms or artefacts physically transferred from another realm.

## 8.4 Diaspora

People, communities and cultures with a historical origin in another realm but a persistent Overworld population history.

## 8.5 Historical-contact

Content whose Overworld presence derives from prior trade, diplomacy, war, migration, old portal use, expeditions or other legitimate historical realm contact.

## 8.6 Anomaly

Unusual Overworld content whose origin is unresolved or genuinely anomalous.

Anomaly does not mean “foreign realm” automatically.

## 8.7 Realm-derived

Content directly caused by, transformed through or produced from a specific realm relationship where provenance is established.

They may survive as:

- confirmed Void contamination;

# 12. One Canonical Portal Per Realm

All major current non-Overworld realms use one specific canonical portal family for normal physical entry.

The rule is absolute at the content-canon level:

> **Portal clues, dreams, roots, graves, sky travel, deep mining, lava, rituals, contracts, ancient roads and realm resonance may support discovery or context; they are not alternate ordinary interdimensional access systems.**

## 12.1 Universal Realm Access Ownership

The universal Realm Access system owns:

- portal runtime;
- route binding;
- transition orchestration;
- destination resolution;
- multiplayer authority;
- save/load behaviour;
- portal linking;
- safe-return state;
- failure/recovery handling;
- final mandatory dependency graph.

FCC-01 owns the Overworld-facing content required to discover, research, construct, supply, contextualise and politically interact with the six portal families.

## 12.2 Exact Recipes Are Downstream

FCC-01 does not finalise exact portal recipes or quantities.

Those belong to FCC-13 after FCC-12 material reconciliation and Realm Access progression validation.

## 12.3 Covenant Portal as Guaranteed Non-Circular Root

The first Covenant Portal is explicitly designed to be constructible from pre-realm Overworld/universal capabilities.

This provides at least one proven non-circular realm-access anchor for the global dependency graph.

It does **not** force Verdant Covenant to be the first realm discovered or visited in every seed.

## 12.4 Discovery Order Versus Dependency Order

Discovery order may vary substantially between seeds.

The player may know of a realm long before they can reach it.

The final mandatory dependency graph is a global progression responsibility and must remain non-circular and recoverable.

# 14. Progression Philosophy

## 14.1 Capability-Based Progression

Leyforge progression is fundamentally based on **what the player or civilisation can actually do**, not which named tier or realm an item came from.

A provider is valid when it satisfies the required capability and applicable environmental/system compatibility.

Examples include:

- cutting capability;
- mining strength;
- heat resistance;
- pressure resistance;
- mana conduction;
- filtration;
- structural strength;
- containment;
- seismic sensing;
- cooling;
- navigation;
- magical stabilisation.

## 14.2 No Universal Realm Gear Ladder

The realms do not form a mandatory sequence such as:

> Verdant gear < Ancestral gear < Somnolent gear < Ascendant gear < Deep gear < Ashen gear.

Realms specialise in different problems.

A later-discovered material is not automatically a universal upgrade over all earlier materials.

## 14.3 Cross-Realm Capability Freedom

Overworld tools, armour, machines, magic and engineering can solve compatible realm problems when they genuinely meet the requirement.

Realm-developed capabilities can solve compatible Overworld problems by the same rule.

No normal item is disabled merely because it is being used in the “wrong dimension.”

## 14.4 Environmental Requirements Remain Real

Capability freedom does not erase environmental differences.

Equipment may still require:

- cooling;
- pressure protection;
- filtration;
- corrosion protection;
- mana support;
- anchoring;
- shielding;
- atmosphere control;
- compatible law/contract context for context-sensitive functions.

These are genuine functional requirements, not arbitrary tier gates.

# 15. Sequence Breaking and Sandbox Progression

Legitimate sequence breaking is canonically valid.

If the player genuinely possesses:

- required knowledge;
- required materials;
- required capabilities;
- site access;
- portal eligibility;
- legal/political permission where relevant;
- survivability;
- recovery path;

the game should recognise the progression even if it occurs earlier than an expected path.

Pre-access realm materials may exist through legitimate history, but no unguaranteed archaeological/imported material may become a mandatory first-access dependency.

Discovery variation is a feature, not an exploit.

The Overworld supports a broad progression arc without requiring one linear quest chain.

A typical capability expansion may move through:

1. immediate survival;
2. shelter and basic tools;
3. resource gathering and food security;
4. crafting and basic processing;
5. settlement interaction or foundation;
6. farming, mining, construction and trade;
7. regional routes and logistics;

9. magic infrastructure;
10. politics, diplomacy and faction influence;
11. portal research and realm contact;
12. advanced cross-realm industry, magic and civilisation;
13. megaprojects, major authorities, titans and world-scale consequences;
14. persistent endgame sandbox.

This is an organising model, not a mandatory sequence for every player.

# 17. Player-Founded Civilisation

The player may remain independent or become deeply involved in civilisation.

The world supports:

- joining existing settlements;
- helping settlements;
- founding settlements;
- building infrastructure;
- creating production networks;
- employing/organising labour through valid systems;
- establishing trade;
- defending territory;
- negotiating alliances;

Settlement and political ownership are not assumed simply because the player constructed something nearby.

Detailed settlement, economy and government canon is owned by FCC-01F.

# 18. Universal Death and Respawn Boundary

FCC-01 does not redefine universal death.

The universal death/failure system remains authoritative for:

- player death;
- respawn location;
- recovery objects/tombstones where enabled;
- inventory recovery;
- death penalties;
- difficulty settings;
- related save/recovery behaviour.

The Ancestral Veil is not automatically invoked when the player, NPC or creature dies.

No Overworld death event automatically creates:

- a Veil spirit;
- a Veil location;
- a one-to-one Ancestral record;
- a resurrection route.

Funerary culture may matter historically, politically, socially or magically without becoming universal soul-routing code.

---

# 19. Sleep and Somnolent Boundary

Universal sleep remains authoritative for ordinary sleeping/rest mechanics.

Overworld sleep may receive bounded Somnolent resonance where legitimate conditions exist.

Possible informational outputs include:

- visions;
- warnings;
- prophecy fragments;
- symbolic dreams;
- quest clues;
- Dreamgate research hints.

Ordinary sleep cannot:

- physically move the player into Somnolent Expanse;
- move inventory into the realm;
- create Somnolent materials;
- duplicate the player body;
- provide normal physical exit/entry.

Normal physical Somnolent access remains Dreamgate-based.

---

# 20. Ancestral Historical Boundary

The Overworld supports generated history, archaeology, memorialisation and selected historical relationships with the Ancestral Veil.

However:

- every death is not mirrored;
- every grave is not mirrored;
- every settlement is not mirrored;
- every historical event does not create a Veil location;
- an Ancestral reflection is not automatically objective truth;
- a memory-being resembling a historical person is not automatically the resurrected original.

Historical reflections require explicit eligibility/provenance.

Detailed history and Ancestral interface canon is owned by FCC-01I.

---

# 21. Impossible Deep Boundary

Impossible Deep is a separate persistent realm with a strong thematic and research relationship to deep Overworld exploration.

The Overworld may contain:

- deep resonance;
- seismic anomalies;
- impossible-depth measurements;
- ancient Deep-contact ruins;
- imported fragments;
- stabilisation machinery;
- historical evidence;
- Deepgate research.

But:

> **No amount of ordinary digging, cave exploration, mining, shafts, subterranean travel or Greatroot routing physically enters Impossible Deep.**

Normal access requires Deepgate.

---

# 22. Ascendant Reach Boundary

The Overworld has its own:

- sky;
- high altitude;
- storms;
- aerial ecology;
- floating terrain;
- sky settlements;
- aerial vessels;
- gravity anomalies where authored.

None of those facts make the area part of Ascendant Reach.

Flying upward does not cross the realm boundary.

Ascendant religion, relics, weather science, migrants and aerial engineering may influence Overworld civilisation only through legitimate contact/provenance.

Normal physical access requires Ascension Gate.

---

# 23. Ashen Lower Realms Boundary

Overworld volcanic, geothermal, ash-rich and magma environments remain Overworld geology unless provenance establishes otherwise.

Ordinary volcanic content does not imply:

- Infernal origin;
- Ashen law;
- Contract magic;
- Ashen ecology;
- Ashgate access;
- Void corruption.

No mine, lava lake, basalt cavern or volcano naturally descends into Ashen Lower Realms.

Normal access requires Ashgate.

---

# 24. Verdant Covenant Boundary

The Overworld supports native forests, colossal-root ecology, magical forests, Greatroot systems, agriculture, ecological restoration and nature magic without those things automatically becoming Verdant.

Greatroot networks do not cross realms physically.

Moonroot/Dreaming-Root-style resonance may provide bounded clues or interactions only where explicit provenance exists.

Normal physical access to Verdant Covenant requires Covenant Portal.

# 25. Native Magic Versus Realm Influence

The Overworld contains genuine native magic.

Therefore the following do not prove foreign-realm origin by themselves:

- mana-rich ecology;
- strange plants;
- spirits;
- dreamlike imagery;
- gravity anomalies;
- darkness;
- magical weather;
- curses;
- blight;
- temporal anomalies;
- unusual geology;
- enchanted ruins.

Realm provenance must be established separately.

This prevents the Overworld from becoming a blended reskin of the six other realms.

# 26. Void Corruption Boundary

Genuine Void corruption requires actual Void provenance.

Possible recognised vectors include:

- confirmed Void fracture/interface;
- Void-contaminated entity;
- Void-tainted material;
- propagation from established Void corruption;
- contaminated cargo/site/portal;
- explicit authored Void-linked event.

The following are not automatically Void corruption:

- native blight;
- disease;
- rot;
- shadow magic;
- nightmare;
- spirit activity;
- hostile divinity;
- oppressive law;
- deep pressure/gravity;
- infernal fire;
- contract magic;
- ordinary curses;
- native magical instability.

Corruption remains state/variant first where the underlying identity survives.

A new permanent definition is created only when a genuinely irreversible mechanically distinct transformation justifies it.

# 28. World Events Are Not Mandatory Player Quests

World events can arise from actual simulation conditions, schedules, factions, ecological pressure, history, weather, magic and realm interfaces.

Events may progress without the player.

Ignoring an event does not automatically choose the worst outcome as punishment.

The affected world systems and available actors determine consequences.

Player quests may expose opportunities to intervene, but a quest does not create the world's underlying event truth.

Detailed dynamic-world canon is owned by FCC-01I.

# 29. Multiplayer and Shared-World Compatibility

Core content canon must not assume only one player exists.

Where implemented, multiplayer must be able to preserve:

- shared world state;
- individual player identity;
- individual reputation where applicable;
- settlement ownership/permissions;
- portal state;
- dungeon state;
- discovered information where sharing rules permit;
- persistent world consequences.

FCC-01 does not define network architecture, replication algorithms or session transport.

Those remain technical-system concerns.

# 30. Accessibility, Difficulty and World-Profile Compatibility

Content must remain compatible with configurable world profiles and accessibility settings.

Peaceful or reduced-threat worlds may preserve:

- ecology;
- bosses as non-hostile world actors;
- migration;
- events;
- diplomacy;
- research;
- building;
- civilisation;
- archaeology;
- realm contact;
- world history.

Reduced threat does not delete the world's identity.

Likewise, difficult worlds may increase consequence without violating progression solvability or save safety.

# 31. POC and Legacy Supersession

The proof-of-concept has completed its purpose.

Old POC content may survive only when it is:

- migrated into a valid canonical family;
- retained as non-shipping archive/reference material;
- converted into a regression fixture;
- deliberately promoted through the FCC process.

No retired POC name, fixed placement, hardcoded starter dependency or prototype resource becomes production canon merely because it once existed in implementation.

This includes the retirement of assumptions such as:

- one fixed Forest Hamlet;
- one fixed starter region;
- fixed portal arrangement;
- fixed scripted raid;
- fixed NPC constellation;
- prototype-only resource identities.

Legacy realm concepts and obsolete source terms are governed by explicit migration registers rather than silent reuse.

# 32. Content Reuse and Single-Definition Principles

## 32.1 Universal Content Is Reused

The Overworld does not create duplicate definitions for universal content merely to make it “Overworld themed.”

## 32.2 Block / Item Single-Definition Rule

A placeable object that recovers as the same thing is one canonical Block plus its inventory projection, not a duplicate authored Item.

## 32.3 Derived Forms Must Be Physically Justified

No automatic:

> Every Material × Every Shape

expansion is permitted.

Forms exist only where physically, culturally or gameplay-wise justified.

## 32.4 State Versus New Definition

Conditions such as:

- wetness;
- damage;
- occupation;
- blight;
- corruption;
- restoration;
- season;
- age;
- contamination

remain states on canonical definitions unless irreversible transformation genuinely creates a distinct identity.

# 36. Implementation-Facing Constraints

Any implementation of the Overworld must preserve the following content truths.

1. The world is deterministic from seed plus persistent deltas.
2. No normal production world depends on POC placements.
3. The default world supports Minecraft-scale exploration extent.
4. Surface, ocean, sky, underground and finite deep layers remain one Overworld identity.
5. Imported content retains provenance.
6. Realm-themed similarity does not establish realm origin.
7. One canonical normal portal family exists per current non-Overworld realm.
8. No portal-free Deep access exists.
9. Ordinary sleep does not physically enter Somnolent.
10. Death does not automatically route to Ancestral Veil.
11. Flying upward does not enter Ascendant Reach.
12. Volcanic/deep terrain does not enter Ashen Lower Realms.
13. Roots do not create generic Verdant travel.
14. Genuine Void corruption requires provenance.
15. Capability is more authoritative than realm/item tier identity.
16. Compatible sequence breaking is valid.
17. Player construction and world changes persist.
18. Dynamic world outcomes survive LOD/streaming.
19. Universal systems are consumed rather than cloned.
20. Later documents may add detail but cannot silently contradict this document.
