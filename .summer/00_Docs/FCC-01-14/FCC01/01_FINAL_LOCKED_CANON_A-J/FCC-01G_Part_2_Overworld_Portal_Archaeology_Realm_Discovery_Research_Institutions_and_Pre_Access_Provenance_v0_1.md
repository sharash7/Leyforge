# 1. Purpose

FCC-01G Part 2 defines how the Overworld can contain **physical, cultural and historical evidence of other realms before the player personally travels to them**.

It owns content-level canon for:

- ancient and recent portal ruins;
- historical portal sites;
- realm-contact archaeology;
- generic broken portal/threshold evidence;
- research institutions;
- archives, observatories and specialist communities;
- portal clue families;
- discovery-order variation;
- lost knowledge and reconstruction;
- existing historical operational gates;
- portal ownership/access context;
- pre-access realm-material provenance;
- safe first-arrival requirements inherited from each realm;
- legitimate sequence breaking.

Realm discovery should feel like:

- archaeology;
- history;
- science;
- magic research;
- religion;
- diplomacy;
- migration;
- exploration

rather than a portal recipe automatically appearing because the player reached a level.

---

# 2. Discovery Is Not Access

The Overworld may know a realm exists before the player can physically enter it.

Possible knowledge states include:

1. rumour or myth;
2. unexplained anomaly;
3. credible evidence;
4. realm identification;
5. portal-family identification;
6. partial construction theory;
7. component/attunement understanding;
8. activation eligibility;
9. validated first traversal.

Not every realm uses every stage in exactly this order.

> **Knowing a realm exists does not automatically unlock its portal. Discovering the portal family does not automatically provide its construction resources, activation state, destination knowledge or political permission.**

# 4. Generic Ancient Portal Content

Legacy/source content may include:

- Ancient Portal Frame;
- Broken Portal Block;
- Unstable Rift;
- Ancient Portal Rubbing;
- damaged threshold technology;
- unidentified gate ruins.

These remain valid where useful as:

- teaser structures;
- archaeological evidence;
- historical remains;
- research sites;
- migration-compatible content;
- generic failed/obsolete portal technology;
- unresolved threshold phenomena.

They are **not automatically one of the six canonical gates**.

A site must be resolved through evidence before it is treated as a specific canonical portal.

# 5. A Broken Frame Is Not a Universal Repair Recipe

Replacing missing blocks in a generic Ancient Portal Frame does not automatically activate realm travel.

For a portal ruin to become functional it must resolve to:

- a valid canonical portal family;
- the correct physical/semantic portal contract;
- compatible components/providers;
- sufficient portal knowledge;
- activation/attunement conditions;
- destination/safety requirements;
- applicable Realm Access authority/runtime conditions.

Otherwise it remains:

- a ruin;
- incomplete technology;
- a failed experiment;
- a research structure;
- historically ambiguous infrastructure.

# 7. Existing Operational Historical Gates

A world may legitimately generate an **already operational canonical portal** when generated history supports it.

Access may still depend on:

- ownership;
- public/private status;
- law;
- diplomatic permission;
- safety;
- quarantine;
- knowledge;
- local politics.

This creates meaningful seed variation without violating portal identity.

In one seed, a realm may be considered myth.

In another, an Overworld capital may have maintained diplomatic travel through the correct canonical gate for generations.

---

# 8. Discovery Order Is Not Dependency Order

Which realm the player **learns about** first can vary according to:

- geography;
- nearby culture;
- generated history;
- archaeological placement;
- religion;
- research institutions;
- migrants;
- trade routes;
- anomalies;
- the player's travel direction.

A player may discover strong evidence of the Ashen Lower Realms before they can construct or use an Ashgate.

Therefore:

> **Discovery order is flexible and seed-dependent. The final mandatory material/dependency order is a separate global progression problem.**

---

# 9. Multiple Discovery Paths

No realm should depend on one irreplaceable:

- NPC;
- book;
- ruin;
- quest;
- structure;
- generated coordinate.

Realm discovery must support several compatible evidence families.

If one clue is:

- destroyed;
- missing from the local region;
- politically restricted;
- misunderstood;
- inaccessible

other legitimate paths can still exist.

This protects Minecraft-scale procedural worlds from one-site progression softlocks.

# 12. Research Requires Evidence

A sufficiently advanced institution cannot simply derive an arbitrary realm from nothing because its “research level” is high.

Research begins from real:

- observation;
- artefact;
- record;
- anomaly;
- witness;
- recovered component;
- historical hypothesis;
- measurable resonance.

Research can:

- interpret;
- compare;
- translate;
- reconstruct;
- test;
- corroborate;
- falsify.

It does not generate omniscience.

---

# 13. Independent Rediscovery

If historical records are lost, a capable civilisation or player may independently rediscover portal principles from surviving physical/magical evidence.

This protects progression after:

- archive destruction;
- civilisation collapse;
- censorship;
- lost language;
- long abandonment.

Rediscovery still needs evidence and capability.

It is not a free technology-tree unlock.

---

# 14. Portal Knowledge Can Be Political

Portal knowledge may be:

- public;
- secret;
- sacred;
- state-controlled;
- guild-controlled;
- culturally protected;
- disputed;
- stolen;
- translated;

Information control can change:

- player access;

- quests;
- faction conflict;
- research paths.

It cannot change the physical truth that a valid canonical gate works when its actual portal contract is satisfied.

# 15. Fragmentary Knowledge

Portal understanding may be distributed across separate discoveries:

- gate geometry;
- frame/foundation role;
- keystone concept;
- stabilisation;
- attunement;
- activation sequence;
- destination logic;
- safety procedure;
- return requirements;
- failure history.

One magic book does not need to contain the entire solution.

This allows long-form archaeology/research without making the system depend on one required object.

- cultivation traditions;

This does not force every player or every seed to personally visit Verdant first.

# 25. Pre-Access Realm Materials

Realm-native materials may legitimately exist in the Overworld **before the player personally visits the source realm** when generated history supplies a real physical transfer history.

Critical principle:

> **The player's first contact is not necessarily civilisation's first contact.**

Therefore a museum, family collection, old ruin, abandoned portal station or current merchant can legitimately contain foreign matter before the player's first crossing.

# 26. Valid Pre-Access Provenance Channels

| # | Provenance Channel | Why It Is Valid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Historical portal trade | Goods physically moved through a legitimately active canonical gate before the player gained personal access. |
| 2 | Ancient expedition | An earlier expedition entered a realm and returned with material, records, organisms or equipment. |
| 3 | Diaspora / migration | People transported possessions, crops, animals or artefacts through legitimate realm contact. |
| 4 | Diplomatic gift | Authenticated realm-contact diplomacy physically transferred the object/material. |
| 5 | Inherited collection | A current holder inherits an object with traceable older transfer/custody history. |
| 6 | Old war / conflict | Material entered through a real historical conflict, invasion, retreat or negotiated contact route. |
| 7 | Archaeological recovery | A previously transferred object is excavated from a valid historical deposit/site. |
| 8 | Wrecked shipment | A real shipment or cargo transfer failed and left recoverable foreign goods. |
| 9 | Abandoned portal facility | A former canonical portal complex retains legitimate transferred material/components. |
| 10 | Prior realm-contact enclave | A historically active realm-linked settlement/embassy left objects or infrastructure. |
| 11 | Authenticated relic | A provenance-bearing artefact can be verified as realm-derived or physically transported. |
| 12 | Current third-party trade | A functioning gate/contact network imports material before the player personally travels. |


Every case requires a plausible physical transfer/custody history.

# 27. What Is Not Valid Provenance

| # | Insufficient Evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Thematic biome resemblance | A magical forest is not sufficient evidence of Verdant matter. |
| 2 | Random chest rarity | A generic rare-loot roll does not establish realm provenance. |
| 3 | Generic dungeon tier | A deep/high-level dungeon does not make its loot realm-derived. |
| 4 | Volcano / lava / basalt | Does not create Ashen matter or Ashgate components by itself. |
| 5 | Deep cave | Does not create Impossible Deep provenance. |
| 6 | Sky island / extreme altitude | Does not create Ascendant provenance. |
| 7 | Grave / cemetery | Does not create Ancestral material by itself. |
| 8 | Dream / sleep | May transfer information under valid conditions, not physical Somnolent matter. |
| 9 | Strange / dark magic | Does not establish Void or any realm origin without a real provenance pathway. |


> **Similarity is not provenance.**

This prevents theme-driven realm loot leakage.

# 28. Frequency of Pre-Access Realm Goods

Pre-access foreign goods are:

- history-dependent;
- bounded;
- traceable.

They are not globally common merely because designers want realm teasers.

A world with centuries of Verdant trade might contain substantial legitimate Verdant goods.

A world where Verdant is mostly myth may contain only a handful of disputed artefacts.

Exact prevalence remains a later worldgen/balance parameter.

---

# 29. Can Pre-Access Material Satisfy a Portal Role?

Yes — but only when all of the following are true:

1. the final portal contract actually accepts the material/provider;
2. the object/material has legitimate provenance;
3. the player can physically and legally acquire/use it where required;
4. global progression validation confirms the route remains reachable.

A random imported rarity cannot become a mandatory first-access ingredient merely because one seed happened to contain it.

This enables **legitimate sandbox sequence breaking** without turning progression into RNG.

---

# 30. Legitimate Sequence Breaking

If the player legitimately possesses:

- required knowledge;
- compatible materials/components;
- required practical/magical capability;
- physical site access;
- valid activation conditions,

then earlier-than-expected portal construction or use is valid.

The game should not reject a physically valid solution solely because the player ignored the expected quest sequence.

Capability/provenance governs progression rather than arbitrary chapter flags.

---

# 31. No Circular First Access

First access to a realm cannot require materials obtainable only after entering that realm unless a legitimate **guaranteed** pre-entry source is deliberately authored into the final graph.

Realm-local portal FCCs repeatedly preserve this rule.

After the realm is accessible, its native materials may be used for:

- upgrades;
- safer operation;
- deeper/advanced destination targeting;
- throughput;
- specialised stabilisation.

That does not make first access circular.

---

# 32. First-Arrival Safety

Portal construction knowledge and destination knowledge are separate.

A first valid activation must obey the destination realm's safe-arrival contract.

Important examples:

- Ascension Gate → validated lower Ascendant destination.
- Deepgate → survivable/recoverable Impossible Deep destination context.
- Ashgate → validated Stratum I.

Discovering a dangerous coordinate does not override safe first-arrival rules.

---

# 33. Later Destination Binding

The same canonical portal family may later gain:

- destination knowledge;
- better stabilisation;
- safer arrival;
- greater cargo capability;
- advanced targeting.

This does not create a second portal family.

Examples:

- upper Ascendant targeting remains an **Ascension Gate** function;
- deeper Ashen targeting remains an **Ashgate** function.

---

# 34. Portal Damage, Closure and Restoration

Portal structures/sites may exist in states such as:

- inactive;
- ready;
- active;
- destination-unbound;
- destination-bound;
