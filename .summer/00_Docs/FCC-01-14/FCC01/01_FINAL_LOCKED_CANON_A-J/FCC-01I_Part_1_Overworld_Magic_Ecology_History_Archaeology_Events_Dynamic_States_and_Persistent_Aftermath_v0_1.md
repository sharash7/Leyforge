# 1. Purpose

FCC-01I Part 1 defines the Overworld as a world where:

- magic is native and geographical;
- mana is conserved and usable by persons and civilisation;
- leylines are real world geography rather than decorative lore;
- runes, wards, rituals, enchanting, alchemy and magical infrastructure obey declared system contracts;
- magical weirdness does not automatically imply another realm or Void corruption;
- pre-player history is generated causally from world/actor/place relationships;
- archaeology reveals evidence rather than perfect lore truth;
- historical provenance follows objects, places and peoples;
- events arise from valid conditions and leave persistent aftermath;
- dynamic states describe ongoing conditions rather than duplicate identities;
- distant/off-screen processing preserves the same world truth as nearby simulation.

It does not finalise exact spell numbers, mana quantities, event frequencies, timer lengths, technical algorithms, final stable IDs, palette values, VFX or audio.

---

# 2. Overworld Magic Is Native

The Overworld possesses genuine native magic.

It does not require another realm to explain:

- ambient mana;
- leylines;
- Raw Mana Crystal;
- rune traditions;
- native magical fauna;
- wards;
- rituals;
- magical weather interaction;
- magical sites;
- native anomalies;
- magical agriculture;
- magical civilisation infrastructure.

Realm-derived magic can coexist with native Overworld magic, but provenance must identify the difference.

> **Mana-rich does not mean realm leak. Strange does not mean Void. Native magical does not mean imported.**

# 8. Mana Scope Architecture

S29 distinguishes several useful mana contexts:

| Mana Context | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Personal mana | Direct-casting resource associated with a compatible person/entity. |
| Stored mana | Mana held in a battery, buffer, crystal or other validated storage provider. |
| Network mana | Mana being transferred/managed through connected infrastructure. |
| Ambient mana | Local environmental magical availability. |
| Leyline mana | High-capacity geographically anchored mana associated with leyline systems. |
| Realm-sourced mana | Mana supplied through a valid realm-specific source/interface where the universal magic system permits it. |

These are contexts/sources, not necessarily six separate substances.

---

# 9. Personal Mana Versus Infrastructure Mana

A person standing near a city ward does not automatically become its battery.

Infrastructure uses explicit external providers.

Personal contribution can occur only through a defined compatible action/ritual.

This avoids turning every mage into an invisible passive power source.

---

# 10. Mana Conservation

External mana transfer requires real:

- source;
- destination;
- amount;
- capacity;
- compatibility;
- connection;
- permission;
- efficiency/loss rule where applicable;
- transaction/commit evidence.

Off-screen simulation cannot invent mana.

A ward, machine or portal does not refill merely because it was unloaded.

---

# 11. Mana Geography

Ambient mana is geographically uneven.

S29 locks broad readable environmental bands:

| Band | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Dormant | Little/no accessible ambient magical activity for ordinary local use. |
| Low | Weak magical background; specialised use may still be possible. |
| Ambient | Normal usable magical environment. |
| Strong Natural | Naturally mana-rich area with stronger ecological/infrastructure consequences. |
| Special Threshold | Exceptional bounded environment such as major convergence, unusual magical site or realm-interface threshold. |

High mana is **not corruption by itself**.

# 12. Leylines

Leylines are real geographical high-capacity magical structures.

They may:

- cross regions;
- intersect;
- feed mana wells/taps;
- affect ecology;
- influence settlement placement;
- support observatories;
- support infrastructure;
- shift under authored world events.

Civilisation may:

- detect;
- tap;
- stabilise;
- relay;
- regulate;
- route local infrastructure around them.

Civilisation does not arbitrarily create a natural leyline because a building was placed.

A Mana Well accesses/conditions legitimate local mana.

A Leyline Observatory measures/researches legitimate magical geography.

# 20. Enchanting and Alchemy

Enchanting attaches persistent magical properties to valid objects/equipment.

Alchemy performs declared transformations.

Neither may:

- duplicate items;
- transmute arbitrary low-value matter into unlimited high-value output;
- erase physical provenance;
- create unrestricted resources.

Final physical definitions/recipes remain FCC-12/FCC-13-owned.

# 21. Summoning and Entity Provenance

“Summoning” describes a magical operation, not one universal ontology.

A summoned presence may be:

- transported;
- manifested;
- temporarily created;
- projected;
- bound.

Summoning does not automatically:

- duplicate persons;
- duplicate inventory;
- erase personhood;
- imply realm origin.

An important object may preserve:

- origin;
- maker;
- first owner;
- later owners;
- place history;
- incident history;
- theft;
- loss;
- excavation;
- restoration;
- custody;

# 36. Ancestral Reflection Boundary

The Ancestral Veil can preserve bounded historical reflections where explicit eligibility/provenance exists.

It does **not** mirror:

- every death;
- every grave;
- every settlement;
- every event;
- the entire Overworld save.

Ancestral reflections are evidence.

They are not automatically complete or objective historical truth.

# 37. Dream / Prophecy Historical Boundary

- clue;

- possible future;
- historical interpretation.

# 39. World Events Are Causal State Changes

A World Event is a time-bounded/phase-driven change arising from valid current conditions.

It is distinct from:

- a static hazard;
- a persistent state profile;
- a quest;
- a random pop-up.

A valid event identifies:

- eligibility;
- cause;
- participants;
- location;
- warnings/signs;
- phases;
- intervention space;
- resolution;
- aftermath.

---

# 40. Event Lifecycle

Recommended conceptual lifecycle:

> **Eligibility → Signs / Warning → Active Phases → Resolution → Aftermath → Historical Record**

A player may:

- intervene;
- support one side;
- redirect;
- prepare;
- evacuate;
- ignore;
- discover the event late.

“Ignore” does **not** universally mean the worst possible authored outcome.

The world resolves based on actual participating systems.

---

# 41. Event Versus State Versus Hazard

**Event:** time/phase-driven transition.  
**State:** authoritative ongoing condition.  
**Hazard:** damaging/risky environmental or systemic factor.

Example:

- Drought Front = event.
- Seasonally Stressed = state.
- dehydration/heat/fire risk = hazards.

Keeping these separate prevents one registry from owning everything.

---

# 42. Off-Screen Time

Events can progress while off-screen through bounded simulation.

The game should not punish players through uncontrolled real-wall-clock progression while the game/save is closed unless a specific mode explicitly chooses that behaviour.

Loaded/unloaded state must not change the event's underlying truth.

# 44. Event Provenance Safeguards

Some event names require explicit source gating.

## Portal Tide

Requires actual portal/Realm Access conditions.

It cannot arise merely because a magical site exists.

## Realm Breach

Requires a genuine realm-interface failure/contact.

It cannot be used as a generic “weird magic” event.

## Dream Contagion

Can represent socially spreading dreams/nightmares/prophecy.

Physical Somnolent matter or organism transfer still requires a physical pathway.

## Spirit Return

Does not automatically mean every dead person returns from Ancestral Veil.

It must identify what spirit/memory/people are involved and why.

## Corruption Bloom

Must identify the corruption/blight source.

It is **not automatically Void corruption**.

## Realm Stabilisation

Repairs/contains a legitimate realm boundary/interface problem.

It does not create a new portal family.

# 47. State Persistence and Recovery

States persist until a real transition occurs.

Recovery may produce:

- Restoring;
- Rebuilding;
- Reconciled/Reformed;
- Restored/Rerouted;
- Cleansed/Stabilised;
- Succeeded/Rebalanced.

Recovery does not rewind the world.

It creates a new state with evidence/history of what happened.

---

# 48. Disasters

“Disaster” is a severity/context interpretation rather than a completely separate universal registry.

A flood, fire, war, migration or magical failure becomes a disaster when consequences cross the relevant threshold/context.

This avoids duplicate event identities.

---

Major events do not end when their active phase stops.

- casualties;
- refugees;
- economic effects;
- route change;
- ecological succession;
- political change;
- reconstruction;
- memorials;
- legal claims;
- knowledge;
- corruption treatment.

These changes persist and can seed future history/events.
