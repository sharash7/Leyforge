# 1. Purpose

FCC-01F Part 2 defines **where Overworld civilisations live and how their material economies function**.

It owns content-level canon for:

- settlement definition and hierarchy;
- rural/urban/nomadic/coastal/underground/aerial population geography;
- exactly thirty source settlement archetypes with one future/deferred Pocket-Realm entry, leaving twenty-nine current Overworld archetypes;
- the eleven district families;
- the seven universal settlement needs as consumed by Overworld content;
- hinterlands, suburbs, satellite communities and regional service networks;
- mixed populations, migration and refugee settlement;
- settlement growth, decline, abandonment and reoccupation;
- regional resource specialisation;
- real markets and finite stock;
- trade routes, caravans, ports and intermodal freight;
- industry, labour and infrastructure;
- cross-realm commerce after legitimate portal access;
- physical warehouse/buffer/cargo conservation;
- economic world-state such as shortages, booms, route loss and recovery.

It does **not** own exact prices, wage numbers, currencies, financial formulas, recipe quantities, throughput, tax rates, building blueprints or pathfinding.

# 2. Settlement Canon Statement

A settlement is a **persistent living community**, not a cluster of structures with an NPC count attached.

A valid settlement combines some form of:

- residents/households;
- shelter;
- provisions;
- work;
- storage;
- services;
- routes;
- governance/social coordination;
- ownership;
- history;
- maintenance;
- interaction with surrounding land/resources.

A settlement can be:
- tiny or enormous;
- permanent or seasonal;
- fixed or mobile;
- terrestrial, aquatic, underground or aerial;
- prosperous, damaged, occupied or declining.

Its identity survives state changes where continuity remains.

# 4. Settlement Scale Ladder

The primary Overworld settlement capability ladder is:

1. **Camp**
2. **Hamlet**
3. **Village**
4. **Town**
5. **City**
6. **Capital**
7. **Magical Metropolis**

Scale represents capability/complexity, not one fixed population number or visual size.

A fortified settlement is a form/state that can exist at multiple scales.

Therefore **Fortified Village remains a valid settlement archetype**, but “Fortified Village” is not required as a universal extra scale rung between Village and Town.

---

# 5. Scale Is Not Morphology

Two Towns can be radically different:

- dense river trade town;
- dispersed forest town;
- underground hall;
- cliff terrace settlement;
- caravan town;
- stilt settlement.

A Capital does not have to be the world's largest city.

Capital means a recognised political/administrative centre.

A huge industrial city may be larger than a small historical capital.

# 8. Critical Archetype Boundary Rules

## 8.1 Deep Cavern City

A Deep Cavern City is physically located in the finite **Deep Overworld**.

It cannot tunnel into Impossible Deep.

Deepgate remains required.

## 8.2 Sky Freehold

A Sky Freehold is an Overworld aerial/floating-island settlement.

Its citizens may fly, glide or use air routes.

It cannot reach Ascendant Reach by altitude alone.

## 8.3 Forge Town

A Forge Town may use native volcanic geology, heat industry and basalt.

That does not make it Ashen.

## 8.4 Portal Town

A Portal Town can form only around a legitimate canonical portal or historically valid portal facility.

The settlement archetype does not create a portal.

## 8.5 Realm Embassy Enclave

A Realm Embassy Enclave requires legitimate diplomatic/contact history when it claims actual realm representation.

## 8.6 Research Colony

Research Colony may form around:
- archaeology;
- meteor/Star Iron;
- geology;
- magic;
- ecology;
- caves;
- ancient machines;
- ruins;
- realm-contact sites.

It is not automatically tied to a deferred World-Engine or another realm.

# 10. Districts Coordinate Real Things

A district boundary creates **no capacity by itself**.

A Residential district has Housing only if real valid homes exist.

An Industrial district produces only through functioning:
- workplaces;
- machines;
- labour;
- power;
- input stock;
- output storage;
- routes.

A Magical district has no mana merely because it is coloured purple on a planner overlay.

The planning hierarchy is broadly:

**Building → Parcel → Cluster → District → Campus/Complex → Settlement Plan → Megaproject/Wonder**

Each level coordinates functioning lower-level content.

# 12. Seven Universal Settlement Needs

Overworld settlements consume the existing seven-needs model:

| Need | Meaning | Typical Providers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Housing | Suitable shelter, beds/residence and household compatibility | Homes, dormitories, refuge, climate protection |
| Provisions | Food, water, fuel and reserve resilience | Farms, fisheries, kitchens, stores, water systems |
| Health | Treatment, sanitation, recovery and prevention capacity | Healers, clinics, hospitals, clean water, quarantine |
| Work | Jobs, productive activity, tools, education and specialist succession | Farms, mines, workshops, schools, guilds, industry |
| Safety | Threat detection, guards, emergency response and protection | Watch, walls, refuge, wards, fire/rescue |
| Infrastructure | Storage, roads, logistics, utilities, governance and maintenance | Warehouses, roads, bridges, ports, power/water/mana networks |
| Morale | Community, identity, culture, recreation, remembrance and confidence | Halls, inns, festivals, shrines, public space, monuments |

These remain the only main settlement needs.

Education, prestige, defence coverage, density and administration can be important capacities/causes without becoming new top-level needs.

# 13. Needs Come From Real Providers

No abstract town-stage bonus can replace:
- housing;
- food;
- water;
- medicine;

- jobs;
- safety;
- roads;
- utilities.

A decorative hospital shell does not provide Health.

A warehouse sign does not provide storage.

A painted district does not produce Work.

Settlement simulation consumes actual functioning facilities, resources and residents.

# 24. Economy Canon Statement

The Overworld economy is a **physical conserved regional economy**.

Goods exist because they were:
- harvested;
- mined;
- crafted;
- grown;
- imported;
- salvaged;
- transferred.

Money/ledgers can abstract value/claims, but physical goods remain real.

A shop window does not generate infinite stock.

Part 1 owns authority/law.

# 46. Embargo and Sanctions

Embargoes/sanctions affect legal commerce where political authority can meaningfully enforce them.

They may cause:
- route changes;
- shortages;
- substitute goods;
- black markets;
- diplomatic pressure.

They do not erase existing inventory.

- destination.

Smuggler Networks are factions; smuggling itself is an economic/legal activity.
