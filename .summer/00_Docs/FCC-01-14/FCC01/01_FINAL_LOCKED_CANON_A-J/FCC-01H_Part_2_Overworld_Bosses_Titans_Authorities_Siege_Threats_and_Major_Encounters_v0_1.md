# 2. Major-Encounter Canon Statement

A major encounter is a **persistent world actor, system, authority or crisis before it is a fight**.

Bosses and Titans may:

- command territory;
- regulate ecology;
- govern people;
- maintain infrastructure;
- embody a law or memory;
- move through regions;
- lead war;
- preserve history;
- control routes;
- destabilise settlements;
- require rescue;
- need repair;
- create diplomatic problems.

The governing rule is:

> **A boss is not an oversized health bar placed around loot.**

Combat may be one legitimate resolution.

It is not the mandatory definition of the content.

---

# 3. Boss, Species, Role and Personhood Must Remain Separate

“Boss” describes **world/encounter significance**, not biological species.

A boss may be:

- exceptional animal;
- ecological sovereign;
- person;
- ruler;
- undead;
- spirit;
- construct;
- distributed intelligence;
- site mind;
- catastrophe;
- mobile infrastructure;
- Titan.

Therefore:

- boss ≠ species;
- boss ≠ morality;
- boss ≠ non-person;
- boss ≠ mandatory kill;
- boss ≠ unique loot material.

An exceptional member of a normal creature family remains that family plus its individual/title/authority state unless genuine biological identity differs.

---

# 4. Personhood

If a major actor is a person, encounter design must respect:

- speech/communication where physically/culturally possible;
- surrender;
- law;
- arrest;
- diplomacy;
- consent;
- faction relationships;
- reputation;
- political consequence;
- non-lethal outcomes where coherent.

Hostility cannot erase personhood.

Likewise:

- undead person remains a person;
- Awakened Construct remains a person;
- collective intelligence may be a person;
- ruler remains a person even when oppressive.

# 6. Evidence and Foreshadowing

Major encounters should generally announce themselves through the world before direct commitment.

Evidence may include:

- tracks;
- nests;
- territorial damage;
- missing populations;
- wrecks;
- changed weather;
- collapsed roads;
- migration;
- faction warnings;
- songs/calls;
- altered mana;
- historical records;
- supply disruptions;
- cult/religious activity;
- political mobilisation.

The player should be able to ask:

> **What is happening here?**

before being forced to answer:

> **How many hit points does it have?**

# 7. Dormancy, Awakening and Escalation

A boss may begin:

- dormant;
- migrating;
- hidden;
- politically inactive;
- sealed;
- wounded;
- hibernating;
- contained.

Activation can follow actual causes:

- season;
- ecological pressure;
- resource extraction;
- faction action;
- damaged seal;
- player disturbance;
- world event;
- Titan movement;
- realm-provenance event.

The world may also de-escalate through:

- restoration;
- treaty;
- migration;
- repair;
- containment;
- route change.

Boss activation is therefore not simply “player crossed an invisible arena trigger.”

# 8. Encounter Spaces

Major encounters can occur through:

- roaming territory;
- migration corridor;
- dungeon/site;
- settlement crisis;
- siege;
- open ocean;
- airspace;
- subterranean route;
- moving Titan body;
- ritual/research interface;
- political audience;
- containment project.

A circular boss arena is optional.

Terrain, voxel damage, civilians, ecology, structures and routes remain part of the world.

---

# 9. Encounter Phases

Phases should normally represent meaningful **state changes**, such as:

- goal change;
- injury;
- damaged armour/component;
- ally arrival/departure;
- territory shift;
- weather transition;
- machinery shutdown;
- ritual completion;
- law/permission change;
- retreat;
- surrender;
- environmental collapse.

Health thresholds may contribute when appropriate.

They are not mandatory universal phase logic.

---

# 10. Preparation

Players can prepare through:

- research;
- reconnaissance;
- gear;
- armour;
- environmental equipment;
- consumables;
- settlement stock;
- NPC specialists;
- automation;
- wards;
- traps;
- route control;
- evacuation plans;
- diplomacy;
- ritual;
- legal evidence;
- infrastructure;
- environmental engineering.

Preparation should change the actual encounter/world state.

A repaired floodgate matters because it physically changes water pressure, not because it gives +10% boss damage.

---

# 11. Capability Freedom

No boss requires a tool solely because it has the “correct realm/tier name.”

If a player possesses another provider that genuinely satisfies the needed:

- mining;
- structural;
- pressure;
- heat;
- electrical;
- magical;
- navigation;
- containment;
- legal;
- research

capability, it can be valid.

Real environmental/context requirements remain authoritative.

# 12. Alternate Resolutions

Where the fiction supports them, major actors can be resolved through combinations of:

- defeat/kill;
- repel;
- capture;
- cleanse;
- cure;
- restore;
- repair;
- reprogram;
- seal;
- contain;
- relocate;
- redirect;
- tame/bond;
- bargain;
- negotiate;
- prove claim;
- expose fraud;
- depose;
- replace/succeed;
- recognise sovereignty;
- protect;
- coexist;
- withdraw/avoid.

Not every foundation needs every outcome.

But primary progression must not assume killing every significant being.

---

# 13. Failure, Retreat and Recovery

Failure may create persistent consequences such as:

- retreat;
- injury;
- capture;
- damaged settlement;
- occupied route;
- lost supplies;
- migration;
- siege escalation;
- closed infrastructure;
- political loss;
- recovery/rescue work.

Failure should normally produce a changed sandbox rather than a silent hard reset.

Major encounters should preserve a readable retreat/recovery path where possible.

---

# 14. Reward Philosophy

Rewards may include:

- knowledge;
- maps;
- recipes/technique eligibility;
- resource rights;
- legitimate materials;
- salvage;
- trade;
- route access;
- settlement safety;
- alliance;
- political authority;
- title/status;
- research;
- restored infrastructure;
- ecology recovery;
- protected territory;
- relics with provenance.

The boss does not need a unique “Boss Heart” or “Boss Essence.”

---

# 15. Recurrence and Succession

Possible recurrence models include:

- **Permanent unique** — the individual is gone/changed permanently after final resolution.
- **Successor** — a role/territory later gains a new holder.
- **Migratory** — actor may leave and later return through real movement.
- **Cyclical** — event/entity recurs through an authored environmental/historical cycle.
- **Reconstructable** — machine/construct can return only if physically rebuilt.
- **Resurrectable** — only with a real resurrection/source mechanic.
- **World-setting dependent** — selected recurrence behaviour may vary by server/world settings.

> **A unique individual never respawns merely because a generic cooldown elapsed.**

---

# 16. Peaceful and Reduced-Hostility Worlds

Peaceful settings should preserve major content where possible through:

- observation;
- research;
- migration;
- rescue;
- restoration;
- diplomacy;
- ecology;
- politics;
- construction;
- containment.

A Titan can remain a world-scale experience without mandatory combat.

Some explicitly hostile story content may be disabled/opt-in according to settings without deleting its non-combat systems.

# 19. Native Weirdness Is Not Automatic Realm Provenance

The 36-boss roster deliberately contains:

- dreamlike beings;
- spirits;
- gravity users;
- storm creatures;
- volcanic creatures;
- deep creatures;
- ancient constructs.

These do **not** automatically belong to another realm.

Examples:

- Thunder-Glass Wyvern ≠ Ascendant;
- Worldburrower ≠ Impossible Deep;
- Ashcaldera Broodmother ≠ Ashen;
- Dreamshore Somnambulist ≠ Somnolent by appearance;
- Drowned Bell Matron ≠ Ancestral by spirit identity;
- Gravity-Horn Skyram ≠ Ascendant/Deep by gravity ability.

Realm provenance requires evidence.

---

# 20. Void-Specific Boss Boundary

A genuine **Rift-Fed Houndlord** is different because its identity is explicitly Void-fed.

Therefore a valid instance requires:

- established Void fracture/interface;
- Void contamination;
- legitimate Void-linked history;
- another globally recognised Void pathway.

A random native gravity scar or magical anomaly cannot be relabelled into this foundation just because it looks suitably creepy.
