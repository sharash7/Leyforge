# 1. Purpose

FCC-01E Part 2 defines the authoritative Overworld content canon for:
- native magical fauna and spirits;
- magical parasites;
- undead and necromantic ecology;
- non-Void blight creature foundations;
- provenance-valid Void-derived hostile ecology;
- provenance-valid Ashen-derived transformed fauna;
- constructs, golems and created ecological actors;
- personhood/classification boundaries;
- corruption/state-versus-species rules;
- creature-material provenance;
- exceptional-individual/boss/titan separation.

It does not create a generic biological kingdom called “monsters.”

Its governing question is:

> **What kind of being is this, where did it come from, what role does it have, is it a person, what state is it in, and which system owns the remaining behaviour?**

---

# 2. Canon Statement — Monster Is Not a Species Class

Danger, hostility and monster presentation do not decide ontology.

A being may be dangerous because it is:
- a predator;
- territorial;
- hungry;
- diseased;
- magically unstable;
- defending a place;
- under orders;
- corrupted;
- politically hostile;
- malfunctioning;
- trapped in obsolete instructions.

The locked rules are:

1. **Hostile does not mean non-person.**
2. **Magical does not mean hostile.**
3. **Undead does not mean non-person.**
4. **Construct does not mean property.**
5. **Spirit does not mean Ancestral provenance.**
6. **Dreamlike does not mean Somnolent provenance.**
7. **Storm/flight does not mean Ascendant provenance.**
8. **Depth/pressure does not mean Impossible Deep provenance.**
9. **Heat/ash/infernal appearance does not mean Ashen provenance.**
10. **Blight, curse or darkness does not mean Void.**
11. **Corruption normally uses state/variant data unless identity is genuinely transformed.**
12. **Boss, mount, guardian, summoned and war-beast are roles, not species taxonomies.**

---

# 3. Personhood Boundary

Personhood is independent from body material, origin, death-state, hostility, faction or an ownership claim.

Examples:
- ordinary deer: non-person creature;
- magical animal: usually creature unless personhood is established;
- mindless Hollow Walker: non-person undead foundation;
- intelligent undead citizen: person;
- tool golem: may be non-person construct;
- awakened golem: person;
- Goblin raider: hostile person, not monster species;
- collective/hive: may qualify for personhood where the universal framework says so.

A disputed or uncertain being must not be automatically reduced to harvestable property merely because classification is inconvenient.

---

# 4. Classification Layers

Keep separate fields/concepts for:

- **ontology/body:** biological, spirit, undead, construct, magical, colony;
- **personhood:** person, non-person, unresolved;
- **provenance:** native, imported, transformed, realm-derived, anomaly;
- **state:** healthy, damaged, blighted, corrupted, dormant, bound, malfunctioning;
- **faction/ownership:** wild, household, settlement, faction, claimed, emancipated, autonomous;
- **behaviour:** friendly, neutral, territorial, hostile, fleeing, defending;
- **role:** wildlife, worker, guardian, mount, companion, boss, pest, authority.

Collapsing these categories would break personhood, law, provenance and ecological truth.

# 6. Native Magical Ecology

The eight principal native magical/spirit fauna foundations are:

- Ley Mote;
- Hearth Spirit;
- Raincaller Frog;
- Moonhare;
- Rune Antler;
- Mirrorfish;
- Whisperwing;
- Orchard Warden.

This locks an important world rule:

> **The Overworld has its own native magical ecology. Other realms do not need to explain every magical creature.**

Native magical fauna may respond to ambient mana, leylines, rune sites, rituals, wards, magical agriculture and historical magical locations.

Magic is a real ecological variable, not permission for random powers.

---

# 7. Ley Mote

Ley Motes are ambient mana-indicator fauna/foundations.

They may form visible flows or concentrations along:
- leylines;
- conduits;
- portal activity;
- mana storms.

They are not a replacement for the mana system, a free Mana Crystal, or proof of realm leakage.

Lightweight presentation is valid; underlying magical/environmental truth remains authoritative.

---

# 8. Hearth Spirit

Hearth Spirits connect magical ecology with settlement care, continuity and place memory.

Source relationships include shared meals, care and repair.

They are **not automatically owned pets**.

A culture may honour, fear, study or ignore them. Personhood must come from actual agency/cognition, not simply the word “spirit.”

---

# 9. Raincaller Frog

Raincaller Frogs are native weather-linked amphibians.

Their chorus can act as a weather clue and may interface with supported local ritual-weather systems.

They do not own climate, guarantee rain or prove Ascendant contact.

FCC-01B remains authoritative for weather.

---

# 10. Moonhare

Moonhares are night-active magical prey with illusion-adapted behaviour.

They demonstrate that dreamlike or illusion-like wildlife can be native Overworld content.

They do not create Dreamgate travel or Somnolent provenance by appearance.

---

# 11. Rune Antler

Rune Antlers are mana-adapted grazers whose shed antlers can contain natural glyph patterns.

This is a strong example of renewable magical creature-material provenance.

Exact harvested Items, grades and rune recipes are FCC-12/FCC-13 work.

The animal remains fauna, not a mobile ore deposit.

---

# 12. Mirrorfish

Mirrorfish are magical freshwater fauna with reflection/memory-associated behaviour.

They remain distinct from Ancestral realm fauna.

Memory-like behaviour does not prove Ancestral origin.

---

# 13. Whisperwing

Whisperwings are messenger-like spirit birds associated with roads, shrines, graves and settlements.

Repeated fragments of names or warnings are evidence/behaviour, not perfect truth.

Knowledge systems decide confidence, provenance and interpretation.

---

# 14. Orchard Warden

Orchard Wardens are managed-grove spirit-beasts connecting agriculture, stewardship, pest control and magical ecology.

“Stewardship respected” must resolve through actual ecological/cultural/system state, not invisible moral scoring.

Managed relationship does not automatically settle ownership or personhood.

---

# 15. Crystal Burrower

Crystal Burrowers are mineral-feeding tunnellers associated with crystal caverns and mana pockets.

They may expose existing deposits and destabilise walls.

They cannot create infinite ore. Geology/resource state remains authoritative.

Their magical/mineral ecology is sufficiently central to place them in S20 rather than ordinary S18 fauna.

---

# 16. Mana Tick

Mana Ticks are magical parasites affecting mana-rich environments and beings.

They may drain charge and create magical illness pressure.

Set 29 owns actual disease/illness/physiology resolution.

They are magical parasites, not automatically Void organisms.

---

# 17. Undead and Necromantic Ecology

The principal undead/necromantic foundations are:
- Hollow Walker;
- Grave Root;
- Lantern Wraith.

Undead ecology may relate to disturbed burial sites, battlefields, abandoned settlements, necromantic activity and cursed history.

Universal death/respawn remains authoritative.

A player or NPC dying does **not** automatically create a new undead population or move them into Ancestral Veil.

---

# 18. Hollow Walker

Hollow Walker is explicitly a mindless reanimated-traveller foundation.

It can provide danger, historical evidence and disease pressure.

Its own mindlessness does not make “all undead are non-person” a rule.

---

# 19. Grave Root

Grave Root is a necromantic plant-animal colony associated with burial layers and remains.

It is not ordinary FCC-01D flora, not automatically an Ancestral organism, and does not justify a generic Soul resource.

Necromantic ecology remains distinct from Ancestral realm citizenship.

---

# 20. Lantern Wraith

Lantern Wraith is a predatory memory-light/spirit-like undead foundation associated with ruins, roads and mist.

It can imitate safe lights and create identity/fear pressure.

Personhood still depends on actual cognition/agency; appearance and hostility do not decide it automatically.

---

# 21. Non-Void Blight Ecology

The principal current non-Void blight foundations are:
- Blight Hound;
- Canker Swarm.

This locks:

> **Overworld blight can exist without Void corruption.**

Possible native/local causes may involve ecological, magical, disease, curse, alchemical or industrial sources where separately authored.

Actual Void corruption requires real Void provenance.

---

# 22. Blight Hound

Blight Hound is a blighted pack-predator foundation.

Its source ecology can spread compatible blight through wounds, carcasses and habitat.

If a particular Blight Hound later gains actual Void corruption, that is an additional validated state rather than proof that the base family was always Void-derived.

---

# 23. Canker Swarm

Canker Swarm is destructive blight ecology affecting crops, timber and living structures.

It is especially suited to scalable representation:
- local agents;
- infestation region;
- cohort;
- structure condition;
- regional pressure.

Thousands of individually persistent swarm actors are not required.

---

# 24. Void-Derived Hostile Ecology

Rift Leech and Nullwing require actual Void provenance.

Valid contexts may include a confirmed Void scar, portal contamination, legitimate cross-realm transfer or another explicitly established Void pathway.

The following are insufficient:
- darkness;
- purple/black art;
- ordinary magic instability;
- a creepy ruin;
- generic portal proximity;
- nightmare imagery.

---

# 25. Rift Leech

Rift Leech is a Void-derived portal/mana parasite.

It can drain stability and hitchhike through legitimate transfer vectors.

This makes it an important cross-realm ecology example, but every valid instance must have traceable provenance.

---

# 26. Nullwing

Nullwing is a Void-derived aerial absence-feeder.

Source effects include sound suppression and weaker magical navigation.

Native nocturnal birds or ordinary silent zones do not become Nullwing content.

---

# 27. Ashbone Herd

Ashbone Herd is an **Ashen-derived transformed ungulate population**.

This is deliberately distinct from ordinary Overworld heat/volcanic fauna such as Magma Shell or Ember Ant.

Valid Ashbone Herd provenance requires:
- legitimate Ashgate-linked history;
- transferred Ashen organism/material;
- explicit transformation;
- or another validated Ashen contact path.

A volcano alone cannot produce it.

---

# 28. Constructs and Created Ecology

The eight principal construct foundations are:

1. Hauler Golem
2. Ward Hound
3. Orchard Automaton
4. Archive Mite
5. Gearwing
6. Quarry Crawler
7. Bridge Sentinel
8. Runic Custodian

Constructs can participate in the living world through labour, transport, maintenance, inspection, excavation, guarding and infrastructure.

They are not biological species, but they still require persistent identity/state appropriate to their role.

---

# 29. Construct Personhood and Ownership

Construct status does not settle personhood.

A construct may be:
- non-sapient tool;
- limited autonomous machine;
- social but non-person;
- awakened person;
- copied/transferred person;
- disputed emerging personhood.

Ownership can apply to a non-person tool.

Ownership cannot erase genuine personhood.

If a construct is or becomes a person, personhood/civilisation law becomes authoritative.

---

# 30. Hauler Golem

Hauler Golem is a programmable transport construct.

It requires real cargo, route, power/fuel/mana where applicable, maintenance and task authority.

It cannot teleport freight or generate throughput because a walking animation plays.

---

# 31. Ward Hound

Ward Hound is a patrol/anomaly-detection construct.

It may detect authored categories such as corruption, spirits or unauthorised mana.

Its sensor output is evidence from a defined capability, not omniscience.

---

# 32. Orchard Automaton

Orchard Automaton performs agricultural maintenance such as pruning, carrying and pest deterrence.

Misconfiguration can damage plants or ecology.

Automation scales capability; it does not erase agriculture/ecology constraints.

---

# 33. Archive Mite

Archive Mite is a tiny preservation/indexing construct.

Its source identity includes the possibility of incorrectly reorganising or consuming material that its rules classify badly.

This creates archive-maintenance gameplay without making knowledge stores magically perfect.

---

# 34. Gearwing

Gearwing is a courier and inspection flyer around factories, roads and towers.

Flight does not give it Ascendant provenance.

It is a created Overworld construct foundation unless actual history says otherwise.

---

# 35. Quarry Crawler

Quarry Crawler supports excavation and hauling.

It can create noise, dust, maintenance demand and strategic value.

It cannot bypass geology, protected blocks or resource conservation.

---

# 36. Bridge Sentinel

Bridge Sentinel is an infrastructure guardian.

Its challenge behaviour may use ownership, permission and active instructions.

A sentinel enforcing obsolete rules creates a world-state conflict; it does not make those rules morally or legally correct.

---

# 37. Runic Custodian

Runic Custodian is a magic-site maintenance construct.

Possible states include:
- dormant;
- functioning;
- damaged;
- hostile under obsolete instruction;
- repaired;
- awakened.

This supports persistent ruin restoration rather than one-shot enemy clearing.

---

# 38. Summoned and Bound Beings

“Summoned” is not a species.

A summoned actor might be:
- temporary magical construct;
- transported real creature;
- spirit manifestation;
- elemental;
- copy/projection;
- bound person;
- created temporary organism.

The source system must state what physically/metaphysically occurred.

One generic summon taxonomy must not erase provenance or personhood.

---

# 39. Elementals and Material-Bodied Beings

Elementals may exist where separately defined by magic content.

They are not automatically part of the 26 principal S20 roster unless explicitly listed.

A fire-shaped being is not automatically Ashen. A stone-bodied being is not automatically a construct.

Identity comes from actual definition and provenance.

---

# 40. Corruption, Curse and Transformation

Default rule:

> **state/variant first; new definition only when identity truly changes.**

Use states for temporary/reversible:
- corruption;
- blight;
- disease;
- curse;
- injury;
- magical charge;
- contamination;
- mutation.

Create a distinct permanent identity only when transformation is genuinely irreversible/self-propagating or functionally/anatomically distinct enough to warrant separate canon.

---

# 41. Void Boundary

Void corruption requires actual Void provenance.

Scary art, nightmare behaviour, undeath, infernal origin, Deep anomaly, divine conflict, blight or old curses are not enough.

This preserves the identity of every other magical ecology.

---

# 42. Sapient Hostility Is Not Monster Taxonomy

People remain persons when they are raiders, soldiers, criminals, cultists, bandits or enemy factions.

A Goblin raider may share combat AI capabilities with creatures, but is still a person.

FCC-01F owns ancestry/civilisation identity.

---

# 43. Boss / Titan Boundary

A principal creature family is not automatically a boss.

A boss can be an exceptional individual, magical mutation, political person, construct, site authority or titan.

FCC-01H owns boss/titan/authority canon.

An exceptional member of a normal fauna family should normally remain that family plus individual state, not a duplicate species.

---

# 44. No Generic Boss Material

Defeating a major creature does not justify inventing “Boss Essence,” “King Fang Metal,” “Titan Ore” or similar filler.

Rewards must follow real provenance:
- actual biological material;
- actual constructed parts;
- existing rare resources;
- knowledge;
- territory;
- rights;
- relics;
- political outcomes.

---

# 45. Creature-Material Provenance

Every creature-derived material should be able to answer:
1. which creature/body/state produced it;
2. whether it is biological, magical, constructed or transformed;
3. whether collection is lethal;
4. whether it is renewable;
5. whether age/health/state affects grade;
6. whether it is an ordinary material, specialist material or provenance variant;
7. whether corruption changes state or identity;
8. whether it survives export;
9. whether specialist magic needs compatible conditions;
10. what final Item/recipe represents it.

FCC-01E owns source/provenance. FCC-12/FCC-13 own final material and Item/recipe structure.

---

# 46. Magical Creature Materials

Source-compatible material paths include:
- shed Rune Antler;
- magical parasite samples;
- magically charged biological tissue where actually defined;
- construct components/salvage;
- specialist blight samples.

A magical creature does not automatically contain a “core.” Core-like Items require real anatomy or construction.

---

# 47. Undead Material and Soul Boundary

Ordinary physical remains can exist where biology supports them.

FCC-01E does **not** create:
- generic Soul;
- universal Ancestral Essence;
- automatic soul shard;
- death currency.

Undead existence does not make souls inventory resources.

---

# 48. Construct Salvage and Personhood

Non-person constructs may yield recoverable components according to their actual construction.

A person-construct's body cannot become free salvage merely because it is made from metal, stone, wood, glass or rune components.

Personhood remains authoritative.

---

# 49. Culture, Law and Ethics

Societies may hunt, protect, worship, study, farm, regulate, quarantine, cleanse, salvage or coexist with different beings.

Those are culture/law/faction overlays.

They do not change objective biology, provenance or personhood truth.

---

# 50. Knowledge and Misclassification

In-world cultures may incorrectly claim:
- a spirit is Ancestral;
- a volcanic creature is Ashen;
- a dreamlike animal is Somnolent;
- a blight is Void;
- a construct is mindless;
- an intelligent undead person is a monster.

Such beliefs can persist as folklore, prejudice, scholarship or contested theory.

Developer canon remains separate.

---

# 51. Cross-Realm Organisms and Suitability

Imported realm creatures require real physical transfer and then use suitability states such as:
- compatible;
- managed habitat;
- stressed;
- dormant;
- loses specialist capability;
- naturalised;
- invasive;
- cannot survive.

Realm-specific physics/law does not automatically accompany an exported creature.

A creature may survive but lose specialist behaviour if the required environment is absent.

# 53. Events, Persistence and Reoccupation

S20 foundations may participate in magical migrations, undead rises, blight outbreaks, construct failures, portal infestations, Void contamination and realm-derived introductions.

S31 owns event lifecycle. S35 owns cross-realm propagation rules.

Clearing a hostile site does not guarantee permanent emptiness. Reoccupation depends on habitat, population sources, ownership, restoration and state.

A repaired construct site can remain repaired, become public, reactivate under new ownership or later degrade. Blind encounter reset is rejected.

---

# 54. Peaceful / Reduced-Hostility Worlds

Difficulty settings may reduce aggression, hostile eligibility or outbreak severity without deleting magical ecology, spirits, constructs, migrations, research or restoration.

Non-combat roles must survive peaceful play.

---

# 55. Registry Discipline

Do not create new principal definitions for every:
- age;
- sex;
- coat/colour;
- seasonal morph;
- corrupted stage;
- domestic breed;
- named individual;
- boss individual;
- injury state;
- regional morph.

Prefer family + state/variant/individual records unless identity genuinely changes.

---

# 56. Stable-ID Boundary

Older 24F IDs remain valuable migration evidence, but FCC-01E does not re-certify every namespace row merely by repeating the source.

Final definitive registry identity belongs downstream.

Content identity is preserved first; aliases/migration are handled later.

---

# 57. Forge / Art Handoff

Future creature authoring must support:
- biological and constructed body families;
- named parts/sockets;
- movement variants;
- condition overlays;
- corruption/blight states;
- juvenile/adult or breed variants where required;
- harness/saddle compatibility;
- material-producing anatomy;
- construct maintenance/damage states.

Art must not use “evil-looking” as a substitute for hostility or provenance.

Final palette, model, rig, animation, VFX and audio remain later art governance.
