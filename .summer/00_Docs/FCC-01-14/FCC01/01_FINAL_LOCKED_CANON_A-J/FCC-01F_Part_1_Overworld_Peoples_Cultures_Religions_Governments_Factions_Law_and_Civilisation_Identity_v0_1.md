# 1. Purpose

FCC-01F Part 1 defines **who Overworld people and societies can be** and how those identities remain distinct while interacting.

It owns content-level canon for:

- the twelve principal ancestry/personhood foundations;
- lineage, heritage, mixed origin and transformation;
- personhood boundaries;
- the twenty principal culture foundations;
- culture variation, diaspora and hybridisation;
- language, script and naming identity;
- religion, philosophy and cosmological traditions;
- Ascendant-contact religion and pilgrimage boundaries;
- the fourteen government profiles;
- the twenty-four faction archetypes;
- citizenship and legal status;
- law, jurisdiction, evidence and crime;
- property/inheritance recognition;
- contract-versus-law boundaries;
- territory, borders, sovereignty and political recognition;
- diplomacy, treaties, war, conquest, occupation, resistance and peace;
- migration, refugee and diaspora political identity;
- civilisation change, succession and persistent political history.

It does **not** own individual personality, social Trust/Affection/Fear, dialogue resolution, economic prices/wages/currency amounts, combat resolution, pathfinding, biological needs, or final art.

---

# 2. Why FCC-01F Is Split

S21–S25 together cover nearly the entire civilisation layer.

Part 1 contains **identity, culture and formal political truth**.

Part 2 contains **settlement, population geography and physical economy**.

The split prevents two distinct questions from becoming one giant document:

> **Part 1 asks “Who are these people and institutions?”  
> Part 2 asks “Where and how do their communities materially function?”**

# 3. Civilisation Identity Statement

Leyforge does not infer a society from ancestry.

A person may simultaneously have:
- ancestry;
- lineage/heritage;
- culture;
- subculture;
- language competencies;
- faith/philosophy;
- household;
- settlement residence;
- citizenship;
- legal status;
- profession;
- guild membership;
- faction memberships;
- government office;
- personal beliefs;
- personal loyalties;
- realm-contact history.

Those identities may align, conflict, overlap or change independently.

> **People are not factions. Factions are not cultures. Cultures are not ancestries. Governments are not settlements. Faiths are not governments. Realms are not biological homelands.**

No ancestry is inherently:
- heroic or evil;
- civilised or primitive;
- intelligent or unintelligent;
- peaceful or hostile;
- magical or technological;
- suited to one profession, government, religion or class.

# 4. Authority Boundaries

## 4.1 FCC-01F Part 1 Owns

- authored Overworld civilisation foundations;
- ancestry/culture/government/faction rosters;
- formal political content;
- realm-contact civilisation expressions;
- content-level legal/political relationships.

## 4.2 Universal Personhood Owns Truth of Personhood

FCC-01F recognises content that can be a person, but no local government may make an actual person cease to be a person.

Legal recognition may be:
- recognised;
- denied;
- disputed;
- incomplete.

That changes law and treatment, not underlying personhood truth.

## 4.3 Social Systems Own Personal Relationship State

Formal membership is not Loyalty.

Citizenship is not affection.

Treaty is not friendship.

A government may be recognised while hated.

## 4.4 Economy Owns Monetary Resolution

Government may possess lawful authority to tax, tariff or borrow.

Economic systems own:
- rate;
- amount;
- collection;
- payment;
- market consequence;
- treasury/budget.

## 4.5 Movement Owns Physical Travel

Law can permit or prohibit border crossing.

Movement answers whether the person can physically cross the terrain/route.

A legal passport does not grant flight.

## 4.6 Magic Owns Magical Truth

Faith can claim supernatural facts.

Magic/cosmology canon determines which supernatural claims are objectively true.

# 5. Identity Stack

A persistent person may carry multiple independent identities.

Examples:

**Person A**
- ancestry: Goblin;
- heritage: coastal mixed lineage;
- culture: Wayfarer Mosaic;
- language: March Trade plus local harbour creole;
- faith: Veilkeeper philosophy;
- citizenship: reef-port city;
- profession: navigator;
- faction: Merchant League;
- political view: reform bloc.

**Person B**
- ancestry: Awakened Construct;
- culture: Frosthearth Circle;
- citizenship: mountain republic;
- profession: physician;
- faith: Civic/Ethical;
- faction: Personhood League;
- body: repaired stone/metal frame.

Neither profile is exceptional merely because the identities cross older fantasy stereotypes.

# 6. The Twelve Principal Ancestry / Personhood Foundations

S21 locks exactly **twelve principal current Overworld ancestry/personhood foundations**.

| # | Foundation | Body / Shared Direction | Social Range | Canonical Safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Human | Highly varied and adaptable body profiles with broad environmental tolerance. | Any rural, urban, nomadic, maritime, subterranean, aerial, magical or realm-contact society. | Body diversity does not make Human the neutral/default culture. |
| 2 | Goblin | Typically smaller-bodied and dexterous, with strong low-space mobility and wide local variation. | Cities, villages, guilds, clans, trade networks, communes, academic orders, hostile factions and mixed settlements. | Goblin is ancestry, never an enemy type. |
| 3 | Dwarf | Robust compact body ranges; strong endurance; some lineages may have vibration/stone-sense traditions. | Surface, underground, volcanic, frost, maritime, urban, pastoral and realm-contact societies. | Underground affinity does not lock culture, profession or government. |
| 4 | Orc | Powerful but highly varied body frames shaped strongly by lineage and environment. | Pastoral, urban, maritime, agricultural, scholarly, mercantile, military, spiritual and mixed societies. | Orc does not imply warrior, clan culture or hostility. |
| 5 | Elven Kindreds | Related long-lived body families shaped over generations by ecology, magic, memory and realm exposure. | Many independent cultures and political identities across the Overworld and legitimate realm-contact histories. | There is no single universal Elven civilisation. |
| 6 | Awakened Construct | Created material bodies with genuine personhood; forms may be repaired, rebuilt, transferred or self-modified. | Civic assemblies, guilds, adopted cultures, freed communities, machine societies and mixed settlements. | Construct body does not imply property; maintenance is not automatically Hunger. |
| 7 | Tidekin | Amphibious or aquatic body families adapted to rivers, reefs, coasts, deltas or deep water. | River communities, reef communes, harbour districts, traders, farmers, explorers and land-based diaspora. | Aquatic adaptation affects infrastructure/access, not social worth. |

| 9 | Mycelian | Fungal or colony-bodied peoples whose individuality, memory and reproduction vary by lineage. | Cavern settlements, forest enclaves, medical orders, archives, cities, farms and migratory networks. | Collective or fungal embodiment does not reduce personhood. |
| 10 | Emberkin | Scaled or heat-adapted peoples with wide variation in thermal tolerance, size and magical affinity. | Volcanic, forge, desert, scholarly, mercantile, military, agricultural and cold-climate diaspora societies. | Heat/ash adaptation does not automatically imply Ashen provenance. |
| 11 | Rootborn | Plant-bodied or symbiotic peoples with seasonal, grafted, seed-grown or cultivated forms. | Forest, garden, mobile-grove, city, farm, monastery, Verdant-contact and mixed settlements. | Plant body does not imply Verdant citizenship or ecological ideology. |
| 12 | Veilborn | Spirit-material persons anchored by memory, relic, body, place, oath or recurring manifestation. | Memorial houses, traveller communities, research enclaves, cities and mixed households. | Spirit-material embodiment does not make every Veilborn Ancestral-Veil native. |

The older production documents sometimes classified the latter six as expansion candidates. That **scope-tier label is superseded for FCC-01 content canon**: S21 retains all twelve as the principal current foundation roster.

This does not mean every generated region or settlement must contain all twelve.

# 7. Ancestry Is Body-Level Identity

An ancestry/body definition may affect:

- body envelope;
- equipment fit;
- reach/accessibility;
- senses;
- inherited or persistent physical traits;
- environmental adaptation;
- reproduction/repair context;
- movement capability;
- certain innate magical/biological traits.

It does not determine:

- culture;
- morality;
- intelligence;
- profession;
- class;
- faction;
- government;
- religion;
- hostility;
- technology level.

# 8. Personhood

Personhood can apply to:

- biological persons;
- awakened constructs;
- intelligent undead;
- spirits;
- transformed beings;
- copies with valid continuity;
- collective/distributed persons;
- other future embodiments.

A person cannot be routed into ordinary:
- livestock;
- animal capture;
- pet sale;
- harvest;
- monster-loot;
- inventory-object

runtime merely because they are non-human, hostile, enslaved, occupied, legally unrecognised or inconvenient.

# 9. Lineage and Heritage

Lineage/heritage describes inherited, regional, transformed, mixed or realm-linked variation.

Examples:
- regional lineage;
- magical lineage;
- mixed lineage;
- diaspora adaptation;
- transformed heritage;
- realm-returned heritage;
- generational environmental adaptation.

Heritage is **not automatically culture**.

An Emberkin born into Hearthland Commons culture remains Emberkin ancestry and Hearthland culture.

A Human whose family has lived in Ashen-contact settlements for generations may have Ashen-linked heritage without becoming “Infernal” by arbitrary label.

---

# 10. Transformation and Continuity

Body change does not automatically change every identity.

A person may:
- transform;
- receive a replacement body;
- become undead;
- become spirit-material;
- transfer into a constructed body;
- gain realm adaptations.

The system must preserve:
- personhood;
- history;
- relationships;
- legal disputes;
- citizenship where law permits;
- culture;
- personal continuity

unless a specific event actually changes those facts.

# 12. Body Accessibility and Civilisation Equality

Settlement content must support relevant body plans.

Compatibility concerns may include:
- doorway/ceiling size;
- furniture;
- water access;
- climbing;
- ramps/lifts;
- gliding/flight safety;
- pressure/air requirements;

A settlement that includes Tidekin residents should solve aquatic/amphibious access where needed rather than silently treating them as decorative visitors.

# 15. Realm-Linked Culture Safeguard

Several cultures preserve genuine realm-contact history, but their existence does not create portal access or export realm physics.

- Verdant Pact Enclaves do not create Covenant Portals merely by settlement identity.
- Veilkeeper Houses do not turn graves into Veilgates.
- Dreamweaver Cantons do not make sleep physical Dreamgate travel.
- Cloudward Freeholds do not provide Ascendant entry by flying upward.
- Ashbound Contract Cities do not make every agreement magically binding.
- Engine-Civic Assemblies may exist through Overworld construct/machine civilisation without requiring the deferred World-Engine realm.

Realm-linked identity requires legitimate history/provenance where a specific realm is claimed.

- mixed settlement;
- intermarriage;
- trade;
- migration;
- conquest;
- occupation;
- common disaster;
- frontier coexistence;

- time;
- shared institutions;
- common practices;
- language/material blending;
- historical continuity;
- distinct identity

to be more than a temporary demographic mixture.

# 19. Language, Scripts and Translation

Language is separate from ancestry and culture.

A person may know:
- household language;
- regional language;
- trade creole;
- ritual register;
- technical register;
- signed language;
- written script.

Language identity/competency belongs to civilisation content.

Actual comprehension, mistranslation and information confidence belong to the social/communication system.

Magic may provide translation only through real magic capability.

A Goblin is not born knowing a “Goblin language” merely because of ancestry.

# 23. Belief, Membership and Objective Truth

Keep separate:
- formal religious membership;
- personal belief;
- institutional claim;
- objectively verified supernatural fact.

A person may:
- formally belong but privately doubt;
- believe without formal membership;
- follow multiple traditions;
- be secular;
- change faith.

The existence of gods, spirits, ancestors or magic does **not** mean every doctrine about them is true.

Contradictory theologies can coexist as legitimate in-world beliefs even where developer canon knows more.

# 25. Government

Government describes formal authority and decision structure.

Government is independent from:
- culture;
- ancestry;
- settlement archetype;
- faction identity.

One culture may use several governments.

One government may rule several cultures.

A settlement may contain municipal, regional, customary and faction authorities simultaneously.

---

| # | Government Profile | Authority Base | Decision Form | Typical Pressure Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 3 | Constitutional Monarchy | Crown plus chartered institutions | Monarch within written law; ministers, courts and councils | Succession disputes, royal overreach, unequal charters |
| 4 | Central Monarchy | Dynasty, conquest, sacred legitimacy or inherited office | Royal decrees through appointed officials | Succession, corruption, distance, rebellion |
| 5 | Guild Oligarchy | Recognised craft, trade or professional guilds | Weighted guild council and negotiated charters | Exclusion, monopoly, labour conflict, guild rivalry |
| 6 | Clan Confederacy | Households, clans, bands or regional communities | Seasonal moot, delegates and oath network | Fragmentation, feud, uneven obligations |
| 7 | Civic Republic | Citizenship, districts, elections and public office | Elected councils, magistrates and courts | Faction capture, disenfranchisement, corruption |
| 8 | Plural Temple Compact | Recognised faith communities plus civil delegates | Interfaith council with secular administration | Doctrinal conflict, minority access, sacred-law disputes |
| 9 | Frontier March | Military charter, crown grant or settlement compact | Warden with civilian council and emergency authority | Militarisation, emergency abuse, settler/native conflict |
| 10 | Cooperative Federation | Workplaces, communes, districts or member settlements | Delegated federation with recall and shared services | Coordination burden, local inequality, secession |
| 11 | Machine Consensus | Recognised persons linked by verified civic protocols | Auditable proposals, deliberation cycles, consensus thresholds | Protocol manipulation, access inequality, maintenance dependence |
| 12 | Ancestor Mandate | Living custodians plus recognised ancestral/spirit witnesses | Ritual consultation interpreted through councils and law | Interpretive monopoly, false claims, past/present conflict |

| 14 | Customary Network | Households, routes, reciprocity and precedent | Distributed negotiation without one permanent centre | Ambiguity, enforcement gaps, conflict between customs |

Profiles are reusable and can be:
- combined;
- constitutionally modified;
- layered at different scales;
- replaced by political events.

They are not moral alignments.

# 28. Factions

A faction is an organised group with:
- membership;
- goals;
- leadership;
- assets;
- resources;
- methods;
- relationships;
- internal politics;
- history.

A faction is not:
- an ancestry;
- a culture;
- necessarily a government;
- necessarily hostile.

A government may be operated through factions, but the identities remain separate.

# 39. Personhood Under Oppression

# 40. Contract Versus Law

A contract is an accepted agreement between valid parties under the universal contract framework.

Law is jurisdictional political authority.

They can interact but are not the same.

A law may:
- recognise contracts;
- regulate them;
- void certain terms;
- punish fraud;
- define courts.

A contract requires actual acceptance.

Coercion, fraud and fabricated consent remain distinct.

An Ashbound culture does not make every conversation magically contractual.

# 43. Sovereignty, Recognition and Legitimacy

# 47. Portal Authority and Route Sovereignty

- the destination realm;
- destination settlements;
- all cross-realm trade;
- the realm's political institutions.

This rule applies to all six current canonical realm portals.
