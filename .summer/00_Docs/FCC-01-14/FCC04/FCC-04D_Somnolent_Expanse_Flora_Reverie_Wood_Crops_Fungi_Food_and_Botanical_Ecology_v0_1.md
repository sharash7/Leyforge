# 1. Purpose

FCC-04D defines the authoritative **botanical, fungal, agricultural and plant-food ecology** of the Somnolent Expanse.

It owns realm-local canon for:

- the exact twelve named flora/fungal foundations;
- the five canonical cultivated crop foundations;
- the principal native timber organism, **Reverie Tree**;
- biological provenance of **Reverie Wood**;
- **Sleepbloom** as both a living plant and a principal realm resource;
- **Emotion Bloom** state ecology;
- **Nightmare Thorn** and the biological source of Nightmare Sap;
- **Dream Reed** wetland/fibre ecology;
- **Omen Grass** and **Time-Seed** botanical provenance;
- **Waking Ivy** mixed-reality ecology;
- **Drowsecap Fungus** as the principal named edible/medicinal fungus;
- **Mirrorleaf**, **Hush Moss** and **Deepbell** ecological roles;
- **Dreamfruit Vine** and Dreamfruit provenance;
- Sleepgrain, Lucid Root, Reverie Bean and Calmleaf crop biology;
- native food foundations and ordinary survival-food compatibility;
- cultivation, propagation, harvest, restoration and ecological consequence;
- biome suitability and coherence-state interaction;
- export and off-realm cultivation boundaries;
- botanical interfaces consumed by creatures, settlements, structures, recipes, events and corruption systems.

FCC-04D does **not** own:

- final material definitions after harvest or processing - FCC-04C;
- creature definitions, pollinator behaviour, grazing, predation or animal-derived materials - FCC-04E;
- civilisation-specific farming industries, property law, consent law or settlement food policy - FCC-04F;
- final Blocks, Items, exact growth-stage assets, recipe quantities, food recipes or processing-machine definitions - FCC-04G;
- dungeon-specific botanical encounter execution - FCC-04H;
- detailed nightmare propagation, Void-corruption transformations or restoration state machines - FCC-04I;
- final stable-ID normalisation and art/material inheritance - FCC-04J, FCC-12, FCC-13 and FCC-14.

The goal is to make Somnolent botanical content **complete but bounded**: strange enough to express dream ecology, yet structured enough to world-generate, farm, trade, restore and simulate deterministically.

# 2. Botanical Canon Statement

The Somnolent Expanse is not a realm where every passing thought generates a new permanent flower species.

Its flora is persistent native ecology shaped by:

- coherence;
- Dreamwater and Dream Soil;
- long-lived dream geography;
- cultivation;
- settlement reinforcement;
- expectation-responsive environmental contracts;
- prophecy exposure;
- natural nightmare pressure;
- waking-law intrusion;
- regional events;
- rare Void corruption.

The realm therefore distinguishes between:

1. **canonical gameplay-bearing flora/fungi**, which receive persistent named identities and defined ecological/gameplay roles;
2. **canonical cultivated crop families**, which support agriculture and food economies;
3. **ambient ecological vegetation**, which may provide visual biodiversity without each receiving a separate registry identity;
4. **runtime botanical states**, which alter appearance, yield, behaviour or hazard response without multiplying species definitions.

The governing rule is:

> **Somnolent botanical variety may be visually and symbolically broad, while the gameplay-bearing species roster remains bounded, persistent and mechanically justified.**

A new named plant or fungus should not be added merely because another dream metaphor could be represented botanically.

# 4. Botanical Naming and Registry Rule

Somnolent flora follows the universal Leyforge naming direction:

- one canonical player-facing name per gameplay identity;
- predictable derived names;
- states do not become separate species unless biology and gameplay genuinely diverge;
- cultural aliases may exist in dialogue without creating duplicate registry entries.

Examples:

- Reverie Tree -> Reverie Log / Reverie Wood -> Reverie Planks / Beam where admitted by FCC-04G;
- Nightmare Thorn -> Nightmare Sap -> Nightmare Resin under FCC-04C/G;
- Dreamfruit Vine -> Dreamfruit;
- Omen Grass -> Omen Dust and rare Time-Seed provenance where valid;
- Emotion Bloom -> emotional state profile, not one species per emotion.

No system should register Joy Bloom, Grief Bloom, Fear Bloom, Wonder Bloom and similar emotional states as independent species by default.

# 5. Reverie Tree

## 5.1 Canonical Identity

The **Reverie Tree** is the principal native timber-bearing tree family of the Somnolent Expanse.

It is especially associated with coherent cultivated dream landscapes and long-lived settlements, but it may also occur in natural stable regions where Dream Soil, Dreamwater availability and local coherence permit persistent woody growth.

The Reverie Tree is characterised by:

- persistent woody anatomy rather than temporary illusion;
- strong compatibility with cultivated dream landscapes;
- responsiveness to long-term cultural and environmental reinforcement;
- timber suitable for ordinary construction once harvested and processed;
- moderate off-realm stability concerns for highly active living specimens;
- the potential to express bounded symbolic or coherence-linked visual states without becoming separate tree species.

## 5.2 Principal Habitat

Preferred habitat includes:

- **Reverie Gardens**;
- stable **Lucid Shores** islands;
- selected stable **Memory City** courtyards and reconstructed green districts;
- maintained **Sleepwalker Road** settlements;
- compatible **Shattered Waking Isles** where Dream Soil remains viable.

It is not a defining native tree of highly unstable Deep Dream zones or severe Nightmare Front conditions unless a local state explicitly supports it.

## 5.3 Material Provenance

Reverie Tree is the biological source of **Reverie Wood**, whose final material identity is owned by FCC-04C.

Potential harvested outputs include:

- Reverie Log / wood-form Block identity;
- bark;
- branches;
- leaves;
- viable seeds or propagation material;
- ordinary timber derivatives after processing;
- minor botanical reagents where an exact FCC-04G recipe later requires them.

The tree does not automatically produce Dream Motes, Prophecy Ink, Memory Thread or Lucid Glass merely because it grows in a dream realm.

## 5.4 Cultivation

Reverie Trees may be cultivated through ordinary planting systems adapted to:

- Dream Soil fertility;
- Dreamwater availability;
- local coherence;
- biome suitability;
- settlement or garden maintenance;
- sufficient growth space.

Expectation or emotion may influence supported tree **states**, but cannot instantly grow arbitrary mature trees from freeform thought.

## 5.5 Harvest Consequences

Forestry must respect universal resource-conservation and settlement-ecology rules.

Possible consequences of destructive overharvest include:

- reduced local canopy;
- poorer soil stability;
- lower habitat quality;
- diminished settlement aesthetics or morale interfaces;
- reduced Muse Moth / grazer suitability where later ecology defines such relationships;
- slower local botanical recovery.

These are ecological consequences, not moral punishment systems.

# 8. Nightmare Thorn

## 8.1 Canonical Identity

**Nightmare Thorn** is a thorny woody/shrub organism adapted to natural nightmare ecology.

It is not inherently Void-corrupted and is not evidence of moral evil.

Its ecology is associated with:

- Nightmare Thickets;
- natural fear-responsive regions;
- Nightmare Front states;
- selected managed Nightmare Wards or protected nightmare settlements.

## 8.2 Nightmare Sap

Nightmare Thorn is the canonical botanical source of **Nightmare Sap**.

Nightmare Sap may then harden or be processed into **Nightmare Resin** under FCC-04C/G.

This establishes the chain:

**Nightmare Thorn -> Nightmare Sap -> Nightmare Resin**

Nightmare Resin therefore does not require a separate resin-mineral deposit or generic nightmare-monster drop.

## 8.3 Fear Relationship

Nightmare Thorn may respond to sustained regional fear pressure through bounded states such as:

- increased thorn density;
- accelerated sap production;
- defensive growth;
- reduced palatability;
- altered flowering/seed production.

The plant cannot read arbitrary private thoughts.

## 8.4 Cultivation

Cultivation is possible where a settlement or research site can safely maintain the required nightmare ecology.

Possible management purposes include:

- defensive hedges;
- controlled sap production;
- nightmare research;
- habitat restoration;
- culturally significant gardens.

Cultivating Nightmare Thorn does not automatically create or spread Void corruption.

## 9.2 Habitat

Dream Reed prefers:

- Dreamwater shallows;
- stable Lucid Shores;
- protected settlement wetlands;
- calm channels near route infrastructure;
- compatible Shattered Waking Isle wetlands.

## 9.3 Memory Thread Boundary

Accepted canon requires **Memory Thread** to use existing canonical ecology/cultivation rather than inventing a thirteenth plant solely for provenance.

However, the accepted source decisions do **not** lock Dream Reed, or any other single FCC-04D species, as the exclusive biological source of Memory Thread.

Therefore FCC-04D locks the following boundary:

- no new plant species may be introduced solely to source Memory Thread;
- Dream Reed and other existing canonical cultivation interfaces may participate where later provenance/recipe validation supports them;
- exact final source mapping must be completed without expanding the twelve-species roster and without contradicting FCC-04C/E/G.

This is an explicit preserved boundary, not permission to invent an unreviewed fibre species.

Entering the Somnolent Expanse does not replace ordinary player survival systems.

Somnolent food may satisfy ordinary food needs while also contributing bounded realm-specific effects.

There is **no separate dream-hunger meter**.

Named plant/fungal food support includes:

- Dreamfruit;

- Lucid Root;
- Reverie Bean;

# 24. Memory Thread Botanical Boundary

**Memory Thread** is a canonical principal realm material under FCC-04C.

The accepted Somnolent source decisions establish that its provenance must come from **existing canonical ecology/cultivation** and must not cause roster inflation.

- FCC-04J must reject any orphan Memory Thread definition lacking a valid source.

This preserves completeness without silently adding unsupported biology.

# 25. Botanical Coherence Interaction

Plants do not all react identically to coherence.

## 25.1 Anchored / Stable

May support:

- ordinary persistent agriculture;
- mature Reverie Trees;
- reliable seed cycles;
- predictable crop yields;
- settlement horticulture.

## 25.2 Mutable

May support:

- bounded crop/flower state variation;
- more visible symbolic traits;
- unusual but persistent cultivation conditions;
- expectation-responsive species states where explicitly defined.

## 25.3 Unstable / Fragmenting

May produce:

- growth interruption;
- reduced harvest reliability;
- state shifts;
- plant dormancy;
- habitat retreat;
- specialist Deepbell activity;
- increasing need for stabilisation.

## 25.4 Dissolving

Long-term ordinary agriculture is generally unsuitable without exceptional stabilisation.

Dissolving does not mean Void corruption.
