# 1. Purpose

FCC-04E defines the authoritative **creature and dream-ecology canon** of the Somnolent Expanse.

It owns realm-local canon for:

- the exact twelve named gameplay-bearing creature families;
- the five original Set 24 creature foundations and their FCC reconciliations;
- the seven FCC additions required to complete the realm's ecology;
- peaceful fauna, herd ecology, pollinators, route animals, predators, aquatic fauna, mixed-reality fauna and Deep Dream anomaly fauna;
- the difference between a temporary thoughtform and a canonical creature;
- the boundary between creature classification and personhood;
- native nightmare ecology versus Void corruption;
- Fearling aggregation states;
- Fear Chitin provenance through Nightmare Stalker anatomy;
- sustainable and destructive creature-material acquisition paths;
- taming, husbandry, aquaculture, managed populations and protected-species relationships;
- Waking Devourer as both a canonical creature family and a source of exceptional authority-capable individuals;
- Dreamgate hitchhiker ecology and bounded cross-realm leakage;
- biome suitability, migration and event interfaces;
- authoritative persistence and low-end simulation expectations;
- creature-side interfaces consumed by settlements, dungeons, authorities, resources, recipes and corruption systems.

FCC-04E does **not** own:

- final plant definitions or crop biology - FCC-04D;
- final material processing definitions or material-family ownership - FCC-04C/G;
- civilisation personhood law, husbandry law, protected-species policy or settlement industries - FCC-04F;
- exact creature drops, food recipes, equipment recipes, spawn-table numbers, taming costs or entity-component implementation - FCC-04G and implementation/balance systems;
- dungeon guardian families, dungeon-specific encounter scripts or authority-resolution structures - FCC-04H;
- detailed Void-corruption state machines, nightmare restoration systems or cross-realm ecology campaigns - FCC-04I;
- final stable-ID namespace normalisation and art handoff - FCC-04J, FCC-12, FCC-13 and FCC-14.

The goal is to make Somnolent creature content **complete, ecologically connected and bounded**, without allowing every symbolic manifestation or passing dream image to become permanent registry content.

# 2. Creature Canon Statement

The Somnolent Expanse contains persistent native fauna and creature-like entities whose biology, dream-state ecology and behaviour are shaped by the realm's laws.

Its creature life is influenced by:

- coherence;
- Lucidity-sensitive interaction;
- Dreamwater and Dream Soil ecology;
- persistent dream geography;
- natural nightmare pressure;
- collective emotional conditions;
- prophecy-responsive environmental states;
- Sleepwalker Road stability;
- waking-law fractures;
- Deep Dream concept states;
- civilisation and husbandry;
- rare Void corruption.

The realm does **not** interpret every temporary dream image as a species.

The governing rule is:

> **A Somnolent entity becomes a canonical creature family only when it possesses sufficiently persistent gameplay identity, repeatable behaviour, ecological or encounter function, and world-state persistence to justify registry ownership.**

A one-off symbolic figure may remain:

- ambient dream manifestation;
- encounter dressing;
- scripted illusion;
- dungeon role;
- prophecy image;
- temporary thoughtform;
- local environmental effect.

It does not require a permanent creature definition unless later content governance explicitly promotes it.

# 5. Creature Naming and Registry Rule

Somnolent creature naming follows universal Leyforge canon rules:

- one canonical player-facing name per gameplay identity;
- state names do not become new species by default;
- boss-capable individuals do not automatically duplicate their creature family;
- corruption begins as a state/variant before a new creature definition is considered;
- juvenile, seasonal, emotional, coherence and regional states do not automatically become separate creature entries;
- cultural aliases may exist without duplicating the registry.

Examples:

- Fearling Swarm / Aggregate -> state of Fearling ecology, not automatically a new creature family;
- authority-scale Waking Devourer -> exceptional individual of Waking Devourer, not a duplicated species;
- Void-Corrupted Nightmare Stalker -> corruption state unless irreversible transformation creates genuinely new anatomy and gameplay identity;
- protected Lucid Manta migration population -> population state, not a new species.

# 7. Thoughtform Versus Creature Boundary

**Thoughtform** is a broad Somnolent phenomenon category.

A thoughtform does not become a canonical creature merely because it:

- moves;
- speaks once;
- appears dangerous;
- resembles an animal;
- occurs in a prophecy;
- manifests in a nightmare;
- accompanies a dungeon scene.

Promotion to a canonical creature definition requires sufficient persistence such as:

- repeatable behavioural identity;
- persistent or reproducible anatomy/presentation;
- ecological relationships;
- lifecycle or population behaviour;
- settlement or faction relationship;
- creature-material provenance;
- durable encounter identity;
- meaningful worldgen distribution.

This rule prevents unrestricted dream symbolism from causing registry explosion.

---

# 8. Creature Classification Versus Personhood

Creature classification does **not** decide personhood.

A being's body, origin or dream/nightmare nature does not automatically make it:

- non-person;
- property;
- livestock;
- a monster;
- harvestable;
- kill-on-sight.

Durable identity, moral agency, communication, culture, social continuity and other universal personhood criteria remain authoritative.

The twelve FCC-04E ecology foundations default to creature/ecology treatment unless a specific individual or population later meets personhood criteria.

Sapient dream beings, intelligent nightmare societies and durable constructed/thoughtform persons belong under the civilisation/personhood framework in FCC-04F and universal governance.

The binding rule is:

> **Origin from dream, nightmare, collective fear or symbolic cognition does not remove personhood.**

# 9. Lucid Manta

## 9.1 Canonical Identity

The **Lucid Manta** is a large peaceful ray-like dream creature associated with Dreamwater, open dream-space and stable navigational currents.

It preserves the Set 24 role of a **large peaceful dream navigator**.

## 9.2 Behaviour

Lucid Mantas:

- travel predictable currents under healthy realm conditions;
- undertake visible migrations;
- react to coherence and route stability;
- can provide indirect navigation clues;
- are not ordinary hostile predators;
- may avoid severely unstable or corrupted routes.

The older source phrase that they provide safe passage "while lucidity is maintained" is retained as a relationship between traveller clarity, route interpretation and Manta-following reliability rather than as a literal magical escort guarantee.

## 9.3 Principal Habitat

Primary suitability:

- Lucid Shores;
- broad Dreamwater bodies;
- stable route-adjacent open dream regions;
- selected coherent spaces between major islands.

They are not ordinary residents of severe Nightmare Thickets or Deep Dream Abyss conditions.

## 9.4 Civilisation Relationship

Potential relationships include:

- protected migration corridors;
- navigation observation;
- Dream Port culture;
- Dream Walker route study;
- seasonal or event-based tourism/ritual significance;
- non-destructive collection of naturally shed material where later recipes justify it.

No progression-critical material currently requires killing Lucid Mantas.

## 9.5 Event Interface

The canonical **Manta Migration** event may alter:

- route confidence;
- navigation clues;
- settlement activity;
- Dream Port traffic;
- Lucid Shore ecology.

# 10. Fearling

## 10.1 Canonical Identity

The **Fearling** is a small emotion-feeding nuisance/scavenger native to Somnolent nightmare ecology.

It preserves the Set 24 role of a small creature that grows around repeated fear.

## 10.2 Feeding / Pressure Rule

Fearlings feed primarily on **sustained fear resonance already present in the environment**.

They do not create fear from nothing solely to justify their own existence.

Their presence can nevertheless create a feedback loop:

1. sustained fear raises Fearling suitability;
2. Fearling density increases nuisance/pressure;
3. increased pressure may make frightened populations reinforce the local fear state;
4. management, calm, route changes, ecological predators or narrative resolution can break the loop.

## 10.3 Aggregation

Large concentrations may enter bounded:

- swarm states;
- aggregate states;
- encounter-scale collective forms.

These remain **Fearling states** unless an exceptional permanent transformation later proves enough distinct anatomy, behaviour and ecology to warrant a new definition.

## 10.4 Principal Habitat

Primary suitability:

- Nightmare Thickets;
- Nightmare Nests;
- fear-heavy settlements or events;
- unstable route regions under Nightmare Pressure;
- bounded Dreamgate hitchhiker events.

## 10.5 Ecological Relationship

Fearlings may serve as prey or pressure-food for nightmare predators including **Nightmare Stalkers**.

They are not automatically Void-corrupted.

## 10.6 Event Interface

The canonical **Fearling Bloom** event is a population-pressure surge tied to sustained fear conditions.

It is not proof of corruption.

# 11. Pillow Moss Beast

## 11.1 Canonical Identity

The **Pillow Moss Beast** is a heavy, slow dream grazer and defensive ambusher whose resting body resembles soft mossy terrain.

It preserves the Set 24 "dormant ambush grazer" identity while removing reliance on an off-realm sleeping body.

## 11.2 Feeding

Its ecology may include:

- dream vegetation;
- Sleepbloom residue;
- ambient drowsiness/coherence energy compatible with its habitat.

It does **not** feed on a player's sleeping body in another realm.

## 11.3 Behaviour

A resting Pillow Moss Beast may remain motionless and terrain-like until:

- disturbed;
- threatened;
- hungry;
- defending young;
- local habitat stability fails.

Its ambush behaviour is therefore primarily defensive/ecological rather than evidence of inherent malice.

## 11.4 Habitat

Suitable environments may include:

- coherent garden margins;
- Sleepbloom-rich regions;
- calm Dream Soil zones;
- selected Lucid Shore islands;
- protected dream pastures.

Extreme low-coherence or waking-law fractures reduce ordinary suitability unless a specific regional state supports survival.

## 11.5 Material / Food Boundary

FCC-04E does not create a mandatory named Pillow Moss Beast material.

Creature-derived food or minor products may be admitted later only where FCC-04G provides a justified recipe and legal/ecological acquisition route.

# 13. Waking Devourer

## 13.1 Canonical Identity

The **Waking Devourer** is a large apex boundary predator attracted to failing interfaces between coherent physical law and unstable dream matter.

It preserves the Set 24 apex-predator and regional-boss ecology foundation while removing the obsolete "unstable sleeper" target premise.

## 13.2 Target Ecology

Waking Devourers may be attracted to:

- destabilised dream fauna;
- exposed coherence anchors;
- broken route edges;
- unstable Dreamgate infrastructure;
- severe Waking Fractures;
- travellers suffering severe Anchor instability;
- regions where dream/waking-law boundaries are failing.

They do **not** hunt an off-realm sleeping body because the player physically enters Somnolent through the Dreamgate.

## 13.3 Principal Habitat

Primary suitability:

- Shattered Waking Isles;
- severe Waking Fracture zones;
- unstable Dreamgate approaches;
- boundary-collapse events.

Exceptional individuals may move far outside normal range during major realm events.

## 13.4 Creature / Authority Single-Definition Rule

Waking Devourer exists **once** as a canonical creature family.

Exceptional ancient, powerful or region-dominant individuals may be promoted into Realm Authority / boss roles under FCC-04H without creating a second species definition.

The creature registry owns biological identity.

The authority system owns:

- exceptional encounter state;
- territory-scale consequences;
- boss resolution;
- aftermath.

## 13.5 Authority Outcomes

Potential authority-scale resolutions include:

- restoring damaged boundaries;
- redirecting the creature toward naturally unstable regions;
- stabilising or safely feeding it where canonically supported;
- driving it away;
- containing it;
- tracking/bonding where a specific narrative supports it;
- killing it.

No outcome may create a permanent non-Dreamgate realm-access bypass.

# 17. Nightmare Stalker

## 17.1 Canonical Identity

The **Nightmare Stalker** is a medium-to-large chitin-armoured predator native to Nightmare Thickets.

It exists as natural nightmare ecology, not as a Void-corrupted creature by default.

## 17.2 Feeding / Predator Role

Nightmare Stalkers may prey on:

- Fearlings;
- weakened dream fauna;
- concentrated nightmare ecology;
- vulnerable prey in suitable thicket regions.

This makes them a pressure-regulating predator rather than merely a combat encounter.

## 17.3 Fear Chitin Provenance

Nightmare Stalker is the approved principal biological source of **Fear Chitin**.

Valid acquisition may include:

- naturally shed plates;
- moults;
- abandoned nests;
- scavenged remains;
- predator kills;
- lawful/ethical hunting;
- trade;
- husbandry/research sources if later civilisation systems justify them.

Killing a Nightmare Stalker is therefore **not** the only canonical route to Fear Chitin.

## 17.4 Material Boundary

Fear Chitin is anatomy-dependent.

It is **not** a generic drop from:

- Fearlings;
- Nightmare Thorn;
- nightmare societies;
- every creature in a Nightmare Thicket;
- Void-corrupted entities generally.

## 17.5 Principal Habitat

Primary suitability:

- Nightmare Thickets;
- Nightmare Nest surroundings;
- fear-dense natural territories;
- some Warden-managed preserves or containment regions.

# 20. Concept Leech

## 20.1 Canonical Identity

The **Concept Leech** is a strange Deep Dream organism that attaches to unstable symbolic structures, entities or bounded environmental states and consumes an active **concept-state**.

It is the principal canonical concept predator of the Deep Dream Abyss.

## 20.2 Concept Suppression Mechanic

Concept Leech effects may temporarily suppress specific authored properties such as:

- door functionality;
- route-marker recognition;
- local light state;
- hostility marker presentation;
- map-symbol recognition;
- a specific dream-law interaction;
- selected machine or structure interpretation where explicitly authored.

## 20.3 Absolute Safety Boundary

Concept Leeches may **not**:

- delete arbitrary game definitions;
- delete items from the registry;
- erase player save data;
- remove permanent player knowledge records;
- corrupt file data;
- rewrite arbitrary scripts;
- freely redefine any concept the player can name.

Their mechanics operate through bounded predefined property/state interfaces.

## 20.4 Principal Habitat

Primary suitability:

- Deep Dream Abyss;
- severe low-coherence regions;
- Concept Suppression hazard zones;
- rare high-tier dungeon states.

They are not ordinary surface fauna.
