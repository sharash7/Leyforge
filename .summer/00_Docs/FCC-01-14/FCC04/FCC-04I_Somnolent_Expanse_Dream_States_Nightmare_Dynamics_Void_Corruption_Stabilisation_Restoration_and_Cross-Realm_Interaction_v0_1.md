# 2. State Canon Statement

The Somnolent Expanse is deliberately mutable, but it is not arbitrary.

A location may be frightening without being corrupted.  
A location may be low-coherence without being damaged.  
A location may be waking-fractured without being Void-touched.  
A location may be influenced by the Deep Dream without becoming the Void Between.  
A nightmare ecosystem may be healthy.  
A highly coherent district may still be morally dangerous or politically oppressive.  
A Void-corrupted site may initially look deceptively calm.

The primary state rule is:

> **No single “dream instability” value is allowed to collapse Somnolent's different state families into one generic good/bad meter.**

The following remain separate authoritative concepts:

1. **Coherence**
2. **Lucidity**
3. **Waking Anchor stability**
4. **Expectation-responsive state**
5. **Nightmare pressure**
6. **Memory overlay/reconstruction**
7. **Prophecy state**
8. **Waking Fracture**
9. **Deep-Dream influence**
10. **Void corruption**
11. **restoration/stabilisation progress**
12. **regional event/authority influence**

These may interact, but one does not automatically imply another.

# 4. Coherence States

The six canonical coherence states remain exactly:

1. **Anchored**
2. **Stable**
3. **Mutable**
4. **Unstable**
5. **Fragmenting**
6. **Dissolving**

FCC-04I owns **transition governance**, not the names.

## 4.1 Anchored

Anchored regions have exceptionally strong persistence.

They may support:

- Dreamgate infrastructure;
- major settlements;
- conventional machinery;
- long-lived archives;
- fixed-route references;
- protected storage.

Anchored does not mean immutable.

## 4.2 Stable

Stable regions are reliably persistent while still behaving as ordinary Somnolent terrain.

Stable is expected to be common around healthy settlements and Lucid Shores.

## 4.3 Mutable

Mutable regions permit stronger bounded dream-state changes without losing reliable identity.

Mutable is not inherently unsafe.

## 4.4 Unstable

Unstable regions have significant route, structural, symbolic or environmental reliability problems.

They may require active management.

## 4.5 Fragmenting

Fragmenting regions are losing continuity between supported states.

Structures, routes, ecology and interaction reliability may degrade.

## 4.6 Dissolving

Dissolving regions approach loss of persistent local form.

Dissolving is a serious state but still does not automatically mean Void corruption.

# 7. Lucidity and World-State Separation

Lucidity is primarily a traveller/player interpretation and resistance system.

Low Lucidity may:

- reduce route confidence;
- increase false presentation;
- make supported symbols harder to interpret;
- increase susceptibility to nightmare influence;
- make prophecy presentation harder to classify.

Low Lucidity does **not** automatically alter the authoritative world state for everyone.

Where a Lucidity-dependent interaction changes world state, the change must be an explicit validated action.

This prevents one player's perception meter from silently rewriting shared multiplayer reality.

# 8. Waking Anchor and World-State Separation

The Waking Anchor / Reality Anchor is the player's internal stable-reference relationship **inside** Somnolent.

Anchor Strain may increase:

- route instability experienced by the traveller;
- false-exit susceptibility;
- difficulty using certain stable interfaces;
- nightmare vulnerability.

It must never create:

- a second player body;
- an off-realm sleeping player actor;
- duplicate inventory;
- automatic physical awakening elsewhere;
- save deletion.

Restoring Waking Anchor stability is a traveller-state process, not a realm-exit method.

- route loops;
- Nightmare Nest activity;

# 12. Healthy Nightmare State

A nightmare region can be considered **healthy or managed** when:

- ecology remains viable;
- territorial boundaries are functioning;
- pressure is not uncontrollably expanding;
- persons are not being treated as mere manifestations;
- settlements/routes have workable agreements or defences;
- predator/prey relationships are sustainable;
- no genuine Void contamination is present;
- local hazards match the intended ecosystem.

Healthy Nightmare Thickets do not need conversion into Reverie Gardens.

A Nightmare Nest can be stabilised without being destroyed.

# 20. Void Corruption — Realm-Local Definition

Within Somnolent, **Void Corruption** means genuine external contamination whose immediate cosmological source is the **Void Between**.

Somnolent FCC does not own the deeper endgame explanation for why the Void itself became corrupted.

A Somnolent site is not Void-corrupted because it is:

- frightening;
- low-coherence;
- dissolving;
- nightmare-dominated;
- Deep-Dream;
- memory-conflicted;
- prophecy-heavy;
- waking-fractured.

Void provenance must be explicit in authoritative state.

---

# 21. Void Corruption Entry Vectors

Potential realm-local Void-entry vectors include:

- unstable or damaged Dreamgates;
- cross-realm Void incursions;
- contaminated imported material/person/creature where global systems permit;
- existing Void-corrupted structures;
- damaged boundary infrastructure;
- authority/event interactions explicitly linked to Void state;
- Deep-Dream or Waking-Fracture regions **only when an actual Void contact/interface exists**.

Low coherence alone is not an entry vector without causal Void contact.

Exact runtime stages and numeric thresholds remain global/system-owned.

The important canon rule is:

> **Void corruption begins as state/contamination wherever practical; it does not automatically replace every affected Block, creature or plant with a new registry identity.**

- contamination value;
- overlay;
- material-state change;
- behavioural state;
- propagation risk;
- hazard;
- structure fault;
- ecology pressure;
- visual profile;

- contaminated Lucid Glass remains Lucid Glass;

- a corrupted-state Nightmare Stalker remains the Nightmare Stalker family;
- contaminated Dreamwater remains Dreamwater unless later canon proves an irreversible fluid transformation.

# 24. Irreversible Transformation Threshold

A distinct transformed definition is permitted only when the change is sufficiently irreversible and creates meaningfully different:

- composition or anatomy;
- physical behaviour;
- propagation/lifecycle;
- processing pathway;
- recipe role;
- equipment/construction role;
- hazard profile;
- export behaviour;
- save/runtime meaning;
- art/material inheritance.

A different colour, glow, hostility state, corruption meter or emotional theme is insufficient.

This threshold applies to:

- materials;
- flora;
- creatures;
- structures;
- fluids;
- thoughtforms.

# 27. Void and Nightmare Interaction

Void corruption can affect natural nightmare ecology.

Possible results include:

- harmful amplification;
- loss of normal fear ecology;
- disrupted territorial behaviour;
- corrupted Nightmare Nest state;
- altered Fearling aggregation;
- Nightmare Architect response;
- Nightmare Warden intervention;
- nightmare-society evacuation or resistance.

But:

> **Void-corrupted nightmare is a corrupted state of nightmare ecology, not evidence that nightmare was always Void-derived.**

Restoration may aim to recover a healthy nightmare ecosystem rather than remove nightmare entirely.

---

# 28. Void and Coherence Interaction

Void corruption may reduce, distort or exploit coherence.

However:

- low coherence does not prove Void corruption;
- high coherence does not guarantee safety from Void corruption;
- a Void-corrupted site may remain structurally coherent;
- stabilising coherence does not automatically cleanse Void contamination.

Coherence stabilisation and Void cleansing are separate operations that can support one another.

---

# 29. Void and Waking Fracture Interaction

Void corruption may exploit Waking Fractures, but Waking Fracture remains independently valid.

A restoration project may therefore need to perform both:

- **boundary-law stabilisation**; and
- **Void cleansing/containment**.

Fixing only one may leave the other unresolved.

# 30. Void and Deep-Dream Interaction

The Deep Dream Abyss is not the Void Between.

A Deep Dream site becomes Void-corrupted only when explicit Void provenance exists.

Deep-Dream instability may:

# 31. Stabilisation, Cleansing and Restoration — Distinct Operations

FCC-04I distinguishes three major recovery concepts.

## 31.1 Stabilisation

Stops or reduces uncontrolled change.

Examples:

- anchoring a Fragmenting region;
- containing a Nightmare Front;
- stabilising a Waking Rupture;
- preventing contamination spread.

## 31.2 Cleansing

Removes or neutralises genuine Void contamination.

Cleansing applies only where Void corruption exists.

## 31.3 Restoration

Returns ecology, structures, routes, communities or systems to a viable intended/native condition.

Restoration may occur after stabilisation and/or cleansing.

A project may need one, two or all three.

---

# 32. Restoration Target Rule

Restoration must restore **appropriate identity**, not enforce one universal ideal.

Examples:

- Nightmare Thicket -> healthy Nightmare Thicket;
- Deep Dream expedition zone -> viable low-coherence Deep Dream state;
- Memory City district -> usable district while preserving legitimate contradictory reconstruction;
- Shattered Waking Isle -> controlled mixed-reality or repaired physical-law state;
- Reverie Garden -> functioning cultivated garden;
- Sleepwalker Road -> safe/reliable enough route state;
- Void-corrupted Lucid Shore -> cleansed and ecologically functional Lucid Shore.

“More coherent” is not always “more restored.”

# 43. Cross-Realm Ownership Rule

FCC-04I may define the **Somnolent side** of a cross-realm interface.

It may not redefine the receiving realm.

Therefore:

- FCC-04 defines what Somnolent sends, detects or requires;
- the receiving realm FCC defines what that interaction means on its side;
- final cross-realm reconciliation resolves conflicts after all realms are complete.

This is especially important for Verdant Covenant and Ancestral Veil, whose own FCC canon is already locked locally.

# 44. Verdant Covenant Interface

The canonical Somnolent–Verdant interface includes **Dreaming Root resonance**.

Permitted interactions include:

- Dreaming Root Depths resonating with Somnolent;
- Dreamroot/compatible Verdant materials participating in dream-related research or recipes where later global recipe documents justify them;
- Dreaming Bridges revealing Somnolent clues or portal knowledge;
- botanical/dream resonance events;
- ecological research.

The interface may not:

- bypass either realm's canonical portal;
- merge Verdant materials into Somnolent materials;
- treat Verdant `Dream Sap` terminology as Dream Motes, Dreamwater or Nightmare Resin;
- let Dreaming Roots become a second Somnolent travel method.

Verdant owns Verdant ecology and material state.  
Somnolent owns Somnolent dream-state response.
