# 1. Purpose

FCC-04A defines the authoritative high-level identity, dream-network topology, realm-law families, progression model, portal-access contract, player-state boundaries, canon ownership, supersession rules, and implementation-facing constraints for the **Somnolent Expanse**.

The Somnolent Expanse is a **complete persistent major realm network** with its own:

- eight major biome families;
- dream seas, coherence islands, stable routes, collective cities, nightmare regions, mixed-reality territories and deep-dream layers;
- ordinary terrain and specialist realm materials;
- native flora, fungi, crops and food ecology;
- native wildlife, nightmare fauna and concept-reactive organisms;
- permanent civilisations, migrant populations, visitor enclaves and dream-native peoples;
- Dream Walkers, Oracle Schools, Nightmare Wardens, Memory Traders and shared-dream cultures;
- settlements, route infrastructure, Dreamgate sanctuaries and coherence technology;
- dungeons, guardian families, realm authorities and emergent fear sovereigns;
- hazards, regional states, events and long-term world consequences;
- trade, construction, agriculture, crafting, research and cross-realm utility;
- bounded interactions with memory, expectation, fear, prophecy, coherence, lucidity and symbolic identity.

FCC-04A does **not** hold every individual asset definition. Later FCC-04 documents own the detailed canon for biomes, terrain, materials, flora, crops, creatures, civilisations, structures, blocks, items, recipes, dungeons, authorities, events, nightmare states, Void corruption, stabilisation, cross-realm interfaces and art handoff.

This document establishes the rules those later documents must obey.

# 2. Canon Statement

The **Somnolent Expanse** is a persistent Dream Network where **expectation, coherence, memory, fear, prophecy, symbolism and collective imagination can influence the physical state of the realm through bounded gameplay systems**.

Its central fantasy is:

> **A vast, self-sustaining dream realm where thought and symbolism can shape reality, but the player remains one authoritative person moving through one persistent world governed by deterministic, save-safe rules.**

The Somnolent Expanse is not merely a sequence of hallucinations, a temporary sleep minigame, or a private dream copy generated for each player. It contains enduring geography, societies, trade, ecology, infrastructure, politics, history and long-term world state.

The realm may contain:

- stable dream cities and ports;
- cultivated Reverie Gardens;
- dream-native peoples and long-established migrant cultures;
- prophetic institutions and competing Oracle Schools;
- nightmare ecologies and intelligent nightmare societies;
- Memory Cities built from overlapping remembered architecture;
- Dreamwater seas and route-dependent navigation;
- Sleepwalker Roads and caravan infrastructure;
- mixed-reality Shattered Waking Isles;
- Deep Dream Abyss regions containing primal symbolic life and ancient dream formations;
- dream fauna, concept predators, fear-feeding organisms and coherence-sensitive creatures;
- Dreamgates and destination-side sanctuaries;
- bounded personal and collective dream influence;
- conditional prophecy and possible-future phenomena;
- Void corruption that is explicitly distinct from natural nightmare content.

The realm exists independently of any one sleeper. Personal or collective psychology may influence bounded regions within it, but the Somnolent Expanse is not created anew whenever someone dreams.

# 3. Explicit Non-Identity: What the Somnolent Expanse Is Not

The Somnolent Expanse is **not**:

- a second character copy spawned while the player's real body sleeps elsewhere;
- a duplicated inventory or duplicated save state;
- a realm entered normally by sleeping, meditation, dreams, dream beds or psychic projection;
- a place exited normally by simply waking up;
- a freeform imagination engine where anything the player thinks or says becomes real;
- a natural-language parser that interprets arbitrary player thoughts as world-edit commands;
- an unrestricted reality-warp sandbox;
- an automatic truth machine whose prophecies are guaranteed;
- a system that can silently delete or rewrite authoritative player memories, Codex records, stable IDs or save data;
- a realm where dream time freely accelerates the entire game's settlement, crop, ageing or economy simulation;
- a realm where every one-off thoughtform becomes a permanent creature registry entry;
- a realm where every nightmare is evil;
- a realm where nightmare automatically means Void corruption;
- a second Ancestral Veil memory/identity simulation;
- a substitute for the Pocket Realm construction system;
- a hidden form of the Void Between;
- a portal bypass into other dimensions through Sleepwalker Roads or waking labyrinth exits.

These exclusions are canon, not implementation suggestions.

Older source directions that implied routine sleep-entry, waking-body vulnerability, multiple independent access families, body/sleeper bridging, or waking-based exit are superseded or reclassified by FCC-04.

# 5. Realm Fantasy Pillars

## 5.1 Dream as Persistent Geography

The Somnolent Expanse is a world, not a sequence of disconnected cutscenes.

Dream logic may affect:

- terrain state;
- route availability;
- architecture;
- navigation;
- local environmental rules;
- symbolic presentation;
- creature behaviour;
- settlement stability;
- dungeon state;
- prophecy visibility.

Those effects must still resolve through persistent, deterministic world state.

## 5.2 Coherence Makes Dreams Durable

Dream content ranges from strongly anchored and physically reliable to unstable and dissolving.

Long-term persistence can be reinforced by:

- repeated dreaming;
- collective cultural reinforcement;
- settlements;
- coherent architecture;
- Dreamstone and Waking Stone foundations;
- Dreamgate infrastructure;
- Coherence Cores;
- Lucid Beacons;
- powerful entities;
- route networks;
- long historical continuity;
- deliberate stabilisation.

The realm therefore supports both mutable dream environments and permanent cities without contradiction.

## 5.3 Expectation Shapes Bounded Possibility

Expectation matters, but only where a site, dungeon, creature or region supports defined expectation-responsive states.

The realm may ask the player to understand how belief, fear or assumption is influencing an authored situation. It must not attempt to infer unrestricted human thought or grant arbitrary world-edit power.

## 5.4 Nightmares Are Ecology, Not Moral Failure

Nightmares may arise from fear, warning, trauma, defence, cultural symbolism, predation, natural dream ecology or collective anxiety.

A nightmare can be:

- dangerous;
- territorial;
- intelligent;
- protective;
- culturally meaningful;
- ecologically necessary;
- politically organised;
- predatory;
- misunderstood.

Nightmare content is not inherently evil and is not automatically Void-corrupted.

## 5.5 Prophecy Is Conditional

Somnolent prophecy exposes possible futures, conditional outcomes, probability states and symbolic forecasts.

A prophecy may become less accurate or cease to apply when the player changes the conditions that produced it.

The realm supports foresight without creating immutable destiny or developer-spoiler machines.

## 5.6 Memory Is Interpretation

Memory Cities, Memory Theatre content and Memory Thread systems may preserve, reconstruct, stage or encode information.

A dream reconstruction is not automatically objective historical truth.

Contradiction, missing context, bias and interpretation are valid content states.

## 5.7 One Person, One Authoritative Character

A player entering the Somnolent Expanse remains the same authoritative player entity.

The realm may alter presentation, symbolic appearance, local abilities or dream-aspect overlays, but must not create a second persistent person whose inventory, progression or identity can diverge from the original.

# 6. Canonical Realm Laws

Somnolent realm laws are divided into bounded rule families. They are gameplay contracts, not unrestricted simulation.

## 6.1 Lucidity Law

**Lucidity** represents how strongly a traveller maintains conscious control, interpretation and self-direction while inside the realm.

Lucidity may affect:

- route clarity;
- resistance to expectation distortion;
- recognition of false exits;
- interpretation of symbolic environments;
- certain dungeon interactions;
- nightmare-pressure resistance;
- map reliability;
- selected social or magical interactions.

High Lucidity does not make the player immune to ordinary hazards, predators, politics or physical damage.

Low Lucidity may create temporary confusion, distortion or increased susceptibility to dream mechanics, but it may not silently corrupt authoritative save data.

Lucidity is a **realm-specific state**, not a replacement for health, mana, hunger or universal player survival.

## 6.2 Expectation Law

Expectation can influence reality only through **predefined expectation-responsive contracts**.

Valid examples include:

- a hidden door state becoming available after supported expectation conditions;
- a Nightmare Nest strengthening when a defined fear-response state repeats;
- a bridge or route changing between authored stability states;
- NPC groups collectively reinforcing one of several possible district states;
- a puzzle responding to repeated assumptions represented by explicit gameplay flags.

**Prohibited implementation:** parsing every spoken, typed or inferred player thought and converting it into world mutation.

Expectation systems must be:

- bounded;
- deterministic from saved inputs;
- debuggable;
- reproducible;
- reversible where the content contract requires it;
- explicit enough for player learning.

## 6.3 Coherence Law

**Coherence** controls how persistent, stable and physically reliable dream matter is.

The canonical high-level coherence states are:

1. **Anchored**
2. **Stable**
3. **Mutable**
4. **Unstable**
5. **Fragmenting**
6. **Dissolving**

These are regional/site/content states rather than continuous per-voxel metaphysical calculations.

Coherence may affect:

- terrain solidity;
- route reliability;
- structure persistence;
- Dreamwater behaviour;
- machinery compatibility;
- creature behaviour;
- settlement viability;
- Dreamgate stability;
- exportability of highly dream-dependent materials;
- dungeon state.

A lower coherence state does not authorise arbitrary loss of player-owned blocks or inventory without a specific supported mechanic and clear persistence rules.

## 6.4 Waking Anchor Law

Earlier source material used waking-body safety as a core risk. Current canon reinterprets that fantasy as **Waking Anchor / Reality Anchor stability** carried by the traveller inside the realm.

A traveller's anchor represents stable connection to:

- personal identity;
- waking-reality expectations;
- reliable route logic;
- Dreamgate return context;
- selected stabilisation effects.

Anchor strain may contribute to:

- false exits;
- route instability;
- reduced Lucidity;
- stronger nightmare pressure;
- symbolic identity distortion;
- difficulty navigating toward safe infrastructure.

The player does not have a second body lying elsewhere that must be simulated or defended.

## 6.5 Dream-Time Law

Dream-local time may diverge from universal world time through **bounded authored ratios, windows or site states**.

Allowed uses include:

- local event timers;
- dream-NPC schedules;
- prophecy windows;
- dungeon sequences;
- local ecological cycles;
- subjective presentation of long or short experiences.

Dream time must not directly multiply universal:

- crop growth;
- NPC ageing;
- settlement production;
- pregnancy/child growth;
- economy simulation;
- off-realm faction simulation;
- automation throughput;
- world-event cadence

unless the universal time system explicitly exposes a reconciled interface.

The governing rule is:

> **Somnolent may alter local experienced time; it does not silently accelerate the entire game.**

## 6.6 Dream-Aspect Law

A traveller may temporarily acquire a **dream aspect** represented as a presentation/state overlay on the same authoritative character.

A dream aspect may alter:

- symbolic appearance;
- selected local abilities;
- role assignment inside a Memory Theatre;
- temporary equipment presentation;
- social interpretation;
- fear or prophecy interactions;
- dream-specific permissions.

A dream aspect may not:

- create a second persistent player identity;
- duplicate inventory;
- create independent progression;
- permanently overwrite the player's underlying character record unless a separately approved universal transformation system explicitly supports it.

## 6.7 Prophecy Law

Prophecy in the Somnolent Expanse represents **possible, conditional or high-probability futures**.

A prophecy may be:

- accurate;
- incomplete;
- symbolic;
- manipulated;
- outdated;
- conditional;
- probabilistically unlikely;
- misinterpreted;
- deliberately falsified by a faction or machine.

Learning a prophecy may change the future because the player or factions act upon it.

No Somnolent system guarantees one immutable fate unless a specific authored story intentionally does so and remains compatible with player agency.

## 6.8 Memory Presentation Law

Somnolent memory systems may encode, stage, replay or reconstruct information through:

- Memory Cities;
- Memory Theatre content;
- Memory Thread;
- symbolic environments;
- records;
- consensual memory products;
- historical dream residue.

They must not automatically read, copy or sell every private NPC memory.

Player-owned Codex knowledge, discovered information and save records may be temporarily obscured in presentation where a supported hazard requires it, but underlying authoritative data must remain intact.

## 6.9 Nightmare Ecology Law

Natural nightmare phenomena are a native Somnolent category.

Nightmare pressure may respond to:

- sustained fear;
- repeated avoidance behaviour;
- trauma-rich environments;
- collective anxiety;
- intentional fear cultivation;
- Nightmare Architect activity;
- emergent Collective Fear Sovereigns;
- region events.

Fear may strengthen appropriate authored nightmare states, but courage alone does not erase physical predators, territorial societies, structures or political conflicts.

## 6.10 Personal and Collective Influence Law

Personal and collective psychology may shape **bounded** Somnolent regions.

Personal influence may affect:

- small recurring dream zones;
- symbolic objects;
- authored memory spaces;
- temporary environmental states.

Collective influence may affect larger persistent areas when reinforced by:

- many minds;
- culture;
- settlement history;
- institutions;
- repeated ritual;
- shared fear;
- shared art;
- long-lived stories;
- political power.

The realm itself remains independent of any one dreamer.

# 7. Dream Network Topology

## 7.1 Network Structure

The Somnolent Expanse generates as a network rather than one ordinary Euclidean continent.

Large-scale topology may include:

- coherence islands;
- Dreamwater seas;
- Lucid Shore arrival regions;
- Sleepwalker Roads;
- Memory Cities;
- Reverie Garden regions;
- Prophecy Dunes;
- Nightmare Fronts and Thickets;
- Shattered Waking Isles;
- Deep Dream Abyss layers;
- route stations;
- moving or shifting authored sites;
- restricted territories;
- sovereign regions;
- dungeon networks.

## 7.2 Route Identity

Routes may be spatially strange while remaining deterministic.

A Sleepwalker Road may connect regions according to:

- route coherence;
- discovered anchors;
- settlement infrastructure;
- faction maintenance;
- current regional state;
- event conditions;
- Dream Walker knowledge;
- saved route identity.

Routes may change between bounded states, but discovered route identity and progression must persist.

## 7.3 No Interdimensional Route Bypass

Sleepwalker Roads, Dreamwater currents, Waking Labyrinth exits, visions, dreams, personal zones and symbolic thresholds may connect **within** the Somnolent Expanse or reveal information about other realms.

They do **not** become alternate normal interdimensional travel systems.

Normal physical realm travel uses the Dreamgate.

## 7.4 Personal Pocket-Zone Reclassification

Older source references to personal dream pocket zones are narrowed.

Somnolent may contain:

- temporary personal dream sites;
- bounded memory spaces;
- authored personal symbolic zones;
- generated side-regions keyed to player/world state.

These are parts of the Somnolent network, not independently owned Pocket Realms and not permanent duplicate worlds for each sleeper.

FCC-11 retains ownership of constructed Pocket Realms.

# 8. Portal Access Contract

All major Leyforge realms use **one canonical normal portal type**.

The Somnolent Expanse's canonical portal is the:

# **Dreamgate**

The universal Realm Access system owns:

- portal runtime;
- route binding;
- transition orchestration;
- destination resolution;
- multiplayer authority;
- save/load behaviour;
- return safety;
- portal linking;
- failure/recovery handling;
- throughput and transport execution.

FCC-04 owns the Dreamgate's:

- identity;
- realm-specific material language;
- progression role;
- local discovery context;
- realm-specific construction inputs;
- destination-side presentation;
- stabilisation requirements;
- Somnolent-specific failure states.

## 8.1 Physical Entry Model

The player's **actual authoritative character** crosses the Dreamgate.

Entry does not:

- leave a second body behind;
- create a dream-copy player;
- duplicate inventory;
- fork progression;
- require another player's protection of a sleeping body.

The realm translates the traveller into a dream-compatible state while preserving one authoritative character record.

## 8.2 Dreamgate Presentation

The portal surface should initially reflect or echo the traveller's current surroundings before distorting into impossible dream imagery as activation stabilises.

This presentation reinforces the realm's identity without changing the underlying universal portal contract.

## 8.3 One Normal Portal Rule

Older source access concepts such as:

- sleep rituals;
- dream beds;
- rare fungi;
- oracle sites;
- psychic creatures;
- collective ceremonies;
- memory objects;
- dream anchors;
- controlled sleep;
- psychic projection

are **not** alternate normal methods for physically entering the dimension.

Where retained, they may become:

- clues used to discover Dreamgate knowledge;
- recipe hints;
- portal-location clues;
- activation context;
- prophecy or vision triggers;
- permission requirements;
- stabilisation aids;
- story events;
- local Somnolent interactions.

They do not bypass the Dreamgate.

## 8.4 Return Travel

Normal exit also uses Dreamgate infrastructure.

Simply sleeping, waking, meditating, losing Lucidity or completing a dream does not physically return the player to another realm.

Emergency recovery may use universal Realm Access failure/recovery rules where the broader system explicitly supports them.

## 8.5 Dreamgate Recipe Deferral

The exact Dreamgate recipe, quantities, mandatory cross-realm dependency and final construction tier are intentionally deferred to the **global portal progression reconciliation**.

This is not a missing FCC-04 realm-design decision.

The final progression web must compare every realm portal together before deciding which prior-realm materials are mandatory.

Current locked requirements are:

- portal name: **Dreamgate**;
- one canonical normal portal type;
- native Somnolent component families may include Lucid Glass, Dream Motes, Waking Stone and coherence technology;
- Dreamgate knowledge may be foreshadowed through dreams, Dreaming Root resonance, Oracle content, old structures or research;
- established portals are normally reusable rather than requiring complete reconstruction per trip;
- first valid arrival must provide a safe or recoverable return path.

# 9. Progression Architecture

The Somnolent Expanse supports broad progression without requiring one mandatory linear quest sequence.

## 9.1 Phase 1 - Foreshadowing and Discovery

The player learns that the realm exists through source-supported channels such as:

- unusual dreams;
- bounded Somnolent resonance during sleep;
- prophecy fragments;
- Oracle research;
- Dreaming Root phenomena;
- Memory-related clues;
- broken or ancient portal evidence;
- rare world events;
- Codex research;
- NPC scholarship;
- creature or material evidence.

Dream resonance may communicate **information** before the realm is physically accessible.

It does not transport the player.

## 9.2 Phase 2 - Dreamgate Knowledge

The player discovers:

- the Dreamgate's existence;
- broad construction logic;
- stabilisation principles;
- any prerequisite knowledge or prior-realm component required by the final progression graph.

No one specific procedural world site may be the only possible discovery route unless world generation guarantees it safely.

## 9.3 Phase 3 - First Dreamgate Establishment

The player builds or restores a valid Dreamgate through the universal Realm Access framework.

The process must be:

- deterministic;
- recoverable;
- save-safe;
- readable;
- compatible with multiplayer authority;
- free of circular requirements that demand Somnolent-only materials before first entry unless those materials are explicitly obtainable elsewhere.

## 9.4 Phase 4 - Lucid Arrival and Adaptation

Early realm play introduces:

- Lucidity;
- Waking Anchor stability;
- coherence;
- Dreamwater;
- Dreamstone and Dream Sand;
- stable and mutable routes;
- Dreamgate Sanctuary infrastructure;
- local native ecology;
- Dream Walker guidance;
- safe interpretation of expectation-responsive content.

The player is not required to join an Oracle School, Nightmare Warden order or other faction merely to survive.

## 9.5 Phase 5 - Network Expansion

Progression opens wider regions through combinations of:

- route knowledge;
- Lucid Beacons;
- Dream Walker relationships;
- caravan infrastructure;
- settlement reputation;
- improved coherence technology;
- native materials;
- Dreamwater navigation;
- dungeon outcomes;

## 9.6 Phase 6 - Civilisation and Law

The player increasingly engages with:

- Dream Walkers;

- Memory Traders;
- shared-dream cultures;
- nightmare societies;
- mixed-reality settlements;
- consent-law disputes;
- prophecy politics;
- Memory City claims;

Progression may involve alliance, neutrality, opposition, investigation, trade or reform rather than mandatory faction membership.

## 9.7 Phase 7 - Deep Somnolent Systems

Later realm progression may include:

- Nightmare Thickets;
- Shattered Waking Isles;
- Deep Dream Abyss expeditions;
- Lucid Observatory content;

## 9.8 No Mandatory Boss-Kill Gate

Realm mastery does not require killing every major authority.

Authority outcomes may grant progression through:

- knowledge;
- prophecy;
- route access;
- stabilisation techniques;
- political recognition;
- settlement permission;

- negotiated boundaries;

# 10. Death, Respawn and Failure Ownership

The Somnolent Expanse does **not** define a special death system.

Player death remains owned by Leyforge's universal death/recovery systems.

Death inside Somnolent may use realm-specific presentation, but the underlying result follows universal canon.

The realm does not:

- automatically wake the player as a death substitute;
- return the player to a sleeping body;
- create a separate dream-death inventory;
- own tombstones;
- own universal respawn;
- delete dream-acquired progression solely because the realm is dream-themed.

If universal death rules create a tombstone, recovery object, respawn choice or other failure result, Somnolent consumes that interface without redefining it.

---

# 11. Hunger, Survival and Physicality Boundary

The player's normal universal survival systems continue to apply unless a universal rule explicitly says otherwise.

Somnolent native foods may satisfy ordinary food needs.

Special Somnolent foods may additionally modify:

- Lucidity;
- nightmare resistance;
- prophecy sensitivity;
- coherence interaction;
- local dream states.

Imported food remains authoritative inventory and normally works.

Highly dream-dependent exports may require stabilisation to preserve special effects outside the realm.

Somnolent does not add a separate mandatory "dream hunger" bar.

# 12. Civilisation and Personhood Principles

## 12.1 Permanent Populations

The Somnolent Expanse may contain permanent populations including:

- dream-native peoples;
- long-established migrants;
- visitor communities;
- shared-dream societies;
- nightmare societies;
- constructed/thoughtform persons;
- mixed settlements.

The realm is not inhabited only by temporary sleepers.

## 12.2 Dream Walker Classification

**Dream Walker** is a profession, tradition or cultural role, not an ancestry.

Dream Walkers specialise in:

- unstable-region travel;
- Lucidity training;
- coherence anchoring;
- Dreamgate expeditions;
- traveller rescue;
- route mapping;
- dream-law interpretation.

## 12.3 Oracle Schools

Oracle Schools are multiple independent institutions, traditions or factions.

They may disagree about:

- disclosure of prophecy;
- political use of foresight;
- acceptable intervention in possible futures;
- ownership of prophetic data;
- interpretation;
- whether some futures should remain unseen.

## 12.4 Nightmare Wardens

Nightmare Wardens are specialist orders that may use:

- containment;
- negotiation;
- ecological management;
- restoration;
- research;
- rescue;
- defence;
- hunting where appropriate.

They are not defined as exterminators of all nightmare life.

## 12.5 Memory Traders

Memory Traders deal in deliberately encoded or legitimately acquired:

- stories;
- art;
- experiences;
- records;
- Memory Thread products;
- consensual memory goods.

Somnolent canon does not grant automatic access to every private memory possessed by every NPC.

## 12.6 Nightmare Societies

Intelligent nightmare beings may possess:

- personhood;
- settlements;
- laws;
- trade;
- territory;
- political rights;
- diplomacy;
- conflicts;
- cultures.

Origin from fear or nightmare does not remove personhood.

## 12.7 Consent Law

Somnolent cultures may regulate:

- entry into bounded personal dream spaces;
- memory extraction;
- shared-district alteration;
- induced fear;
- prophecy disclosure;
- dream-aspect impersonation;
- emotional manipulation;
- thoughtform personhood;
- use of another person's symbolic identity.

There is no single universal Somnolent legal code. These are civilisation hooks consumed by local cultures and factions.

# 13. Nightmare, Instability and Void Corruption Boundary

## 13.1 Natural Nightmare

Natural nightmare phenomena may be frightening, dangerous or hostile without being Void corruption.

Examples include:

- Fearlings;
- Nightmare Stalkers;
- Nightmare Thickets;
- Nightmare Nests;
- fear-fed structures;
- Collective Fear Sovereigns;
- Nightmare Architect content.

## 13.2 Dream Instability

Low coherence, route loops, Waking Fractures, Memory Overlays, Deep Dream surges and concept suppression are native Somnolent phenomena unless explicit Void provenance exists.

## 13.3 Void Corruption

Genuine corruption remains a distinct cross-realm phenomenon ultimately owned by the Void Between canon.

Void corruption may exploit:

- weak coherence;
- unstable Dreamgates;
- Nightmare Fronts;
- Deep Dream regions;
- Waking Fractures;
- damaged settlements or anchors.

It must remain mechanically and narratively distinguishable from ordinary nightmare pressure.

## 13.4 State Before Duplicate Definition

Corruption is represented first as a state or overlay on an existing:

- creature;
- material;
- block;
- structure;
- settlement;
- biome;
- dungeon;
- route.

A separate canonical corrupted definition is created only when irreversible transformation produces sufficiently different anatomy, physical properties, behaviour, crafting role or gameplay identity.

No automatic `Corrupted X` registry explosion is permitted.

# 15. Cross-Realm Interface Summary

## 15.1 Verdant Covenant

Verdant Dreaming Root regions may possess rare resonance with the Somnolent Expanse.

Allowed interactions may include:

- dream-like environmental behaviour;
- Dreamroot clues;
- symbolic flora/fauna responses;
- sleep visions;
- Dreamgate research hints;
- compatible crafting or research inputs.

This does not create a second portal method.

## 15.2 Ancestral Veil

Memory, dream and identity themes may interact, but the realms remain distinct.

Somnolent may interact with:

- remembered imagery;
- dream interpretations of history;
- Memory Thread;
- symbolic reconstruction.

Somnolent does not own:

- Veil-native spirits;
- Ancestral identity laws;
- Veil memory ecology;
- universal historical truth.

A dream of a dead or historical person is not automatically that original person's actual spirit.

## 15.3 Overworld

- tagged interaction volumes;
- bounded probability tables;
- discrete prophecy states;

The implementation may simplify *how* an effect is calculated, but should preserve:

- readability;
- player agency;
- save safety;
- deterministic authority;
- the realm's identity;
- intended gameplay consequences.
