# 1. Purpose

FCC-04F defines the authoritative **civilisation, settlement and social-content canon** of the Somnolent Expanse.

It owns realm-local canon for:

- who can live permanently in the realm;
- the separation between ancestry, culture, faction, polity, profession and realm origin;
- Dream Walker traditions;
- Oracle Schools;
- Nightmare Wardens;
- Memory Traders;
- Shared-Dream cultures;
- intelligent Nightmare societies;
- visitor, migrant and mixed communities;
- settlement archetypes across all eight major biomes;
- settlement coherence and stabilisation requirements;
- housing and civic adaptation to dream-network conditions;
- government, citizenship, diplomacy, law and sovereignty interfaces;
- bounded Somnolent consent-law categories;
- settlement-scale economy, trade, work, agriculture, husbandry and creature stewardship;
- ordinary and realm-adapted infrastructure families;
- mobile and route-dependent settlement forms;
- imported building and blueprint adaptation;
- the seven canonical signature structure identities and their civilisation roles;
- Dreamgate-adjacent settlement support;
- player settlement integration;
- deterministic world-generation and settlement-state persistence;
- low-end simulation and accessibility requirements.

FCC-04F does **not** own:

- universal NPC cognition, schedules, households, jobs or needs;
- universal economy pricing, currency, supply/demand or trade simulation;
- universal faction, diplomacy, government or reputation runtime;
- universal movement, pathfinding, transport physics or caravan simulation;
- universal agriculture, fishing, husbandry or food-consumption mechanics;
- exact placeable Block definitions, true portable Item definitions, recipe quantities or processing-station records - FCC-04G;
- dungeon, guardian, authority and event execution - FCC-04H;
- detailed nightmare-state propagation, Void-corruption systems, restoration mechanics and cross-realm state interaction - FCC-04I;
- final stable-ID normalisation or realm art profiles - FCC-04J, FCC-12, FCC-13 and FCC-14.

The governing rule is:

> **FCC-04F defines what Somnolent societies inhabit, build, maintain, trade through, regulate, remember, predict, protect and contest. Shared Leyforge systems execute those social and settlement contracts.**

The aim is to make the Somnolent Expanse a **lived-in realm with real societies**, not a sequence of dream encounters populated only by temporary sleepers.

# 2. Civilisation Canon Statement

The Somnolent Expanse supports persistent civilisations whose existence is not dependent on one current sleeper.

Permanent populations may include:

- dream-native peoples;
- long-established migrant populations;
- descendants and cultural successors of earlier migrants;
- visitor and research enclaves;
- Shared-Dream societies;
- intelligent Nightmare societies;
- constructed or thoughtform persons admitted by universal personhood rules;
- mixed-ancestry and mixed-origin settlements;
- route communities;
- Oracle institutions;
- Dream Walker organisations;
- Warden communities;
- Memory City populations;
- caravan cultures;
- mixed-reality colonies;
- Deep Dream expedition or isolated communities.

The realm is therefore not socially defined by one ancestry, one empire, one Oracle tradition, one nightmare ideology or one universal dream culture.

Its core civilisation fantasy is:

> **Societies endure inside a reality that can respond to memory, expectation, fear, prophecy and collective reinforcement, and they have developed laws, institutions, architecture and infrastructure that make long-term life possible without removing the realm's strangeness.**

No dream-native population is automatically mystical, benevolent, unreal or politically unified.

No Nightmare population is automatically evil.

No visitor population is automatically more materially “real” than a realm-native person.

No long-lived Shared-Dream district automatically belongs to whoever first imagined it.

# 3. Ancestry, Culture, Faction, Polity, Profession and Origin Separation

FCC-04F adopts the global Leyforge separation rule.

- **Ancestry** describes biological, constructed or metaphysical lineage where the global ancestry system recognises one.
- **Culture** describes learned practices, values, language, art, architecture, food, law, ritual and social behaviour.
- **Faction** describes organised allegiance or interest.
- **Polity** describes a governing political community or sovereign territory.
- **Settlement** describes a populated place or mobile community site.
- **Profession / Order** describes vocational, institutional or trained function.
- **Realm origin** describes provenance, not morality, culture or citizenship.
- **Dream-native** describes origin or long-term realm formation, not a mandatory ancestry definition.
- **Nightmare-origin** describes ecological/metaphysical provenance, not automatic hostility or non-personhood.

Any approved Leyforge ancestry may potentially participate in Somnolent societies where world history, settlement conditions and progression allow.

Likewise, a being with unusual dream or nightmare origin may qualify as a person through universal personhood rules without being forced into an ancestry category merely for convenience.

The following data concepts must remain separate:

- ancestry;
- culture;
- citizenship;
- faction membership;
- settlement residence;
- profession;
- Dream Walker training;
- Oracle affiliation;
- Warden affiliation;
- Nightmare origin;
- realm provenance;
- Dreamgate visitor status.

Earlier World Content Atlas material described:

- cultures maintaining shared dream districts;
- dream ports;

FCC-04F preserves the useful civilisation foundations while reconciling superseded assumptions.

## 4.1 Sleeping Communities

Older “sleeping community” language must not imply that the residents' authoritative bodies are stored outside the realm.

It may survive only as a cultural description for communities that:

- value sleep and dream practice;
- maintain dormitory or meditation traditions;
- study dreams;
- use rest-related ritual or medicine;
- host travellers recovering from Lucidity or coherence strain.

The inhabitants remain physically authoritative wherever universal character systems place them.

## 4.2 Predatory Thoughtforms

Predatory thoughtforms do not become a single civilisation category by default.

They may remain:

- creature ecology;
- temporary manifestations;
- dungeon inhabitants;
- hostile non-person phenomena;
- or, where durable agency/culture/personhood exists, members of actual societies.

Personhood is evaluated independently of body or origin.

## 4.3 Sleeper Sanctuaries

Older “sleeper sanctuary” structure ideas are reclassified as:

- traveller recovery spaces;
- Lucidity-restoration facilities;
- dream-study hostels;
- protected sleep/meditation spaces;
- coherence shelters.

They are not body-storage facilities for off-realm dream copies.

## 4.4 Shared-Dream Districts

Shared-Dream districts are retained fully, but they are persistent realm districts whose long-term form is reinforced by communities, institutions, art, ritual and history.

They are not temporary multiplayer dream lobbies.

# 5. Final Civilisation / Cultural Foundation Roster

FCC-04F locks the following **seven principal civilisation/cultural foundations**.

They are reusable archetypes, not seven mandatory singular factions.

1. **Dream Walkers**
2. **Oracle Schools**
3. **Nightmare Wardens**
4. **Memory Traders**
5. **Shared-Dream Cultures**
6. **Nightmare Societies**
7. **Visitor, Migrant and Mixed Communities**

A single settlement may contain several of these foundations simultaneously.

A Dream Port may host Oracle scholars, Memory Traders, a Warden office, Roadback caravans and a visitor district without becoming five separate settlements.

# 6. Dream Walkers

**Dream Walker** is a profession, discipline or cultural tradition, not an ancestry.

Dream Walkers specialise in activities such as:

- Lucidity training;
- unstable-region traversal;
- coherence-state interpretation;
- route mapping;
- Sleepwalker Road navigation;
- Dreamgate expeditions;
- traveller rescue;
- route recovery after regional shifts;
- anchor placement or maintenance;
- symbolic hazard interpretation;
- reconnaissance of Nightmare Fronts;
- mixed-reality navigation;
- Deep Dream expedition support.

Dream Walker organisations may be:

- civic route services;
- guilds;
- hereditary traditions;
- independent guides;
- military scouts;
- charitable rescue orders;
- research institutions;
- commercial expeditions;
- Oracle-linked navigation schools;
- Warden-linked frontier units.

No Dream Walker organisation automatically owns the realm's roads.

Dream Walker expertise should consume universal movement, navigation, profession, faction and knowledge systems rather than creating a parallel character class framework.

# 7. Oracle Schools

**Oracle Schools** are plural institutions focused on conditional prophecy, interpretation, probability, symbolic foresight, Omen Dust, Prophecy Ink, Time-Seeds and related Somnolent phenomena.

They are not one unified religion or government.

Schools may disagree over:

- whether all prophecies should be disclosed;
- whether some possible futures should be deliberately obscured;
- political use of foresight;
- military use of prophecy;
- ownership of prophetic observations;
- sale of prophecy services;
- intervention in futures once observed;
- whether self-fulfilling predictions should be restricted;
- how uncertainty should be communicated;
- whether the Sleeping Oracle should remain dormant;
- whether Prophecy Engine outputs deserve legal weight.

Possible institutional forms include:

- academies;
- temples;
- universities;
- caravan schools;
- civic forecasting offices;
- private houses;
- guilds;
- court advisers;
- itinerant traditions;
- radical anti-prophecy movements studying how to avoid predictive dependence.

Prophecy remains conditional and may become obsolete when actions change the conditions that produced it.

# 8. Nightmare Wardens

**Nightmare Wardens** are specialist orders that manage dangerous nightmare ecology and dream-instability crises.

They may use:

- containment;
- ecological management;
- negotiation;
- restoration;
- rescue;
- research;
- defensive patrol;
- relocation;
- habitat management;
- controlled trials;
- hunting where an actual hostile threat requires it;
- Void-corruption response where the problem is genuinely Void-derived.

Nightmare Wardens are **not defined as exterminators of nightmares**.

Different Warden orders may have incompatible doctrines.

One order may protect healthy Nightmare Thickets as necessary ecology.

Another may seek aggressive containment near settlements.

A militant order may wrongly equate nightmare with corruption, creating political conflict without making that doctrine cosmologically correct.

Warden status does not grant authority over all Nightmare societies or nightmare-origin persons.

# 9. Memory Traders

**Memory Traders** are individuals, institutions and cultures involved in legitimate trade of deliberately encoded, recorded, authored or consensually transferred memory-related products.

Tradeable goods/services may include:

- encoded Memory Thread works;
- autobiographical records voluntarily authored for sale or preservation;
- historical reconstructions;
- licensed sensory artworks;
- dream journals;
- Memory Theatre scripts;
- maps;
- recorded performances;
- educational memory constructs;
- consensual experience archives;
- restoration of damaged records;
- authentication services;
- provenance certification;
- secure storage or escrow.

The following are **not automatically tradeable commodities**:

- arbitrary private NPC memories;
- player Codex data;
- a person's identity;
- unwillingly extracted experiences;
- protected personal records;
- a sentient dream-aspect;
- a person reduced to a memory object.

Universal property, consent, crime and personhood systems remain authoritative.

Memory trade is therefore a knowledge/cultural industry, not a licence for universal mind-reading.

---

# 10. Shared-Dream Cultures

**Shared-Dream cultures** intentionally reinforce persistent communal environments through repeated use, collective symbolism, public art, ritual, architecture, memory and shared expectation.

They may maintain:

- collective districts;
- public symbolic architecture;
- community gardens;
- recurring festivals;
- communal dream law;
- shared visual motifs;
- cultural weather or ambience where bounded systems permit;
- common Dreamgate arrival symbolism;
- public story spaces;
- protected memory districts;
- emotion-sensitive civic gardens.

Long-term reinforcement may improve coherence, but collective agreement does not grant unlimited reality editing.

A Shared-Dream district remains bounded by authored environment states, settlement systems, structure state and world persistence.

Social conflict may arise when:

- generations disagree over district identity;
- migrants alter established symbolism;
- a powerful institution dominates public imagery;
- collective reinforcement unintentionally strengthens fear;
- old symbolism becomes harmful;
- a district's apparent historical form conflicts with documentary evidence;
- ownership of collaborative creation is contested.

# 11. Nightmare Societies

Intelligent Nightmare-origin beings may form full civilisations, polities, settlements and cultures.

Possible society forms include:

- fortress communities;
- protective nightmare clans;
- fear-feeding polities;
- Warden-allied enclaves;
- trauma-born communities;
- hostile predatory dominions;
- neutral shadow settlements;
- artistic or ritual societies using fear deliberately;
- refuge communities protecting members from extermination by outsiders.

A Nightmare society may negotiate:

- territory;
- safe hunting or feeding boundaries;
- trade;
- migration;
- Dreamgate access;
- Warden oversight;
- conservation zones;
- routes;
- prisoner or refugee exchange;
- joint defence against Void corruption.

No universal system may classify Nightmare citizens as harvestable monster resources solely because of origin.

Where a being is a person, personhood rules override creature-harvest assumptions.

# 13. Consent-Law Foundation

Set 24 identified **consent law** as a defining Somnolent social hook.

FCC-04F retains it as a broad family of local legal traditions rather than one universal Somnolent statute book.

Somnolent polities may regulate:

1. entry into bounded personal or communal dream-space;
2. extraction, copying or sale of memories;
3. alteration of shared districts;
4. deliberate fear induction;
5. emotional manipulation;
6. prophecy disclosure;
7. commercial use of prophecy;
8. dream-aspect impersonation;
9. use of a person's symbolic likeness;
10. construction of thoughtforms resembling real persons;
11. ownership and maintenance of collectively reinforced structures;
12. use of Dreamgate arrival information;
13. research on Deep Dream phenomena;
14. containment or relocation of nightmare-origin persons or ecology.

Different cultures may disagree strongly.

The game must distinguish:

- socially offensive behaviour;
- civil liability;
- contract breach;
- property violation;
- privacy violation;
- personhood violation;
- criminal behaviour;
- emergency powers;
- culturally accepted practice.

Consent law must use explicit game-state permissions, agreements and recognised actions rather than unrestricted natural-language interpretation.

# 14. Dream-Space and Shared-Property Rights

Somnolent law may recognise multiple kinds of space.

## 14.1 Public Dream Space

Public settlement areas where ordinary civic rules apply.

## 14.2 Private Dream Space

Homes, studios, archives, therapeutic spaces or bounded personal symbolic sites protected by property/privacy rules.

## 14.3 Shared Dream Space

Collectively reinforced districts where ownership may be communal, institutional, cooperative, contractual or politically contested.

## 14.4 Temporary Personal Zones

Bounded authored personal dream sites may receive temporary access controls without becoming independent Pocket Realms.

## 14.5 Nightmare Territory

Territory occupied by Nightmare societies or protected nightmare ecology may carry ordinary sovereignty, conservation or danger restrictions.

“Dream-like” does not mean “ownerless.”

A visually unstable building, symbolic garden or Memory City reconstruction can still have legal occupants, owners and civic status.

# 15. Prophecy and Public Decision-Making

Somnolent societies may use prophecy in government, trade, travel and risk management.

Possible public applications include:

- weather/event forecasting;
- route planning;
- disaster preparation;
- caravan timing;
- agricultural decisions;
- political advice;
- security planning;
- research prioritisation.

Prophecy must not automatically override ordinary evidence or player agency.

A polity may choose to grant Prophecy Engine output high legal weight, while another may legally prohibit prophecy from determining criminal guilt, succession or property rights.

Conflicts over predictive governance are legitimate political content.

# 16. Memory Cities and Civic Legitimacy

Memory Cities are persistent urban regions assembled from overlapping remembered architecture and cultural imagery.

Settlement law must not treat apparent historical form as automatic ownership proof.

A reconstructed building may be:

- historically accurate;
- partly accurate;
- culturally blended;
- outdated;
- symbolically distorted;
- generated from several competing memories;
- newly occupied and legally integrated.

Civic legitimacy may therefore rely on combinations of:

- current residence;
- recognised property records;
- settlement law;
- historical evidence;
- repair/stewardship;
- negotiated claims;
- political recognition;
- treaties;
- universal ownership systems.

Memory appearance is evidence, not absolute legal truth.

The Somnolent Expanse has no single government model.

Polities may use universal government profiles such as:

- councils;
- assemblies;
- monarchies;
- oligarchies;
- guild government;
- academic governance;
- communal cooperatives;
- order-led administration;
- religious institutions;
- constitutional systems;
- hybrid arrangements;
- individual sovereign rulers where world content justifies them.

Citizenship may be based on ordinary legal rules plus bounded Somnolent records such as:

- settlement residence;
- recognised identity;
- citizenship record;
- Dreamgate visitor permit;
- guild/order status;
- property ownership;
- treaty status;
- asylum/refugee status;

The system must distinguish:

- being dreamed about;
- appearing in a Memory City;
- holding legal identity;
- holding citizenship;
- owning property;
- having access permission;
- being a person.

These are not interchangeable.

# 24. Settlement Coherence Requirement

Every substantial Somnolent settlement requires a credible method for maintaining local persistence and habitability.

That method may be technological, architectural, cultural, ecological or institutional.

Possible stabilisation foundations include:

- stable Dreamstone foundations;
- Waking Stone;
- crafted Coherence Cores;
- Lucid Beacons;
- anchor structures;
- Dreamgate-linked infrastructure;
- repeated collective reinforcement;
- longstanding public rituals;
- maintained route networks;
- powerful stable entities;
- culturally reinforced shared symbolism;
- compatible Dreamwater/soil management.

No single technology is mandatory for every culture.

A low-technology settlement may be highly stable because its architecture, culture and long history reinforce local coherence.

A technologically advanced research colony may instead rely heavily on Waking Stone and Coherence Cores.

Settlement coherence should be represented through bounded aggregate systems rather than continuously simulating every resident's thoughts.

# 26. Imported Buildings and Blueprint Adaptation

Players and migrants may use ordinary Overworld or other-realm building designs inside the Somnolent Expanse.

They do not require a separate “dream-building mode.”

However, a blueprint may require adaptation depending on region.

Possible additional requirements include:

- stable foundations;
- Dreamstone replacement for unsuitable substrate;
- Waking Stone supports;
- coherence anchors;
- Coherence Cores;
- Dreamwater-resistant foundations;
- nightmare defences;
- route access;
- locally compatible agriculture;
- modified machinery mounts;
- environmental maintenance.

Shattered Waking Isles are generally the easiest major biome for conventional imported buildings because waking-like physical law is stronger.

Low-coherence Deep Dream sites may require significant stabilisation or remain unsuitable for ordinary permanent construction.

Blueprint validation should consume universal construction/project systems.
