# 2. Canon Statement

The **Ashen Lower Realms** is one colossal, persistent, physically explorable infernal realm composed of **nine world-scale Lower Strata**.

Its central fantasy is:

> **A descent through enormous civilised lower worlds shaped by ash, heat, war, contracts, coercive power, hunger, ruin, ambition, dominion and ancient furnace industry — where the deeper journey becomes more extreme, but the player remains free to solve physical progression through real capability rather than arbitrary locks.**

The realm contains:

- ash plains and volcanic frontiers;
- lava canals and fortress roads;
- Cinder Seas and chained islands;
- Contract Houses and commercial capitals;
- prisons, emancipated communities and refugee corridors;
- Hunger-Pit ecologies and food-security systems;
- ruined industrial landscapes and salvager settlements;
- rising powers and established dominions;
- Furnace-Crown works and ancient sovereign infrastructure;
- native agriculture and managed habitats;
- dangerous but functioning wildlife;
- trade, diplomacy, law, labour, rebellion and ordinary settlement life;
- multiple competing interpretations of legitimacy, ownership and sovereignty;
- world-scale authorities whose outcomes can change politics, industry, ecology and routes.

The Ashen Lower Realms is **infernal without being a universal evil realm**, **lower without being an afterlife**, **dangerous without being a mandatory combat ladder**, and **progressive without becoming nine key-locked levels**.

Its people are people. Its states are political states. Its contracts are explicit obligations. Its geology is physical geology. Its heat is a real environmental condition. Its deepest regions are difficult because of actual world conditions and capability requirements, not because the game silently disables tools that came from somewhere else.

The Ashen Lower Realms is **not**:

- an unordered cluster of nine separate dimensions;

- a set of isolated level instances chosen from a menu;

- an excuse to duplicate universal temperature, hunger, breathing, law, economy, settlement, vessel, mining, combat or death systems under “Infernal” names;
- an excuse to make every lower-realm material, flora type, creature role or political title a separate universal system.

# 5. Realm Fantasy Pillars

## 5.1 Descent Is the Realm

The Ashen Lower Realms is built around **meaningful physical descent**.

The player begins in the upper realm through a validated Stratum-I Ashgate arrival and may then descend through increasingly extreme territories.

Descent can change:

- environmental severity;
- geological resistance;
- industrial scale;
- available materials;
- infrastructure requirements;
- political power;
- contract complexity;
- food-security pressure;
- war intensity;
- sovereign influence;
- access to ancient works and Furnace-Crown systems.

However, the realm does not require a single linear quest path through all nine strata.

The player may descend through:

- intended infrastructure;
- diplomacy;
- paid route access;
- discovered natural weak points;
- old mines;
- conquered routes;
- player excavation;
- powered drilling;
- magic;
- explosives;
- restored machinery;
- another faction’s tunnel;
- sufficiently capable cross-realm technology.

The **world recommends routes; physical capability determines what is possible**.

## 5.2 Nine Strata Are Worlds, Not Levels

Each Lower Stratum is world-scale.

A stratum may contain:

- multiple biome families;
- sub-biomes and transition zones;
- wilderness;
- agriculture;
- cities;
- dungeons;
- political borders;
- industrial regions;
- refugee territories;
- mines;
- sea or vertical environments;
- safe districts;
- highly dangerous districts;
- multiple cultures and governments.

The nine strata are therefore not nine corridor levels or one-theme arenas.

## 5.3 “Lower” Does Not Mean “Afterlife”

The Ashen Lower Realms is a physical realm with native ecology, geology, reproduction, migration, settlement and history.

Its inhabitants are not automatically dead beings.

Its religions and cultures may contain beliefs about:

- punishment;
- damnation;
- debt;
- rebirth;
- souls;
- moral consequence;
- divine judgement.

Those beliefs do not transfer ownership of universal Leyforge death, respawn or afterlife logic to FCC-08.

## 5.4 Infernal Peoples Are Actual Peoples

The realm deliberately supports:

- ordinary households;
- labourers;
- farmers;
- traders;
- doctors;
- smiths;
- sailors;
- miners;
- bureaucrats;
- soldiers;
- prisoners;
- liberated people;
- refugees;
- scholars;
- rulers;
- criminals;
- reformers;
- rebels;
- friends;
- enemies.

Infernal origin is not a morality flag.

## 5.5 Heat and Industry Shape Civilisation

Heat is not merely environmental damage.

It shapes:

- architecture;
- cooling systems;
- water security;
- farming;
- transport;
- material choice;
- storage;
- hospitals;
- industry;
- settlement placement;
- trade;
- war;
- emergency planning.

Industry likewise shapes terrain, weather, labour politics, resource pressure and settlement viability.

## 5.6 Contracts and Law Are Distinct Sources of Power

Contracts, ownership, declared authority, debt and legal recognition can matter materially and politically.

But the realm preserves a hard distinction between:

- **accepted obligation**;
- **claimed territorial law**;
- **ownership**;
- **route permission**;
- **portal control**;
- **political sovereignty**.

No one claim type silently becomes all the others.

## 5.7 Deeper Does Not Mean Universally More Evil

As depth increases, conditions broadly become more extreme, but moral character does not follow depth.

A stable Furnace-Crown enclave may be safer than an upper warfront.

A Floor-IV emancipated city may be freer than a Floor-I predatory frontier state.

A Floor-VIII Dominion may be protective or tyrannical.

Depth changes the **capability and pressure envelope**, not the moral worth of everyone who lives there.

## 5.8 Multiple Outcomes Matter

Major conflicts may resolve through:

- negotiation;
- reform;
- legal challenge;
- alliance;
- treaty;
- liberation;
- repair;
- restoration;
- technical intervention;
- succession;
- deposition;
- sabotage;
- physical breach;
- combat;
- killing where appropriate.

Fixed Realm Authorities are not mandatory kill-bosses.

# 6. Canonical Nine-Stratum Structure

Ashen Lower Realms uses exactly **nine canonical major Lower Strata**.

They are persistent world regions, not separate realms and not top-level biome categories.

| Stratum | Canonical Name | Primary Thematic Weighting |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **Ashen Frontier** | ash, heat, survival, trade, cooling refuges, frontier extraction, ordinary infernal civilisation |
| **II** | **War Marches** | conquest, moving borders, siege logistics, fortress roads, war industry and military states |
| **III** | **Contract Realms** | contracts, ownership, commerce, debt, courts, Contract Houses and political leverage |
| **IV** | **Chain Depths** | coercive authority, prisons, chain infrastructure, punishment, liberation and refugee corridors |
| **V** | **Hunger Deeps** | scarcity, appetite, consumption, feeding ecology, ration systems and food-security pressure |
| **VI** | **Ruin Hollows** | exhaustion, failed industry, ruined empires, salvage, abandoned infrastructure and displacement |
| **VII** | **Ambition Realms** | rising powers, expansion, elite houses, advanced industry and political ascendance |
| **VIII** | **Dominion Realms** | established great powers, sovereign law, ancient states, immense works and hubris |
| **IX** | **Furnace Crown** | deepest ordinary Lower Stratum, extreme industry, ancient powers, Furnace-Crown systems and realm-climax authority territory |

## 6.1 Strata Versus Biomes

A stratum is a **macro depth/political/geological world layer**.

A biome is an **environmental/ecological landscape family**.

The realm retains exactly eight top-level biome families for current FCC purposes, owned in detail by FCC-08B.

Those biome families may:

- recur across multiple strata;
- develop depth-dependent expressions;
- overlap political territories;
- mix in transition zones;
- become more or less common at different depths.

No requirement exists for one biome to map to one stratum.

## 6.2 No Hidden Tenth Stratum or Secret Micro-Realm Ladder

Current FCC canon contains exactly nine major Lower Strata.

Future content may add:

- local caves;
- hidden sites;
- sealed districts;
- sub-biomes;
- ruins;
- sovereign territories;
- dungeons;
- internal pocketed spaces that remain part of the realm.

It may not silently introduce a hidden “real” tenth floor or multiple unrelated micro-realms beneath the nine-stratum structure without an explicit future canon expansion.

# 7. Difficulty, Depth and Regional Variation

Depth generally trends toward:

- stronger environmental pressure;
- greater geological resistance;
- more extreme industrial capability;
- more dangerous fuel systems;
- larger or older political powers;
- more demanding logistics;
- rarer and more specialised materials;
- more consequential authority conflicts.

However:

> **The realm does not enforce a strict rule that every location on Stratum N+1 must be harder than every location on Stratum N.**

Regional state may dominate depth trend.

Examples:

- a major Stratum-II warfront may be more dangerous than a stable Stratum-IV trade district;
- a cooling failure in Stratum I may become lethal;
- a protected Stratum-IX enclave may be survivable with correct infrastructure;
- an ancient predator or exceptional authority may make a shallower site extremely dangerous.

This allows world state, politics and ecology to matter alongside progression.

## 8.1 Descent Thresholds

- fortified descent road;

- safer terrain;

- trade infrastructure;
- clearer navigation;

It is **not** an unbreakable progression checkpoint.

# 9. Inter-Stratum Geological Boundary Law

The nine strata are separated by broad **geological transition zones**, not arbitrary invisible walls.

These zones may include:

- dense basalt facies;
- heat-fused volcanic strata;
- compacted ash-basalt;
- industrially contaminated geology;
- high-temperature metamorphic formations;
- vents;
- faults;
- caves;
- lava channels;
- ruined mines;
- ancient shafts;
- fractured weak points.

Their exact material definitions and distribution belong to FCC-08B/C.

## 9.1 Capability-Based Resistance

Boundary geology exposes real extraction/harvest capability requirements.

The system asks:

> **Can this extraction method physically overcome this material under current conditions?**

It does **not** ask:

> “Did this tool come from the correct floor?”

## 9.2 Legitimate Breach Methods

Valid methods may include:

- stronger hand tools;
- improved alloys;
- enchanted tools;
- mining spells;
- powered drills;
- siege machinery;
- explosives;
- creature-assisted excavation;
- restored ancient machinery;
- natural weak points;
- pre-existing tunnels.

Each method must satisfy its own costs, constraints and capability contract.

## 9.3 Normal Drops and Persistence

Mineable boundary terrain follows ordinary material/drop rules where physically appropriate.

Legitimate player excavation persists through save/load according to the universal voxel-edit and world-state systems.

Boundary geology does not disappear as a progression-only fake block with no material identity.

# 10. Universal Sandbox Capability Law

FCC-08 locks a broader Leyforge rule:

> **Normal materials, tools, vehicles, creatures, magic and machines retain their real capabilities across realms unless an explicit physical or magical incompatibility explains otherwise.**

Therefore:

- a sufficiently capable Impossible Deep mining tool may breach Ashen geology;
- a powerful Overworld alloy tool may work if it meets the requirement;
- Ascendant engineering may solve a compatible structural or atmospheric problem;
- Ashen materials may solve problems outside the Ashen Lower Realms where their real properties apply;
- future-realm technology may sequence-break older content when legitimately obtained and physically compatible.

Realm identity may impose actual conditions such as:

- heat;
- atmosphere;
- corrosion;
- pressure;
- stabilisation;
- fuel compatibility;
- magical field requirements.

It may not impose arbitrary origin invalidation.

---

# 11. Sequence Breaking Is Legitimate Progression

If a player obtains higher capability through:

- exploration;
- trade;
- diplomacy;
- theft;
- salvage;
- inheritance;
- authority reward;
- cross-realm progression;
- player engineering;
- restored ancient infrastructure;

then that capability functions according to its real contract.

The game does not disable a valid tool merely because the player has not completed an expected quest sequence.

Consequences remain physical and political.

A player who tunnels into a deeper stratum may:

- emerge into dangerous territory;
- lack heat or atmosphere preparation;
- miss a safe road;
- bypass useful settlements;
- enter claimed territory;
- create an invasion route;
- attract faction attention;
- discover deeper content early.

The freedom is real; so are the consequences.

---

# 12. Discovery and Progression Recognition

Physical discovery is authoritative.

If the player legitimately reaches a deeper stratum through:

- intended descent;
- tunnelling;
- machinery;
- magic;
- natural weak point;
- existing faction route;
- another valid traversal method;

then discovery/progression systems recognise that the player has reached that stratum.

No hidden quest flag may pretend the player has not arrived because they skipped the preferred route.

This principle also applies to:

- map discovery;
- route discovery;
- deeper Ashgate destination eligibility;
- local Codex knowledge;
- settlement encounter availability;
- resource provenance.

Exact implementation belongs to universal progression, map, portal and save systems.

# 13. Player-Created and Faction-Created Routes

A sufficiently stable player-created descent may become part of the living world.

Where navigation, settlement, AI and faction systems support it, a new route may become:

- trade corridor;
- smuggling route;
- military approach;
- refugee corridor;
- mining access;
- service tunnel;
- faction border issue;
- transport shortcut.

Factions with adequate capability may also conduct their own inter-stratum excavation.

The player is not the only actor capable of changing the realm’s route graph.

Ability to excavate does not erase ownership or law. Mining through someone else’s fortress, territory or infrastructure can produce political or legal consequences.

# 14. Contract Law Family

Ashen Lower Realms contains magical and non-magical contract cultures.

A **magically binding contract** requires **explicit recognised acceptance of readable terms**.

Where relevant, a contract should expose sufficient information about:

- parties;
- obligations;
- duration;
- cost;
- collateral;
- permissions;
- breach conditions;
- penalties;
- termination;
- amendment;
- appeal or dispute process where present.

## 14.1 Accepted Contract Scope

Supported contract effects may include explicitly defined states relating to:

- currency;
- items;
- land;
- services;
- bounded labour periods;
- access permissions;
- faction obligations;
- reputation;
- recognised debt;
- declared boons or powers;
- specified penalties.

## 14.2 Prohibited Contract Scope

Contracts may not:

- delete the player character;
- own or replace universal death/respawn;
- corrupt save integrity;
- secretly seize player controls;
- erase arbitrary memories;
- silently accept themselves;
- rewrite unrelated universal systems;
- convert a sapient person into a generic inventory commodity;
- create unrestricted permanent ownership of player agency.

## 14.3 Amendment Rule

A Contract Forge, ruler, court or institution cannot secretly rewrite an accepted contract.

Any valid amendment must follow the contract’s explicit amendment/acceptance conditions or another universally supported legal process.

Fraud may exist as world content. The underlying contract system itself may not falsify consent.

# 15. Declared Authority and Law Family

A territorial authority may attempt to enforce local law.

Examples include:

- taxation;
- customs;
- border enforcement;
- trespass rules;
- property seizure under claimed jurisdiction;
- imprisonment;
- trade restrictions;
- military law;
- ration law;
- labour regulation;
- route control.

This is political/legal enforcement.

It does **not** mean the player magically accepted a contract.

The canonical distinction is:

> **Contract = explicitly accepted obligation.**  
> **Law = authority attempting to enforce a rule within claimed jurisdiction.**

# 16. Ownership, Control and Sovereignty Are Separate

The following are distinct concepts:

- owning land;
- controlling a road;
- controlling an Ashgate site;
- possessing an Ash Keystone;
- operating a Cinder Embassy;
- enforcing local law;
- holding a contract right;
- controlling a Descent Threshold;
- occupying a dungeon;
- claiming a stratum;
- being recognised as a sovereign;
- actually exercising political control.

No single state automatically implies all others.

Examples:

- a faction may physically control an Ashgate without owning the realm;
- a Contract House may hold route rights without owning the territory crossed by the route;
- a Furnace Crown Sovereign may claim all nine strata without controlling all nine;
- a liberated Chain Citadel may retain infrastructure while changing government;
- a player may own a mine while remaining subject to local territorial law.

# 17. Infernal, Demon and Imp Identity Boundaries

## 17.1 Infernal

**Infernal** is a broad realm-linked category.

It may describe peoples, cultures, lineages, beings, architecture, technology or history associated with the Ashen Lower Realms.

It is not one biological species and not a morality tag.

## 17.2 Demon

**Demon** is a broad cultural, historical or supernatural label.

Its meaning may vary by speaker and context.

It is not one generic canonical species definition.

## 17.3 Imp

**Imp** is a sapient infernal ancestry/family of peoples rather than a generic hostile wildlife category.

Individual imps may be:

- allies;
- enemies;
- bureaucrats;
- farmers;
- merchants;
- soldiers;
- scholars;
- rulers;
- labourers;
- criminals;
- refugees.

Older Ash Imp / Ember Imp hostile-creature assumptions are superseded by the sapient-person interpretation unless a later distinct non-sapient species is explicitly authored.

## 17.4 Ancestry Does Not Determine Morality

Biology, infernal origin, constructed origin, realm of birth and ancestry do not determine:

- morality;
- faction allegiance;
- citizenship;
- legal status;
- hostility;
- intelligence;
- political ideology.

# 18. Construct Personhood Boundary

Constructed origin does not automatically determine personhood.

Ashen civilisation may contain:

1. **Tool/Machine Constructs** — non-sapient engineered systems;
2. **Bound Intelligent Constructs** — demonstrably sapient beings under legal or technological constraint;
3. **Free/Awakened Construct Persons** — sapient persons with normal personhood protections.

Where a construct is sapient, the universal personhood/character system remains authoritative.

A sapient person cannot be reduced to an ordinary transferable Item definition merely because a coercive state claims ownership.

This does not erase slavery, imprisonment, debt bondage or emancipation as political world content. It prevents those systems from collapsing character identity into commodity inventory data.
