# 2. Canon Statement

The Ashen Lower Realms is **not biologically barren**.

Its ecology supports extensive native:

- woody producers;
- shrubs;
- reeds;
- moss-like growths;
- lichen-like mineral symbionts;
- fungi;
- carnivorous/semi-carnivorous producers;
- cultivated luxury flora;
- ash-soil crops;
- heat-tolerant food systems;
- decomposition networks;
- settlement agriculture.

Native primary production may draw usable energy and matter from combinations of:

- dim or filtered light;
- geothermal gradients;
- chemical/mineral metabolism;
- ash-derived nutrients;
- mana;
- fungal symbiosis;
- mineral symbiosis;
- thermal cycling;
- moisture;
- decomposed organic matter;
- controlled settlement lighting.

Ordinary surface photosynthesis may occur where conditions permit, but it is not the only ecological foundation.

The realm therefore supports real agriculture, ordinary food production, ecosystem recovery and civilisation-scale population without requiring all food to be imported.

# 3. Producer-Ecology Principles

Ashen producer ecology follows these rules:

1. **Native biology is adapted to Ashen conditions rather than immune to every hazard.**
2. **Heat tolerance is not the same as total fire immunity.**
3. **No single energy pathway powers the entire realm.**
4. **Ash, minerals, heat, fungi, moisture and mana can all participate in native production.**
5. **Some regions are naturally sparse or barren without implying the whole realm is barren.**
6. **Wild and cultivated forms may remain the same biological lineage.**
7. **A crop does not require a duplicate wild-species definition when it is a domesticated form of an existing producer.**
8. **Imported plants are allowed when environmental needs are met.**
9. **Native Ashen crops exported elsewhere are likewise governed by actual suitability.**
10. **Agriculture interacts with universal Hunger/Nutrition rather than creating an Infernal Hunger system.**
11. **Harvesting uses universal gathering/cutting/digging/farming capability where possible.**
12. **A producer family may have ecotypes and cultivars without top-level registry explosion.**
13. **Ordinary Ashen biology is not automatically Void corruption.**
14. **Material identity remains owned by FCC-08C where a producer yields a previously locked material family.**
15. **Final harvested Items, crop-state Blocks and recipes remain owned by FCC-08G/FCC-13.**

# 5. Cinderwood Tree

## 5.1 Identity

The **Cinderwood Tree** is the principal native timber-producing organism of the Ashen Lower Realms.

Its processed timber is **Cinderwood**.

It is adapted to:

- ash-rich soils;
- warm climates;
- intermittent fire;
- low-to-moderate moisture;
- mineral-heavy substrates;
- settlement-managed irrigation;
- smoky conditions within survivable limits.

It is especially important in regions where ordinary imported timber would struggle.

## 5.2 Morphology

Cinderwood is broadly a hardy woody tree family with:

- dark, protective outer bark;
- dense fibrous inner wood;
- deep or spreading root structures depending local substrate;
- seasonal or stress-responsive leaf/needle analogues depending ecotype;
- strong resprouting or recovery capability after moderate fire.

Exact final silhouette is not locked here.

## 5.3 Fire Boundary

Cinderwood is **heat/fire resistant, not fireproof**.

Sufficient:

- sustained flame;
- lava contact;
- furnace exposure;
- extreme Furnace Surge;
- industrial accident;

can still char, kill or destroy it.

This distinction is important for:

- wildfire;
- farming risk;
- building materials;
- believable ecology;
- settlement management.

## 5.4 Ecological Role

Cinderwood provides:

- habitat;
- shade;
- ash-soil stabilisation;
- fungal symbiosis;
- browse/seed ecology;
- shelter;
- firebreak potential when managed;
- long-term carbon/organic matter.

## 5.5 Civilisation Role

Cinderwood supports:

- ordinary timber;
- handles;
- furniture;
- lightweight frames;
- farm structures;
- carts;
- crates;
- tools;
- domestic construction;
- charcoal/fuel where culturally appropriate.

Final Cinderwood material forms belong to FCC-08G/FCC-12/FCC-13.

## 5.6 Distribution

Strong affinity:

- Ashen Frontier;
- Black Ember Hollows;
- managed settlement zones;
- cooled Ember Marches pockets;
- selected Contract Realm estates;
- protected ruin basins.

Deeper ecotypes may exist where irrigation, cooling or specialised adaptation makes them viable.
