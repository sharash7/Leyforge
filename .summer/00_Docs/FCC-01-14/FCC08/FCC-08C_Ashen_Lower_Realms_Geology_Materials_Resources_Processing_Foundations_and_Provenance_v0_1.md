# 2. Canon Statement

Ashen Lower Realms material design follows a **small number of coherent material lineages with meaningful physical, industrial, biological and magical specialisations**.

The realm does **not** use:

- one new ore for every stratum;
- one new stone for every biome;
- one arbitrary “better metal” for every floor;
- a universal infernal material tier that automatically outclasses all external matter;
- duplicate materials created only because an old document used a different adjective;
- progression walls made from non-resource “game logic rock”;
- realm-origin checks that invalidate otherwise capable tools.

The material system instead asks:

1. **What is this matter physically or magically?**
2. **How did it form?**
3. **What transformation, if any, created it?**
4. **What properties make it useful?**
5. **What extraction capability is required?**
6. **Which processing systems can legitimately work it?**
7. **What changes when it leaves the realm?**
8. **Is a legacy name a separate material, a state, a variant, a product, a cultural label, or merely a role descriptor?**

This material canon is deliberately compatible with the wider Leyforge capability-pathway philosophy:

> **Progression is produced by real capabilities, not by a single universal ore ladder.**

A material can be advanced because it improves:

- thermal performance;
- structural performance;
- tensile strength;
- sealing;
- magical binding;
- instrumentation;
- cooling;
- energy handling;
- excavation machinery;
- biological survival;
- chemical processing;

without needing to be “the next pickaxe metal.”

# 3. Canonical Material Architecture

The Ashen Lower Realms material architecture is organised into seven broad groups.

## 3.1 Foundation Geology

- **Basalt** — principal ordinary native stone/geological foundation.
- **Infernal Ash** — realm-specific ash/mineral particulate and sediment family.
- **Inter-Stratum Facies** — depth-conditioned resistant geological expressions of Ashen geology rather than separate top-level fantasy ores.

## 3.2 Principal Metal Lineage

- **Ember Iron** — common native Ashen iron-bearing metal foundation.
- **Contract Iron** — specialised processed/bound Ember Iron lineage with contract/seal functionality.
- **Chain Metal** — specialist high-toughness processed alloy family.
- **Infernal Alloy** — broader high-tier Ashen alloy family.

## 3.3 Mineral / Chemical Resources

- **Ember Salt**
- **Cooling Crystal**
- **Hunger Crystal**

## 3.4 Glass Lineage

- **Hellglass** — canonical specialist Ashen glass family.
  - **Ash Glass** — common/lower-grade ash-rich expression within the Hellglass lineage.
  - **Sootglass** — dark specialist Hellglass variant associated strongly with Sootglass Gardens.
  - **Heat Glass** — legacy/function-role wording, not an independent material family.

## 3.5 Biological / Biomineral Materials

- **Cinderhide**
- **Cinder Pearl**

## 3.6 Energetic / Thermal Resources

- **Heat Gas**
- **Black Ember**
- **Cold Fire**
- **Furnace Heart**

## 3.7 Processed Construction Family

- **Ash Brick** — canonical processed ash/mineral construction family.
  - Legacy **Infernal Brick** is reconciled into this lineage.

This hierarchy is authoritative for FCC-08.

Future expansion may add genuinely distinct material families when composition, provenance, transformation, system behaviour or industrial role requires them. Future content may not multiply synonyms, biome-labelled duplicates, arbitrary depth-tier materials or cosmetic-only resource families without explicit reconciliation.

# 4. Material Classification Rules

All FCC-08 materials follow these rules.

1. **A new stratum does not automatically justify a new metal, stone, glass, salt or crystal family.**
2. **A new biome does not automatically justify a biome-branded material.**
3. **A geological facies remains a facies when the underlying matter remains materially continuous.**
4. **A state remains a state when behaviour changes without creating genuinely different matter.**
5. **A processed product remains processed matter rather than becoming a native ore.**
6. **A cultural or industrial term does not automatically create a new material family.**
7. **A role descriptor such as “heat glass” may be fulfilled by any material that actually satisfies the role contract.**
8. **Creature-derived material identity and creature biology are separate ownership concerns.**
9. **Biominerals are not ores merely because they occur in deposits or shells.**
10. **Atmospheric resources are not automatically solid Items.**
11. **Phenomenon resources are not automatically terrain Blocks.**
12. **A relic, trophy, legal record, crown object or ancient machine is not a material tier.**
13. **A placeable Block that remains/recoverably returns as that same Block uses one canonical Block definition with inventory projection rather than a duplicate Item.**
14. **Derived forms are created only where gameplay, construction, processing or readability requires them.**
15. **Final global taxonomy, namespace normalisation and duplicate cleanup belong to FCC-12/FCC-13.**

# 9. Contract Iron

## 9.1 Identity

**Contract Iron** is a specialised processed and magically/legally configured form of the Ember Iron lineage.

It is **not** a second unrelated native ore.

Its defining advancement is its capacity to hold, register, verify or participate in explicit supported contract/seal functionality when processed through compatible systems.

Canonical lineage:

> **Ember Iron → specialised metallurgical refinement + contract/seal binding process → Contract Iron**

## 9.2 Functional Identity

Contract Iron may support:

- seal plates;
- contract plaques;
- permission tokens;
- legal infrastructure;
- registry components;
- authenticated storage;
- bounded access control;
- recognised obligation systems;
- arbitration or amendment systems.

It does **not** create unrestricted mind control, hidden acceptance, save ownership or universal legal authority.

## 9.3 Physical Versus Contract Properties

Contract Iron remains real metal.

Its physical properties survive export.

Its specialist binding behaviour requires the **universal contract/binding framework** and compatible conditions.

Taking Contract Iron into another realm does not automatically convert local political law into magical contract law.

## 9.4 Progression Boundary

Contract Iron is not automatically a stronger mining metal than Ember Iron merely because it is more specialised.

Its specialisation is primarily:

- binding;
- inscription;
- verification;
- permissions;
- legal/magical state handling.

Its physical strength may still be useful, but FCC-08 does not define Contract Iron as “Ember Iron + one universal mining tier.”

## 9.5 Stable Resource Anchor

Contract Iron preserves:

- `res.realm.ashen_lower_realms.02` — **Contract Iron**

The resource anchor remains canonical even though FCC-08 corrects its material-role interpretation.

Individual Infernal Alloys may combine:

- Ember Iron;
- Contract Iron;
- Ember Salt;
- external compatible metals;
- specialised fluxes;

Depending on composition, an Infernal Alloy may advance:

- tool durability;
- cutting performance;
- heat resistance;
- structural strength;
- corrosion resistance;
- machine-frame capability;
- armour;
- weapon performance;
- high-temperature excavation;
- Furnace-Crown machinery.

# 23. Furnace Heart

## 23.1 Identity

**Furnace Heart** is an exceptionally dense stabilised thermal/magical core associated with the highest levels of Ashen geological and industrial energy systems.

A Furnace Heart may be:

- naturally formed in rare extreme Furnace-Crown geological conditions;
- artificially created through advanced Ashen furnace engineering.

It is **not** an organ merely because it contains the word “Heart.”

## 23.2 Provenance

Furnace Hearts may occur through:

- rare Stratum-IX geological systems;
- ancient Furnace-Crown infrastructure;
- high-tier engineered manufacture;
- recovered sovereign works;
- restored civilisation-scale machinery.

## 23.3 Gameplay Roles

Furnace Hearts primarily support:

- extreme furnaces;
- civilisation-scale thermal systems;
- high-tier alloy manufacture;
- major machines;
- industrial power;
- excavation machinery;
- Furnace-Crown Works;
- advanced infrastructure;
- later Ashgate upgrades where globally justified.

## 23.4 Hand-Tool Boundary

Furnace Heart is not primarily designed as a hand-pickaxe component.

A Furnace-Heart-powered boring system may vastly exceed a hand tool while preserving the distinction between:

- material strength;
- machine power;
- thermal operating envelope;
- excavation capability.

## 23.5 Transport

Furnace Hearts remain transportable physical/magical cores outside the realm if correctly stabilised and handled.

Unsafe handling may produce legitimate heat, energy or magical consequences.

The material does not vanish merely because it crosses a portal.

## 23.6 Stable Resource Anchor

Furnace Heart preserves:

- `res.realm.ashen_lower_realms.06` — **Furnace Heart**

# 24. Furnace Core Distinction

Older source material uses the term **furnace core** in War-Furnace industry.

FCC-08 distinguishes:

- **Furnace Heart** — canonical major specialist Ashen material/core family.
- **Furnace Core** — ordinary or advanced manufactured machine component category.

A furnace core may be made from many materials.

It is not automatically a Furnace Heart and does not inherit Furnace Heart rarity or behaviour.

This prevents machine-component terminology from multiplying the resource roster.

# 25. Legacy “Deep Ember” Reconciliation

The Ashen building-pack term **deep ember** is retired as an independent canonical Ashen resource name.

Reasons:

- **Deepfire Ember** is already the canonical Impossible Deep energetic material.
- Ashen Lower Realms now owns **Black Ember** and **Furnace Heart** as distinct energetic identities.
- Retaining “Deep Ember” as another formal Ashen resource would create avoidable cross-realm ambiguity.

Old Ashen references to deep ember must be resolved contextually to:

- Black Ember;
- Furnace Heart;
- ordinary Ashen fuel;
- another declared thermal-resource role.

Legacy wording may survive in migration metadata but must not create a third competing ember family.

# 26. Inter-Stratum Geological Facies

## 26.1 Identity

The eight major boundaries between the nine Lower Strata are not made from eight named “barrier ores.”

They are broad **depth-conditioned geological transition zones** composed from Ashen geology altered by:

- heat;
- pressure;
- mineralisation;
- metamorphism;
- ash compaction;
- repeated lava intrusion;
- industrial contamination;
- slag inclusion;
- long-term magical conditions;
- regional structural history.

## 26.2 Example Facies

Potential expressions include:

- compacted ash-basalt;
- heat-fused basalt;
- dense volcanic caprock;
- slag-bearing industrial strata;
- glass-rich fused zones;
- chain-anchor bedrock;
- extreme Furnace-Crown metamorphic formations.

These examples are facies/formation descriptions unless a future transformation is proven to create genuinely distinct matter.

## 26.3 No Eight-Resource Barrier Ladder

FCC-08 explicitly rejects:

- Barrier Ore I;
- Barrier Ore II;
- Barrier Ore III;
- and similar depth-number resource proliferation.

The world uses physical material properties and capability requirements instead.

# 27. Harvest & Excavation Capability Law

## 27.1 Core Rule

Every mineable Ashen geological material exposes real **harvest/excavation capability requirements**.

The system asks whether the active extraction method can overcome the material under current conditions.

It does not ask:

- which realm the tool came from;
- whether the player completed the “correct” quest;
- whether the item was found on the intended floor.

## 27.2 Valid Extraction Methods

Compatible methods may include:

- hand tools;
- improved alloy tools;
- enchanted tools;
- mining spells;
- powered drills;
- siege machinery;
- explosives;
- creature-assisted excavation;
- ancient restored machinery;
- industrial boring systems;
- natural weak points;
- pre-existing mines and tunnels.

## 27.3 Cross-Realm Validity

A tool or system from another realm works if its actual capability is sufficient and the environment does not create a genuine incompatibility.

Examples:

- an advanced Overworld mining system;
- Impossible Deep mining technology;
- Ascendant engineering;
- future machinery.

Origin is not a validity gate.

## 27.4 Environmental Access Versus Material Strength

Material capability and environmental survival remain separate.

A player may have:

- a tool capable of breaking the rock;
- but inadequate cooling;
- inadequate atmosphere protection;
- inadequate route support.

The game should communicate the actual blocker rather than silently denying mining.

# 28. Natural Weak Points & Geological Exploration

Inter-stratum geology must not have identical resistance across every metre.

Natural and historical weak points may include:

- faults;
- vents;
- caves;
- magma channels;
- fractured basalt;
- collapsed mines;
- old shafts;
- abandoned fortifications;
- industrial boreholes;
- creature passages;
- erosion channels.

Exploration can therefore beat brute force.

A clever player may discover a lower-capability route through damaged geology rather than crafting the strongest available drill.

This is intentional sandbox play.

# 29. Boundary Material Drops & Persistence

Mineable transition geology follows ordinary material and drop rules where physically meaningful.

Boundary materials do not:

- disappear into nothing purely because they are progression terrain;

- become uncollectable “wall logic.”

Legitimate player excavations persist according to universal voxel/world-state systems.

Where a mined facies is materially still Basalt or ash-bearing Basalt, final drops should resolve to the correct underlying material/processed drop contract rather than inventing a unique barrier Item.
