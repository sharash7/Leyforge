# 2. Realm Certification Statement

The **Ashen Lower Realms** is one enormous persistent infernal realm containing exactly **nine world-scale Lower Strata**.

It is:

- physically explorable;
- heavily civilised;
- ecologically functional;
- industrial;
- politically fragmented;
- volcanically and thermally extreme;
- shaped by contracts, law, ownership, labour, hunger, war, ambition and sovereignty;
- internally traversable by physical routes, excavation and infrastructure;
- externally entered and exited through one canonical portal family: **Ashgate**.

It is **not**:

- nine separate dimensions;
- a generic afterlife;
- a mandatory morality-alignment realm;
- a sequence of nine key-locked levels;
- reachable from the Overworld by digging indefinitely;
- governed by one universal infernal empire;
- governed by a realm-specific death system;
- inherently Void-corrupted.

The current FCC locks the realm's complete theoretical content foundation for current production purposes while preserving later global reconciliation, art direction, implementation balancing and future expansion.
