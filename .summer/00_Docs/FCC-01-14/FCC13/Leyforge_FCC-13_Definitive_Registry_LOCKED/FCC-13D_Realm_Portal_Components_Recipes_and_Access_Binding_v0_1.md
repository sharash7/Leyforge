# 1. Purpose

FCC-13D defines the definitive current Realm Access binding for Leyforge.

It locks:

- exactly six normal external portal families;
- the global first-access dependency graph;
- portal-family physical contracts;
- canonical portal core/keystone identities;
- first-access construction counts;
- first-access provenance requirements;
- exact portal assembly requirements;
- exact component recipes where they were locked in Q1–Q268;
- provider-based component roles where exact material identity is not required;
- activation rules;
- repair/recovery;
- upgrade architecture;
- destination calibration;
- first-arrival constraints.

FCC-13D does not create a seventh generic portal.

It does not turn portal knowledge into a material.

It does not resurrect rejected filler such as Portalium, Dimension Ore or generic Realm Essence.

# 2. Fidelity Boundary

The accepted FCC-13 interview locks the **portal-level assembly counts and dependency decisions**.

In several cases it intentionally leaves the internal quantity of a provider-built support component to the generic FCC-13C component/process profile.

Therefore FCC-13D uses two recipe levels.

## Level A — Portal Assembly Recipe

Exact portal-family structure count and exact identity-bearing components.

## Level B — Component Recipe

Exact where Q1–Q268 locked explicit component quantities.

Where the interview specifies only a provider-built component/material role, FCC-13D preserves that provider role and delegates its ordinary physical quantity profile to FCC-13C rather than inventing a new unapproved count.

This document therefore adds no hidden post-interview design decisions.

# 3. Exactly Six Portal Families

The canonical current normal external Realm Access families are:

1. **Covenant Portal** — Verdant Covenant
2. **Veilgate** — Ancestral Veil
3. **Dreamgate** — Somnolent Expanse
4. **Ascension Gate** — Ascendant Reach
5. **Deepgate** — Impossible Deep
6. **Ashgate** — Ashen Lower Realms

No seventh normal external portal exists.

Deferred systems do not become current normal Realm Access:

- World-Engine;
- Elemental Confluences;
- playable Void Between;
- Pocket Realm Construction.

All six portal families use one universal Realm Access runtime contract with family-specific:

- structure validator;

- core/keystone;

Each family may use:

- exact core/keystone object;

# 6. Global First-Access Philosophy

FCC-13 locks a **hybrid portal progression graph**.

Early Realm Access retains meaningful sandbox freedom.

Later Realm Access deliberately requires cross-realm integration.

The graph is not a simple Realm 1 → Realm 2 → Realm 3 ladder.

# 8. Early Realm Access

Three realm families are independently reachable from Overworld progression.

## Verdant Covenant

Player builds a Covenant Portal from Overworld-accessible providers, components, knowledge and attunement.

## Ancestral Veil

Player restores a guaranteed historical Veilgate Remnant containing authentic Ancestral portal matter.

## Somnolent Expanse

Player builds around a guaranteed authenticated Somnolent artefact package recovered from a Dream-Leak site.

The player may pursue these early routes in different orders where world/progression access allows.

---

# 9. Ascendant Dependency

First Ascension Gate requires:

> **one authentic stabilisation/attunement component from Verdant, Ancestral or Somnolent.**

Therefore at least one early realm must have been accessed legitimately.

---

# 10. Impossible Deep Dependency

First Deepgate requires:

> **two qualifying realm-stabilisation components from two distinct previously accessed realm provenances**

selected from:

- Verdant Covenant;
- Ancestral Veil;
- Somnolent Expanse;
- Ascendant Reach.

This requires broader preparation without forcing one fixed sequence.

---

# 11. Ashen Dependency

First Ashgate requires:

> **one exact Impossible Deep-origin extreme-environment component: Deepfire Ember Containment Core.**

Impossible Deep is therefore a required predecessor to first Ashen access.

The first Ashgate does not require Ashen-native matter.

---

# 12. Guaranteed Progression Rule

Any mandatory foreign portal material/component must have at least one deterministic acquisition route.

Valid authored channels include:

- guaranteed site;
- historical portal remnant;
- deterministic research chain;
- guaranteed faction/diplomatic reward;
- quest contract;
- guaranteed trade path;
- inherited canonical structure;
- authenticated expedition/cache.

Random rare loot may supplement these paths.

Random rarity may never be the sole mandatory portal dependency.

---

# 13. Sequence Breaking

If the player legitimately possesses:

- required materials/components;
- required knowledge;
- required process capability;
- valid portal site/structure;
- valid destination data;

the Realm Access runtime accepts the solution.

The game does not reject a physically/canonically valid portal merely because the player acquired its components earlier than expected.

---

# 14. Portal Knowledge

Every portal family requires knowledge/recognition/attunement separate from physical materials.

Portal knowledge may come from:

- archaeology;
- research;
- records/books;
- NPC instruction;
- faction/authority knowledge;
- experimentation where the owning system permits.

At least one deterministic knowledge path must exist for any mandatory portal.

Knowledge is not an inventory ingredient.

---

# 15. Portal Activation Consumables

Activation consumables are family-specific.

There is no universal rule that every portal consumes one catalyst.

Current first-access rules:

- Covenant Portal — consumes 1 Verdant Growth Catalyst on first activation.
- Veilgate — no default one-use activation material.
- Dreamgate — no default one-use activation material.
- Ascension Gate — none.
- Deepgate — none.
- Ashgate — none.

---

# 16. Portal Upgrade Architecture

Portal upgrades use a hybrid model.

## Physical Upgrades

Use replaceable support modules/components.

## Attunement / Destination / Calibration

Use persistent portal/core/interface state and discovered knowledge.

The portal remains the same family.

No “Advanced Dreamgate”, “Deep Ashgate” or other seventh/eighth family is created.

---

# 17. Portal Repair Architecture

Portal repair uses the FCC-13C staged repair transaction model.

Common rules:

- frame damage repaired using exact/provider material according to family;
- exact identity-bearing components require exact repair/replacement;
- intact cores/keystones are recoverable;
- persistent calibration survives where the preserved core/interface physically retains it;
- destroyed components produce actual salvage where recoverable.

---

# 18. Portal Recovery

Whole portals do not become inventory Items.

Dismantling may return:

- frame pieces;
- fittings;
- interfaces;
- cores/keystones;
- upgrade modules;
- remaining installed stabilisation stock.

Recovery depends on physical condition.

No generic portal refund token exists.

---

# 19. Destination Binding

Every portal stores validated destination information.

First destination must be canonically legal.

Later destinations require:

- physical discovery;
- mapping;
- validation;
- calibration;
- stabilisation where required.

Destination knowledge does not become disposable “dimension key” filler by default.

---

# 20. Portal Fault Feedback

A portal activation interface must expose readable reasons for failure, such as:

- incomplete frame;
- wrong core;
- missing stabiliser;
- unrecognised destination;
- unsafe calibration;
- insufficient power/mana;
- missing knowledge;
- damaged component;
- environmental failure.

A portal must not fail silently because of hidden state.

# PART I — COVENANT PORTAL

# 21. Covenant Portal Identity

**Covenant Portal** is the one canonical normal external portal family to the Verdant Covenant.

It is a rooted, living/magically attuned portal architecture.

It remains distinct from other realm portals.

---

# 22. Covenant Portal Canonical Objects

Canonical physical objects/components include:

- Covenant Portal Frame family;
- Covenant Keystone Socket;
- Covenant Heart;
- Carved Heart Core;
- Botanical Binding;
- Mana-Conductive Insert;
- Attunement Vessel;
- Verdant Growth Catalyst.

---

# 23. Standard Covenant Portal Assembly

A standard portal requires:

- **12 total Covenant Portal Frame-family pieces**
  - including 2 rooted-foot state/geometry pieces;
- **1 Covenant Keystone Socket**;
- **1 Covenant Heart**;
- one valid enclosed aperture;
- valid Verdant Resonance Attunement;
- valid Realm Access destination binding.

The rooted-foot pieces remain members/states of the same frame family.

---

# 24. Covenant Frame Recipe

One craft produces:

> **2 Covenant Portal Frame Blocks**

Inputs per craft:

- **2 units approved cut-stone structural material**;
- **1 unit approved structural hardwood/timber**;
- **1 unit approved plant-fibre/vine binding**;
- **1 low-tier mana-conductive or magical-attunement component**.

Six crafts provide the 12 frame-family pieces needed for the standard gate.

---

# 25. Covenant Cut-Stone Provider

The cut-stone slot is provider-based.

It requires:

- compatible stone;
- cuttable structural form;
- adequate durability/strength.

Canonical default candidates include:

- Granite;
- Limestone;
- Sandstone;
- Slate;
- Basalt;
- Marble;
- Gneiss;
- Quartzite.

Shale is not a default structural provider unless a qualifying processed grade/form meets the physical threshold.

---

# 26. Covenant Timber Provider

The timber slot is provider-based.

It requires a structural timber profile.

Eligible examples may include:

- Oak Heartwood;
- Ironbark;
- Blackwood;
- Beech;
- Redbark;
- Maple;
- other timbers that satisfy the structural threshold.

Botanical identity alone does not qualify a timber.

---

# 27. Covenant Plant Binding Provider

The binding slot is provider-based.

Accepted examples include:

- Sunflax Fibre;
- Mirehemp Fibre;
- processed Willowreed binding;
- Mixed Plant Fibre where it is a real mixed product.

`Plant Fibre` does not become a universal exact material.

---

# 28. Covenant Mana-Attunement Provider

The low-tier magical component is provider-based.

Default pre-realm sources include:

- Mana Shard derived from Raw Mana Crystal;
- qualifying Ley Crystal component.

The exact selected material remains recorded.

---

# 29. Covenant Keystone Socket Recipe

Output:

> **1 Covenant Keystone Socket**

Inputs:

- **2 Covenant Portal Frame Blocks or equivalent component value**;
- **1 approved universal precision metal fitting**;
- **1 approved magical-attunement component**.

The socket accepts a valid Covenant Heart.

---

# 30. Covenant Precision Fitting Provider

Eligible early metal providers include:

- Bronze;
- Copper;
- Iron;
- Steel;

where the fitting passes required workmanship/strength.

The fitting is a component, not a new material family.

---

# 31. Covenant Heart

The Covenant Heart is the exact functional core/keystone object for the portal.

Output:

> **1 Covenant Heart**

Required subcomponents:

- **1 Carved Heart Core**;
- **4 Botanical Binding units**;
- **2 Mana-Conductive Inserts**;
- **1 Attunement Vessel**;
- Verdant Resonance Attunement condition.

Verdant Resonance Attunement is knowledge/ritual state, not an Item.

---

# 32. Carved Heart Core

Carved Heart Core uses an approved Overworld structural timber/hardwood provider and carving capability.

The exact selected timber remains visible in composition/provenance.

---

# 33. Botanical Binding

Covenant Heart requires:

> **4 Botanical Binding units**

These use the accepted plant-binding provider rule.

They are physical fibre/binding components, not abstract “nature essence”.

---

# 34. Mana-Conductive Insert Recipe

Exact recipe:

> **1 Mana Shard + 1 Copper Wire → 1 Mana-Conductive Insert**

Covenant Heart requires:

> **2 Mana-Conductive Inserts**

A qualifying Ley Crystal may replace the Mana Shard only where the provider resolver confirms equivalent low-tier mana-conduction and the enabled recipe variant permits it.

---

# 35. Attunement Vessel

Attunement Vessel is an exact component archetype with material-flexible construction.

Eligible vessel materials include:

- ordinary Glass;
- Clear Quartz;

The actual selected material remains part of the object composition.

The mana charge is an energy transfer, not a generic Mana Essence Item.

---

# 37. Growth Catalyst Botanical Providers

Valid botanical inputs include healthy:

from approved Overworld botanical/crop families.

Portal subcomponent construction requires combinations of:

# 39. Covenant First Access

# PART II — VEILGATE

# 44. Veilgate Identity

**Veilgate** is the one canonical normal external portal family to the Ancestral Veil.

It is a quiet, recognised threshold rather than a generic violent rift.

Its completed physical contract preserves six exact Ancestral roles.

---

# 45. Veilgate Mandatory Roles

Every complete Veilgate contains:

1. Memory Stone frame/foundation;
2. Memory Glass threshold/interface;
3. Soul Silver spirit-conductive fittings;
4. Echo Pearl-based crafted keystone/core;
5. Grave Salt stabilisation;
6. recognition/inscription layer.

FCC-13 resolves these roles into the global construction contract.

---

# 46. Veilgate Standard Assembly

A full newly built Veilgate requires:

- **12 Memory Stone frame-family pieces**;
- **2 Memory Glass threshold/interface panels**;
- **4 Soul Silver fittings**;
- **1 Veil Keystone**;
- **2 Grave Salt stabilisation-channel units**;
- **1 Veil Recognition Plate**;
- valid recognition knowledge;
- valid Realm Access destination binding.

---

# 47. Veilgate First-Access Model

The first Overworld route uses:

> **a guaranteed historical Veilgate Remnant**

The remnant contains or preserves authentic Ancestral material roles needed for restoration.

It is the same Veilgate family.

It is not an Ancient Portal seventh family.

---

# 48. Guaranteed Veilgate Remnant

Progression/world validation guarantees at least one legitimate restoration route.

The remnant may contain:

- surviving Memory Stone;
- surviving Memory Glass;
- Soul Silver fittings;
- recoverable Veil Keystone or material required to restore it;
- Grave Salt channel stock;
- Recognition Plate/inscription evidence.

The exact condition may vary, but the route cannot depend solely on random loot.

---

# 49. Memory Stone Frame

A full new Veilgate uses:

> **12 Memory Stone frame-family pieces**

The geometry remains recognisably Ancestral and is not forced into a generic rectangular portal.

---

# 50. Memory Glass Interface

A full Veilgate uses:

> **2 Memory Glass threshold/interface panels**

Memory Glass remains an exact material role.

Ordinary Glass cannot satisfy this exact role.

---

# 51. Soul Silver Fittings

A full Veilgate uses:

> **4 Soul Silver fittings**

The fitting is a crafted precision component made from Soul Silver plate/wire using the generic precision-fitting process profile.

FCC-13D does not invent a new unsurveyed internal plate-to-fitting quantity.

---

# 52. Veil Keystone

The Echo Pearl role is resolved into a crafted exact component:

> **Veil Keystone**

Canonical object identity:

`leyforge:object/portal/veilgate/keystone`

It is a component, not a material.

---

# 53. Veil Keystone Echo Pearl Requirement

Each Veil Keystone contains:

> **1 Echo Pearl**

The Echo Pearl is processed/calibrated into the crafted core rather than installed raw as the entire portal core.

---

# 54. Veil Keystone Supporting Construction

The accepted interview locks the Echo Pearl quantity and Veil Keystone identity but does not lock a new numerical supporting-material micro-recipe.

Therefore:

- supporting core housing/conduction uses the canonical Veilgate material/component roles;
- the component recipe follows FCC-13C exact/provider conservation;
- no additional exact material count is invented in this writing pass.

The final component data row must preserve the Echo Pearl as the identity-defining core material.

---

# 55. Grave Salt Stabilisation

A complete Veilgate uses:

> **2 Grave Salt stabilisation-channel units**

These are installed/replenishable stabilisation stock.

They are not consumed every activation by default.

---

# 56. Veil Recognition Plate

Exact component:

> **Veil Recognition Plate**

Recipe:

- **1 fired Spirit Clay tablet**;
- **1 Ancestor Ash inscription dose**.

Output:

> **1 Veil Recognition Plate**

---

# 57. Recognition Knowledge

The Recognition Plate is physical.

Recognition/inscription understanding remains a separate knowledge/permission condition.

Possessing the Plate without valid recognition knowledge is not automatically sufficient for activation.

---

# 58. Veilgate First Access

First access does not require prior entry to another realm.

The guaranteed remnant solves the Ancestral-material circularity through legitimate historical provenance.

Universal support materials/tools may assist restoration but do not replace the six completed Ancestral identity roles.

---

# 59. Veilgate Activation Consumable

Default:

> **none**

Grave Salt is stabilisation stock, not a per-activation payment.

---

# 60. Veilgate Repair

After Ancestral access:

- Memory Glass role → Memory Glass;
- Soul Silver fittings → Soul Silver;
- Veil Keystone → exact Keystone repair/rebuild;

- Memory Glass panels;

- Veil Keystone;

# 62. Veilgate Upgrades

Later upgrades may improve:

- stability;
- safety;
- recognition handling;
- cargo;
- destination reliability.
