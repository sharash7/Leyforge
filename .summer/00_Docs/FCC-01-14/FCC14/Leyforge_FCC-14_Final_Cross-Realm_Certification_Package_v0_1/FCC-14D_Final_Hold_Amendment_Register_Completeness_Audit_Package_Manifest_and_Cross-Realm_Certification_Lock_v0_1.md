# 12. Final Protected Cross-Realm Doctrines

FCC-14D does not duplicate all 126 FCC-14A invariants. The following are the highest-level protections that must survive all downstream work.

## 12.1 Authority doctrine

- FCC-14 certifies; it does not seize detailed realm/material/registry/runtime ownership.
- Realm A–J packages retain detailed local content ownership.
- FCC-12 retains universal material semantics.
- FCC-13 retains stable identity/physical binding/recipe/portal/migration semantics.
- engineering documents implement rather than rewrite certified meaning.

## 12.2 Travel doctrine

Thematic similarity is not inter-realm travel.

Ordinary sleep, death, deep digging, altitude, volcanism, divine visions, Void exposure, roots, graves, caves or local intra-realm routes do not create hidden normal external portal bypasses.

## 12.3 Matter doctrine

- material identity persists across realm boundaries;
- provider equivalence does not merge materials;
- form does not automatically create new matter;
- state/provenance/quality remain separate dimensions where applicable;
- generated standard forms inherit source material identity;
- specialist authored physical forms remain valid where semantic geometry requires them.

## 12.4 Ecology doctrine

- origin/provenance remains distinct from current suitability;
- foreign does not automatically mean invasive;
- naturalisation does not erase provenance;
- environmental support/capability decides survival/function rather than realm-name shortcuts;
- cross-realm organisms and goods require legitimate transfer paths.

## 12.5 Event doctrine

Cross-realm consequences require an explainable source, transfer vector, transferred subject/information, receiving owner, scope and persistence.

The receiving realm does not become the source realm merely because a consequence arrives there.

## 12.6 Void doctrine

Void corruption requires actual Void provenance.

Native Verdant blight/rot, Ancestral predatory darkness, Somnolent nightmare/coherence failure, Ascendant law/divine conflict, Impossible Deep pressure/Blackwater/gravity/folds and Ashen infernal conditions do not become Void corruption merely because they are dangerous, strange or dark.

## 12.7 Personhood doctrine

Realm origin, hostility, infernal/divine/dream/death association, construct origin, corruption or authority/boss role does not automatically erase sapient personhood.

## 12.8 Restoration doctrine

Damage, occupation, corruption, cleansing, repair and restoration are not automatic history erasure.

Persistent aftermath/history remains meaningful unless an owning source explicitly establishes otherwise.
