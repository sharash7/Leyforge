# 1. Purpose

FCC-14B is the final **semantic art-handoff authority** for the current Leyforge Final Content Canon scope.

It translates the certified project-wide meanings in FCC-14A into a maintainable set of presentation requirements so later global material/style governance, realm-specific art-direction documents, The Forge, UI/UX and production asset work can answer:

> **What must remain visually or otherwise perceptually distinguishable so that later art does not accidentally rewrite canon?**

FCC-14B locks **semantic readability obligations**.

It does **not** choose the final art itself.

The governing rule remains:

> **CERTIFY WHAT IS LOCKED; ROUTE WHAT BELONGS ELSEWHERE; REOPEN ONLY A GENUINE CONTRADICTION.**

The primary composition doctrine is:

> **Material Identity + Physical Form + Condition/State + Provenance + Relevant Quality/Grade Dimensions + Approved Contextual Overlays = resolved presentation.**

Not every dimension must always be visible. A distinction must become perceptible when recognition, gameplay, safety, provenance, ecology, law, crafting, construction, restoration or player comprehension depends on it.

# 4. Semantic Presentation Doctrine

## 4.1 Identity hierarchy

Later presentation resolves the following layers in order:

1. **Canonical identity** — what physical material/object/organism/site/portal actually is.
2. **Physical form** — block, slab, beam, pane, rod, plate, organism body, machine assembly, portal structure, etc.
3. **Current condition/state** — damaged, wet, charged, dormant, unstable, occupied, corrupted, restored, etc.
4. **Provenance/history** — source realm, trade/import history, occupation history, restoration history or other consequential origin.
5. **Quality/grade/purity/composition** — only where the owning canon uses those dimensions.
6. **Contextual overlays** — culture, faction, law, biome, climate, heraldry, decoration, ritual use and similar presentation layers.

A downstream art system may combine these efficiently, but may not reverse their semantic order.

## 4.2 Material identity is not shape

A material remains the same material when it appears in eligible standard forms.

Therefore a canonical material should remain recognisably related across compatible:

- full blocks;
- slabs;
- stairs;
- beams;
- pillars;
- panes;
- rods;
- plates;
- wire;
- trim;
- other approved generated forms.

Shape can change silhouette and function without inventing new matter.

## 4.3 State is not automatically identity

Wear, damage, wetness, heat, charge, dormancy, occupation, instability, corruption and restoration are normally state/condition layers.

A new canonical identity exists only where the owning FCC material/content authority crosses its accepted irreversible-transformation threshold.

## 4.4 Provenance is selective presentation

Provenance is authoritative even when invisible.

Visual provenance cues are required only where origin affects:

- identification;
- ecology;
- law/ownership;
- trade;
- safety;
- support/environment requirements;
- restoration;
- narrative or Codex comprehension.

Being imported does not automatically require a permanent visual recolour.

## 4.5 Provider equivalence is not material equivalence

Two materials may satisfy the same provider/capability role while remaining distinct exact identities.

Art must not turn a valid substitute relationship into visual evidence that the materials are the same substance.

## 4.6 Quality is not one rarity colour

Quality, grade, purity and composition are multidimensional semantics.

FCC-14B rejects any assumption that they must collapse into one universal:

- rarity colour;
- star count;
- glow tier;
- border tier;
- brighter-is-better rule.

A later UI/art system may use such tools for specific dimensions, but only where the owning system defines the meaning.

## 4.7 Generated forms versus specialist authored forms

Generated standard forms should inherit canonical material identity.

Specialist authored geometry remains valid for content whose physical meaning requires unique structure, including:

- machines;
- portal cores/components;
- magical devices;
- unique structural mechanisms;
- specialist biological/constructed forms;
- content with sockets, ports, pivots or moving parts.

# 5. Visual Distinction Classification

FCC-14B uses four distinction classes.

## Class A — Identity-Critical Distinction

The player/authoring tool must be able to tell two canonical identities apart whenever confusion could alter gameplay, crafting, construction, ecology, provenance, portal use, law, restoration or migration.

## Class B — State-Critical Distinction

The same canonical identity must remain recognisable while important current states remain perceptible.

Examples include damage, instability, quarantine, corruption and restoration.

## Class C — Contextual/Provenance Distinction

A difference may be shown selectively when provenance, occupation, culture, ecology, law or history matters, but it must not be mistaken for a new base identity.

## Class D — Optional Cosmetic Variation

Decorative variation may exist freely when it does not alter gameplay truth, stable identity or player safety.

No Class D variation may visually masquerade as a Class A or Class B distinction.

# 10. Native State vs Void Corruption Doctrine

## 10.1 Global rule

> **Void corruption requires actual Void provenance.**

Later art must not use one generic “dark, purple, black, glitchy, scary, corrupted” visual shorthand to classify all dangerous supernatural content.

Native states may be frightening, destructive, hostile or physically strange while remaining native.

## 10.2 Realm-specific separations

- Verdant natural rot ≠ blight ≠ Void corruption.
- Ancestral Predator Dark/native memory hazard ≠ Void corruption.
- Somnolent nightmare/low coherence/Waking Fracture/Deep Dream ≠ Void corruption.
- Ascendant storm/gravity/Sacred Law/divine hostility ≠ Void corruption.
- Impossible Deep pressure/gravity/Stoneflow/Blackwater/Fold Shift ≠ Void corruption.
- Ashen heat/ash/war/contract/hunger/industrial crisis ≠ Void corruption.

## 10.3 Corruption presentation requirements

Genuine Void-corrupted presentation must:

- be identifiable as corruption through redundant cues when gameplay depends on it;
- preserve enough underlying base identity for provenance and restoration;
- not automatically imply moral alignment;
- not automatically create a new species/material/portal family;
- remain distinct from ordinary damage/fault/restriction;
- remain compatible with reduced-effects accessibility/performance modes.
