It reconciles FCC-06A through FCC-06I into one final realm-local manifest, verifies theoretical completeness, records preserved IDs and migrations, separates intentionally deferred global decisions from missing design, and provides the content handoff for later realm art documents.

# 2. Realm Certification Statement

The **Impossible Deep** is a distinct, persistent, portal-entered major realm of effectively vast or potentially unbounded underground reality.

Its identity includes world-scale caverns, fossil continents, Blackwater Vault-Seas, pressure systems, folded routes, Stoneflow, seismic geology, non-standard gravity and ancient civilisations.

It is not literal Overworld geology continued downward, is not reachable by digging indefinitely, is not inherently Void-corrupted, and is not one unified underworld empire.

The current FCC locks its playable/systemic foundation while preserving later exploration of its effectively infinite scale.

# 4. Stable Realm & Portal Identity

**Realm ID:** `realm.major.impossible_deep`  
**Canonical portal:** **Deepgate**

Deepgate is the normal physical interdimensional entry/exit method. Worldroots, Abyss Lifts, folds, tunnels, shafts and machine routes remain internal infrastructure, clues or interfaces and cannot replace the Deepgate.

The Deepgate contract is:

1. reinforced structural frame;
2. stabilisation/anchoring layer;
3. crafted **Deep Keystone**;
4. pressure/depth attunement;
5. activation interface;
6. validated destination binding.

The first Deepgate cannot require inaccessible Impossible Deep-native materials. Later upgrades may use Pressure Stone, Abyssal Iron, Seismic Crystal, Pressure Pearl, Ventglass, Gravity Nodule technology and Deepfire Ember.

Exact portal recipe and quantities remain globally deferred.

Universal Leyforge death/respawn remains authoritative.

# 24. Corruption Registry

Native authorities and fauna are not automatically Void-corrupted.
