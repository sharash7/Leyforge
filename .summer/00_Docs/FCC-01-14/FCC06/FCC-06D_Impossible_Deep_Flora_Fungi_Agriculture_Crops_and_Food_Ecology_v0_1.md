- native producer ecology;

# 2. Canon Statement

Impossible Deep does **not** use ordinary surface photosynthesis as its universal native ecological baseline.

Its native producer systems can derive usable energy and matter through combinations of:

- geothermal energy;
- chemosynthesis;
- mineral symbiosis;
- fungal networks;
- microbial mats;
- pressure-adapted biological chemistry;
- mana or realm-native magical energy where justified;
- bioluminescent ecological systems;
- specialised local light;
- deep-fluid chemistry;
- vibration-responsive growth;
- long-duration mineral exchange.

Imported surface plants can exist, but typically require engineered habitat.

The realm therefore supports a living, cultivable underground biosphere without pretending that every cavern is an ordinary green forest hidden below ground.

---

# 3. Producer-Ecology Principles

Impossible Deep producer ecology follows these rules:

1. **Native producer life is biome-adapted.**
2. **No single energy pathway dominates the whole realm.**
3. **Photosynthesis may occur where light exists, but is not the universal native baseline.**
4. **Fungi, mats, mineral-symbiotic organisms and plant-like life all contribute to food webs.**
5. **Many producer organisms are pressure-, gravity-, heat-, brine- or vibration-sensitive.**
6. **Imported agriculture is possible through engineering rather than prohibited by arbitrary realm law.**
7. **The same producer family may have local ecotypes without creating a separate registry family for every biome.**
8. **Cultivated forms may differ from wild forms while remaining the same biological lineage.**
9. **A harvested material remains owned by FCC-06C even when FCC-06D owns the producer organism.**
10. **Food recipes and final processed products belong to FCC-06G/FCC-13.**

# 5. Resonance Tree

## 5.1 Identity

The **Resonance Tree** is the principal woody/structural producer lineage of the Seismic Forests.

Despite the name, it does not need to resemble an Overworld tree.

Canonical morphology is broadly:

> a stone-organic columnar organism with branching resonant growths and woody/mineral internal fibres.

## 5.2 Ecological Role

Resonance Trees:

- anchor Seismic Forest producer ecology;
- respond to vibration;
- provide habitat;
- exchange minerals with surrounding stone;
- support fungi and small fauna;
- affect local resonance behaviour;
- create quiet or resonant microhabitats depending on state.

## 5.3 Material Provenance

Resonance Trees produce **Resonance Wood**.

FCC-06C owns the material definition.

FCC-06D owns:

- organism biology;
- growth;
- harvesting ecology;
- cultivation conditions;
- biome role.

## 5.4 Vibration Response

Resonance Trees may respond to:

- tremors;
- Seismic Caller activity;
- Seismic Choir output;
- mining;
- industrial vibration;
- large migrations;
- regional Seismic Swarms.

Responses may include:

- growth change;
- branch orientation;
- temporary rigidity;
- resonance pulses;
- defensive closure;
- fruiting/growth-state change in associated ecology.

Not every vibration causes a dramatic event.

# 23. Native Agriculture Philosophy

Impossible Deep agriculture is **possible, important and specialised**.

The realm does not impose:

- universal barren-cave starvation;
- arbitrary crop prohibition;
- automatic failure of all surface agriculture;
- one single farming method.

Native agriculture can use:

- pressure gardens;
- sealed farms;
- fungal halls;
- brine terraces;
- seismic orchards;
- thermal farms;
- controlled gravity plots;
- aquaculture;
- producer-mat cultivation.

This creates agricultural diversity suited to different societies and biomes.

Sealed farms are a major settlement technology because they allow communities to control:

- pressure;

# 25. Imported Agriculture

Overworld, Verdant or other compatible crops may be cultivated in Impossible Deep.

They generally require engineered conditions such as:

- pressure normalisation;
- artificial or magical light;
- controlled soil;
- safe water;
- stable temperature;
- breathable atmosphere;
- protection from local toxins;
- gravity compatibility.

Imported crops must not simply naturalise across every Deep biome by default.
