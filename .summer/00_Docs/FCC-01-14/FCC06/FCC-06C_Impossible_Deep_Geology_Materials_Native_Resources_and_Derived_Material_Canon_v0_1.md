# 2. Canon Statement

Impossible Deep uses a **small number of coherent material lineages with meaningful regional states and transformations**, rather than one unrelated material for every biome concept.

The realm's material identity is organised into:

## 2.1 Foundation Geology

- **Pressure Stone** — principal native structural stone and ordinary Deep geological family.
- **Bone Stone** — mineralised fossil matrix formed from colossal ancient remains.
- **Emberbone** — Bone Stone transformed by prolonged deep heat, magma and forge conditions.

## 2.2 Principal Native Metal

- **Abyssal Iron** — principal native metallic ore lineage.

## 2.3 Principal Crystal Lineage

- **Seismic Crystal** — principal native resonance/seismic crystal family.
  - Orientation Crystal state/form.
  - Compressed Crystal state/form.
  - Coreward/Core Crystal state/form.

## 2.4 Major Specialist Native Resources

- **Pressure Pearl**
- **Black Brine Salt**
- **Ventglass**
- **Deepfire Ember**

## 2.5 Additional Specialist Native Resources

- **Pressure Resin**
- **Gravity Nodule**
- **Pressure Oils**

## 2.6 Archaeological / Manufactured Material Category

- **Fossil Alloys** — ancient manufactured alloys recovered from Fossil Metropolises and related archaeological sites.

This hierarchy is authoritative for FCC-06.

Future expansion may add genuinely distinct materials where provenance, physical identity or system role requires them, but may not multiply synonyms, biome-labelled duplicates or cosmetic-only resource families without reconciliation.

# 3. Material Classification Rules

Impossible Deep materials must be classified by **what the matter actually is**, not merely where it was found.

The realm follows these rules:

1. **A new biome does not automatically require a new stone, metal, crystal, wood, fibre or fuel family.**
2. **A regional condition remains a state/facies when the underlying matter remains materially continuous.**
3. **A distinct material family is justified when provenance, composition, transformation, behaviour or processing is materially different enough to require separate canon.**
4. **A crafted product is not reclassified as a native resource simply because it is strongly associated with the realm.**
5. **Archaeological manufactured matter remains manufactured matter.**
6. **Creature anatomy does not become geology merely because it mineralises after death.**
7. **Fossilised ancient material can become geology when mineral replacement and geological transformation have genuinely changed its material identity.**
8. **Placeable/recoverable Blocks do not receive duplicate separately authored Item definitions merely to exist in inventory.**
9. **States and variants remain states unless irreversible transformation creates genuinely new matter.**
10. **Final global material namespace normalisation belongs to FCC-12/FCC-13.**

# 4. Principal Geological Family — Pressure Stone

## 4.1 Identity

**Pressure Stone** is the principal native structural stone of Impossible Deep.

It is the default geological family for ordinary:

- cavern walls;
- tunnel strata;
- structural shelves;
- deep foundations;
- pressure basins;
- gravity-conditioned formations;
- mined building stone;
- pressure-resistant masonry.

It provides the realm with a coherent native stone foundation rather than requiring every biome to invent a separate structural geology.

## 4.2 Formation

Pressure Stone forms across broad Deep geological conditions under long-duration:

- compression;
- mineral deposition;
- tectonic activity;
- thermal cycling;
- high-pressure fluid interaction;
- realm-native impossible-geology conditions.

Its exact mineral composition may vary regionally without requiring separate top-level material families.

## 4.3 Gameplay Roles

Pressure Stone supports:

- ordinary construction;
- deep foundations;
- pressure architecture;
- retaining structures;
- sealed chambers;
- structural bracing;
- tunnels;
- Deepgate reinforcement;
- Deep Forge construction;
- Pressure Sanctuary construction;
- Abyss Lift support structures.

Its final harvest tier and exact structural values remain deferred.

## 4.4 Canonical Derived Forms

Justified Pressure Stone forms include:

- natural Pressure Stone;
- cut Pressure Stone;
- Pressure Stone brick;
- slab;
- stair;
- wall;
- pillar;
- pressure arch;
- selected reinforced structural components.

The derived-form system must not generate every conceivable decorative shape automatically.

# 5. Pressure Stone Regional Facies & States

## 5.1 Inverted Stone

**Inverted Stone** is not a separate geological family.

It is a gravity-conditioned Pressure Stone facies/state formed under long-duration non-conventional gravity.

It may exhibit:

- altered fracture direction;
- unusual bedding/orientation;
- changed load behaviour;
- gravity-aligned growth;
- distinct structural suitability.

Its underlying geological family remains Pressure Stone.

## 5.2 Floating Stone

**Floating Stone** is not a separate stone family.

It is a gravity-conditioned Pressure Stone state/facies associated with Gravity Wells and compatible anomaly regions.

Its apparent weight or local movement may differ under compatible gravity conditions.

It does not imply universal levitation outside suitable gravity/anomaly systems.

## 5.3 Other Local Facies

Additional Pressure Stone facies may exist where justified by:

- Blackwater mineralisation;
- deep thermal alteration;
- extreme compression;
- Stoneflow history;
- Coreward formation;
- fossil adjacency.

Such facies remain Pressure Stone unless the transformation creates genuinely distinct material matter.

# 6. Legacy Deepstone Boundary

The pre-FCC registry contains a generic **Deepstone** concept.

FCC-06 does **not** convert that legacy generic block into the Impossible Deep's principal native stone.

Canonical resolution:

- **Deepstone** remains a generic/deep-geology concept pending FCC-12/FCC-13 reconciliation.
- **Pressure Stone** owns Impossible Deep's distinctive structural geology.
- Existing legacy Deepstone references may still appear in older content or Overworld deep-strata systems.
- Final global reconciliation decides whether Deepstone remains an active generic family, becomes a tag/category, or is otherwise normalised.

Impossible Deep must not depend on Deepstone as a substitute for Pressure Stone.

Its prior intended roles are reconciled into:

# 8. Fossil Geology — Bone Stone

## 8.1 Identity

**Bone Stone** is mineralised fossil material formed from ancient colossal biological remains after geological replacement, compression and long-term mineralisation.

It is no longer ordinary fresh bone.

## 8.2 Provenance

Bone Stone may occur in:

- Emberbone Forges;
- Fossil Metropolises;
- fossil continents;
- buried megafauna;
- ancient organism strata;
- Coreward fossil systems.

Not every colossal fossil shares the same biological origin.

## 8.3 Canonical Forms

Justified forms include:

- natural Bone Stone;
- cut Bone Stone;
- structural fossil block;
- selected slab/pillar/carved forms where culturally or architecturally justified;
- archaeological samples.

## 8.4 Ethical / Lore Boundary

Harvesting Bone Stone does not inherently mean harvesting a freshly killed titan or person.

Most canonical Bone Stone is ancient mineralised geological matter.

Some cultures may still regard particular fossil formations as:

- sacred;
- ancestral;
- protected;
- politically claimed;
- scientifically restricted.

Those are civilisation/provenance rules rather than a universal material prohibition.

# 27. Stoneflow Material Boundary

Stoneflow is an environmental/geological **state behaviour**, not a separate generic resource family.

A Stoneflow-capable region may contain:

- Pressure Stone;
- mineral inclusions;
- Abyssal Iron-bearing strata;
- fossil material;
- thermal material.

Stoneflow activation does not automatically rename the affected matter to “Stoneflow Stone.”

If irreversible flow/heat/pressure transformation creates genuinely different matter, that new matter requires explicit future material justification.

# 28. Material Harvest & Tool-Capability Principles

FCC-06C does not assign final numeric harvest tiers, but it locks the following principles:

1. material harvesting follows universal tool/material capability rules;
2. no realm arbitrarily invalidates a sufficiently capable tool merely because it came from another realm;
3. stronger Deep materials may require higher universal harvest capability;
4. specialised hazards may require survival or engineering preparation in addition to mining capability;
5. a tool capable of cutting the material is not automatically sufficient to survive the environment around it;
6. creature-produced materials follow creature/ecology harvesting rules;
7. archaeological materials may be physically harvestable while still carrying political, legal or cultural consequences.

Exact capability tiers remain deferred to FCC-12/FCC-13 and universal tool progression.

# 29. Processing Philosophy

Impossible Deep reuses universal processing systems wherever possible.

Ordinary operations such as:

- stone cutting;
- woodworking;
- basic metalworking;
- simple refining;
- ordinary crafting;

- transport

should use universal stations and systems unless Deep conditions genuinely change the process.

Realm-specific infrastructure is justified when it performs materially distinctive work.

# 32. Derived-Form Discipline

Impossible Deep follows Leyforge's global justified-form rule.

A material may receive a derived form when that form has an actual gameplay, building, equipment, machine, traversal, industrial or cultural role.

The realm must avoid automatic production of:

- every stone × every shape;
- every wood × every furniture form;
- every crystal × every block shape;
- every reagent × decorative block;
- every specialist material × weapon/tool tier.

This preserves:

- registry clarity;
- asset workload;
- visual identity;
- crafting readability;
- material meaning.
