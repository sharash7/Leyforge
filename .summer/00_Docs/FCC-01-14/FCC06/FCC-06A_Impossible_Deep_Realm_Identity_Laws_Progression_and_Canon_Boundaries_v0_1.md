# 2. Canon Statement

The **Impossible Deep** is a distinct, persistent, portal-entered major realm in which the idea of geological depth has become physically impossible.

Its central fantasy is:

> **An effectively vast underground reality of nested cavern-worlds, pressure seas, inverted mountains, fossil continents, changing gravity, folded distance, flowing stone and seismic civilisation — a place where “deeper” remains meaningful even when local physical down no longer does.**

The realm is not merely an unusually large cave biome.

It must support the feeling that entire civilisations, ecosystems and historical eras can exist beneath or beyond other civilisations and ecosystems, separated by enormous distances, pressure boundaries, folded strata, deep oceans, unstable routes and regions that may never have been mapped.

Its scale may include:

- caverns large enough to contain their own horizons;
- nested cavern systems inside larger cavern systems;
- entire seas sealed inside geological vaults;
- inverted landscapes attached to ceilings or walls;
- continent-scale fossils incorporated into terrain and settlement;
- world-scale fractures extending toward Coreward regions;
- Gravity Wells with multiple local gravity centres;
- routes whose topological distance differs from apparent geometric distance;
- deep regions whose existence may be known only through seismic evidence, ancient records or indirect observation.

The realm remains a coherent playable world rather than unrestricted impossible-space simulation.

Its impossible geometry, pressure, gravity, Stoneflow and seismic behaviour are expressed through **bounded, deterministic, save-safe gameplay systems**.

# 3. Explicit Non-Identity: What the Impossible Deep Is Not

The Impossible Deep is **not**:

- literally the lowest layer of normal Overworld geology;
- reachable by simply mining far enough downward from the Overworld;
- a hidden continuation of ordinary Overworld chunks;
- a second Overworld cave-generation system;
- a generic lava dimension;
- a universal “underground realm” containing every cave concept in Leyforge;
- a simple stack of increasingly difficult horizontal floors;
- required to obey one universal local down direction;
- required to contain a mundane planetary core at its deepest point;
- a random-teleport maze in which maps and routes are meaningless;
- a realm where every rock block behaves like liquid;
- a realm where pressure is simulated continuously for every voxel;
- a realm where gravity is arbitrarily calculated per block;
- a realm where earthquakes randomly delete settlements without warning;
- a realm where every dark, strange or impossible phenomenon is Void corruption;
- an alternate route into the Void Between;
- a place where folds, roots, lifts or shafts bypass canonical realm portals;
- the owner of universal player death or respawn;
- an excuse to duplicate universal Blocks, Items, machines or settlement systems under “Deep” names.

These exclusions are canon, not implementation suggestions.

Older source material that implied direct physical descent from the Overworld or multiple ordinary access families is narrowed by FCC-06.

# 5. Realm Fantasy Pillars

## 5.1 Depth Is Conceptual as Well as Geometric

Impossible Deep preserves a broad sense of progression toward deeper and more extreme regions, but local orientation may diverge from that progression.

A route may require the traveller to:

- descend conventionally;
- climb relative to local gravity;
- cross a Gravity Well;
- traverse an inverted mountain;
- move horizontally through a folded passage;
- descend through water rather than air;
- pass through multiple sealed pressure bands;
- use a lift whose local movement does not correspond to global “down.”

Therefore:

> **Local physical down and realm-conceptual deeper are related but not identical.**

The realm may be mapped through discovered route and regional relationships rather than one simple global vertical coordinate.

## 5.2 Pressure Defines Civilisation

Pressure is not only a player hazard.

It shapes:

- settlement architecture;
- transport;
- farming;
- storage;
- materials;
- creature biology;
- water behaviour;
- mining;
- industrial processing;
- emergency planning;
- trade routes.

Communities survive through engineering, adaptation, biological compatibility, magic, specialist materials and controlled habitat boundaries.

## 5.3 Gravity Is Regional

Gravity varies by chamber and region.

Possible profiles include:

- conventional downward gravity;
- inverted gravity;
- sideways or wall-oriented gravity;
- weak gravity;
- heavy gravity;
- multiple-centre Gravity Wells;
- bounded transition/shear zones.

These are explicit regional profiles, not unrestricted per-voxel gravity simulation.

## 5.4 Stone Can Behave Like Geology in Motion

Some Deep geology enters **Stoneflow** states in which rock slowly deforms, migrates, channels or advances under compatible pressure, heat, magic and geological conditions.

Stoneflow may:

- open routes;
- close routes;
- expose deposits;
- bury structures;
- reshape tunnels;
- threaten foundations;
- create new terrain states.

Stoneflow does not mean every stone block behaves as ordinary fluid.

## 5.5 Routes Can Fold Without Becoming Random

Folded strata may produce topological relationships that do not match ordinary Euclidean expectations.

A passage may:

- connect two regions that appear extremely distant;
- become much longer than its apparent geometry suggests;
- close and reopen through a bounded valid state;
- reveal previously unreachable branches;
- change the practical relationship between settlements.

Maps remain meaningful because they record discovered route topology and state.

## 5.6 Seismic Change Is World History

Earthquakes and tectonic activity are not merely combat hazards.

They can alter:

- roads;
- caves;
- mines;
- pressure boundaries;
- settlements;
- archaeological access;
- resource distribution;
- creature migration;
- Blackwater systems;
- Stoneflow activity;
- dungeon state.

Large geological events leave persistent evidence.

## 5.7 Civilisation Exists Deep Below Civilisation

Impossible Deep contains permanent cultures whose history does not revolve around the player or the Overworld.

Some communities may be separated by such great distance, pressure or topology that they have:

- never met;
- only traded indirectly;
- preserved incompatible historical accounts;
- developed different maps of what “deeper” means;
- known of distant regions only through seismic evidence, relics or legend.

## 5.8 The Deep Can Remain Unknown

FCC-06 defines a complete playable content foundation without requiring the realm to become fully knowable.

The existence of:

- unmapped cavern-worlds;
- unknown ecologies;
- unreachable strata;
- ancient histories beneath other ancient histories;
- deeper regions beyond current progression;
- uncertain ultimate depth

is compatible with canon.

Future expansions may explore those possibilities without requiring a rewrite of this document.

# 7. Canonical Physical Law Families

Impossible Deep uses bounded law families rather than unrestricted physical simulation.

## 7.1 Pressure Profile Law

Pressure is represented through explicit regional, chamber or fluid-environment profiles.

Profiles may include relative states such as:

- low/ordinary Deep pressure;
- elevated pressure;
- severe pressure;
- extreme pressure;
- crushing pressure;
- specialist fluid-pressure environments.

These profiles are consumed by universal systems for:

- player survival;
- creatures;
- structures;
- fluids;
- vehicles;
- machinery;
- equipment.

FCC-06 does not require real-time computational fluid dynamics across the whole realm.

### Pressure Transition Rule

Major pressure changes must be readable through compatible content such as:

- locks;
- seals;
- decompression chambers;
- pressure doors;
- fluid transitions;
- architecture;
- instruments;
- visible biological adaptation;
- warning systems.

Routine traversal should not cross invisible one-block boundaries into unexplained lethal pressure.

## 7.2 Structural Pressure Law

Settlement and machine viability may depend on regional pressure class.

Compatible infrastructure may require:

- reinforcement;
- seals;
- bracing;
- compartmentation;
- pressure-compatible materials;
- emergency isolation;
- maintenance.

This is a bounded construction/simulation requirement rather than full structural-engineering physics.

## 7.3 Regional Gravity Law

Gravity is defined through explicit region and transition profiles.

It may influence:

- movement;
- fluids;
- falling objects;
- structures;
- creature locomotion;
- transport;
- mining;
- orientation.

The game must not calculate arbitrary gravity separately for every ordinary block.

### Gravity Readability Rule

Gravity transitions should be readable through environmental or engineered cues such as:

- debris orientation;
- waterfalls;
- plant growth direction;
- architecture;
- Orientation Crystals;
- dust movement;
- route markers;
- specialised instruments.

## 7.4 Folded-Distance Law

Folded strata create authored or deterministic intra-realm topological relationships.

Valid folds may:

- shorten travel;
- lengthen travel;
- connect non-adjacent regions;
- close;
- expose new branches;
- reconnect within the realm.

They do **not**:

- randomly teleport the player without state;
- replace mapping;
- create uncontrolled save divergence;
- act as alternate portals into other realms.

## 7.5 Stoneflow Law

Only Stoneflow-capable geology and compatible regional/event states support active Stoneflow.

Player construction in Stoneflow regions may require:

- stabilisation;
- reinforced foundations;
- barriers;
- bypass routes;
- evacuation planning.

Unsafe construction can be damaged through readable, deterministic hazard states.

## 7.6 Seismic Law

Tremors, earthquakes, tectonic shifts and deep resonance may alter terrain through bounded validated transitions.

Possible consequences include:

- route opening;
- route closure;
- cracks;
- resource exposure;
- pressure change;
- Stoneflow activation;
- creature migration;
- infrastructure damage;
- archaeological exposure.

### Seismic Fairness Rule

Major physical seismic events generally expose appropriate precursors where practical.

Precursors may include:

- Seismic Crystal response;
- Seismic Caller behaviour;
- Seismic Choir data;
- tremor sequences;
- fluid behaviour;
- geological sounds;
- NPC warnings;
- historic risk knowledge.

Routine simulation does not arbitrarily erase settlements without readable cause or counterplay.

# 8. Overworld Relationship and Deep-Strata Boundary

Impossible Deep is strongly affiliated with Overworld deep exploration, but it remains a separate realm.

Deep Overworld strata may reveal:

- geological resonance;
- unusual seismic patterns;
- impossible-depth clues;
- ancient ruins;
- Deepgate knowledge;
- related materials or fragments where deliberately authored;
- old stabilisation infrastructure;
- cultural evidence of prior Deep contact.

Deep Overworld mining does **not** physically transition into Impossible Deep without a Deepgate.

No world seed may silently treat a sufficiently low Y-coordinate as interdimensional passage.

This preserves:

- the one-portal-per-realm rule;
- save integrity;
- realm world ownership;
- clear progression;
- coherent generation boundaries.

# 9. Universal Death and Respawn Ownership Exclusion

Impossible Deep does not own universal player death, respawn or recovery.

## 9.1 Player Death

Player death remains governed by Leyforge's universal death/failure system.

Depending on universal progression and settings, recovery may involve:

- a valid bed;
- shrine;
- home;
- village;
- world spawn;
- tombstone or equivalent recovery object;
- another universally defined respawn point.

Dying from:

- pressure;
- heat;
- gravity;
- collapse;
- Blackwater;
- Stoneflow;
- a Coreworm;
- a Realm Authority

does not create a realm-specific respawn system.

## 9.2 No Depth-Based Afterlife

Falling, drowning, being crushed, reaching an extreme Coreward region or dying near a Fossil Crown does not redirect the player into another realm or special Deep afterlife.

## 9.3 Authority Exclusion

The following do not own universal death/respawn:

- Pressure Titan;
- Blackwater Matriarch;
- Fossil Crown;
- Deepgate;
- Deep civilisation institutions;
- Seismic Choirs;
- Worldroot Bastions.

# 10. Canonical Portal Access Contract

All normal physical entry and exit use one canonical realm portal:

# **Deepgate**

The universal Realm Access system owns:

- portal runtime;
- transition orchestration;
- destination resolution;
- save/load behaviour;
- multiplayer authority;
- portal linking;
- safe return;
- failure and recovery;
- destination-binding framework.

FCC-06 owns Deepgate's:

- identity;
- physical-content contract;
- Deep-specific stabilisation concept;
- progression role;
- first-arrival envelope;
- Deep-native upgrade relationship;
- realm-local discovery hooks.

## 10.1 Deepgate Identity

A Deepgate is a **heavily anchored threshold built to express compression, depth, stability and controlled passage**.

It is not required to look like an open hole in the ground.

Its realm-local physical contract includes:

1. **reinforced structural frame**;
2. **stabilisation/anchoring layer**;
3. crafted **Deep Keystone**;
4. **pressure/depth attunement**;
5. **activation interface**;
6. **validated destination binding**.

The exact final materials, quantities and cross-realm dependencies are intentionally deferred to global portal progression reconciliation.

## 10.2 First-Entry Rule

The first successful Deepgate entry must resolve to a validated low-to-moderate-risk arrival region suitable for:

- recovery;
- orientation;
- initial pressure adaptation;
- Pressure Sanctuary support;
- a valid return route.

First entry should teach the realm rather than immediately place the player inside an unknowable crushing-pressure or Coreward death state.

## 10.3 Return Rule

Normal interdimensional return also requires Deepgate infrastructure.

The following are **not** alternate normal exits:

- Ancient Lifts;
- Abyss Lifts;
- Pressure Tunnels;
- Worldroot Descents;
- Worldroot Bastions;
- Machine Shafts;
- Natural Folds;
- Coreward Chasms;
- ordinary upward travel.

## 10.4 Legacy Access Reclassification

Older source access families are retained only through reclassification:

| Legacy Access Concept | FCC-06 Canonical Role |
|---|---|
| Deep Anchors | Stabilisation, portal component context, route infrastructure |
| Seismic Gates | Ancient/local Deep architecture, clue site, internal infrastructure; not second portal family |
| Ancient Lifts | Internal Deep transport |

| Pressure Tunnels | Internal routes |
| Machine Shafts | Internal routes and future World-Engine interface placeholder |
| Natural Folds | Intra-realm topological shortcuts |

## 10.5 Deep-Native Upgrades

Once Impossible Deep resources are available, later Deepgate upgrades may use materials or technologies such as:

- Pressure Stone;
- Abyssal Iron;
- Seismic Crystal;
- Pressure Pearl;
- Ventglass;
- Gravity Nodule technology;
- Deepfire Ember.

Possible improvements include:

- stability;
- cargo throughput;
- pressure-safe arrival;
- emergency recovery;
- advanced destination binding.

These upgrades do not create another portal type.

## 10.6 Destination Progression

Initial entry targets a validated safe arrival envelope.

Later progression may permit discovered, mapped and stabilised Deep destinations to become valid portal targets under the universal destination system.

Extremely dangerous Coreward targeting is late/advanced progression.

The first Deepgate does not permit immediate endgame destination skipping.

# 13. Difficulty and Progression Gradient

Danger generally increases toward more extreme, Coreward and unstable regions, but this is **not an absolute global difficulty floor**.

A deep civilisation may maintain a highly safe habitat.

A shallower region may become extremely dangerous because of:

- seismic crisis;
- Void corruption;
- pressure failure;
- hostile occupation;
- Stoneflow;
- collapsed infrastructure;
- Blackwater upwelling.

Therefore:

> **Depth influences difficulty, but local state, ecology, infrastructure and civilisation determine actual risk.**

This prevents the realm from becoming a rigid numerical dungeon ladder.

# 15. Civilisation, Ancestry and Adaptation Boundary

- biological ancestry;
- culture;
- faction;
- citizenship;
- settlement identity;
- pressure adaptation method;
- gravity adaptation;
- profession;

# 30. Realm-Local Lock Summary

The Impossible Deep is a complete major realm whose identity rests on **depth without simple geometry**.

Its core laws are:

- pressure matters;
- gravity is regional;
- physical down and conceptual deeper can diverge;
- distance can fold through bounded topology;
- some stone can flow through bounded geology states;
- seismic events can permanently change routes and world state;
- Deep civilisation is adapted rather than temporary;
- Deepgate is the only normal physical realm-access method;
- universal death remains universal;
- strange Deep physics are not automatically Void corruption;
- implementation may simplify calculation, but not authoritative world truth.

The realm is content-complete for FCC purposes while remaining intentionally capable of future expansion into even more remote and unknown depths.
