# ADR-0003 — Leyforge and Zylann ownership and fork policy

## Context / Problem

The project needs an explicit boundary between upstream code, local adapters, patches and any maintained fork before third-party source is admitted.

## Proposed Decision

Reconstruct the intended ownership mode and decide it with provenance, patching, sync, build and exit obligations visible. Direct edits to an unidentified upstream copy remain prohibited.

## Reconstruction Basis

B-OPS-06 foundational backlog and ENG-GOV-15. No fork status is asserted at R5.

## Reconsideration Triggers

- `DEP-ZYLANN` receives a verified source and revision.
- Local modification requirements are known.
