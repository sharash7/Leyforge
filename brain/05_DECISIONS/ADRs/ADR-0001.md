# ADR-0001 — Godot V1 platform foundation

## Context / Problem

Branch B identifies the Godot V1 platform choice as foundational. The later PRD audit and owner-locked PROD architecture converge on Godot 4.7.2 stable as the production engine-shell baseline. The owner accepted this ADR on 26 September 2026.

## Authoritative Constraints

- R8 gameplay permission is closed.
- No legacy POC runtime or inferred engine version may be installed as R5 evidence.
- The fixed Godot 4.8-dev runner is proof tooling only and must not silently become the production engine.
- Production implementation remains closed until PG-00 admission and governed P01 work.

## Accepted Decision

Adopt **Godot 4.7.2 stable** as the production engine-shell baseline for PROD/P01. Godot supplies runtime, editor, platform and service facilities beneath Leyforge-owned semantic, simulation, persistence, protocol, content and Forge authority.

Acceptance of this ADR approves the version line and ownership boundary only. It does not install Godot, open PG-00, begin P01 or certify a binary. P01 must still pin the exact official acquisition artifact, hashes, platform/export profile and reproducible diagnostics before activation or completion.

## Reconstruction Basis

B-OPS-06 and ENG-GOV-15 provide the foundational dependency rules. PRD-03 retains Godot 4.7.2 stable as the audited reference baseline while treating 4.8-dev evidence as non-authoritative. PROD-00 and PROD-03 carry that baseline into the owner-locked corpus. This accepted decision does not itself activate Godot.

## Reconsideration Triggers

- The owner approves, rejects or revises the PROD production baseline.
- A later controlled technology delta qualifies a different stable version.
- Supported platforms, Zylann compatibility, persistence, export or lifecycle evidence disqualifies the proposed baseline.
