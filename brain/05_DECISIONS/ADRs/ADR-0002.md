# ADR-0002 — Zylann Voxel foundation

## Context / Problem

Branch B lists the voxel foundation as an architecture-critical dependency decision. The rebuild contains no active addon and R5 cannot infer a revision from the retired POC.

## Proposed Decision

Reconstruct and evaluate the intended Zylann Voxel basis at the architecture/dependency gate, with exact upstream revision, licence, supported Godot pairing and proof evidence.

## Reconstruction Basis

B-OPS-06 and ENG-GOV-15. This record preserves the backlog without accepting or installing the dependency.

## Reconsideration Triggers

- Voxel proof programme and architecture gate are authorized.
- Exact compatibility and performance evidence exists.
